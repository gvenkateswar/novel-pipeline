# Reader-test blurbs

*October 27, 2026. One blurb per deep-checked angle, written to the PG-13 packaging rule in the brief: the heat is signalled, never described. Each is built to avoid the blurb complaints found in review mining (overselling darkness or angst, names that don't match the book, promising a payoff the book doesn't deliver). Meant for a quick preference test with 5–10 readers of each category, or for the author to read cold.*

## A3 — Romantic suspense, protection plot (working title: *Ten Days Out*)

Beth Calloway has worked the Cutter Island ferry for eleven years and never once looked at what the night crews unload. The one time she does, two men see her do it.

Deputy US Marshal Cal Reyes is sent to keep her alive until she can testify. The plan is simple: one week at a safe house on the mainland. Then a November storm shuts the ferry down, the island is cut off for ten days, and the safe house becomes Beth's own cottage with a stranger sleeping on the couch.

Cal is careful, quiet, and better at reading her than anyone has been in years. He is also the only person who knew where she would be the night someone tried the back door.

Ten days. One way off the island. And the man she can't stop wanting may be the reason the men who want her dead keep finding her.

*A steamy, standalone romantic suspense with a guaranteed happy ending. Book one of the Cutter Island series.*

## A1 — Second-chance midlife, open door (working title: *Sixty Days*)

Dana Whitaker is forty-seven, newly divorced, and sixty days from losing the restaurant she spent twenty years building. The kitchen failed inspection. The landlord won't extend. And the only contractor in Harlow who can rebuild it in time is Nick Serrano, the man she left standing at a bus station when she was twenty-four.

Nick is a widower now, with a teenage daughter, a reputation for never missing a deadline, and no interest in hearing why Dana walked away. He takes the job because the town needs the restaurant. He makes it clear that's the only reason.

Sixty days of early mornings, shared coffee, and the slow discovery that the two people they were at twenty-four aren't the two people in the kitchen now.

Dana has been running from this town for half her life. This time, there's nowhere left to go.

*A steamy second-chance romance for readers who like their heroines grown up and their heroes slow to forgive. Book one of the Harlow series.*

## Notes for the test

- Ask readers which book they would open first, and what heat level they expect from each blurb. The A1 test matters most: if readers expect a sweet or closed-door book from the Harlow blurb, the packaging needs a stronger steam signal.
- Ask whether the A3 blurb reads as romance-first or thriller-first. Pen Pal and Lights Out were punished for the mismatch.
- Both blurbs name the series and promise the happy ending outright, because the A1 comps were punished for blurbs that oversold angst and the A3 comps for blurbs that hid the ending.
