# Chapter 10 Issues

1. **MUST-FIX.** Contradicts Ch 09. Ellen made the cash number at 4:40 p.m. at the office adding machine, with pencil labels. At 2:15 she was running departures, and the red marker was used at 4:10 a.m. and around noon.
   - Quote: "She had made that number herself, at two-fifteen on Saturday, with a red marker."
   - Fix: "She had made that number herself, at twenty to five on Saturday, on the adding machine."

2. **MUST-FIX.** Echoes "Count it twice," which the beat sheet banned (pre-flight 9). The candy count of fourteen also duplicates "Fourteen minutes" in Scene 4.
   - Quote: "There were fourteen butterscotch candies in the bowl. She counted them again, under her breath, and got fourteen."
   - Fix: "There were nineteen butterscotch candies in the bowl. She counted them under her breath."

3. **MUST-FIX.** The core of the declaration is ambiguous, and it departs from the Ch 07 sentence. Ch 07 reads "And you would always— Whether it was you I—", and Ray reads it as "me or the money". "The two" has no named antecedent. The narration says "wrong order" in Ch 07 but "backward" here.
   - Quote: "At your truck I had it backward," she said. "It was you first. I wanted you before I knew whether I could carry this building. If I had taken your money, you would have spent the rest of your life wondering which of the two I wanted, and I would have let you. I love you."
   - Fix: "At your truck I put it in the wrong order. It was you first. I wanted you before I knew whether I could carry this building. If I had taken your money, you would have spent the rest of your life wondering whether I wanted you or the check, and I would have let you. I love you."

4. **SHOULD-FIX.** The declaration repeats a claim Ray already rebutted in Ch 07 ("I'd have known it was me"), and Ellen conceded she had no answer. Ch 10 never acknowledges his rebuttal.
   - Quote: "you would have spent the rest of your life wondering whether I wanted you or the check" (as revised in item 3)
   - Fix: Insert before "and I would have let you": "You told me you would have known it was you. I would have spent the rest of my life checking."

5. **SHOULD-FIX.** Banking logic gap. Judy's stated objection is that the underwriter could not see Saturday's cash on the 28th. His one question, whether any of it belonged to someone else, addresses ownership and does not address visibility. Nothing on the page says what makes the Monday-deposited cash countable.
   - Quote: "Approved. With a condition. You keep twenty-five thousand minimum on deposit, reported to me monthly, through next February. He wants to see the safe from now on."
   - Fix: Before "Approved." add Judy: "He'll take the safe because your register report and Monday's slip match to the dollar."

6. **SHOULD-FIX.** Ray's declaration undercuts his Ch 09 line "I'd like to be asked in" and his profile (he never enters uninvited). It also undercuts the coda's "asking me first" theme.
   - Quote: "I'm going to fix the hinges on anything you own whether you ask or not. I love you."
   - Fix: "I'm going to fix the hinges on anything you own, and write each one on the card. I love you."

7. **SHOULD-FIX.** Ellen's interior recaps Ch 07 in two sentences and restates the 6:28 figure. The beat sheet allows one sentence (rule 21).
   - Quote: "She had said this once already, on the second of February, to her own windshield, somewhere inside six minutes and twenty-eight seconds. She had got every word of it right, and he had been forty yards away in a dark truck."
   - Fix: "She had said this once already, to her own windshield."

8. **SHOULD-FIX.** Ambiguous antecedent. After sentences about Judy, "Her face" reads as Judy's. The idea that her calm hides things is also now in its third chapter (Ch 07, Ch 09, Ch 10).
   - Quote: "Her face would look exactly like this right up until Judy shook her head, and nobody in the lobby would know the difference."
   - Fix: "Ellen's own face, in the glass, would look exactly like this right up until Judy shook her head."

9. **SHOULD-FIX.** The "Feb 2 — they split" square is not in Ch 07 final or the continuity log. The outline has Denise cross it out, not the holder. "Gerry" is a new unplanted name (walk-ons stay unnamed, and Normie is the one named Bench member).
   - Quote: "February second." She tapped the line drawn through it. "'They split.' Whoever wrote that crossed it out himself Saturday, which I'll give him." … "Gerry's sorest."
   - Fix: "February second. 'They split.' Somebody's idea of a joke. I drew the line through it that night." … "Normie's sorest."

10. **SHOULD-FIX.** "Across the bottom of the sheet" collides with the WEDDING DATE row, which is the bottom row and must stay legible and outermost.
    - Quote: "She wrote across the bottom of the sheet in pen. NO WINNER. POT TO DERBY KIDS DIV."
    - Fix: "She wrote across the top of the sheet in pen."

11. **SHOULD-FIX.** Arlene's answer sets up a before/after contrast of priorities (price versus boiler man). The beat sheet warned against this pattern, and contrast constructions are banned outright.
    - Quote: "You asked me the name of my boiler man before you asked my price. I wrote it on the back of your offer."
    - Fix: "You asked me the name of my boiler man. I wrote it on the back of your offer."

12. **SHOULD-FIX.** Arlene's line after "Voyons donc" is a cryptic non sequitur.
    - Quote: "Voyons donc. He's paid by the hour."
    - Fix: "Voyons donc." Arlene went to the door.

13. **SHOULD-FIX.** Scene 2 ends on the keys in Arlene's fist. The beat sheet says do not end on the key.
    - Quote: "Arlene went down one step at a time with the keys in her fist and her other hand on the rail."
    - Fix: "Arlene went down one step at a time, one hand on the rail."

14. **SHOULD-FIX.** Rule 2 (nobody does work they aren't qualified for). Ray alone makes up the connections on a neon sign transformer, and no licensed hand is on the page.
    - Quote: "He opened the disconnect, took the old transformer out, set the new one in its place, and made up the connections the way he had done the store's sign for thirty years."
    - Fix: "He opened the disconnect and took the old transformer out. The sign shop's electrician from Laconia, standing on the shoulder in a hard hat, made up the connections on the new one, and Ellen held the ladder."

15. **SHOULD-FIX.** The Linda's ball thread is left open. The bag was left on the lane 5 settee in Ch 09, and Ch 10 never says where it went. Locker 14 is banned in this chapter, but the ball is not.
    - Quote: "Linda's 187 card was still in the case across the room."
    - Fix: Add after the next paragraph's first sentence: "Linda's ball rode to Tuesday league in its blue bag now, and he bowled it."
