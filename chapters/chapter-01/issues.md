**Issue 1**
- Problem: The reefer unit's history contradicts the bible (Research note 6 and Style Reference: Beth has heard that unit drone across the deck "every Thursday for six years"). The draft says five, and adds a Monday day-boat run that appears nowhere in the bible or outline.
- Location: "Five years of Thursdays, and every Monday on the day boat since, that unit had run so loud you raised your voice to talk over it."
- Suggested fix: Change to "Six years of Thursdays" and cut the Monday day-boat clause.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: Crossing time. The bible fixes the crossing at 45 minutes. Beth's log puts the ramp at Pruett Harbor at 9:44 p.m., after clearing the breakwater at 9:00. Mid-bay would then be about 22 minutes out, not 26. Beth counts when frightened, so a wrong count reads as an error.
- Location: "She counted instead. Twenty-six minutes to Pruett Harbor."
- Suggested fix: Change to "Twenty-two minutes to Pruett Harbor."

**Issue 3**
- Problem: Deck geography. The deckhouse sits on the starboard side of the car deck, so its door and the ramp-light switch face the car deck (inboard/port face). Walking "along the starboard side of the deckhouse" puts her on the outboard side between the deckhouse and the bulwark, away from the door and switch. Ch 09 depends on this geography.
- Location: "She crossed the stern and came forward along the starboard side of the deckhouse at the speed she always walked, which was not fast."
- Suggested fix: Replace "the starboard side of the deckhouse" with "the deckhouse wall" so she approaches the door and switch from the car-deck side.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: Vehicle order is inconsistent. The reefer is "nose to the ramp", so on a bow-loader it is nearest the bow ramp and drives off first at Pruett. The draft unloads the propane truck and van first and the reefer last. The opening also has Beth check the propane truck and van before the reefer, then walk aft past the reefer to "the rest of the line", although those two are already done.
- Location: "Then the trucks. The propane truck, the van. Then the reefer. She had to release its binders herself, standing beside the cab."
- Suggested fix: Rework the Pruett unloading so the reefer is released first, and put the propane truck and van forward or aft of it consistently with the opening walk.

**Issue 5**
- Problem: Height. The wheelhouse and its wing sit on top of the deckhouse, reached by an eleven-rung ladder. A wing "three feet above" a person on the car deck is impossible.
- Location: "Ned was out on the wing three feet above her, calling for the stern line."
- Suggested fix: Change to "ten feet above her".

**Issue 6**
- Problem: The bank bag's route conflicts with the outline's Ch 02 plant. There, Dane arrives on the inbound morning boat and sees Tess hand the bag to the deckhand for the return run. Ch 01 says the bag "rode the first boat over" each morning, and Beth calls the 8:00 out of Pruett "the first one". That implies a Cutter-out boat about 7:15, earlier than Dane's boat, which Dane could not watch. The Pruett 8:00 departure (arrives Cutter about 8:45) needs recording in the continuity log.
- Location: "The bag rode the first boat over to the credit union every morning, half the island's money in a canvas zip bag with Tess's initials on the tag." and "That's the first one," Beth said.
- Suggested fix: Change the bag line to "The bag rode the morning boat back to the credit union" and make Beth's reply "That's the first one out of Pruett."

**Issue 7**
- Problem: Unexplained delay. Beth sees the plastic bricks at about 8:40 p.m., photographs the plate at 9:44, is home by about 10:35, and does not phone until 1:10 a.m. The draft gives no reason for waiting nearly three hours. The bible forbids a senseless choice in danger, and the outline's 1 a.m. report needs a reason the reader can see.
- Location: "The stove clock said 1:10."
- Suggested fix: Add one sentence in Beth's counting mode giving her reason for waiting until 1:10.

**Issue 8**
- Problem: Private talk on an open channel. The bible says everyone hears VHF, and later in this chapter Beth refuses to use the mic for exactly that reason. Ned's bible voice on the radio is clipped and formal. Here Ned and Beth discuss Kenny's illness and sending her home over the handheld.
- Location: ""Beth." Ned, from the wheelhouse. "I can work it short. Go on home."" and "She keyed it. "Kenny's in bed with a bucket.""
- Suggested fix: Move the exchange off the radio, with Ned calling down from the wing and Beth answering aloud or with a hand signal.

**Issue 9**
- Problem: New lock and key on the back door. The bible defines that door by its sticking thumb-latch, and the Ch 02 intruder beat (latch lifts, Dane answers) assumes the latch is the only fastening. A locked door is a new world fact that Ch 02 must account for (unlocking for Dane, whether the door opens at 11:40). It is not recorded in the bible.
- Location: "Nobody on Cutter locked a door. She went looking for the key anyway."
- Suggested fix: Cut the key business and have her wedge the latch or the door with the chair. If the lock stays, add it to the continuity log and Ch 02 beats.

**Issue 10**
- Problem: Stated small-town line. The Genre Clichés ban forbids a stated "everyone knows everyone" line; the book must show it. The final paragraph already shows the exposure (the guest mooring square below her window), and then states it.
- Location: "Everyone on Cutter knew which house was the Calloway place."
- Suggested fix: Delete that sentence and end the paragraph on "so this window sat square above it."

**Issue 11**
- Problem: Chapter heading. The chapter runs from Day 0 into the dawn of Day 1 (the trooper's call at 6:10 a.m., Friday). World Rule 3 requires combined days to read "Day X to Day Y". The Calendar lists Ch 01 as Day 0 and Day 1.
- Location: "**Day 0 — Thursday**"
- Suggested fix: Change to "Day 0 to Day 1 — Thursday to Friday".

**Issue 12**
- Problem: The carton count is not on the page. Beth's log says "14 cartons seen" and the narration says "Fourteen cartons she'd seen in that trailer". The rear-door scene never shows her counting, and the bible has her count when frightened. As written, the number appears from nowhere.
- Location: "Fourteen cartons she'd seen in that trailer, maybe more behind."
- Suggested fix: Add a half-line in the rear-door scene where she counts the stack as she cinches the strap.

**Issue 13**
- Problem: The tank-loan plant is weak. Tess's line drops "tank loan" from the bible's wording, and Beth's narration later says only "that loan". A first-time reader cannot connect "dirt with steel in it" to a fuel-tank debt, which is a required Ch 01 plant for the leak's motive.
- Location: ""Eighteen hundred a month, Bethie." Tess dropped the slip into the canvas bag. "For dirt with steel in it. Christ on a bike.""
- Suggested fix: Have Tess say "That tank loan's eighteen hundred a month, Bethie. For dirt with steel in it."
