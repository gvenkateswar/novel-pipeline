## Chapter 01 — Night Freight (Day 0 to Day 1, Thursday to Friday)

**What happened:** On the 8:40 pm night freight run, Beth finds the lobster reefer's unit dead and a sprung lid showing vacuum-sealed bricks under the top layer; the hooded man (unnamed; favors his left leg) and the driver (unnamed) see her see it. She reseals the lid as routine ("you'll lose your count"), counts fourteen cartons, rides the crossing in the wheelhouse with Ned, photographs the plate and trailer number under the Pruett Harbor ramp lights at 9:44 pm, and from her kitchen landline at 1:10 am reports it to the State Police barracks, having waited on purpose so the stop could not be traced to the ferry. At dawn the trooper calls back: the truck was stopped on Route 1; a federal agent will call; the Marshals Service is sending someone on the first boat (the 8:00 out of Pruett, arriving about 8:45). A nor'easter is building (barometer 1004 at supper, 1000 at 1 am, 996 at dawn). Ned says he'll work the boat short. Beth switches off the kitchen light.
**What changed:** Beth is a witness. Two men know her face. The storm is coming.
**Who knows what:** Beth knows the plate, trailer number, the fourteen cartons, the dead reefer, and the hooded man's limp. She has told the State Police only. Ned knows she saw something and is keeping her off the next run ("work it short"). Tess knows nothing yet on the page (but see plants). The reader does not know the hooded man's name, the driver's name, or what the bricks are; "fentanyl" is not used.
**Objects / details introduced:** Beth's phone photo of the plate (9:44 pm). The paycheck handed to Tess at 4 pm for the morning bank bag; the bag rides the 8:00 morning boat back to the credit union. Tess's tank loan: "eighteen hundred a month." The fuel float at Tess Marr's lit at nine with the Margaret Ann (red hull, named on the page only by description: red-hulled dragger) alongside; Tess closes the pump at six every night. Ned's hearing aid (he switches it off). The back door has a sticking thumb-latch and no lock; Beth wedged a chair under the latch bar on Day 0 night (Ch 02 must show the chair moved before the 11:40 pm latch beat). The guest mooring lies square below Beth's bedroom window; the dragger moved to it overnight. Kenny has the Thursday night runs since last winter and is sick ("in bed with a bucket"). Reefer history: six years of Thursdays. Ramp controls are worked from the wheelhouse at both slips. VHF in the wheelhouse monitors 16 and 13.
**Ends on:** Dawn, Day 1 (Friday, November 13). Beth at the kitchen window, the dragger on the guest mooring below, storm building, Marshals expected on the 8:00 boat. She switches off the kitchen light.



## Chapter 02 — The Weather Hold (Day 1, Friday)

**What happened:** Dane Reyes arrives on the 8:00 boat from Pruett (lands ~8:45); from the passenger deck he sees a short woman in a red vest (Tess, unnamed to him) hand a canvas bank bag up to Ned at the rail, reads it as island banking, notes Ned will not look at him, and reads MARGARET ANN, GLOUCESTER, MASS. off the dragger's transom on the guest mooring. He serves Beth the grand jury subpoena (Monday, November 23) and says "fentanyl" once. Plan is the 2 pm boat to a mainland safe house with a second deputy meeting them at Pruett. Ned cancels the 2 pm at 1:15 for weather. Beth and Dane agree to leave the chair off the back-door latch (second exit; the sticking latch is an alarm); Beth moves it herself. While Dane checks the windows from the road, Beth phones Tess on the landline to be counted for the storm net privately ("home, don't send anybody, keep it off the air"); Tess drops a coin, laughs late, mentions she is net control and emergency management director, and says "Channel 9 if the phones go." Beth tells Dane she has "taken care of the check-in." The storm wrecks the slip; Ned gives the ten-day repair estimate over the VHF; landlines jam; Dane calls Pike from the yard on the Iridium; Pike says Monday is the last grand jury sitting in time (Pelletier's Rule 5.1 clock). "Ten days counting today is Sunday the twenty-second. That's one to spare." At 11:20 pm Beth sees Dane on the porch on the sat phone through the two-inch shade gap, something small and flat in his other hand. At 11:40 the back-door latch lifts; Beth wakes Dane without a light and gets behind the chimney mass; whoever it was leaves. "Who knew you were here?" "You." Dane reloads the stove and leaves the blanket on the couch arm.
**What changed:** They are cut off for ten days. Someone knows she stayed. Beth's first suspicion of Dane is on the page.
**Who knows what:** Beth knows Tess knows she is home; she has not told Dane about the call's content, only that the check-in is handled. Dane knows nothing of the call. Ned knows Beth missed the 9:15 but not where she slept. Dane does not yet have the order to assess Beth (that comes Day 2). The reader has met Tess (named by Beth), Ned, Pike (by name on the phone), Pelletier (by name), the Margaret Ann (by name). The hooded man and the skipper remain unnamed.
**Objects / details introduced:** Dane's Iridium sat phone; his Rite in the Rain notebook (not yet seen clearly by Beth). The subpoena. The front-room shade left up two inches. Barometer: 992 after the morning Pike call, then 994→990→986 sequence as the day falls (only falls). The Ruthie C (a boat Beth remembers on channel 9). Tess's coin drop. Dane: "Half this island watched me walk up your road. Nobody knows you stayed." The chair now sits by the stove.
**Ends on:** Night of Day 1, after the latch. Beth back in her room, Dane on the couch, the blanket on the arm, door unlatched by choice.

## Chapter 03 — The Check-In Log (Day 2, Saturday)

**What happened:** Morning: a boot print with a heavy right heel on the back step; Beth keeps the limp to herself. Dane picks the Ashby place on the north point from Ned's three shut houses (Lathams' on the cove road, Hallorans' behind the church); Beth names the shore path over the bar at low water and rules it out ("nobody comes in that way"). Beth overhears Dane on the sat phone out past the eave: "No. She doesn't know. ... Daily. Understood." (the Day 2 order: the $9,000 deposit, assess her, don't tell her; Beth hears only fragments and reads "she" as a wife or a judge). The chair-and-hands beat: she sees him choose the chair; he looks at her hands. Russ, on storm rounds, takes the prowler report and asks where she'll sleep ("Asking because somebody will"). Russ says he'll walk the shore path that afternoon. The store, in daylight, with Dane: the check-in log on the counter shows "Calloway — home (phone)" with a Saturday 0800 tick (Tess counted her anyway with the line dead); Tess's phone buzzes when nobody else has signal ("the booster's on the generator"; any phone gets a bar inside the store); Tess finishes Ned's barge-schedule sentence before he says it on the radio; Ned tells the bridge story with his one "goddamn"; Ned says the dealer "always runs his reefer unit," which Beth knows is false; Arsenault (named in Beth's thoughts only; "from away"; the dragger is his) comes to the counter and looks at Dane; Beth tells Dane the six others are island. Tess: "Coffee's double for you. Off-island rate." "Generator's eating forty a day." At the propane cage, with the generator covering their voices, Ned gives Beth the Ashby key (brass Schlage, tag torn off and pocketed) and says "You stay off my boat till this is done." Beth hands the key to Dane; they reason it out: Ned lied about the truck, which makes him complicit, not the one who tried a door he has a key to; Dane asked Portland for a boat and the Coast Guard won't put a crew out till the sea lies down. The Ashby drive is 340 steps. In the generator shed (20 kW diesel standby, hand primer, no pull cord) a held moment: his hand stops; he steps back first, holds the door, takes the heavier jug. Night at the Ashby place; Beth hears the 11:20 porch call.
**What changed:** They have moved. Beth has a reason to doubt Dane ("She doesn't know") and a reason to doubt Ned (the lie). Suspicion has spread to the store. First real charged contact.
**Who knows what:** Ned knows they are at the Ashby place (he gave the key) and tells Tess everything (not shown yet; Ch 08 admits it). Tess knows Beth stayed home Day 1 and will learn the Ashby move from Ned. Russ knows there was a prowler and has asked where she sleeps. Dane now has the order and the $9,000 fact and has not told Beth. Beth knows: Ned lied; Tess has signal; Arsenault owns the dragger. Arsenault has seen Dane.
**Objects / details introduced:** Boot print, heavy right heel. The check-in log and the 0800 tick. The cell booster on the store generator. Ned's cage key on an orange float; the Ashby key. The Ashby generator shed and hand primer. 340 steps of drive. Russ's shore-path walk (afternoon of Day 2). Dane asked Portland for a boat; refused until the sea lies down (sets up the Ch 06 window).
**Ends on:** Night of Day 2 at the Ashby place, after the 11:20 call.

## Chapter 04 — Glassing the Harbor (Day 3, Sunday)

**What happened:** First light: Beth and Dane go to Lookout Hill by the woods path, Dane armed. Through the glasses Beth picks out the sternman's walk (left-leg limp, heavy right heel) as he walks round her empty cottage and bails the skiff with a cut-down jug; Ned, on the float, turns his back on the skiff. Beth tells Dane the heel matched the print and that she waited to watch him walk before saying so ("Third piling from the ladder, Reyes. He's bailing." is the only time she says "Reyes" aloud). From the hilltop the handheld reaches the store: Tess runs a warm 0800 net (Mrs. Dyer, the April credit, "home and dry", "I mean it, Captain"); nothing sinister. Dane on the sat phone: "Quist ran the boat ... I'll ask her." Dane glosses Quist for Beth: case agent, Homeland Security, what he sees goes to her; "she'd prefer we do nothing clever." Beth registers that "She picked him out" and "I'll ask her" are about her and sets them beside Saturday's "She doesn't know"; she does not ask. On the camp road Russ, leaning on his tailgate with a cooling thermos cap, is too curious about the Ashby generator; he says he walked her shore path yesterday ("Alders to the ledge, ledge to the cove road. He knew the way. Rain had the rest.") and "Still mine, Calloway." In the generator shed Beth teaches the fuel-line bleed (day tank ran dry; hand primer, bleed screw) one injector nut at a time while Dane asks about money ("Anything come in lately?"); he talks about Lowell, his bus-driver father and dispatcher mother, his father asking every Christmas whether he's got a horse yet, Spanish fine with his father and bad on the phone, why he does the work, and one line on the lost witness: "She asked for ten minutes. I gave them to her." The kiss: wanted on both sides; she pulls him round until the door is behind him; he stills his hand, looks over his shoulder at the open door, stops: "I can't keep you alive with my hands on you. That's not no. Not with that door open behind me." He holds on to the rag before he lets go. She says "Dane." for the first time. The fish house is mentioned in passing. Night: the furnace ticks on at 11:10; at 11:20 Beth goes to the dark front room and watches the porch call through the gap in the sheet; Dane reopens the notebook he had closed; his pen is still moving.
**What changed:** Beth has identified the hooded man by his walk. The attraction is open between them and stopped on his terms. Beth has three "she" fragments stacked against Dane and has not asked. Suspicion now has reasons to touch Russ (generator curiosity, shore path) and Ned (turned his back).
**Who knows what:** Dane knows Beth has identified the sternman and that Quist is running the boat. Beth knows Quist exists and reports to her. Russ knows where they are (the generator) and that she goes about with Dane. Tess has had no contact with Beth since the Day 1 call on the page (the net is open air). Ned has seen the skiff and looked away.
**Objects / details introduced:** Cut-down bailing jug. Hauled boats Gray Lady and Hartley's Lisa B.; the Pinkhams' woodpile. The sheet over the Ashby front-room window with a gap. The day tank, bleed screw, injector nuts. "Not with that door open behind me."
**Ends on:** Night of Day 3. Beth in the dark front room; Dane on the porch, notebook open, pen moving.

## Chapter 05 — Half Tide (Day 4, Monday) — MIDPOINT

**What happened:** Morning, dead low water: Beth walks the bar with Dane and points out the fish house (her father's; "the one I pay taxes on"; nobody in it in four years). Dane: "Your constable walked this Saturday. Whitcomb." (Russ's surname first on the page.) Beth: at low water, in daylight, with a constable's reason; nobody sets a watch on the bar and nobody tries it on a flood. They pack go-bags (Icom switched off in a freezer bag; Pelican case; the gun) by the seaward door. 10 pm: the generator shed burns; a windshield waits at the mouth of the drive; they go out by the shore path on the young flood, knee-deep, 48°F, ninety seconds, her hand on his belt; across the bar to the fish house. Dane's sat call: "We moved. Secondary location. Not on this phone. Not where." Sex scene 1 (875 words; hands and mouths; no intercourse; "It's a very disappointing kit"): he says "When this is over, I report this"; she says "I know what you are. I'm choosing anyway." Afterward she tells him about the Friday check-in call to Tess and the open log on the counter. The suspect pool widens: Friday's pool (who knew she stayed home) is Dane, Ned, Tess, plus anyone who read the log before the power went; Saturday's (who knew about Ashby) is Ned, Dane, and whoever Ned told ("Ned tells Tess. Everybody knows that." / "That's not talking."). They turn from hiding to hunting: "We're done hiding." She writes the list on a paint-can lid: Ned; Russ; Saturday — six at the store (with a blank line for Friday's counter); Arsenault; Crew (2) with a line off the 2 to "the heel". Tess is not on the lid; the narration does not point at it. Dane: "He'd met Tess once, across a counter. Whatever he thought of that, he kept."
**What changed:** The Ashby place is burned out. They are in the fish house, which nobody but the two of them knows about. They have crossed into physical intimacy and into open suspicion of the island. Beth has told Dane the one thing she was hiding. Dane has told Pike they moved but not where.
**Who knows what:** Only Beth and Dane know the fish house. Tess learned the Ashby location from Ned (Ch 08 admits it). Pike knows they moved. Beth now knows Dane will report the relationship. Dane now knows about the Tess call and the log.
**Objects / details introduced:** The fish house: oil-drum stove, bench, lantern, her father's square capitals, the paint-can lid list. Go-bags. The seaward door. The windshield at the drive mouth (car never identified). Dane's "I report this" line.
**Ends on:** Night of Day 4 in the fish house, the list on the lid, Dane reading it upside down.
