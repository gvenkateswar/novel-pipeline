## Chapter entries

## Chapter 01 — Night Freight (Day 0 to Day 1, Thursday to Friday)

**What happened:** On the 8:40 pm night freight run, Beth finds the lobster reefer's unit dead and a sprung lid showing vacuum-sealed bricks under the top layer; the hooded man (unnamed; favors his left leg) and the driver (unnamed) see her see it. She reseals the lid as routine ("you'll lose your count"), counts fourteen cartons, rides the crossing in the wheelhouse with Ned, photographs the plate and trailer number under the Pruett Harbor ramp lights at 9:44 pm, and from her kitchen landline at 1:10 am reports it to the State Police barracks, having waited on purpose so the stop could not be traced to the ferry. At dawn the trooper calls back: the truck was stopped on Route 1; a federal agent will call; the Marshals Service is sending someone on the first boat (the 8:00 out of Pruett, arriving about 8:45). A nor'easter is building (barometer 1004 at supper, 1000 at 1 am, 996 at dawn). Ned says he'll work the boat short. Beth switches off the kitchen light.
**What changed:** Beth is a witness. Two men know her face. The storm is coming.
**Who knows what:** Beth knows the plate, trailer number, the fourteen cartons, the dead reefer, and the hooded man's limp. She has told the State Police only. Ned knows she saw something and is keeping her off the next run ("work it short"). Tess knows nothing yet on the page (but see plants). The reader does not know the hooded man's name, the driver's name, or what the bricks are; "fentanyl" is not used.
**Objects / details introduced:** Beth's phone photo of the plate (9:44 pm). The paycheck handed to Tess at 4 pm for the morning bank bag; the bag rides the 8:00 morning boat back to the credit union. Tess's tank loan: "eighteen hundred a month." The fuel float at Tess Marr's lit at nine with the Margaret Ann (red hull, named on the page only by description: red-hulled dragger) alongside; Tess closes the pump at six every night. Ned's hearing aid (he switches it off). The back door has a sticking thumb-latch and no lock; Beth wedged a chair under the latch bar on Day 0 night (Ch 02 must show the chair moved before the 11:40 pm latch beat). The guest mooring lies square below Beth's bedroom window; the dragger moved to it overnight. Kenny has the Thursday night runs since last winter and is sick ("in bed with a bucket"). Reefer history: six years of Thursdays. Ramp controls are worked from the wheelhouse at both slips. VHF in the wheelhouse monitors 16 and 13.
**Ends on:** Dawn, Day 1 (Friday, November 13). Beth at the kitchen window, the dragger on the guest mooring below, storm building, Marshals expected on the 8:00 boat. She switches off the kitchen light.
