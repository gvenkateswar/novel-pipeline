# The series factory: plan (Oct 28, 2026)

**Goal (author's words):** a novel series factory with as few human gates as possible. Aspirational, aim high.

**Constraint we proved this week:** the pipeline can write a book; it cannot yet tell whether the book is boring. Both finished books passed every simulated read and bored the author. A gate can only be removed once something else predicts his verdict. So the factory is built as a **trust ladder**: automate the judgment, measure it against the few human verdicts we have, and retire each human gate when the machine matches him.

## What runs without the author (this build)

1. **Tournaments instead of single drafts.** For every opening chapter, every sample-mark chapter, and any scene a reader scores ≤ 2, the writer produces **three** variants from the same beats (with a different lead instruction each). A **judge panel** reads them blind, in pairs, and picks a winner. Judges answer only "which one would you keep reading" and "where did each one lose you." Three judge personas (buyer, impatient skimmer, craft reader) across two models, so no single voice decides. Procedure: `docs/judging.md`. Agent: `.claude/agents/judge.md`.
2. **Calibration anchors.** `docs/calibration.md` holds every real human verdict we have, with the text it was about. Judge prompts cite them. Every new human verdict (author, later paid readers) is added, and the judges are re-run on the anchors to measure agreement. Agreement is the number that retires gates.
3. **The human gate becomes a veto window.** On autopilot the pipeline sends the sample and keeps going. The author has 48 hours to say stop. Silence is not approval, it is "not yet contradicted": the sample's verdict stays open in the brief and is settled by the next real-reader signal (author, paid readers, or sales).
4. **Pull-gate failures are handled by tournament, not by line edits.** A scene at ≤ 2 or a sample under 3.5 triggers variants at the beat level, judged, then the normal pipeline on the winner.
5. **Series layout.** `books/<series-slug>/series-bible.md` (recurring cast, town, running threads, read-through hooks, what each book promises the next) sits above the per-book folders. The template gains series fields.
6. **Unattended runs.** A scheduled task picks up the next pending step for each active book, commits, pushes, and reports at milestones. Set up after the first book clears its sample under the new gates.
7. **Market as the final gate.** Book 1 of a series ships cheap in KU; page reads and read-through to book 2 decide whether books 2–5 get written. The listing pack (blurb, keywords, categories, cover brief) is generated by the pipeline; the author uploads.

## What we cannot do yet, and why

- **Compare against current bestsellers.** Amazon blocks the web tools; Kindle samples can only be pulled through the author's Chrome when his computer is linked. Until then the judges compare variants against each other and against our known-boring anchors.
- **Run outside models as judges.** Needs API keys. Until then, judge diversity comes from personas and from the two Claude models available here.
- **Know how real buyers behave.** Paid readers or sales. Everything above is a proxy.

## Measuring the ladder

`docs/calibration.md` records, for each human verdict: the text, the verdict, and what each judge persona said about the same text. The score is simple agreement (did the judge's keep/stop match the human's). We report it in every milestone message. No gate is retired below 80% on at least 10 fresh samples.
