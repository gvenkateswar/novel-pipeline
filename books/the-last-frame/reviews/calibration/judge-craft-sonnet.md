# Judge report: Craft persona (Sonnet)

A = anchor-A1-ch01.md
B = anchor-A3-ch01.md

1. **Winner: B**

2. **Why:** B lets the narration want something (she wants him to keep arguing, the ball is warm from his hand), so the sentences have a pulse, while A is a woman doing competent things in neat short lines and I admired it from outside.

3. **Where A lost me:** At about 58%, the incident report to Ray: "Lane seven. Second string, eight-eleven. The table stopped twelve inches above the deck on its way down. The sweep is down. The motor ran four seconds after the table stopped, then quit. There's a burned smell at the back. I cut the power at the panel. I touched nothing." That is competence on display. The index cards, the flight-strip columns and the lineage piles had already started the thumb moving. Denise's voice is the best thing in A, but the book keeps leaving her for procedure.

4. **Where B lost me:** At about 55%, the three-step device collapsing: "First, the ball, which she now had. Third, she would not look at his mouth, and she had already looked, and somewhere she had dropped the second item, the ruling, Verna's, final..." The joke is explained and run too long, and the paragraph before it ("she could tell through the ball, or believed she could, the moment he decided...") is overworked. The long comma-chained sentences also blur Ray and Danny into one slow talker.

5. **Confidence: medium.** A has sharper side characters and cleaner dialogue exchanges, and B's prose is baggier and sometimes trips over itself. But B is the one I'd keep reading.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. Same cast, same ATC-to-bowling jokes (altitude, "cleared to bowl", the Verna ruling, the locksmith call), the same pool-sheet ending beat. The sentence rhythm is very different, clipped in A and run-on in B, so I wouldn't swear to it.
