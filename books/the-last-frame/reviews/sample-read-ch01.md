## 8. What I think is going on

Linda Gagnon is Ray's late wife. Locker fourteen was hers, and his key is the only one that fits it. He wanted the alley because it's where she bowled, and that's why he's been fixing machines for free for twenty years. Ellen was an air traffic controller who had to retire at 56. She'll come up short of the quarter million by March, because tonight she already came up short. Ray will fix lane seven anyway, and she'll end up holding the light for the locksmith tomorrow morning. Ray is the one who won't take a square in the pool, so he's on her side already. My guess for the end is that they own it together, or he lets the March deadline pass.
