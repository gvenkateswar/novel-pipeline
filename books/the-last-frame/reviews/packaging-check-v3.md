## What the manuscript promises that the packaging must carry

- The first chapter puts a woman behind a counter counting a room, a man who will not give back a key, a dead sign and a bet on when she quits. A cover that shows a couple embracing under fairy lights promises a different book. Cover brief: the alley (the K on its pole, lit or half-lit), winter dusk, no faces or two figures at a counter; type that says dry rather than sweet.
- The blurb must lead with the deadline (ninety days to make the note), the key, and the bet, and say the hero is the man who buys the building if she misses. That is the book's hook and it is on the page from Ch 01 s1.
- The blurb must not promise a comedy of errors, a grumpy-sunshine pairing or a small-town ensemble romp. The town is on the page but it does not drive the plot.

5. **Cover brief:** alley and dusk, not a couple under lights; "novella" on the product page.
