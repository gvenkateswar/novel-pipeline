## MUST-FIX

1. **Ch 01 vs Ch 04 and Ch 05, the sheet cake.**
   - Quote: "The cake had been vanilla."
   - Conflict: Ch04 has "What kind of cake?" / "Marble." Ch05 has "the afternoon of the marble cake" and "What kind?" / "Marble."
   - Fix: Ch01 becomes "The cake had been marble."

2. **Ch 07 vs Ch 01, Ray's arrival time on league nights.**
   - Quote: "He had pulled into this lot at six-fifteen every league night since 1996, by Denise's account, and never once before six."
   - Conflict: in Ch01 Ray knocks at 5:15 ("I open on league nights... Lights and coffee. Tuesdays and Thursdays... Since ninety-six.").
   - Also in Ch07: "Ray coached Gagnon & Son on Tuesdays and Thursdays." Ch01 has Gagnon & Son as a Tuesday team.
   - Fix: "He had pulled into this lot at five-fifteen every league night since 1996, by Denise's account, and never once sat in it with the engine running." Change the Ch07 coaching line to "Ray coached the league on Tuesdays and Thursdays."

3. **Ch 10 vs Ch 09, when the derby money reached HELD.**
   - Quote: "The derby's raffle money went into my HELD account Saturday night on its own slip, and the meals tax went with it."
   - Conflict: Ch09 has the raffle cash going into "the HELD bag tonight." The roads were closed and Sunday was closed. Ellen herself says the receipts "came in through your drive-up at eight this morning."
   - The underwriter's question turns on this date.
   - Fix: "The derby's raffle money went into the HELD bag in the floor safe Saturday night, counted twice, and across your drive-up this morning on its own slip, and the meals tax went with it."

## SHOULD-FIX

4. **Ch 03, cash rule wording.**
   - Quote: "documented, with register reports and a deposit slip, both dated."
   - Problem: it can be read as requiring the slip to be dated by the 28th. The Ch10 slip is dated March 1, and the underwriter has to rule on it.
   - Fix: "documented, with register reports dated by then and a deposit slip."

5. **Ch 06, the Ch 05 kiss is dropped in the next scene.**
   - Quote: "Told her last night."
   - Problem: the bible says a kiss is never forgotten in the next scene. The Ch06 bob-house scene and the ledger scene never acknowledge it.
   - Fix: add one Ray line after it, for example: "He did not mention the rest of last night, and Hollis, who missed nothing, let him not."

6. **Ch 06, the new supper scene on a Friday.**
   - Quote: "Friday at six Ray carried a twenty-pound bag of rock salt up the steps of the rented camp past the narrows."
   - Problem: Friday is glow night (Ch03), and glow nights are her best nights. Ellen is home without her vest and chipping steps at four, and the scene never says who has the building.
   - Fix: add one line, for example: "Denise has the counter till nine. Glow doesn't start till then."

7. **Ch 08, a plan item that landed only in part.**
   - Quote: "*Low. Let the lane have it.*"
   - Problem: Ch08 item 3 asked for physical action only, with no italic maxim at Pine Ridge.
   - Fix: delete the italic line. The next sentence ("He brought his hand down six inches...") carries it.

8. **Ch 09, the locker 14 bag.**
   - Quote: "Ray came out of the pit door and opened locker 14. He took out the blue bag"
   - Problem: in Ch08 Ray took the bag out, "left the locker door standing open," and carried it to Pine Ridge. Nothing shows it going back.
   - Fix: add a clause, for example: "the blue bag he had hung back in its locker Wednesday."

9. **Ch 09, Hollis and Gagnon & Son's roster.**
   - Quote: "Gagnon and Son shows three bowlers."
   - Problem: Hollis bowls second for Gagnon & Son (Ch01, Ch07). He left for the plow before the final ("Back at five-thirty for your lot"), and the page never accounts for him. Ray's "Danny's the only fourth I had" reads as if Hollis isn't on the team.
   - Fix: add "Hollis is on the plow" to Ray's line, or have Verna name him as absent.

10. **Ch 09, Hollis's role in Ray's reply.**
    - Quote: "Hollis has been telling me that on the ice since the funeral, a little every Sunday."
    - Conflict: in Ch06 Hollis "had not asked him one question in five hours," and the bible makes him a man of questions and no speculation.
    - Fix: "Hollis has had me on that pail every Sunday since the funeral, and he never once put her name in it."

11. **Ch 02, the one clear contrast construction.**
    - Quote: "I didn't fix it for you. Thursday league's got a match."
    - Problem: contrast constructions are banned in dialogue as well as narration. The same line appears in the bible's sample dialogue.
    - Fix: "Thursday league's got a match." If the author wants to keep it as written, whitelist it.

12. **Ch 01, dead-K and sign-buzz overuse.**
    - Quotes: "because the sign out front said ETTLE LANES and FOR SALE BY OWNER" and "Its K was dark, and ETTLE LANES hung over the plow bank" (two uses of the dead K in one chapter, against a limit of once per chapter). Also "Out at the road the sign was buzzing on its pole."
    - Fix: cut the buzzing sentence. That brings the book to 3 buzzes. Reduce the final-scene K to "the plow bank, lit red" or cut it.

13. **Ch 02, key ring.**
    - Quotes: "He took his own ring out of his jacket and found the brass key" and "He weighed his own ring once, and the keys jingled against the orange tag" and the scene end "was Arlene's key, which opened nothing."
    - Problem: the ring appears three times in the chapter, against a limit of once per chapter and never as a closing image. One scene ends on it.
    - Fix: cut the "weighed his own ring" sentence. End the scene on "He walked to the truck with his hands in his jacket pockets."

14. **Ch 01, scene endings.**
    - Quote: "Down the building, nine lanes clattered on. Lane 7 sat dark between 6 and 8, its table still hanging a foot off the deck."
    - Problem: three scenes in the chapter end on an image or object (lane 5's ball, lane 7, the taillights). The limit is two.
    - Fix: end the lane-7 scene on Verna's line, "The secretary is aware of no precedent since 1979." Cut the final paragraph.

15. **Ch 10, a sentence that explains what the scene shows (rule 16).**
    - Quote: "Either answer made the winter something done to her."
    - Fix: cut it. The preceding two sentences carry the point.

16. **Ch 07 and Ch 09, tidy scene-closing lines (rule 18).**
    - Ch07 quote: "The truck door didn't open. She had said effective now, and he had heard her."
    - Ch09 quote: "She sat down in the desk chair. He had not seen her sit once all day."
    - Fix (Ch07): end on "The truck door didn't open."
    - Fix (Ch09): end on "She sat down in the desk chair."

17. **Ch 09, the number 26 now repeats.**
    - Quote: "Twenty-six and a quarter inches."
    - Problem: 26 already appears in Ch05 (children, $26,000, "Twenty-six") and in Hollis's 26 derbies (Ch06).
    - Fix: "Twenty-seven and a quarter inches."

18. **Ch 08 vs Ch 09, the tape arithmetic.**
    - Ch08 quote: "A thousand dollars short on Judy's first line."
    - Ch09 quote: "THROUGH 02/20 . . . 24,300.00" and "VAC WK 02/22–26 . . . 3,900.00"
    - Problem: 24,300 + 3,900 = 28,200, which is 1,800 under $30,000, not 1,000 under. The Ch09 tape has no line for ordinary Feb 21–26 business, which the bible's ~$29,000 projection assumes. The final total of $30,850 sums correctly, but only because the Open line carries the gap.
    - Fix: needs an author ruling. The cleanest option is to change Ch08 to "Eighteen hundred dollars short on Judy's first line." Alternatively, rename the second Ch09 line "VAC WK AND REGULAR NIGHTS 02/21–26" and keep the totals.

19. **Ch 10, HELD account age.**
    - Quote: "HELD has been open since the first of December."
    - Problem: in Ch03 Ellen opens it after the Judy meeting, which is after Dec 2.
    - Fix: "HELD has been open since the first week of December."
