## 7. The Kindle Market Strategist

*Evaluates title, length/pricing, categories, comps, blurb, and cover direction.*

### 1. The Length & Pricing Reality Check

First, you must address the word-count gap. At **~14,700 words (~55–60 Kindle pages; a 60–75 minute read)**, you cannot market this as a full-length novella at $2.99 without getting hammered by 1-star and 2-star Amazon reviews saying *"Way too short! Felt like a sample!"*

* **If you publish at ~15,000 words:** Price it strictly at **$0.99** (and enroll in KDP Select / Kindle Unlimited), and explicitly label it on the cover and subtitle as **`A Clean Small-Town Romance Novelette (A 90-Minute Read)`** or **`A Kettle Lanes Short Romance`**.
* **If you expand it to 25,000–32,000 words (Recommended):** You can price it at **$2.99** (where you earn the **70% royalty rate** instead of the 35% royalty rate on $0.99 titles!) and double your KU page-read payout per borrow.
