## The verdicts this round answers (boredom first)

- **Pull gate failed on the manuscript.** Sample reader: buy no at the end of the sample; sample average 3.08; book average 2.98; eleven scenes at 2 (2.3, 2.4, 4.1, 4.3, 5.1, 6.1, 6.2, 6.4, 8.4, 9.5, 10.1); chapters at 5 or under: 2, 4, 6, 8, 9. Would not buy the next book.

- **The ending is priced from about 78%**: the K ordered in Ch 08 and billed March 1; Kate's "I'm going to want something for it"; Judy's "formality" rule in Ch 06.
- **Every scene ends on a landed line** (31 of 44), and the readers see the next one coming.
- **Ch 01–03 stand as the author passed them.** The sample reader's 2s in Ch 02 (s3, s4) and the "buy no" are logged in `docs/calibration.md` and reported to the author above every score; they are not reworked in this round (the Oct 28 exception). Only the line items below touch those chapters.

## Rules for this round

1. No scene gets longer except Ch 09 s1 (allowed +250). Everything else holds or shrinks. The book may fall to about 31,000 words.
2. No digits anywhere in prose. No new dollar figure: the four money statements stay as they are (Ch 08 "I clear by six hundred dollars"; Ch 09 "Fourteen hundred dollars under, with the fries counted"; Ch 10 "Nine hundred dollars under" / "Same number twice"). The rule of what the bank counts is said once, in Ch 09 s6, in words with no figure.
3. Every cut is paired: when a line of explanation goes, the beat it carried gets an action or an object.
4. Kept lines stay word for word: "Honey."; "Ms. Daly. Net thirty."; "Take it." / "And somebody else can coach it."; "She had friends."; "I let it cost you."; "Not yet."; "The line went dead."; "Third. I love you." / "Third item. You love me."; "Both words."; "Tell me what you'd miss."; every line in the epilogue's office scene.
5. End at least one scene per chapter (Ch 04–10) mid-action: cut the landed closing line and stop on the action or object before it. Not on a kept line. The writer names which in the change list.
6. Side voices: Hollis and Dale must not share a sentence shape (Hollis: short, past tense, Fish and Game; Dale: present tense, the trucks, never a full sentence when half will do). Danny talks like Ray's son (the store's words), not like Ellen.
7. Change lists after `---` in every draft, moved to `edit-log.md` by the orchestrator.
