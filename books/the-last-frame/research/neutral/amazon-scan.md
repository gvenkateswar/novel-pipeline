# Neutral Amazon Scan — Where Short Indie Books Sell

*Collected October 26, 2026, through the author's own Chrome (personal profile), signed in to Amazon.com. Top 50 Kindle bestsellers in 55 fiction categories: 1,864 unique books, each checked on its product page. Categories were chosen to cover the whole genre-fiction market, not any particular setting or culture.*

## How to read this

- **Indie** = no publisher listed on the product page (self-published).
- **Recent indie** = indie and released April 26 – October 26, 2026. High numbers mean new self-published books can break in.
- **Indie <300 reviews** = indie books with fewer than 300 ratings. High numbers mean lesser-known authors reach the list, not only established names.
- **Short indie** = indie books of 200 pages or fewer. A ~30,000-word novella is roughly 110–130 Kindle pages, so this is the direct test of whether books your length sell in that category.
- **Sales/day at #25** = estimated from the #25 book's Kindle Store rank using Kindlepreneur's free calculator. Includes Kindle+ reads. Rough, not Amazon data.

**Limits:**
- One snapshot on one day. Late October boosts horror, Halloween romance, and holiday titles.
- Kindle Unlimited was renamed Kindle+ on October 22, 2026. The product pages couldn't reliably show which books are in it, so that column isn't included.
- Thriller/Crime (#25) and Police Procedurals (#50) had odd ranks, probably preorders. Read those two rows with care.

## What kinds of short indie books actually chart

75 self-published books of 200 pages or fewer rank inside the Kindle Store top 15,000 in these categories. They fall into a few clear groups:

1. **High-heat romance novellas, especially monster, alien, shifter, and fairy-tale retellings.** This is the biggest group. Examples: Ruby Dixon's *Ice Planet Barbarians* (188 pp, 54,000 ratings, still #461 overall after 11 years), the "Knotted by…" series, Siena Stone's fairy-tale retellings, "Monsters' Bride Market," and orc romances. Nearly all are explicit and part of long series.
2. **Short entries in long cozy mystery series.** Tonya Kappes (book 21, 186 pp), Jenna St. James (book 16), Kathi Daley, Beth Byers, Patti Benning (81 pp). Also historical cozies: Cara Devlin's *House of Peril* (162 pp) is #1 in Historical Mystery. The model is a short, steady series, often a book every month or two.
3. **Short "betrayal and grovel" women's fiction and later-in-life romance.** For example *What Caroline Heard* (124 pp, #155 overall) and the many "neglected wife" titles. These are released fast and under many pen names.
4. **Clean small-town and women's friendship series.** Fiona Baker, Katie Winters, Grace Meyers (69 pp). Low heat, long series.
5. **Short thrillers by already-famous authors.** Freida McFadden's short stories (52–86 pp). They sell because of the name, not the format.
6. **Short horror.** *Fervor* (120 pp), Adrian Blue's "Creatures of the Night" (30–45 pp), *The Haar* (194 pp). This is a real but small group, mostly in thin categories.
7. **Short military and space-fleet serials.** Joe Durham's "Officer of the Union Space Fleet," roughly 190 pages per book, released monthly.
8. **Halloween and seasonal romance.** *Scary in Love*, *No Bones About It*, *Knife Thrower*. Strong in October, so this snapshot overstates them.

**The pattern:** nearly every short indie book that charts is either **(a) explicit romance** or **(b) one book in a long series released quickly.** Standalone short books with no series behind them almost never appear, outside short horror.

## Categories that look most open to a new short book

These score well on all three tests at once: new indie authors break in, short books chart, and #25 still sells a meaningful number.

| Category | Why it looks open | Catch |
|---|---|---|
| Mystery/Cozy (plus Amateur Sleuth, Historical) | 24 recent indie; short series entries chart; #25 ~100/day | Readers expect a series; the first book is a series opener |
| Romance/Clean & Wholesome | 36 of 50 are recent indie; 17 low-review authors; #25 ~100/day | Long small-town series dominate; low heat is the rule |
| Romance/Science Fiction + SciFi/First Contact | 8 short indie books in SF romance; *Ice Planet Barbarians* proves the length | High heat expected; series |
| Romance/Gothic | 48 of 50 indie; 6 short indie; #25 ~100/day | Mostly explicit monster and dark romance; October-heavy |
| Horror/Monsters & Creatures | 24 recent indie; 17 low-review; 7 short indie | Only ~13/day at #25 |
| Fantasy/Cozy | 33 of 50 recent indie | Short books are rare here (median 316 pp, only 2 short indie) |
| Short Reads/Romance (65–100 pp) | Built for short books; 35 recent indie; #25 ~69/day | A 30k novella is longer than this category (100 pages max) |

## Categories that look closed to a short indie book

Psychological and Domestic Thriller, Epic and Romantic Fantasy, Romantasy, Contemporary Romance, Women's Fiction, Historical Fiction, and Police Procedurals. In these, few recent indie books reach the list, few lesser-known authors do, short books are almost absent, and medians run 350–550 pages.
