**Issue 1** · MUST-FIX
- Problem: Ray's loan line says the offer "went to the credit union." In Ch 02 it never did, and the line never says the credit union loan was against the store, so the two loans are not named plainly.
- Location: "The store's offer went to the credit union, to buy this place off her clean, and that one died at five o'clock on the third of December. The bank in Concord's the one that'll lend me what the appraiser says, to buy it March first if she can't pay Arlene, and I paid them to hold it there."
- Conflicts with: Ch 02 S4, where the credit-union form sits under Ray's left hand with Danny's signature on it, the loan officer's number is by the phone ("one call before five"), and "He does not pick up the phone." Nothing was submitted, and the offer lapsed. Also Ch 04 flags: "which loan is which (the credit union is Ray's committee for the store note; Concord is the alley purchase money: say so once, plainly)." Also the plant "Lucien put the store up in 'seventy-four" (the credit union loan is the one that puts the store behind it).
- Suggested fix: Make the credit union the store loan and the lapse Ray's own act, in one sentence, with Concord as the alley loan in the next. For example: "The credit union would have lent me against the store to buy this place off her clean, and I let their five o'clock go by on the third of December. The bank in Concord is a different loan, the one for the alley itself: it'll lend me what the appraiser says if she can't pay Arlene March first, and I paid them to hold it there." Keep the Ch 04 rule that "Concord" names the bank only.

**Issue 2** · MUST-FIX
- Problem: The time Ellen "gave back" cannot be true. The three minutes were not used up by the speech on the page, which is about fifty-five words, roughly twenty seconds.
- Location: "She gave the forty seconds she had left back to the chair, and sat down on the stool." and, later, "She had the five words, and the floor, and the forty seconds she had given back." Also "under it she was counting Verna's second hand, and had run fast."
- Conflicts with: Verna's "The owner may speak for three minutes" (180 seconds). A 180-second allowance minus forty seconds left means 140 seconds spoken, against the roughly twenty seconds the quoted speech takes. The beat sheet carries the same number ("She yields with forty seconds left"), so the writer copied a figure that the page's own speech contradicts.
- Suggested fix: Either lengthen her briefing to fill about two and a half minutes (no figures, three items, no contractions), or change the number to what the speech leaves: "She gave the two minutes and some she had left back to the chair" and "the two minutes she had given back." The second reading fits her character better (a brief that stops early) and sharpens "the floor" in the later line.
