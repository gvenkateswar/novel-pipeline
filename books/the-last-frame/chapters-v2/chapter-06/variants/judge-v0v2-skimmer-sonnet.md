2. **Why:** A keeps the two of them in a room doing something to each other (the glove going on while she says the line, Ray counting bills out loud so she can't finish her sentence, the pen and the cash in front of Dale), while B spends its back half on a committee vote, an adding machine and a bank statement, which is exactly where my thumb went.

3. **Where A lost me:** only near the end, in the credit union scene, when it stops being two people and becomes Ray and objects: "Through the back of it he could read the teller's careful printing, backward, his own name and the rest of it pressed in hard enough to come through. Beside it lay the envelope with the store's name in the corner in the red ink his father had picked, and beside that the pen on its chain..." (~90%). Even there Danny's "Is this you buying in, or getting out of the way?" had pulled me through just before, and the pen swinging back on the chain is a decent last beat, so I slowed down but did not quit. The one other slow patch is the long glove-fitting paragraph (~28%), but she is talking to him the whole time it happens, so I read it.

4. **Where B lost me:** "Run that against her bank's number," Ray said, "the way a bank'd figure a paydown. You've read Concord's papers, I'd guess." (~84%). That is a man, a son, an adding machine and a tape of figures, and I skimmed the whole "Ray didn't know how far under she was..." paragraph that follows. A smaller skim earlier, on the committee vote ("Motion to put the Open on the derby's Saturday... All in favor") at ~55%, and on the paragraph that begins "He handed back the pen and the pad, the top line still empty. She'd declined to answer him..." (~72%), which explains the plot to me after the scene had already shown it.

5. **Confidence: medium.** The bob-house scene is close to a tie. B's version has more warmth in it, with Hollis's hand going up and Denise's "you've got one shoe laced and one not" speech, and a reader who likes the town will enjoy those. But A's counter scene and Dale scene have real pressure and B's ending is the ledger scene I skip.
