# Judge report: Chapter 6, v1 vs v3 (Craft, Opus)

1. **Winner: A**

2. **Why:** In A she argues back while he gloves her ("I rang it." "He said it across my counter."), so the glove scene is two people fighting in short, different voices. In B he talks at her and the pair wait out a lot of silences that I could feel being held for me.

3. **Where A lost me:** At about 34%, when she reads the terms out again: "Linda's Memorial is the final," Ellen said. "It is bowled on the last Saturday in February, on all ten lanes, for her trophy. All of that stays." / "With anybody off the ice who's got the entry," Ray said. I'd just had those terms on page one of her handout, and for three lines it read like the couple going over a contract. It came back fast with the gloves not parting the sheets. There was a smaller snag at about 90%: "because he had planned this." That's the narrator explaining a man who had already shown me he planned it.

4. **Where B lost me:** At about 40%: "She said nothing to it. The heater hissed. One of her wet gloves turned a little on the guard as it dried, and he let the quiet run as long as it ran, and she let it." That's the third staged silence in two pages, after "Ray let the quiet go on past where a man would say something to be polite, and then past that" (~25%) and "Ray watched her look at that. He swallowed." (~35%). The hissing (heater twice, then fryer and coffee maker) and "did not fill it" / "neither of them filled it" started to sound like a tic. B does have the single best line in either version, "He had never held the alley in one hand before," and the better early joke (Arlene "in twenty years"). Still, in the bob-house it waits where A pushes.

5. **Confidence: medium.** The two drafts are close. A wins because the glove scene is the heart of the chapter, and in A it's a real exchange, with her short corrections in her own register against his "I stood up in front of twenty captains." A also lands the notice ("It was spelled right."), the counting out loud, and Hollis's "Nobody pays to watch one." harder. B's closing at the credit union is a shade better.

6. **Same-author check: Yes.** They have the same scenes, the same lines nearly word for word, and the same rhythms. These are two drafts of one chapter.
