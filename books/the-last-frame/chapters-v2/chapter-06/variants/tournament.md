**v2 wins round 1**, the first time the pace lead has taken a round. The judges' reasons line up: v2 acts out what the others narrate (the drawer counted aloud over her; the gloves fitted finger by finger while he talks; the teller's bolt at five), and it reaches the fight fastest. v1 lost for holding silences the reader could feel being held. v3, written for the ear, lost for the thing that was meant to help listeners: a speaker tag on nearly every line, which four judges said made the two leads sound like they were taking turns. Lesson for the ear-first lead: cues by action and by voice, tags only where two voices could be confused.
