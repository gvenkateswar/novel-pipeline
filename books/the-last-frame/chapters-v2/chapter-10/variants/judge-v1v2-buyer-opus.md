# Judge report: Ch 10, v1 (A) vs v2 (B)

1. **Winner: B**

2. **Why:** In B I could hear Kate as a real tired girl in a hospital garage and I got to sit on that cold step while Ellen worked up to "I love you", so when she said it I'd been waiting for it with her. A hands it to me before I've had time to want it.

3. **Where A lost me:** Two places, neither fatal. First, the step behind seven (~51%): "Down the catwalk the motors were giving up the day's heat one at a time, a tick from behind lane four and a while later one from behind lane nine, while the warmth went out of the kickback steel at their backs." That paragraph goes on and on with the grease smudge, the knee and the pin deck, and my thumb started moving. Then "Third. I love you." arrives two short exchanges later with nothing built under it. Second, the bank (~71%): "the release her lawyer drew in February was never signed in front of anybody. Title won't insure over it." That's a closing, and I don't want a closing in the last chapter. A's best moment is the stuck padlock: "both of them on the one key" with her cold knuckles under his palm. That's the one place A beats B.

4. **Where B lost me:** The same bank line (~75%): "the release from February was never notarized, so title won't insure over it." Same paperwork, same skim, though "Is that Ray Gagnon on my chair?" / "In it." pulled me back quicker than A did. I also slowed down at the glasses cord, "and his zipper with it, and the glasses hung off his jacket between them," because I couldn't picture it. But I didn't put the phone down. Kate's "it's medieval" and Ray's "Left one's been aimed at the ditch since Christmas" kept me smiling into the kiss. "A heading with nothing yet under it" is the line I'd tell a friend about.

5. **Confidence: medium.** Same bones, many of the same sentences. B wins on the two scenes I came for, the daughter's blessing and the declaration. A's padlock is better than B's, and B's middle sags in the same bank office A's does.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. Shared skeleton and dozens of identical lines; B is the same writer letting the people talk longer.
