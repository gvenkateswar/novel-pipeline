A = chapters/chapter-10/variants/v1.md; B = chapters/chapter-10/variants/v3.md. Persona: Craft. Model: Sonnet.

1. **Winner: A**

2. **Why:** A's sentences do more with fewer words and the two of them share the page: both hands on the one key, the sound of her laughing through the kiss. B explains its details more often, and its crowd and kiss paragraphs lean on stock phrasing.

3. **Where A lost me:** "His feet ached from three strings in shoes he'd had resoled the winter nine's belt let go the first time." (about 42%). It is a backstory aside the moment doesn't need, and I had to reread it to follow it. The long motor-ticking paragraph on the catwalk just after it also runs a little past its welcome.

4. **Where B lost me:** "Dale Hutchins among them with his hood still up. Gil Arsenault stood at the rail in his good overcoat, over from Halsey Falls for Linda's day. Judy from the bank had a place in the standing room by the shoe cubbies, her pocketbook held in front of her in both hands." (about 15%). It reads as a roll call, and it is the nearest either text comes to the procedural A1 feel. Smaller slips: "Hollis's golf pencil had him back in at anchor on the Gagnon and Son sheet" (about 35%) is clunky, and "Ellen made a small sound in her throat and kissed him back, slowly" (about 62%) is a romance-novel stock line.

5. **Confidence: medium.** The two are close. They share nearly every beat and many sentences, and B has some lovely things A lacks. These are "Every bob-house on the lake had a free Sunday it hadn't asked for," "as though it had a pen in it," "There was no ring on the glass," "you'd have had to know which," and the slush depth on Judy's blotter. A's physical writing is cleaner, though. Examples are "heavier in his hand than a thing that size had any right to be," "he was still laughing against her mouth when she kissed him, and she kissed him through it until he quit," and "His ring sat light in his pocket the rest of the way up the hill." A also leaves Linda's "short flat line" unexplained, where B explains its tick and loses the mystery. The ending is the same in both.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. Same beats, same ticking-machine texture, and many shared lines of dialogue.
