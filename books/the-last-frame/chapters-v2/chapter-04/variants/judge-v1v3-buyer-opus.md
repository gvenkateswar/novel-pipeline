# Judge report: Chapter 4, v1 vs v3 (Buyer, Opus)

1. **Winner: A**

2. **Why:** The day after that kiss, A has him feeling her pulse go quick and holding her wrist "a beat longer than the lesson needed, and then another," and later grinning down the catwalk "like a fool." That's the morning-after I was waiting for. B gives me the same scenes with her pulse "going along, even" and him just going to nine, so it reads cooler, like they'd agreed to forget it.

3. **Where A lost me:** "First. December of two thousand four, posted on the board and charged from the next league night, in Arlene's hand. That is where I took the form." (~84%). The two of them stand in a shut office a foot apart and she reads me her filing system, First, Second, Third. I skimmed down to "Who put it there?" A pulled me back with "his hands hung at his sides with nothing in them" and the motor "the second before it catches."

4. **Where B lost me:** Same spot: "December of two thousand four," she said. "First, she posted it on that board and charged it from the next league night, in her own hand." (~83%). Then she pulls the ledger down and runs "one finger down Arlene's column to the night the rate changed," which kept me in the account books a paragraph longer than A did. I also went flat earlier at "'Six is set,' he said. / 'Thank you.'" (~47%), a dead exchange where A had him talking to the machine.

5. **Confidence: medium.** These are the same chapter about 85% of the way through. B has lines I liked better: "Her watch was under his palm, where she couldn't have read it," the two of them hearing lane eight the same way, and "the corner of her mouth didn't hold at all." But A gives more of the couple being aware of each other's bodies, and that's what I bought the book for.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. Same scenes, same jokes and mostly the same dialogue, word for word. It reads like two drafts from one desk.
