**Issue 1** · MUST-FIX
- Problem: "D-side" is over its cap of 3 once the title is counted, and the set-piece ATC phrase count runs past six.
- Location: the heading "# Chapter 9: The D-side"; s2 Ellen, "At the Center they call it the D-side."; s2 Ray's readback, "And I'm on the D-side with you. Today, and after today, whatever Monday says."; s3 Ray, "D-side says send them home."
- Conflicts with: MOTIF LIST `\bD-side\b` cap 3, and the bible counts titles ("Separation (incl. Ch 7 title)"). That makes 4. World rule 6 caps a set-piece chapter at six ATC phrases. Counting relief briefing, the item headings (as one), D-side x3, "Read it back" and "Readback correct", the chapter has seven. The draft notes say s2 has five because they count D-side once in s2, and it appears twice.
- Suggested fix: cut the D-side from Ray's readback and have him mirror her own words: "And I'm on it with you. Today, and after today, whatever Monday says." The sentence still answers "I want you on it with me". The count becomes title + Ellen's definition + Ray's s3 line = 3, and ATC phrases drop to six.

**Issue 2** · MUST-FIX

**Issue 3** · MUST-FIX
