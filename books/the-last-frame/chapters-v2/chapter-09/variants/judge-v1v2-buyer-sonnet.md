A gives most of it up for tighter prose. A still wins on the lines I'd remember: "as he held a part he had not yet decided to trust", "Nobody said Linda's name", and "Not in the camp. Neither one reads like the calibration anchors A1 and A2 (flight-strip codes, the couple doing business). Both share the same briefing passage, so it doesn't separate them.
