# Judge report: Chapter 9, v1 vs v2 (Buyer)

1. **Winner: A**

2. **Why:** In A he never leaves her side once the storm comes. His sleeve is against her arm, his forearm lies by the cash box, his shoulder shakes against hers at the auger man. In B, while she hands out ten envelopes, he is just gone, and I'm watching a woman count by herself.

3. **Where A lost me:** At about 57%, Denise's speech: "Keep them, like December, and they're still telling that one at the diner, so bowl Linda's at two and the evening after, and I'll run this fryer till morning, and who pays for coffee? Nobody." I read it twice and still wasn't sure what she was proposing. A second, smaller slip came at about 69%: "Thirty thousand stood where Judy had set it, every envelope went the wrong way from it, and Monday was two days off." That's money talk, and I let it go by. The relief briefing at about 38% ("Traffic. A hundred and fifty in the building, forty-one of them children…") is a list, but he's on his knee looking up at her through it, so I stayed.

4. **Where B lost me:** At about 68%: "Two, three, four. Through the door glass the lot was showing gaps…", then "Six, to a team of sisters from Nashua… Seven, eight, nine." She's doing the envelopes one by one and Ray isn't in the room in any way I can feel. That's a ledger, and my thumb sped up. Earlier, at about 8%, "The clock on his stove said fourteen past. That left sixteen minutes to the weather." That counting at his supper table felt like a timetable, not a date. I'd keep reading B, but the ending scene is what pulled me back, not the middle. "There's someone. He followed me home as far as the plowing goes" is the best line in either version, and I wish A had it.

5. **Confidence: medium.** B is cleaner sentence for sentence, and its kitchen and its phone call are slightly better. A wins because the storm scene keeps the couple touching while B sends him offstage for the evacuation.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. Same scenes and the same jokes, often word for word. One is a light rework of the other.
