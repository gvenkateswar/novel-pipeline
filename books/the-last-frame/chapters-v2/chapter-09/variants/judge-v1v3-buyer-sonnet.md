1. **Winner: A**

2. **Why:** I kept reading A because Ray and Ellen are physically in the room together the whole way through (he holds the bowl rim until she has hold of the other side, he's "watching her mouth," his sleeve is against her arm at the counter, his shoulder shakes when the auger man leaves), and B keeps them a little further apart and spends more of its second half on a line of captains going out the door.

3. **Where A lost me:** "Thirty thousand stood where Judy had set it, every envelope went the wrong way from it, and Monday was two days off." (about 68%). I had to stop and work out what it meant, and it is the bank-math line, where I'd have started skimming. The run of envelopes just before it ("The first went to a Massachusetts captain... The evening went down the drive in license plates") is also close to a list, but it is short and the HUTCHINS beat pays it off.

4. **Where B lost me:** "She said it to the second captain, and the third, and the fourth, in the same words. The bar sat where the bank had set it, and it would sit there on Monday, and the evening was going down her drive one set of taillights at a time." (about 62%). It is the ledger-and-procedure stretch. Ray is also missing from the counter scene for a long while ("Ray said nothing. Out past the glass block the snow went on."), so for a page or two it's Ellen doing business alone. A smaller snag is "Nobody said what it cost Linda's day," which tells me the point instead of letting me feel it.

5. **Confidence: medium.** Close. B's Kate call is the better call: the birthday story ("you walked in the door at twenty past twelve"), and "At work a silence that long meant somebody was writing something down, and she let Kate write." A's call is leaner but still works, and it ends on the same "Ten." A wins on the kitchen and the counter, where the two of them are touching and teasing, and that is what I'd stay up for. Neither reads like the A1 flight-strip code. The briefing scene is the same exercise in both, and in both Ray's readback and "Readback correct" carry it.
