3. **Where A lost me:** "She said it to the second captain, and the third, and the fourth, in the same words. The bar sat where the bank had set it, and it would sit there on Monday" (~63%). By this point I'd sat through the Weather / Equipment / Traffic briefing (~33%), and now it was envelope after envelope with Ray gone quiet beside her. My thumb sped up here. That's the best moment in either version, and B doesn't have it.

4. **Where B lost me:** "Traffic. A hundred and fifty in the building, forty-one of them children. Both morning squads are done. The Memorial at two, on all ten lanes." (~38%). Same list, same dip. But B pulled me back sooner: he lays the wrench across his knee and looks up at her, and a few lines later he puts "the clean hand flat on the beam over her head." I went with it. I dipped a second time, briefly, at "Thirty thousand stood where Judy had set it, every envelope went the wrong way from it" (~70%), because it read like a ledger. The phone call at the end is thinner than A's. I didn't stop.
