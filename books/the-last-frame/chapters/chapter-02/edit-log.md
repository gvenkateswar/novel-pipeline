### Rule pairs for the pipeline

- Stop: letting a numbers speech carry two ways of saying the same figure ("at what you paid, every dollar back"). Do instead: one phrase per fact, and spend the saved words on a line only that person would say.
- Stop: a plant exchange whose second line only confirms the first ("Nobody's cleared thirty… / Then it's been done" with trivia in between). Do instead: let the plant be a move (a tease at her exactness, her answer turning his evidence), so the number arrives inside the argument.
- Stop: stock body-joke lines for a character's age ("his knees had a word to say"). Do instead: pass the body through the character's trade (he diagnoses his own knee by sound, as he does a pinsetter).
