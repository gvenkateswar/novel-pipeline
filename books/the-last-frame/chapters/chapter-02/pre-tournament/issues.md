# Chapter 02 — Continuity issues (draft.md, "Counts" block ignored)

**Issue 1** · MUST-FIX
- Problem: Scene 2 has Ellen refuse the offer flatly ("No."), so Scene 4's premise (the offer is still open and "dies" at five, Ray waits for her to come, "he could ask her now") has no footing, and the page never states that the offer has a deadline.
- Location: S2 "'No,' she said, at the level she'd used on the PA…" and Ray's closing line "'Committee's Thursday at five. After that, I'll see you March first.'" Versus S4 "He could ask her now… there was a minute left before the committee sat" and "The phone by the register could ring all it liked."
- Conflicts with: beats.md header ("His one offer is dead, and she let it die where he could see her do it") and S4 Want ("Ray wants the phone to ring, or the door, before five"). A refusal at 8:30 Wednesday leaves nothing to die on Thursday. Flag 6 and the human-gate "muddled" note also apply: a listener cannot tell why five o'clock matters.
- Suggested fix: Give the window one clause in S2, in Ray's closing line ("Offer stands till they sit. Committee's Thursday at five. After that, I'll see you March first."), and one clause in S4's interior ("She had said no at eight yesterday, and a no could be taken back until five"). Do not write it as a contrast construction.
