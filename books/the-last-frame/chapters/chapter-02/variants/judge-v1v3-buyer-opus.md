# Judge: Buyer (Opus) — A = v3, B = v1

1. **Winner: B.**

2. **Why:** B keeps giving me her face and his noticing (the coffee on the sill he never asked for, the light resting on his shirt pocket a second too long, "he started saving things up to tell her"), and A keeps trimming those out and leaving me with the machine.

3. **Where A lost me:** "Fella I knew in Tilton had an alley over there and wouldn't call me on principle, and drove all over three states looking for a pinsetter man, and found one, too, down in Lowell, eighty-some years old, and the old man listened to the machine a minute and asked him for my number." (~14%) A man telling the Bench how good he is, and I skimmed to find out whether she'd answer him. It got me back at "I'll hold the light," and I didn't drift again.

4. **Where B lost me:** "For the broadcast," she said. "He gave me the diagnosis for nothing and declined the money. Score it however you like." (~17%) It's too neat, a line written to land, and for a second I was aware of someone arranging it. I was back in by the vestibule, and the coffee cup on the sill is where I decided I liked her.

5. **Confidence: medium.** They are the same chapter with the dial turned one notch; I'd keep reading either. B is the one I'd read in bed and A is the one I'd admire. A's "He's the youngest of the four of you" is the better Bench line and "She threw again. It was worse." is the better joke, and it still isn't enough, because B gives me the corner of her mouth going and being put back, and that's what I'm here for.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. Same scenes, same lines, same key at the end; one of them just kept more of her in.
