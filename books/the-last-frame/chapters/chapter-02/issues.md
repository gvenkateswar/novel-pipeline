- Problem: One thrown ball makes lane seven sweep and reset, but in candlepin the fallen wood stays on the deck until the third ball of the box, so the machine would not cycle after a single ball.
- Location (S3): "Throw one," he called. ... "Seven swept and set clean, with no knock under it anywhere, and he heard it was right the way you hear a door shut true." / "Again," he called. / She threw again. It was worse.
- Conflicts with: Bible Research table ("Three balls per box. Fallen pins (wood, deadwood) stay on the lane and can knock down others") and World rule 9 ("Candlepin is never wrong"); Ch 01 ("left four pins standing, with the fallen ones lying across the deck in front of them"); Ch 03 ("After her third ball they went out on their knees, cleared the wood ... and set ten more").
