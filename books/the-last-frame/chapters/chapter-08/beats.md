### Scene 1

**Setting:**
- **Place:** the pit behind lane three.
- **Time:** Thursday, February 4, about five past six, before first ball. The house is lit and nobody is bowling yet.
- **Sensory anchor:** a woman's voice coming down sixty feet of empty lane. It is Ellen's, at the counter phone, carrying the way it carries.

**Characters:** Ray, Hollis. Ellen is heard, then present at the counter for the last beat.

**Want:** Ray wants to keep her machine running on league night without being seen to want anything, and to leave before she knows he was there. In the way: Hollis, who phoned him and let him in the back, and who wants him to say it. Then her voice, saying Linda's whole name to strangers on the phone. The reader learns the want at "Hold the light where I'm working, Hollis."

**Surface action, in order:**

2. **Her voice down the lane (played).** "The Linda Gagnon Memorial Open, Saturday the twenty-seventh." She says it to a caller as plainly as she'd say a lane number. Then "A team from Lewiston. Spell it for me?" Ray's interior, the first long passage: hearing the name in her mouth, on a telephone, every few minutes. Linda drove to Lewiston for opens, as a memory clause and no more. His hands stop on the bracket and start again. No sentence says what he feels.

### Scene 2

**Setting:**
