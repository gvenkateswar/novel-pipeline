A = v3.md, B = v2.md

1. **Winner: B**

2. **Why:** B has the better sentences and the surer ear ("he had put a good many things back together in this building on a Monday and watched them let go by Thursday", Arlene pushing the drawer shut "and watched it go all the way"), while A stages every line of the apology with a gesture until the speech stops being spoken.

3. **Where A lost me:** "at five he had turned the burner off under the pot on School Street, which made him late for his own supper at a quarter past five, and so far he hadn't billed her for a single supper, though he had the line worked out" (~25%). It is one sentence doing three jobs and I had to reread it. The same scene then opens its apology with "In the truck, every way he'd tried it had a reason in it somewhere toward the middle" while he is standing on the step, so the time frame wobbles. The fog-on-the-pane image comes back three times ("faded and came back"), and the "chin came up / mouth pressed shut / folded her arms and unfolded them" business reads as a tic. The final Arlene scene goes thin and ledger-like, with the pure-dialogue run "I clear by six hundred. One storm takes that" (~80%), and I started skimming there.

4. **Where B lost me:** the closing paragraph of the Arlene scene, "At eight this morning, from the store phone, he had ordered the K from the shop in Laconia at the quote he'd given her, and had them date their bill the first of March" (~93%). It is a ledger entry dropped into a stair descent, and the thumb sped up. The money talk before it ("With every lane of the Open full, I clear by six hundred", ~75%) is also a chore, but Arlene's glasses, the drawer and the oilcloth keep a person in the room.

5. **Confidence: medium.** A's Ch 8 scene 3 is the stronger set piece: the hood coming down, the ball sitting on the return, and the long interior paragraph on the word "cruel". If I only read that scene I would take A. But B wins the other three scenes on line quality and economy, and the two texts share most of their dialogue.
