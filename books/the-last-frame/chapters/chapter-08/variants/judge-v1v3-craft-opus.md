A = v1.md, B = v3.md

1. **Winner: B**

2. **Why:** B gives the two apologies room to land. She takes each sentence at the glass, and Ray works his way to the word "cruel" in front of the room. A puts the same apology in one paragraph and gets through it, and I felt it more in B even though A has the funnier Ray.

3. **Where A lost me:** At about 77%, at Arlene's table: "With every lane of the Open full, I clear by six hundred. One storm takes that." From there to the signatures it's three people reading the terms of a release to each other (bank sale, the list, the condo man, the house money), and my eyes went across it without hearing anyone. The radiator that "had views on it" is the only life in there. Before that it held me. I'll admit the door apology going by in one block, "He had put it in order in the truck, and he said it in that order," let a little air out at about 35%, since the biggest moment in the chapter is over in a breath.

4. **Where B lost me:** At about 80%, the same table, the same line: "With every lane of the Open full, I clear by six hundred. One storm takes that." It's the same ledger with nearly the same words, and B cut A's radiator joke, so there's less to hold onto. There was a smaller dip earlier, at about 45%, where the beat-by-beat staging at the glass starts doing its trick twice: "The patch on the pane faded and came back." Once was lovely. The second time I could see the pattern.

5. **Confidence: medium.** Most of the dialogue is the same in both. B wins on two passages: the "cruel" paragraph ("Short, he could have said, or sore, or out of line... Cruel they'd have to sit with") and the room going still a line at a time with the ball sitting on the return. A wins on voice in the small places: "though he'd had some hopes," "fries they'd be sorry about," "a poor time to bring up one letter of a sign." A reader who cares more about Ray's dry humor than about the apology scenes could go the other way.

6. **Same-author check: Yes.** The dialogue is nearly word for word the same, along with the same images (hand on the glass, key on the glove, "Holy mackerel"). They're two drafts of one chapter.
