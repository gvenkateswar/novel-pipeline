# Judge report: Chapter 8, v1 vs v2 (Craft)

1. **Winner: A**

2. **Why:** A lets the people talk. Dale cuts across Gil's terms, Gil talks straight over him, and Ellen's "In fries." gets an actual laugh out of Ray. In B the narrator keeps handling those same moments for them, and in the two places it should go quiet (the wrist, the signature) it slows down to show off how closely it is watching.

3. **Where A lost me:** "If she misses and you aren't standing there, nobody buys it. The Pines gives my room to the next name, and I go on the bottom of that list at eighty-four..." (~57%). Arlene stops being Arlene and walks through the contract's mechanics. The ham, the scanner and the Pelletier girl had me, and this paragraph is the couple doing business by proxy. I came back at the cupboard gap. There was a smaller dip at "Whatever happened to her number now, he could not buy her building if she put the keys in his hand." (~96%), where the book explains a stake I had already worked out.

4. **Where B lost me:** "He gave the terms short, for the settee. If Ray took it, the Highway Department and the Legion went home to Kettle Lanes on Tuesday at the season rate and bowled Linda's Memorial." (~5%). That is the stake read out as a summary of a summary. Then "He looked at the stake Gil had just read out, and worked it to the end." The book is telling me Dale is thinking. It lost me properly at "He gave her the terms in three lines, one at a time, and let each of them go before the next." (~80%), a device announced and then performed, followed by "steady as a good sweep setting down on its stop," which is the craft watching itself sign the page. The long one-sentence third ball (~22%) is good, the best single passage in either version, and it is also the moment B starts admiring its own rhythm.

5. **Confidence: medium.** The two versions share most of their dialogue. A wins on how people sound different from each other and on knowing when to stop. B has better objects: the empty curb below Arlene's window, locker fourteen, and the key gone warm in the pocket her hand kept stopping on, which set up the ending better than A does. If those were in A, this would be high.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. Same scenes, same lines and the same plank-and-cupboard images; one is simply the tighter cut.
