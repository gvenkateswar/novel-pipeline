(about 14%). It's one long paragraph of explaining. I felt my thumb move. The aphorism "The lake kept her word in January. She saved her temper for March and never gave notice." B's Wolfeboro clocks story is the best single thing in either text, and the glove-fitting in B is the warmer version. A reader who wants warmth over restraint could take B.
