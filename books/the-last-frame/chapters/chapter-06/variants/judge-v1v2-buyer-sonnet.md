I had to reread it to work out who said what, and I was still standing in Arlene's kitchen with no Ellen yet. I came back at the ledge of the Danny scene.

4. Where B lost me: ~46%, the "He took that one." run, starting "He took that one. He had watched Arlene tape a box of it shut this morning..." and repeated twice more. It's a counting device. Each of her objections gets a matching paragraph of his inner reaction, so it reads like a ledger. Then at ~52%, "At what I paid, any day you've got it, and it's nobody's business how long that takes," followed by a paragraph of him watching her "run it" (Arlene paid, the building coming back, the lanes staying lit). That's two people doing business, and I'd have skimmed it.

5. Confidence: medium. B's knuckle-against-thumb moment ("It was warm. She didn't take it back") is the more openly romantic beat, and a buyer might give it the win. A's version is quieter but its dialogue and Hollis scene are livelier. Both chapters open with Arlene, an envelope and a contract, which is the A1 problem, and I'd have started skimming in either one before the bowling alley.
