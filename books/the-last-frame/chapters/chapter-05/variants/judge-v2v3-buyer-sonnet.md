A = variants/v3.md ("Midnight"). B = variants/v2.md ("Not Since Midnight"). Persona: Buyer. Model: Sonnet.

1. Winner: A

2. Why: A gives me more of the two of them in a room and more of Ellen's head. The kiss has weight, and the store scene lets me feel what it costs her to say "I am asking" for the first time in her life. B has the same beats but plays them thinner, so the money talk at the end reads more like a meeting.

3. Where A lost me: the paragraph at about 8%, "Ellen heard it come down the line in order, the decision and then the date and then nothing, because nothing was left in it for anyone to weigh. She knew the shape well enough." It's a long, dense paragraph about how the family talks. I slowed down there, though I didn't quit. I also felt the money talk at the end, "my own money lowers the line, slowly, and all of it wouldn't buy back half of January" (about 88%), but I stayed because her wanting his hands on her face is right there.

4. Where B lost me: Denise's speech at about 45%, "Forty people, and I'm leaving out the vestibule, watched the two of you come out of that pit... and who do you think told the Bench, I'll tell you who, all forty." I had to reread it to follow the joke, and it's the only place the snack-bar scene drags. The money exchange at about 85% ("I asked Judy before Christmas...") had my thumb moving faster. B has less emotion around it than A does.

5. Confidence: medium. Both are close variants of the same chapter and share the same weakness, the money talk after the kiss. A is richer and B is cleaner, and I'd keep reading A.
