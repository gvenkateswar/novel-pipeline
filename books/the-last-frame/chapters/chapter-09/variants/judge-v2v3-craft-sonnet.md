1. Winner: A

2. Why: A lets the details do the work (the creased green shirts, "She had said harder things on a frequency, and could not think of one," Kate's "I'd have said no, and then I'd have driven up"), while B keeps stopping to tell me what Ellen is feeling and what the paper means.

3. Where A lost me: "She had kept a great many hours in her life for other people, in order, to the minute, and this one she had driven up the hill to keep for herself, with the key in her vest and no idea yet what she was in this kitchen." (about 12%). The supper scene was already working through the knee and the boot, and this paragraph explains it to me. A lesser slip later is "Ray's money had been closed since January; Judy's rule counted none of it." (about 62%), which is a ledger note dropped into a quiet beat. Arlene's scene is also thin next to B's. It's a hat and a line, and the scanner is just set dressing.

4. Where B lost me: "On any other evening this winter it was a minute she spent checking something, and tonight she was spending it at an oilcloth table with her coat on the back of a kitchen chair and a man's boot against her knee and the bowl cooling in her hands, and she let it go by with her in it, at the speed it went." (about 12%). It's the same move as A's, but longer and more pleased with itself. The worse stretch is the Kate call at about 76%, where Kate asks "And before the fifteenth, if you missed?" and Ellen answers "He'd have bought it at appraisal. Most of the house would have come back to me." / "A bank sale. The house goes with it." That is a mortgage explanation spoken aloud, and my thumb sped up. B also writes "He gave her every number back exact, which he had never once done for her, and it was the best sound she knew," and "She knew where she was going next, and it was to Kate." Both state what the scene should make me feel.

5. Confidence: medium. The two share most of their text, and B has real things A lacks: the Pelkey culvert, "I want my bed, Ellen," and "Then he's got more sense than you." A's counter scene (three vignettes, the Saco woman holding Linda's cup, the Lowell captain) and its telling of the toboggan story are stronger, and A's Kate call stays on the two of them instead of becoming Q&A.

6. Same-author check: Yes. They share the same skeleton, the same recurring lines ("Heard you being early," the readback, "I'm under"), and the same voice. The differences are only in how much each one explains.
