A wins on dialogue, on the toboggan story, and on the better-chosen details: the worn rungs, the green shirts still creased from the package, "She had said harder things on a frequency, and could not think of one." Neither one is A1-style procedure, but the briefing and the tape paragraph are the closest either gets.
