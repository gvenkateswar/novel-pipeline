4. MUST-FIX. Rule 16 and craft (the release reveal). The reveal is built as a reward moment because both characters and the narration explain it. Ellen says aloud what both of them know. Ray's "I read it back" names the readback, which the beats say to show and not name. The narration then tells the reader what the level voice cost her.
   Quote: "'This is dated the sixteenth.' She set each word down by itself. 'I asked you this morning. I asked you in the pit believing you would get this building if I missed.' 'I know. I read it back.'" and "Her voice had stayed level through all of it, and he had listened all day, on the radio and through the pit wall, to what it cost her to keep it there."
   Fix: Keep only "This is dated the sixteenth." Ray answers "Yes." Cut the rest of her speech and his "I read it back." Cut the second sentence of the narration after "Her eyes had gone wet." Let her do something procedural (read the stamp a third time) before the glasses come off.

   Quote: "The door half of the raffle cash sat squared in a stack next to it" and "Both runs came out at $30,850 and $25,600, over both of Judy's circles on paper"
   Fix: Change "door half of the raffle cash" to "the day's raffle cash." In S5 add a clause that the tape carries the house's half only, with the derby's half held out. State in S6 that these are day-of ticket sales.
