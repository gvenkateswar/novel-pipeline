It also happens at about 28% ("Ray's hand stayed on the clipboard, the fingers spread on it as though a wind might take it... Verna's thumbs moved once on the clasp...") and again at about 52% ("Ray's hand had not moved on the clipboard for any of it, and Denise's had not moved on the mop"). The lights-out run ("Then seven and eight... Then five and six... and three and four, and one and two") at about 62% is a list, and I skimmed it.

It is a long run of who sits where, and I wanted someone to speak. I came back when Ellen asked her two questions. A has the same paragraph, so it doesn't count against B. The other soft spot is at about 78%: "She had an answer to that, about Judy's committee and every week left between here and the end of February, and she kept it." That leans toward ledger talk, but it is short and then she says "I never wondered," so I kept reading.
