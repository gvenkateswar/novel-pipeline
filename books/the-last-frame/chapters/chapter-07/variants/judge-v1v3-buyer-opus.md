1. **Winner:** B

2. **Why:** In B I was standing in the dark beside him the whole time she was putting out the lanes, waiting to see if he'd still be there when the boiler shut off, and the key "warm from his pocket" got me in a way A's version of the same scene didn't.

3. **Where A lost me:** ~45%, the second time the whole table gets called out for not moving: "His hand lay on the clipboard, and the clipboard did not move, and Ellen watched it not move. Hollis waited with his eyes on him. Dale's keys lay where they lay. Verna's hands were folded. Denise sat by her mop." I'd already had that list once, at the first silence. When it came again at ~55% ("Hollis's elbows stayed where they were. Ray's hand had not moved on the clipboard for any of it, and Denise's had not moved on the mop"), my thumb was moving. After that the lanes go out in one short paragraph and he's just standing at her counter. The part I'd been waiting for went by faster than the part I was skimming.

4. **Where B lost me:** ~86%, right after his radio line, when she stops to pick it apart: "*On the radio.* He had worn one of her radios on his belt since a Saturday before Christmas, and it was his plain word for where she had worked, and he had chosen it. The rest she had given him herself..." He's just hurt her and I wanted to feel it, not read her taking it to pieces. The "Now you'll clear… *I'll lose the building*" paragraph at ~75% does the same thing on a smaller scale. I didn't stop. I just went faster to get to the keys.

5. **Confidence:** medium. Most of the dialogue in the two is the same line for line, so this comes down to which one let me stay in the room with the two of them. B did, and A made me wait through a lot of people sitting still.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. Whole speeches are identical, and so is the slow, even voice.
