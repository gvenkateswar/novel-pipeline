A = v2.md, B = v3.md

1. **Winner: B**

2. **Why:** The two versions say the same things, but B lets hands, keys and a boiler carry the hurt and trusts me to hear it, while A stops after every blow to explain it to me in Ellen's head, so in A the room keeps going quiet so the narrator can talk.

3. **Where A lost me:** About 10% in, the paragraph after Denise breaks off: "Hollis had told her so in one sentence, and she had built her Sunday around his sentence anyway, and ten days of mail had come up the drive to bear him out, and now Pine Ridge had borne him out by telephone, without being asked." Denise had just done something lovely by leaving her sentence unfinished, and A then spends a whole block telling me what that meant, who sent the seven envelopes and what Hollis said on the ice. It happened again at the end (~90%), where it lost me for good: "He was the one person in the building who knew what her calm was, because she had told him, and he had just told it back to her." His line had already landed, and that sentence explains why. A also leans on "she kept her voice where it was" and its variants until the phrase turns into a tic.

4. **Where B lost me:** Only slightly, about 85% in, right after his best line: "*On the radio.* He had worn one of her radios on his belt since a Saturday before Christmas, and it was his plain word for where she had worked, and he had chosen it." Here B makes A's mistake and explains the knife after it's in, but it does it once and gets out. Up to then the rack scene (his thumb staying on the ball "after the rail had it"), the toggles going dark a pair at a time, and the boiler running while she bargains with herself all kept me reading.

5. **Confidence: medium.** Most of the dialogue is the same word for word, so the difference is all in the narration. That difference is real and it holds the whole way through, but a reader who likes being told what Ellen feels could take A.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. The dialogue is identical, the "secretary" lines and Denise's run-on included, and both have the same habit of going inside Ellen. A just does it about three times as often.
