# Chapter 04 Issues

1. **MUST-FIX. Continuity: Danny does not own the store yet.**
   - Quote: "The store was Danny's to run and his to own."
   - Why: Danny runs the store day to day. Ray gives it to him outright in Ch 08, so Ray still owns it on Jan 7.
   - Fix: "The store was Danny's to run. He had a bank letter folded behind his license."

2. **MUST-FIX. Date math: "thirteen weeks" is the Dec 1 to Feb 28 span.**
   - Quote: "She had a pension and thirteen weeks."
   - Why: On Jan 7 about seven weeks remain.
   - Fix: "She had a pension and seven weeks."

3. **MUST-FIX. Power-cut logic: a sale after the lights go contradicts the fryer, the freezer and Ellen's own closure.**
   - Quote: "The Diner bought fries twice, honey. Second time was after the lights went."
   - Why: Ellen has already said "The house is closed", and the fryer has no power.
   - Fix: "Second time was ten minutes before the lights went."

4. **SHOULD-FIX. Contrast construction (the line the author named).**
   - Quote: "I am not charging anyone to be stranded. I am charging for bowling."
   - Fix: "Nobody has to bowl. Staying is free. Bowling is what I charge for." (Keep no contractions: she is angry here.)

5. **SHOULD-FIX. Contrast construction, and Ellen explains terms Ray already heard on the PA.**

   - Fix: "Sleeping is free." She tore the tape off. "Bowling costs what bowling costs." This also stops the line 4 speech from repeating it verbatim.

6. **SHOULD-FIX. Contrast construction, and a confusing detail.**
