(about 15%). It re-stages the Friday scene in a long recap paragraph and stalls the quarrel. Smaller: "Nothing on her moved but the thumb, which went once along the clip and stopped" is close to the thumb-on-the-clip beat A already used twice. The A1-ish risk is the radio scene, with its clean call-and-answer ledger beats ("All ten. I drew the lanes at Thanksgiving") and the adding machine "ran one short line and stopped" (about 45%). Ellen's rule-setting at the end ("Your hours and your parts, in my format") is two people doing business.

4. Where B lost me: "He had told the Bench, and now he'd told her, and what he'd told the Bench was still so, because he meant to hold it so. A man vouching on nothing but his own word was a thing he'd been before Friday, and nobody had sent him a bill for it yet." (about 25%). The sentence is knotted and says less than it seems to. Denise's rant ("and are they fine, they are going to walk down lane three without him") is garbled where A's version has the better joke ("about as fine as two rowboats"; "If he arrives in them, they're fine"). Neither lapse made me stop.

5. Confidence: medium. B has more voice and a better ending: the hand-in-vest "Pen'll keep," and Ray cutting the first piece and handing it to Normie. A has the funnier Denise exchange and is tighter in places. The two texts share most of their dialogue, so the call comes down to narration texture.
