3. **Where A lost me:** Mostly it didn't. My thumb sped up once in the office scene near the end (~85%): "First. December of two thousand four, posted on the board... Second. 4. **Where B lost me:** The phone call with the Concord loan officer (~38%): "Appraised value, Mr. Gagnon, and not a dollar over. The commitment's good through the thirty-first." / "And after the thirty-first?" / "It lapses." That's a man on a phone getting terms, which is exactly the kind of thing I quit samples over, and Ellen isn't in it. The second spot is the pit rumination (~60%): "In thirty years the clock in his kitchen had watched the pot go on late once... He had always kept time by what broke: the winter the K went, the winter nine's belt went the first time..." I couldn't follow it and I was alone with a man and machines. Then in the last scene "Read paragraph four, Mrs. Daly... it's been sitting in your desk since September" is explained to me in a flat paragraph about the season agreement, and I never feel her reaction. B also opens in boiler-and-weather small talk ("It's fifty-four degrees in here... I'd have said ten") with a ledger slip, in a scene that should be about the kiss.

5. **Confidence: medium-high.** I'd lean on it but not bet the house.
