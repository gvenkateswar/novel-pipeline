3. Where A lost me: the Bench paragraph at about 57%, "A thing said in front of the Bench was said. You didn't get it back; they kept everything they heard on a shelf in their heads, dated, the way he kept a part on a nail..." That runs on into the drawer-counting backstory, "Every morning at six he took the drawer Danny had counted the night before and counted it again, twenties first." I started skimming there. I also slowed at the very top, 1-2%, on "the building was still sore about Thursday, the boiler down cellar coming on and going off and coming on again." It's a lot of building before the people show up.

4. Where B lost me: the same Bench paragraph, about 52%, "A thing said in front of the Bench was said. It went out the door with them and was down Main Street before supper... Under that there was something about evenings, and the house on School Street once the radio had done the weather, and he didn't go down after it." Right after it comes Ray's long run-on toast, "and every season comes round and there I am at six with the lights on...", and my thumb sped up through it. I also drifted at about 30% on the flashback, "Yesterday morning he had been at his own counter with the drawer done twice and put away when Normie asked it from the end stool." It repeats what she had just told him.

5. Confidence: medium. Both chapters have the same bones, and both are mostly money talk after the kiss, which the calibration file flags. A wins on the couple being physically close, a bit more voice and a tighter party scene. It's not a bet-money call.
