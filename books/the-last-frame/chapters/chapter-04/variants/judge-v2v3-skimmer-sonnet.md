A = variants/v2.md, B = variants/v3.md. Persona: Skimmer. Model: Sonnet.

1. Winner: A

2. Why: A gets to the people faster and stays out of Ray's head, and its staff-party scene (the cake, Ray's long toast, Danny holding his cup) is the one place in either chapter where the thumb stopped.

3. Where A lost me: "A thing said in front of the Bench was said. It went out the door with them and was down Main Street before supper, and you couldn't call it back short of moving to Vermont, and that was the use of them." (about 44%). That starts a block of Ray backstory (the father counting the drawer, "something about evenings") and I sped up until Ray held his cup out. The readback and the "thirty hours and nineteen minutes" ledger at about 12% also read like a contract, but B has the same lines.

4. Where B lost me: "He had told the Bench, and now he'd told her, and what he'd told the Bench was still so, because he meant to hold it so. A man vouching on nothing but his own word was a thing he'd been before Friday, and nobody had sent him a bill for it yet." (about 22%). I had to reread it, and then I stopped rereading. The opening ran me off first: "On the drive out he had decided there were two ways to go at this morning" (about 4%) is a man alone in his head, a boiler with feelings, and a paragraph of choices before anyone speaks. The machine-by-machine paragraph on the catwalk ("Seven was running sweet… three was thinking something over") is a man alone with machines. It is short and pretty, but my thumb moved.

5. Confidence: medium. The chapters share most of their dialogue word for word. B has the better close, with the pen, the cold hands, and "neither of them looked down at the trench". It also has the better stake line ("a no that paid him"). Both are mostly the couple talking about money, which calibration marks as A1 territory, so neither gets a pass there. A is the one I'd keep reading because it moves faster and has more people in the room.

6. Same-author check: Yes. Same sentences, same beats, same tics (the radio bits, "Net thirty", the readback). B is A with a different amount of interior commentary.
