# Chapter 03 issues (continuity-checker, Mode 1; read: draft.md)

**Issue 1** · MUST-FIX
- Problem: Denise's ball "took all ten" while the head pin is in Ray's hands and the ten pin is in Ellen's, so the strike cannot happen as written; the deck staging (curtain at her back, pins coming "off the cushion" at two people on the deck, "the deck went off in front of them") also contradicts itself.
- Location: "Ray picked up the head pin, though it was set already, and held it in both hands." / "She found the ten pin by feel and picked it up." / "Halfway through it Denise's ball came down out of the dark and took all ten. The deck went off in front of them, every pin into the cushion at once" / "He was turning the head pin in his hands" / "the ten pin was standing on its spot and her hand was still closed around it."
- Conflicts with: the scene's own state (earlier Ray also holds "another" pin and then "the stray pin"; two of the ten are in hands at the moment of the strike); bible candlepin table (a strike is all ten on the first ball; hand-setting on "ten marked spots"); beat sheet S3 beat 8 ("takes all ten, a strike, rare and loud").
- Suggested fix: Change the line to "took everything standing" (or "every pin left on the deck") and leave Ray's head pin and Ellen's ten pin in their hands, so the reader sees the roar land on a deck that is missing two pins and no one corrects Denise's score; drop "all ten" and "every pin into the cushion at once." Also fix the staging in the opening clause (either "the curtain at her back" or pins coming back off the cushion at them, not both).
