# Chapter 01 issues (draft.md)

**Issue 1** · MUST-FIX
- Problem: Denise is first named mid-scene ("with Denise listening") before she has been introduced, and nothing puts her on shift during Scenes 2 and 3, where Ellen runs the counter, register, phone and PA alone; she then "rings out the register" at a quarter to ten.
- Location: "which she would have to think about later and in private" (preceded by) "she had asked anyway, in front of the fryer, with Denise listening"; later "At a quarter to ten Denise Lavoie rang out the register, in a red Kettle Lanes polo from 2011 gone pink at the shoulders".
- Conflicts with: Bible, Denise Lavoie: "counter, 22 years" and the Ch 1 or Ch 2 plant for her; the scene 2 beat lists "Denise Lavoie (at the register, one line at most)". Also the lane-7 refund, where Ellen "opened the drawer and counted each bowler's lineage back into each hand herself", with no sign of the counter's regular staff.
- Suggested fix: In Scene 2's room count, add one clause placing Denise at the register in the red polo, so the reader meets her before "with Denise listening". In Scene 4, say Ellen takes the drawer from Denise for the refunds.

**Issue 2** · MUST-FIX
- Problem: Candlepin house balls are sorted and labeled by "size", but candlepin balls are one regulation diameter and differ only in weight.
- Location: "Ray was squaring the house balls on the rack between four and five, smallest to largest" and "Ellen had labeled every shelf of the rack, a strip of tape on the old maple with the size written on it."
- Conflicts with: Bible research table: "Ball: no finger holes, max 4.5 in, max 2 lb 7 oz"; WORLD RULES 9, "Candlepin is never wrong."
- Suggested fix: "lightest to heaviest" and "the weight written on it".

**Issue 3** · SHOULD-FIX

**Issue 8** · SHOULD-FIX
