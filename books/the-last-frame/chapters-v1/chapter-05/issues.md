**Issue 1** — MUST-FIX
- Problem: Timeline contradiction. Lane 8's ball return jams at 1:25 p.m., but Ellen says Ray came up front "at noon."
- Location: "Why did you come up front at noon?" she said. "You could have talked a sixteen-year-old through that ball return on the radio."
- Suggested fix: Change to "Why did you come up front at one twenty-five?" (Ellen uses exact numbers.)

**Issue 2** — MUST-FIX

- Location: What she sent said: *Tomorrow 1400. Here. About the league.*

**Issue 3** — MUST-FIX

**Issue 4** — SHOULD-FIX

- Suggested fix: "No office parties. Nothing on the board like today."

**Issue 10** — SHOULD-FIX (contrast construction)

**Issue 11** — SHOULD-FIX (contrast construction)

**Issue 12** — SHOULD-FIX
- Problem: Rule 13. The coach, the lunch-staff head and the Rotary man all use the same tic of repeating their own phrase. Voices blur, and the repetition stops being a signature of any one speaker.

She worked that line for forty-one years, and she fed every one of those boys over there"). Make the Rotary man flat and procedural: "And the banquet, same night? My wife wants to know."
