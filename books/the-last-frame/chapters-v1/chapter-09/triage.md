# Ch 09 cold-read triage (orchestrator, autopilot)

**Verdict:** The pit readback ("I share the position. Today, and after.") is the best-built moment in the book. Denise's broken trophy speech nearly matches it. The rest of the chapter has three problems:
- It is crowded with logistics.
- Too many things are handed to Ellen.
- The money closes with nothing shown.

The release reveal also lands muffled.

**Scope:** One revision round, a prose-writer targeted pass and then a voice-editor pass.

**Keep unchanged:**
- The D-side ask and the readback.
- The forced choice and its cost.
- Ellen subbing on the sanction card and her team losing.
- Denise winning and her broken speech.
- Final figures of $30,850 and $25,600.
- The release dated the 16th.
- The key going into Ray's palm.

**Length:** 3,450–3,700.

## Show the money (one short passage, at the 4:40 tape)

1. Give the reader three tape lines Ellen reads, so the close is earned and visible:
   - Where she stood on the 20th: about $1,000 short on income.
   - The NH vacation week, Mon–Fri Feb 22–26: about $3,900 net.
   - The Open (entries and door taken before the storm moved the draw, less the $1,140 refunded): about $2,650 net.
   - Total: $30,850 against $30,000, and $25,600 cash against $25,000.

   These add up: $24,300 + $3,900 + $2,650 = $30,850. Keep it to the figures and one line of Ellen. Don't make it a ledger paragraph.

## Make it hers, not handed to her

2. **Ray's PA gesture** ("The Linda Gagnon Memorial goes to the Open... Anybody who pays the entry"). The Ch 07 vote made the Memorial the Open's final round for the league's top teams. This line opens it to the public every year. It reads as a gift with no decision behind it. Earlier in the chapter, add one Ellen-POV glimpse of Ray at the trophy case with Linda's bag, before he comes up to the mic. One or two sentences, no explanation.
3. **The release reveal.** Let Ellen register what the date means in one line of dialogue: she called him for help believing he could still take the building. Example shape: "I called you this morning thinking you could still take it." / "I know." Ray then says what he wants in plain words. Replace "I want to be asked" with something like "I'm through wanting to own it. I'd like to be asked in." Then she puts the key in his palm.
4. Cut "Her eyes had gone wet." Show it some other way. For example, she can't get through the stamp a third time, or her glasses come off.
5. Cut the teaser: "There was something he meant to say to Ellen. It would keep until she had slept."
6. **Reorder the ending.** The raffle split ("Count it twice") comes before the release. The chapter ends on the key and on her sitting down: "She sat down in the desk chair. He had not seen her sit once all day." Nothing after it.

## Clarity

7. "At 4:10" should read "At 4:10 that morning" or "At 4:10 a.m."
8. "Kettle Lanes, Pike." On the radio, use "Hollis" or "Kettle Lanes, it's Hollis." "Mr. Pike called the ice" can stay if it's clear.
9. Halsey Falls: identify it once as Pine Ridge's town (Gil's people).
10. The D-side speech. Make "We borrowed their words when ours were not good enough" clear. Say who "they" are, for example the centers or the long-range controllers. Keep it short.
11. Card "02/27. 0640. LANE 09." Make it clear this is his time in from this morning. For example, he writes the time out beside it.
12. "where he had stood on the second of February" should become "where he had stood at the league meeting."
13. Kate's "Fryer first." Give it one clause of resolution: she turns the fryer down herself.

## Cut

14. Cut "She had bought the building for days like this one, a room that would fill with people who needed putting in order..." The book has said it four times.
15. Cut "It came out in the voice. Every item stood in its place..." The dialogue already shows it.
16. Cut the repeated "She had turned down his fifty-five thousand dollars... After today she would never know."
17. The weigh-in: cut Hollis writing the fish twice.
18. The departure logistics: trim to the essentials.
19. The treasurer's outburst: cut to one line.
20. Kate's buttons: keep one of "I've been on a refrigerator" and "You were annoying all day, and you were great." Cut the other.
21. Denise's "That's the first thing you've said all winter I'd have said myself": cut or roughen.
