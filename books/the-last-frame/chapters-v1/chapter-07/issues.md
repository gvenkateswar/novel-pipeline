# Chapter 07 Issues

1. MUST-FIX. Contrast construction in dialogue (Verna, Scene 5). Banned outright.
   - Quote: "The chair doesn't require thanks. Only minutes."
   - Fix: "The chair is paid in minutes." Then she exits as written.

2. MUST-FIX. Characters know something before they should (World rule 14, no convenient coincidences). Ellen announces the rate and the Open for the first time at 6:30, and the Highway captain reacts as if surprised ("We paid in September!"). Yet the 112 asks about mid-season changes before she speaks, and says he spoke to Gil on Sunday, two days before the announcement. Ch 06 says only the derby committee, Hollis and Verna had any hint, and Verna was given no reason.
   - Quote: "Verna. Has this league ever changed anything in the middle of a season?" and "Taylor's Marina is bowling Tuesdays at Pine Ridge starting next week. I talked to Gil Sunday. Gil's got lanes."
   - Fix: Plant the leak in Denise's Scene 2 speech with one clause (for example "and the Bench had it Monday that somebody's raising something"). Change the 112's line to "I called Gil at supper. Gil's got lanes."

3. SHOULD-FIX. Ray knows Ellen's income projection without being told on the page. In Ch 06 Ellen gave him only the cash figure ($19,000 against $25,000) and "the income was already short". She never gave a $24,000 NOI projection.
   - Quote: "That's under your twenty-four, and you never touch a league rate."
   - Fix: Add a clause in Ray's run, such as "Danny backed your twenty-four out of the ledger and the cash you told me," or have Ellen supply the figure when she says "Fifty dollars."

4. SHOULD-FIX. League start time conflicts with Ch 01 and the 1987 precedent. Ch 01 and the bible's sample have league at six-thirty. The 1987 minutes move the Tuesday start to 6:45. Here the Tuesday division bowls at 7:15.
   - Quote: "The Tuesday division bowls at seven-fifteen and has not started late since I took this chair."
   - Fix: "On meeting nights the Tuesday division bowls at seven-fifteen, and it has not started late since I took this chair."

5. SHOULD-FIX. Rule 17: side characters get one short speech per chapter, and the town does not deliver monologues in turn. Denise has two long run-on speeches (Scenes 2 and 5). In Scene 4 the Highway captain, the Legion man, a woman and the 112 each speak in turn.
   - Quote: "Verna's here. She brought the gavel and the binder and a cushion for her chair, her own cushion, plaid, from home," and "He brought that in Monday," she said. "Took it right off his wall at the store and handed it across this counter to me,"
   - Fix: Cut Denise's Scene 2 speech to about 40 words (the 6:29 start and the snack-bar motion). Cut the Scene 5 speech to two sentences (Monday, his wall, "handing back a hammer"). Trim the Legion man's hedge by a third.

6. SHOULD-FIX. Rule 13, distinct voices. Denise borrows Ray's "fella" signature in her simile. Swapping the line to Ray would not be noticed.
   - Quote: "said it like a fella handing back a hammer he borrowed and never used"
   - Fix: "like somebody returning a hammer he borrowed and never used."

7. SHOULD-FIX. Rule 22, Kiss 2 is not named. "Cellar stairs with this man" does not say a kiss happened. The follow-on sentence is unclear (thought about the boiler first, as opposed to what?).
   - Quote: "Six days ago she had been on her own cellar stairs with this man. In six days she had not once thought about the boiler first."
   - Fix: "Six days ago she had kissed him on her own cellar stairs, and in six days she had not once remembered that night as the night of the boiler."

8. SHOULD-FIX. Ambiguous wording. "Move the Open" can be read as relocating the event, and Denise answers with the same verb.
   - Quote: "When she opens the floor, will you move the Open?"
   - Fix: "When she opens the floor, will you make the motion on the Open?" Denise: "I'll make it."

9. SHOULD-FIX. Factual slip. The commitment letter behind his license funds a purchase; the recorded right of first refusal gives him the building.
   - Quote: "a letter behind his license that gave him the building if she failed"
   - Fix: "a letter behind his license that would pay for the building if she failed."

10. SHOULD-FIX. Rule 18 and the Ch 05/Ch 06 cold-read note ("Don't end on another type-and-delete or held gesture"). Ch 05 ended on a held draft, Ch 06 on an open mouth, and Ch 07 ends on a held hand on a key. That is three in a row.
    - Quote: "Ellen kept her hand on the key and didn't turn it again yet."
    - Fix: End on the car turning over a second time and Ray's truck pulling out behind her, or cut at "Ray's truck sat with its headlights on and didn't move."

11. SHOULD-FIX. Rule 16 (don't explain what a scene has shown). The interior paragraph restates the Scene 2 parallel. It also contains an antithesis (contrast construction).
    - Quote: "Same mistake, same kind, at the end of the same kind of shift: every word on the frequency perfect and the distance gone."
    - Fix: Cut that sentence and begin with "This time she had reported it herself inside five hours."

12. SHOULD-FIX. Contrast constructions, borderline (negate then affirm, or antithesis).
    - Quote A: "He didn't argue. He picked the check up off the hood, and the tape with it, and put them both inside his coat."
    - Fix A: "He picked the check up off the hood, and the tape with it, and put them both inside his coat."
    - Quote B: "The aircraft had been safe that night and she had not been, and nobody on the frequency could tell."
    - Fix B: "That night she came apart at her scope, and nobody on the frequency could tell."

13. SHOULD-FIX. ATC phrase discipline. The bible caps the chapter at three phrases and bans two in one sentence. The beat sheet reserved the third slot and said Ellen ends it "in plain short sentences". The draft has "loss of separation", "conflict alert", "the minimum", "Negative", and a briefing-style close. One sentence holds two.
    - Quote: "The conflict alert sounded, and she turned them both and told each pilot where the other was, and the closest point was inside the minimum." and "I will see you at six-thirty, because you coach. That is all I have."
    - Fix: "An alarm sounded, and she turned them both and told each pilot where the other was. The closest point was inside the minimum." Make the second sentence plain: "They passed closer than the rules allow." Cut "That is all I have." and end on "because you coach."

14. SHOULD-FIX. Rule 12/18, tidy scene ending. Scene 1 closes on a neat competence beat, and the beat sheet said to cut on her walking with no last look.
    - Quote: "The two men by the tailgate said good evening, and she said it back to each of them by name."
    - Fix: Cut the sentence. End on "Ellen was walking before he had the engine started."

15. SHOULD-FIX. Vote procedure. Why only captains vote is never stated, though Verna cites an article for every other ruling. "Eight." has no speaker tag. The count of 11 + 8 + the coach implies 20 captains, which the page never establishes.
    - Quote: "Captains only," Verna said. "All in favor." and "Eleven. Opposed." / "Eight."
    - Fix: "Captains only, under Article Three." After the hands go up: "Eleven. Opposed." Verna counted again. "Eight. The coach?" Then continue to the abstention.

16. SHOULD-FIX. Triplets: the bible allows one per scene. Scene 4 has two.
    - Quote: "He passed the snack bar. He passed the door. He came around the end of the counter and stopped at her left, on her side of it." and "Zippers, bag straps, somebody's thermos."
    - Fix: "He came down the aisle past the snack bar and the door and stopped at her left, on her side of the counter." Change the second to "Zippers and bag straps."

17. SHOULD-FIX. World consistency. Glass block is translucent. Exhaust cannot be seen through it, and the bible puts the band along the front of the building, not behind the counter.
    - Quote: "Ellen counted it through the glass-block band behind the counter as one vehicle."
    - Fix: "Ellen counted it through the vestibule door glass as one vehicle."
