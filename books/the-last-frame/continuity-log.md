## Chapter 01 — Cleared to Bowl
**What happened:** Tuesday, December 1, evening, snow. Six hours after closing on Kettle Lanes, Ellen finishes taping the lane-light toggles before she comes down to answer Ray Gagnon's knock; he will not hand over his key, tells her the contract she signed sells him the building by suppertime on March first if she cannot pay Arlene, and walks in past her. In front of the arriving league she phones the only locksmith in Ambrose, which is Gagnon & Son, and books the front and back rekey for eight tomorrow morning; Danny tells her the locksmith is his father, standing in her alley. She opens the league on the PA, throws the owner's ball on lane five into lane six and wipes out Ray's wood, and lane seven's machine grinds and knocks and dies at seven; Ray listens, says "Not my alley," and she refunds the lane and runs nine. Arlene refuses any half-payment because The Pines holds her place only until March first, and the night ends at the pool sheet: March first is blank, and Denise says nobody is holding it because Ray won't.
**What changed:**
- Ellen is the owner and has had one night of it. She is under for the night and has run nine lanes.
- Lane seven is down and Ray has declined to fix it; she has not asked.
- A rekey is booked for 8:00 tomorrow with Gagnon & Son, made in public with Ray listening. His key will stop working.
- Her half-on-March-first contingency is dead (Arlene, The Pines).
- The town has a pool on her failing, and she has tried to buy a square.
**Who knows what:**
- Ray: the rekey, the time, front and back (heard Ellen's whole side of the call); that she did not ask about lane seven; she recorded its noise. He has not seen her phone.
- Danny: the booking and the time; that his father was in the building. He says he will put it "on his sheet."
- The whole league at the cubbies: the rekey call, Ellen's "cleared to bowl," the altitude line, the lane 7 refund, and that Ray was not told about the booking in private.
- Ellen: Ray's key ring and the R.G. 1996 tag (read it, no explanation), Linda's card, locker fourteen's padlock (fits none of her keys; nobody has explained it), the pool sheet, the house-ball rack, the Pines deadline.
- Denise: Ellen's offer to Arlene (half on March first, the balance by end of June, in writing, at the note rate). She holds the pool sheet and the cash.
- Arlene: the same offer, and watched Ray move Ellen's tape label.
- Ellen does not know what Ray's key opens beyond the front door, or whose hand the lane 7 recording is for.
- Nobody on the page knows the amount of the shortfall; Ellen is told only "under."
**Objects / details introduced:**
