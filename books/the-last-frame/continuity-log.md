## Chapter 01 — Cleared to Bowl
**What happened:** Tuesday, December 1, evening, snow. Six hours after closing on Kettle Lanes, Ellen finishes taping the lane-light toggles before she comes down to answer Ray Gagnon's knock; he will not hand over his key, tells her the contract she signed sells him the building by suppertime on March first if she cannot pay Arlene, and walks in past her. In front of the arriving league she phones the only locksmith in Ambrose, which is Gagnon & Son, and books the front and back rekey for eight tomorrow morning; Danny tells her the locksmith is his father, standing in her alley. She opens the league on the PA, throws the owner's ball on lane five into lane six and wipes out Ray's wood, and lane seven's machine grinds and knocks and dies at seven; Ray listens, says "Not my alley," and she refunds the lane and runs nine. ## Chapter 03 — Ground Stop
**What happened:** Thursday, December 17, into dawn Friday, December 18.
