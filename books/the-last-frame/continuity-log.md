## Chapter 01 — Cleared to Bowl
**What happened:** Tuesday, December 1, evening, snow. Six hours after closing on Kettle Lanes, Ellen finishes taping the lane-light toggles before she comes down to answer Ray Gagnon's knock; he will not hand over his key, tells her the contract she signed sells him the building by suppertime on March first if she cannot pay Arlene, and walks in past her. In front of the arriving league she phones the only locksmith in Ambrose, which is Gagnon & Son, and books the front and back rekey for eight tomorrow morning; Danny tells her the locksmith is his father, standing in her alley. She opens the league on the PA, throws the owner's ball on lane five into lane six and wipes out Ray's wood, and lane seven's machine grinds and knocks and dies at seven; Ray listens, says "Not my alley," and she refunds the lane and runs nine. Arlene refuses any half-payment because The Pines holds her place only until March first, and the night ends at the pool sheet: March first is blank, and Denise says nobody is holding it because Ray won't.

**What happened:** Wednesday, December 2, and Thursday, December 3, from Ray's side. At 7:30 Wednesday Ellen plays the lane 7 recording on the counter of Gagnon Hardware; Ray diagnoses it from the speaker (seven's clutch, a spare on a nail behind seven), she writes it down and asks his rate, he has none, and the Bench gives her "the veteran declines." At 8:00 he rekeys her door with her holding the light; talking to the cylinder, he lays out the backup contract, the at-cost offer ("Committee's Thursday at five. Her text at four (*Lane seven. Ten o'clock. Gagnon* unopened under the old clutch. He never asks her again.
