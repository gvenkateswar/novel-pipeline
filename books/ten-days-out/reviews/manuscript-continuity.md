# Manuscript continuity check: Ten Days Out

*Draft read: `manuscript.md`, v2 rebuild, 30,041 words, ten chapters. Checked against `story-bible.md`, `continuity-log.md` (every chapter entry, the plant ledger, the numbers ledger, Master continuity flags, Carried forward) and the "For the author's next bible review" list in `book-brief.md`. Where that list records a deliberate override, this report treats the page as correct and doesn't flag it. Issues are ranked by severity: High (a contradiction or logic hole a reader can catch), Medium (a binding rule or channel broken, or a plant left hanging), Low (ledger drift, small facts, banned-list hits).*

---

## HIGH

**Issue 1**
- Problem: Ned contradicts himself on what happens if the freight contract is lost. In Ch 6 the district cuts to three days a week and somebody else is laid off. In Ch 9 the district ties the boat up for the winter and both deckhands go ashore. Both are Ned's own confessions, and neither is marked as a lie.
- Location: Ch 6: "That contract's the winter boat. Lose it, the district runs her three days a week. Somebody gets laid off, and it wouldn't be me. So I never said." / Ch 9: "Without the contract the district ties her up December to April, and you and Kenny go ashore."
- Suggested fix: Pick one consequence and use it in both chapters. If the Ch 9 version is the truth, add one beat in Ch 9 that marks the Ch 6 line as softened.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: Ned's account of the Day 8 leak doesn't hold together. He says he told the network the boat was a trap "so they'd stay off it". If so, there was no reason to give them the boat in the first place, and the attack goes ahead anyway. CASE FACTS says he called it a trap so they would come from the water. The brief's override list doesn't cover this change.
- Location: Ch 9: "I told them it was a trap, so they'd stay off it, and I kept Tess out of it, like you asked."
- Suggested fix: Bring the motive back in line with CASE FACTS (he called it a trap so they would come by water, away from Russ on the pier), or have Ned say in one clause why he gave them the boat at all.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: In Ch 10 Ned's timer-box key is back on his own ring, but his statement in the same chapter says he lent it to the network (CASE FACTS: he gave it to Arsenault on Day 6). The page never shows how it came back.
- Location: Ch 10: "As the ring went back into the evidence bag she saw the store key on it, and the small brass one that fit the box at her elbow." / Ch 10: "that he lent them his own key to the box that runs the float lights"
- Suggested fix: Add a clause to the statement saying the key was returned to him, or take the brass key off the ring in s1.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: The fish house window can't be seen from the bar, yet the book needs it to be seen. Ch 5 says no part of the bar can see the window. A few pages later Beth watches a flashlight at the bar's Ashby end from under that same window, and Ashby's fire reddens the glass. In Ch 6 the skiff's light finds the glass from the water.
- Location: Ch 5 s3: "The fish house sat under the ledge, and from no road and no part of the bar could anyone see its one window." / Ch 5 s3: "Across the cove Ashby's fire put a dull red in the window." / Ch 5 s4: "Across the cove, at the end of the bar on the Ashby side, a flashlight stood at the water's edge and swept the dark toward them." / Ch 6 s1: "Then it was on the glass over her head, white, and the room went gray around her."
- Suggested fix: Narrow the Ch 5 s3 claim to what the plot needs (for example, the window can't be seen from any road or from the town side), so the later sightlines are allowed.

**Issue 5**
- Problem: The book's last image puts the boat in motion with no master at the wheel. Beth is on the car deck with her hand on Dane's at the binder, and the narration says she has a minute yet before the wheel. Meanwhile the ramp is coming up and the gap of water is opening, which means the boat is leaving the slip. She is the master (Kenny is the only deckhand), and the bible says she never leaves a boat she's crewing with a job undone.
- Location: Ch 10 s6: "She had a minute yet before the wheel." / "Up forward, at the controls, the deckhand started the bow ramp up on its hydraulics, the sodium light narrowing on the steel as it rose, the gap of black water opening between the boat and Pruett, her hand still on his on the binder."
- Suggested fix: Let the gap open only after she's in the wheelhouse, or make the opening gap the ramp's own clearance and not the boat moving off.

## MEDIUM

**Issue 7**
- Problem: Dane states as fact that Beth asked Ned about the float below decks, but by his own words he couldn't hear it, and the page gives him no channel. The log (end of Ch 06) records that Dane doesn't know Ned asked "Which tide?"

**Issue 9**

**Issue 10**

**Issue 11**
- Problem: Three ledger plants are left hanging with no payoff on the page: Beth leaving the hooded man out of her trooper call (ledger: "its cost lands later (the case file, Quist)"); Tess seeing Beth's thumb on Dane's neck (Ch 03); Tess seeing Ned haul two cans up the north road (Ch 05, ledger → Ch 06, 08).

**Issue 12**
- Problem: Ch 9 claims Ned "gave them the whole boat from the back step" after supper at Tess's. In s1, though, Tess answers Beth's bait call with the store bell behind her, minutes after Ned leaves for supper at her house (Ch 8 s6 has smoke from Tess's chimney). The page doesn't say where supper was or when Tess went back to the store. The bible also has Tess in bed by nine, but she's at the store at eleven.
- Location: Ch 8 s6: "Up the road the store's windows were yellow and steady, and smoke stood straight up off Tess's chimney beyond them." / Ch 9 s1: "Behind it, faint, the store door's bell rang once." / "All right, Calloway," Tess said.

**Issue 13**
- Problem: Quist reads a co-defendant's statement aloud to a witness who is still under suspicion, across her own counter, before taking Tess's ledger. Readers have already flagged this as hard to believe (log, Ch 10 "For the manuscript stage"). It is also the only place the latch and truck channels are paid.
- Location: Ch 10 s2: "She read from the pad to Tess, fast and level."
- Suggested fix: Give Quist one reason on the page for reading it to Tess (to get the key count confirmed, say), or move the reading so Tess hears only the parts she has to confirm.

**Issue 14**
- Problem: The bible gives Doucette a body ("wiry, five foot ten") that the face scene contradicts.
- Location: Ch 4 s1: "Young. Wide and pale."
- Suggested fix: Make the description match the bible ("wiry"), or make plain that "wide" is his face.
