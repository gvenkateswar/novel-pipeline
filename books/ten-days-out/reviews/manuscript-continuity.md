# Manuscript continuity check: Ten Days Out

*Continuity checker, manuscript mode, Nov 6, 2026. Text read: `books/ten-days-out/manuscript.md` (18 chapters, 3,912 lines, as of this date; Ch 18 may still get reader line fixes). Checked against `continuity-log.md` (entries Ch 01–17), `outline.md` (including every carry-forward section), `story-bible.md` and `book-brief.md`. Where the bible is stale, the outline and log win, and stale bible items are listed separately at the end, not as manuscript errors. Line numbers are manuscript.md line numbers.*

*Ranked by how much a reader or listener would notice. Issues 1–8 are the ones a careful reader catches. Issues 9–20 are ones a listener would notice on a second pass or a sharp reader would query. Issues 21–30 are small.*

**Issue 1** (Ch 18, line 3679; contradicts Ch 01 line 105 and Ch 08 line 1532)
- Problem: The book says twice, plainly, that Dane's boat viewing was on Saturday. Ch 18 now says it was the day after the call, which was Friday. The Saturday boat is the plant behind the boots, the motel and the HEA boat, so readers will remember it.
- Location: "It was the boat at Pruett he'd come up to see the day after the call came." (Ch 01: "for a boat he was going to look at on Saturday"; Ch 08: "I had a boat to see on Saturday," he said, "before your phone call.")
- Suggested fix: Change the Ch 18 clause to "It was the boat at Pruett he'd come up to see that Saturday."

**Issue 2** (Ch 06, lines 1120–1126; Ch 14, lines 2905–2907; Ch 15, line 3011; Ch 18, lines 3709–3713)
- Problem: The bet joke is never set up. Neither the log nor the bible's plant ledger matches the page here: "Dollar says he's at the front door." / "I don't bet on what I can see. Pantry." is not in Ch 06 (Dane asks "Which window's farthest from him?" and Beth says "Pantry."). So Dane's first "Dollar says" and Beth's refusal both arrive for the first time in Ch 14, and her refusal is cut off before the words "what I can see" are ever said. Ch 15's "Dane stared at her" and Ch 18's "Did you just—" only land if the reader knows Beth never bets. As written, the book's running joke pays off with no setup.
- Location: Ch 14: "Dollar says it holds till light," Dane said. / "I don't bet on—" (no earlier "Dollar says" or "I don't bet" anywhere in Ch 01–13)
- Suggested fix: Put the plant back in Ch 06 s1 as the log records it ("Dollar says he's at the front door." / "I don't bet on what I can see. Pantry."). That makes the book's count 4 of 4 for "Dollar says" and 2 of 2 for "I don't bet". If the Ch 06 line can't come back, plant the full exchange once in another chapter between Ch 07 and Ch 12.

**Issue 3** (Ch 10, line 1944 against lines 2010 and 2115)
- Problem: This contradicts itself inside one chapter. The narration says Dane's kit is at the bottom of the bay. A page later he closes her knee "with thin strips from the trauma kit," and later still Beth uses "the kit's last gauze." Ch 12 (line 2405) also relies on the kit having survived.
- Location: "His cuffs were at the bottom of the bay with the rest of his kit."
- Suggested fix: "His cuffs were at the bottom of the bay, gone off his belt with the wave."

**Issue 4** (Ch 07, line 1240; contradicts Ch 06, lines 1222–1228)
- Problem: Ch 06 makes a point of Beth choosing the route over the rocks: Dane says "You pick the feet. I'll watch the bank," and she puts her feet "only where she knew the rock." Ch 07 opens by saying the route was Dane's. A reader takes it as Dane picking the path that nearly killed him.
- Location: "The line had been his: off the open ledge at the waterline, up onto the big rounded rocks, where the overhang of the bank hid them from the top."
- Suggested fix: "The line had been hers: off the open ledge…"

**Issue 5** (Ch 18, lines 3709–3729)
- Problem: You can't tell who won the last bet. Dane bets the boat is dry by noon and Beth says "Done," but the page never says which side she took. At noon a cup is left in the bottom, she pays him, and then she says "There's a cup left, Marshal," which sounds as if she won. The outline and bible want her first blind bet, lost happily. As written, a listener can't tell whether she lost, won, or paid anyway.
- Location: "Dollar says it's dry by noon." / "Done." … "There's a cup left, Marshal."
- Suggested fix: Make her side plain in one line ("Done. Dollar says it isn't.") and keep the cup line only if she has clearly lost (for example, "It's a cup, Marshal. Take your dollar.").

**Issue 6** (Ch 04, lines 902–904; Ch 11, line 2361)

Beth has said the house is "an hour round by the road," and Russ drove that road about twenty minutes later. Russ says nobody passed him. - Location: Ch 04: "Did anybody pass you on that road?" / "Nobody." Ch 11: "I could see his light from there. I told Tess so."
- Suggested fix: Add one clause to Ned's Ch 11 or Ch 12 answers, for example: "I stood in Ellie Dyer's woodshed till his truck went by."
