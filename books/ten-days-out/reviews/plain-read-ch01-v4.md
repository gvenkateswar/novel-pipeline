# Plain read: Chapter 1, test-v4

Draft read: `chapters/chapter-01/test-v4.md` (the only file opened). Read as a half-listening, text-to-speech listener with no boat, legal or police background.

## 1. Retelling

Beth works on a small ferry. One night the lobster truck comes on board with its cooler switched off, which makes no sense because the lobsters would die. A crate breaks open on the deck, and under the lobsters there are taped bricks of white powder: fentanyl, the drug that killed two kids from her island. A scared young man in a hoodie, with a split lip and a bad left leg, sees that she saw it and tells her she didn't. She tells Ned, the old captain who was her dad's best friend. He tells her to forget it and go home, and he looks scared too. She photographs the truck's license plate anyway and calls the state police, who say it's now a federal case and a marshal is coming tonight. At 2 a.m. someone breaks in through her back door saying her name. She climbs out a window in her socks and hides in her dad's fish shack. The marshal, Dane, gets there in time, chases the man off (he escapes in a boat with no lights), and then Beth hits him with an oar because she thinks he's the bad guy. They get on well right away. He wants to take her off the island for ten days until she testifies. She says no, it's her island. He suspects someone in his own office or in the police tipped off the attacker.

## 2. What's at stake

Beth is in danger from the drug smugglers, who already know her name and broke into her house within two hours of her call. Somebody on the police side seems to be leaking to them. If she fails, they kill her before she can testify, and the drugs keep coming onto her island.

## 3. The romance

Yes, from his side, very clearly. I felt it at:

> "He was in trouble. He knew it right then, standing in the snow holding an oar. He'd protected maybe forty witnesses, and he had never once wanted to make one of them laugh."

and

> "he wanted, very badly and very unprofessionally, to find out what her mouth would do if she ever actually smiled."

From her side it's weaker. She teases him about his boots, her mouth "twitched", and she keeps leaning on him "and she didn't seem to have noticed." I got that she likes him, but only because he's watching her. Her own chapter half has no moment where she wants him (she hasn't met him yet then). That's fine for a first meeting, but I felt his want, not hers.
