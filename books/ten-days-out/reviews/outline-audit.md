- the latch was tried at the house only Dane and Ned knew;
- Ned's promise went into Tess's log within half an hour;
- she owns a heated shed that nobody on the island associates with her any more and that needs no one's key.

She takes a house whose key comes through Ned and whose existence is on Tess's list. The book only "discovers" the fish house after the fire, so the reader will ask why it wasn't the first move. A competent heroine who "knows the island better than anyone" would think of her own father's fish house first.

For example, the Day 1–3 surge puts the cove over the sill and the stove under water. Or Ashby has the generator and water on three sides while the fish house has one door and no exit. Either way, say why Beth doesn't see the color at the 7:40 handoff. For example, the bag rides inside a zipped canvas courier pouch with the seal inside, or it is dark under the Pruett lights with sleet on everything. Put that in the Ch 1 beat.

**Finding 20. Reader knowledge: Ned is the common source of every leak**

**Fails:** Q9.

Tess sees the kitchen dark and then a flashlight moving behind it at 10:40, before Ned arrives, and she writes the log line herself, with Ned's report only confirming it.
