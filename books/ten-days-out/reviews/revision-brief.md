## 2. Structural fixes outside the tournaments (each a single targeted pass)

### 2.1 Option (a), the seal clue (auditor Finding 1)
- **Ch 04 s2:** cut "Tess seals hers yellow" (and "with my envelope in it" if present). Dane must not learn the store's seal color. Keep "Fare bag's red. That's the boat's." and the tag on his wrist.
- **Ch 07 s3 and s6:** make Beth's reading of "Seal noted: red" her own misreading, held privately. She reads red as the teller logging the fare bag by mistake (both bags travel together) and sets it aside. The page must never state it as fact. Cut "The teller had written down the boat's bag. Ned's."
- **Ch 08:** "The money rode over in the boat's bag" stays in Dane's mouth, since he doesn't know the store's color. "She left the seal out" goes (T2).
- **Ch 09:** the resealed bag in the confession is the first time the reader learns the store seals yellow.
- **Completion question:** a fresh reader at the end of Ch 08 cannot say who resealed the bag.

### 2.2 Quiet the early Ned tells (sample reader, auditor Finding 2)
- **Ch 01:** Ned taking both bags and sending her home must read as kindness with a reason ("I'll work it short"). Add one line that gives a second person a chance at the bags. For example, Tess's courier at Pruett, or Kenny on deck.
- **Ch 02:** Beth's "Home" must reach more than Ned. She says it on the radio or at the store counter where Tess and the net can hear. Ned's confession line "You told me home" still works.
- **Ch 06:** cut "Her eye went over it and on." Keep the Marr's sticker as a plain fact in one clause, and move it out of the sentence next to the reefer lie.
- **Completion question:** at the end of Ch 06, a fresh reader holds at least two live suspects.

### 2.3 Answer the shooting (auditor Finding 3, cold readers)
- **Ch 07 s1:** the lifeboat turns away for a reason in one clause (it won't land at a float under fire with a witness in the open). Dane's word to Pike is shown: "Graze."
- **Ch 07 or Ch 08:** Pike gets one line about the shot. Quist has a reason to leave the dragger alone, in one line, delivered through Pike or the notebook: HSI wants her to run to her buyer.
- **Completion question:** why wasn't the dragger boarded on Thursday?

### 2.4 Ned's logic (continuity high 1–3)
- **Freight contract:** "three days a week" in both Ch 06 and Ch 09 (domain Fix 8).
- **The Friday trap:** Ned gave them the boat and told them Russ was on the pier and a marshal aboard, so they'd come by water, where he thought Beth would be safest. "I told them it was a trap, so they'd stay off it" goes. T3 carries this.
- **The float-light key:** Ned's ring comes back without it (Ch 10 s1). Quist's line in T4 says they kept it.

### 2.5 The fish house window (continuity high 4)
- **Ch 05 s3:** "no part of the bar could anyone see its one window" is changed to something true. For example, the window can't be seen from the road or the store, but it looks out on the bar and the Ashby shore. That way the fire, the flashlight and the skiff's light through the glass all work.

### 2.6 Channels (continuity medium, auditor)

- **The Friday truck key:** one clause in T4 or Ch 09.
- **Tess on Friday night:** put her at the store, not her house, when the bait call goes out (she went back down after supper to close up, which is how Lyle found her there). Then Ned's supper is at the store's back room, or earlier at her house before she went down. Choose one and make Ch 08 s6, Ch 09 s1 and Ch 09 s6 agree.

### 2.7 The danger scenes that repeat (auditor)
