# Sample read: sample mark, round 2 (Ch 01–03)

**Drafts read:** `chapters/chapter-01/final.md` (S4 tournament winner), `chapters/chapter-02/final.md` and `chapters/chapter-03/final.md` (after the Oct 29 tournament rewrites). I opened no other files.

**Persona:** KU reader, 35–55, reads on her phone, three or four books a month. Reads open-door romantic suspense and wants the couple and the danger in equal measure. Quits when the heat is all hints or the setup runs long. Has been bored by two books like this before.

---

## 1. Where I stopped

**Finished the sample.**

I'd keep reading, but the store scene in Ch 03 nearly lost me. Too many names, a ledger and a color code for deposit tags. I skimmed it and kept going only because the shaving scene before it had me, and I wanted to know where they'd sleep. If the shed scene hadn't paid off, that store is where I'd have closed the app.

## 2. Buy: yes

It earned it at the end of Ch 02, with the mudroom door going quiet while he's out on the step with his phone lit, and Ch 03 actually got them touching, so I'm not sitting through ten chapters of wrist-grabbing.

## 4. Do I care about these two people yet?

**Yes.** She covers for her own people without thinking, he lets her haul him aboard before he hands her the subpoena, and when the phone comes between them in the shed it costs them both something.

## 6. Did anyone make me want?

Yes. "That lasted until he turned his jaw to the light and ran his thumb along it to check, and she wanted it to be her thumb." Then the shed, where it pays off: "she had both hands in his belt, pulling him in, and she hadn't decided to, and her mouth was asking for things before she'd thought them." Before Ch 03 it was all wrists and collars, and one more chapter of that and I'd have been gone.

## 7. The three most boring passages

1. **Ch 03, the store, about 80% through the sample.** "Tess snapped a yellow tag through the zipper of the deposit bag that would sit in the safe till a boat ran. 'Yellow's mine. Red's the boat's. I won't have my money in Ned's color.'" Then the ledger page, twenty-two forty, the spiral wire. I skimmed all of it and wasn't sure what Tess was telling her.
2. **Ch 01, about 3% through.** "Her hand went along the stern chain across the transom, and she kicked the ice off the ring buoy's bracket on the way by, and the quarter ladder's rungs went past her knee, black and wet. Then the outside ladder to the wheelhouse..." She has just seen bricks of drugs and I'm getting a list of boat parts.
3. **Ch 03, about 85%.** "'Ashby,' Beth said. Her father's fish house had the cove over its sill till Monday. The Connecticut people's place on the north point was shut for the winter, one road in." I didn't follow it. I worked out they were going to an empty house and stopped trying to understand the rest.

Close behind: the Webbers Cove boat-price talk in Ch 01, and Russ on the cable rust in Ch 03.

## 8. What I think is going on

The ferry and the store's deposit bags are moving money, and Dane watched the pouch so closely because he already knows that. Arsenault on the Gloucester dragger is the boss. He's so obvious I'd be annoyed if he turned out to be the whole answer. Whoever sent the mudroom man knew Beth was at home and not on the boat. On the page that's Ned (she told him outright), Tess (she watched the back steps at 22:40 and kept it off the air) and whoever is on the other end of Dane's phone. "Q's request approved. Log daily. Do not disclose to witness" reads to me like Homeland is using her as bait, or watching her, and Dane is going along with it. Ned bothers me most. He'd never sent her home in eleven years until the night of the drugs, he carried the store's bag, the fuel float at Marr's was lit at an hour it never is, and now he knows about Ashby too. I also think her father and the bank are part of the secret, and that "a man in a good coat" wrote her father down as a witness or informant once.

**Suspects (who's leaking her location / working for the smugglers), from the page only:**
- Ned: 35%. He sent her home that night, carried the bags, knew she was at home, gave her the Ashby key.
- Tess: 25%. The fold of hundreds, the too-loud laugh with Arsenault ("it's on your account"), Arsenault's threat about a hard winter, she saw Beth's steps.
- Dane's own side (Pike / Q / Portland, a leak through his calls): 20%. The glow on the step right before the door went quiet, and he just told Portland where she is.
- Russ: 10%. Offered to run her across, asked where she's sleeping "because somebody will."
- Dane himself: 10%. The book wants me to suspect him. I don't, much.
- Arsenault I'm counting as the money behind it, not the leak.
