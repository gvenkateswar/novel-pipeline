Files read: Ch 01, 02, 04, 05 (final.md); Ch 03, 06-10 (final-v2.md); revision-brief; story-bible; synthesis.

## PART 1. CONTINUITY ISSUES

**Issue 1 (Ch 05, brief A2, must fix)**
- Problem: Ch 05 is unrevised and lacks the required lines. Beth never says "Tess knew I was home", never says "Tess has known where I sleep since I was nine", and Dane speaks about Tess ("And the woman who wrote it down."), where the brief says he hears both halves and says nothing yet. Her defense is a logic argument, not love.
- Location: "Then I wasn't the only one who knew." / "And the woman who wrote it down." / "If Tess were selling me, she'd have kept it in her head."
- Fix: After "Then I wasn't the only one who knew." add Beth: "Tess knew I was home." and, in the next breath, "Tess has known where I sleep since I was nine." Delete Dane's "And the woman who wrote it down." and let Beth's defense answer a silence, not a question. The paint-can list can still leave Tess off.

**Issue 2 (Ch 05 against Ch 03)**
- Problem: Ch 03 shows Beth already thinking "Tess knew I stayed" on Day 2 and refusing it. Ch 05 narration says she had believed it was Dane alone, "for four days", as if the thought had never come.
- Location: "She could keep believing it was him alone. She had for four days, because one man was a man she could watch."
- Fix: Change to "She had kept believing it was him alone. Since the bread rack, she had worked at it."

**Issue 3 (Ch 04 against Ch 07 and Ch 08, "hands on her")**
- Problem: Ch 07 and Ch 08 say Dane asked about money "with his hands on her". In Ch 04 the money questions come before the kiss. They are side by side at the generator, and his hands are on the filter and the rag, not on her.
- Location: Ch 07 "He had asked her about money with his hands on her and written down the answers."; Ch 08 "Your hands were on me. You were asking about the money." and "On Sunday I asked you about your account with my hands on you"
- Fix: Cheapest is to reword Ch 07 and Ch 08 to "standing at the filter beside me, asking about money, and then you kissed me". Alternatively add a touch to Ch 04 (his hand over hers on the wrench) during "Anything come in lately?".

**Issue 4 (Ch 10 against Ch 06, saltines)**
- Problem: The saltines were left on the fish-house bench (Ch 06). Beth has not been back since Day 7 night. Ch 10 produces "a sleeve" from "the store bag on the locker" aboard the ferry, and in Ch 06 she had already torn that sleeve open. The Tess's-box condom is also described as coming "out of his jacket".
- Location: "a sleeve of saltines from the store bag on the locker"; "He took one from Tess's box, out of his jacket on the chair."
- Fix: Have the saltines come from the ferry's mess locker or Ned's galley ("a sleeve of saltines from the mess locker"). Say once, in Ch 08 or Ch 09, that Dane carried the store bag out in the go-bag, or cut "out of his jacket" down to "from Tess's box".

**Issue 5 (Ch 02 against Ch 08, the bank bag)**
- Problem: Ch 02 has the captain put the bag "inside his jacket". Ch 08 has Dane recall the captain "taking it below".
- Location: Ch 08 "a big captain who wouldn't look at a marshal taking it below"
- Fix: Change to "taking it inside his jacket".

**Issue 6 (Ch 02 against Ch 07 and Ch 08, call time)**
- Problem: Pike's call is at 11:20 every night (Ch 02, 04, 06). Ch 07 sets his watch alarm for "eleven" and says "Wake me at eleven". Ch 08 has Pike say "You missed eleven."
- Location: "Wake me at eleven," he said. "Night call." / "You missed eleven."
- Fix: Use "eleven-twenty" in both Ch 07 and Ch 08, or have Dane say "Wake me at a quarter past eleven".

**Issue 7 (Ch 02 against Ch 10, time of leaving the cottage)**
- Problem: Ch 10 says the kitchen is "the way she'd left it the morning they went". They left on Day 2 evening, after the store and the generator shed, and Ch 03 shows them walking to Ashby's in the dark.
- Location: "The kitchen was the way she'd left it the morning they went."
- Fix: Change to "the evening they went".

**Issue 8 (Ch 02 against Ch 10, the indictment)**
- Problem: Ch 02 says Monday is "the last day the grand jury sits in time". The bible does too. Ch 10 has an indictment "returned that morning", a Tuesday, which reopens the Rule 5.1 logic.
- Location: Ch 10 "Pelletier on four counts, and Arsenault, Doucette and Teresa Marr in a second, returned that morning."
- Fix: Have the grand jury return Pelletier's indictment Monday afternoon after Beth testifies, with Dane reading the second (superseding) indictment Tuesday: "a second, returned that morning". Or change Ch 02 to "Monday and Tuesday are the last two days it sits".

**Issue 9 (Ch 10 against the bible, who captains the first boat)**
- Problem: The bible says Ned captains the first boat. In Ch 10 Beth takes the wheel ("Take her off"). The coda separately says she has stood relief watches "since the first of the month" with Ned asking the board in secret. So Day 10 reads as an unrecorded plot change.
- Location: "'Take her off,' he said. / She took the wheel."
- Fix: Either record it as a deliberate change, or keep Ned at the wheel and let Beth take it for the last quarter mile. Then the coda "since the first of the month" is the first time.

**Issue 10 (Ch 10, Tess's "Monday")**
- Problem: "Scare you off Monday" is ambiguous. Monday is both the Day 4 fire and the grand jury date.
- Location: "The first days I believed him. Scare you off Monday, nobody hurt."
- Fix: "Scare you off before the grand jury, nobody hurt."

**Issue 11 (bible Heat Rule 2)**
- Problem: The bible's Heat Rule 2 still lists scene 3 as Ch 08, the crew cabin. The brief puts it in Ch 10, Day 9 night, at her cottage. The Revision rules only gesture at A3.
- Location: "Scene 3, Ch 08, Day 8 afternoon, the crew cabin of the *North Reach*"
- Fix: Annotate the bible with "moved to Ch 10 per revision-brief A3". The same applies to bible Tess "Day 8 5:30 p.m. ... after a pause" and "Fate" if edited later.

**Issue 12 (Ch 09, leftover reference)**
- Problem: The removed Ch 08 scene's setting survives as an incidental. The scene took place in the crew cabin's lower bunk. Ch 09 sends Beth to the lower bunk for a blanket. It is harmless but echoes the cut.
- Location: "fetched the navy blanket off the lower bunk in the dark"
- Fix: "fetched the navy blanket from the mess locker". All other cut-scene residue is gone: no condom box in a go-bag, and no "I'll tell them I chose it. In daylight." The Ch 10 "I chose it" is the moved line.

**Issue 13 (Ch 08, the plan against what the island can see)**
- Problem: The bait plan hides Russ. But Ch 08 shows Ned and Russ walking down the pier road together in the last light, and only Ned walks off. Anyone watching from the store sees two go aboard and one leave. Beth then tells Tess "Gone up to Russ's", and Russ's wide cap is on the pier road in the light.
- Location: "two figures were coming in the last light, one big and stooped, one wide in a ball cap" / "Gone up to Russ's with his hip."
- Fix: Have Russ come aboard from the seaward ladder at dusk, or make the walk-in a shape in the dark: "two shapes ... too far to name". Or have Ned say Russ comes aboard by the stern ladder after dark.

**Issue 14 (Ch 09, Doucette's route and Arsenault's knowledge)**
- Problem: Arsenault is in the store and hears (and cuts) "Water side". He knows Beth has been warned, yet Doucette's attack proceeds over the stern, the one route Tess just named. The page does not say why Arsenault doesn't recall Doucette. He is holding "the store's radio" when taken, which might be exactly that call, but it is not stated.
- Location: "Taken in the back office with the store's radio in his hand."
- Fix: Add one clause in Russ's account: "He was calling the skiff back. Doucette had his handheld off, for quiet."

**Issue 15 (Ch 04, no response to Russ's generator remark)**
- Problem: Russ says he heard the Ashby Generac running. The point of the remark is that the location can be inferred by sound, and Dane and Beth stay another night with no discussion. This leaves an unremarked alternative leak route.
- Location: "Heard a Generac running out on the point last night"
- Fix: Add one Dane sentence after Russ leaves: "Four houses with ears. We can't un-ring a generator, so we move if it runs again." Optional.

**Issue 16 (Ch 08, count)**
- Problem: The brief asks for four physical items, trimmed. Ch 08 lists five: the log, the bank bag, Tess's hands, the pencil through the paper, and the EMD line. The pencil-through-the-paper memory ("adding the store's column twice") is introduced here and in no earlier chapter, so it is unplanted. It pays off in Ch 10 ("I add it twice", "Every column added twice").
- Location: "The winter of the tank loan, Tess adding the store's column twice every night."
- Fix: Plant it once earlier (Ch 01 counter, Ch 03 or Ch 06 register: Tess runs the total twice) or cut it from the Ch 08 list. Keep Ch 10.

**Issue 17 (Ch 03, stray italic)**
- Problem: The italic *She doesn't know.* sits between Dane's phone buzz and Arsenault's entrance. The reader reads it as Beth thinking "Tess" is "she", which would settle what Ch 07 treats as a revelation. It is ambiguous and also underlines the clue.
- Location: "*She doesn't know.*" (directly after "Whatever the gentleman's having." / "Rough night to be from away.")
- Fix: Delete it. Dane's overheard phrase first returns at "Daily. Understood." in the Ashby section, and in Ch 07.

**Issue 18 (bible rule leftovers, for the voice pass)**
- Problem: Two or more instances of the banned pattern "put it where she put things" or "filed it" remain (brief cap 0).
- Location: Ch 01 "Beth put it where she put the things she'd look at later."; Ch 02 "She put it where she put things."; Ch 03 "put it where she put things"; Ch 03 "She filed it."; Ch 06 "She filed that beside the fourteen."
- Fix: Cut or vary each; the hearing-aid count is also over the cap of 3 (Ch 01, 03, 06, 07 x3, 08, 10).
