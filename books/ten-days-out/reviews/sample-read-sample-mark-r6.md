# Sample read, sample mark, round 6: Ten Days Out, Ch 01–03

**Drafts read:** Ch 01 `final.md` (S4 tournament winner), Ch 02 `final.md` (Oct 29 tournament), Ch 03 `final.md` (store scene cut to a bridge). No other file opened.

**Persona (from orchestrator):** KU reader, 35–55, reads on her phone, three or four books a month. Reads open-door romantic suspense for the couple and the danger equally. Quits when the heat is all hints or the setup runs long. Has been bored by two books like this before.

**Warning first:** I'd keep reading, but if the next love scene also stops at the zipper because of the phone, I'm out. Two brakes in a row is a pattern, and that pattern is why I quit the last two.

Finished the sample.

## 7. The three most boring passages

1. Ch 01, about 8%: "Her hand went along the stern chain across the transom, and she kicked the ice off the ring buoy's bracket on the way by, and the quarter ladder's rungs went past her knee, black and wet..." I skimmed the boat parts list. I wanted to know if they were following her, not what the ladder looked like.
2. Ch 03, about 75–85%: the store, from "Russ took her in through the office" to "Arsenault paid for both." Five people, two Dane lines in a row, and I couldn't tell who was talking to who.
3. Ch 01, about 85%: "What's a Webbers Cove thirty-four with a rebuilt Cat worth?" through the father's boat and the picnic boat. Nice but slow, and it's sitting where I wanted the bad guy.

## 8. What I think is going on

Someone on the island is moving the drugs through the ferry and the store's money, and the person who leaked her location is close to her. The book keeps pointing at Dane (the glow under his chin on the front step, "Do not disclose to witness," the daily log), and that much pointing feels like a decoy. My real suspect is Ned. He'd "never once sent her home" until the night the reefer went quiet, he carried the store's bag, he's the one she told "Home. Don't tell anybody," and he said "Nobody but me and God" twice. Now he's handed her the key to the empty house where he "runs the generator every storm anyhow," so he knows exactly where she is. I think Tess is in on the money (the fold of hundreds, the fuel float at Marr's lit at night, the hood waiting at Marr's office door), and the Margaret Ann / Arsenault is the muscle and the way off the island. My guess is that Q's request is something like using her as bait, or watching the island through her, and that's what Dane can't tell her.
