## Part 1: the 12 round-1 findings, one by one

### F1 (the premise): STILL OPEN, narrowed
The clean truck answers round 1's question. The text now reads "Cole had unloaded at his cold-storage building in Pruett" (The case and the clock), and Ch 01 has Beth see Cole in the cab ("It was Cole Varney…"). But the outline names the building where the drugs went. A reader with any TV crime drama behind her expects the troopers who stopped a clean truck at 1 a.m., with a witness saying she saw bricks, to search the defendant's own cold storage by morning. If the load is there, Beth is no longer the whole case, and the ten-day clock fails again. The outline also contradicts itself: Ch 02 s1 has the boss say the load went "somewhere in Pruett," while the clock section names the building.
**What the architect must answer:** Did the police search Cole's cold storage, and what did they find?
**Fix, one line in Ch 02 s1 from the boss:** they searched his cold storage at dawn and found lobsters and a hosed-down floor, because the load went straight into another vehicle on the wharf. Make the clock section say the same.

### F2 (the man's route on night 1): CLOSED
The chain is on the page: Ned's call at about 10:00, the skiff at the Pruett float, Russ's line that it was "tied at ten and gone by half past," and Ch 01 edit 3 ("walked off the ramp beside the truck"). The timing works: an hour and a half across, landing a little after midnight, the break-in at 2:00. Two small gaps remain (non-blocking, N9): why the kid's skiff was waiting at Pruett when he crossed with the truck, and how Russ on Cutter knows the times at a mainland float.

### F3 (the house he broke into): CLOSED
Ch 03 s1: "her house is out, because the kid has already been in it." Russ's house is chosen to their faces, with a guard, heat and a generator. The false call that pulls Russ off the porch is planted (Ch 03 s2), proven false (Ch 04 end) and paid (Ch 12). Closing this opened N1 and N2.

### F4 (the hideout and the name): CLOSED
Ch 05 s2: the search, the warm stove, the charger, the newsletter photo. "He sterned on Cole's own boat for cash, and Cole called him 'kid.'" This is an event, and it gives Ch 05 something to happen in.

### F5 (the traceable phone): CLOSED as asked
The prepaid phone, the records pulled over Beth's objection (Ch 08 s2), and their coming back clean (Ch 10 s1) are all on the page. The fix for F3 opened a second channel the outline doesn't close (N1). One smaller point: Dane has proof of a leak at Ch 04's end (Day 2 dawn) and signal on the store steps in Ch 05, yet he doesn't ask for the records until Day 4. One line saying he asked from the store steps and the carriers took three days would fix it (non-blocking).

### F6 (telling the three about the rescue): CLOSED
Option (a) is taken. "The Marine Patrol won't land at a closed island without clearance from its emergency director, and by law that's Tess." Beth picks the beach herself, and telling Ned and Russ is her choice, made with Dane arguing. That's the condemnable choice made knowing the facts, which the map asks for, and its cost (an officer shot) is on the page. **One line still needed (non-blocking):** "she won't leave the island without telling the two men who'd want to know" is satisfied by a phone call from the Marine Patrol boat's deck. Make the reason one a call from the boat can't satisfy: she asks them to come and see her off, because it may be the last time (Ned does come, which pays it). The "by law" claim goes to the domain checker.

### F7 (the ambush as proof; the lighthouse): STILL OPEN, new form
Round 1's suggested fix, the hour, was taken, but it doesn't hold as written. Two holes:
1. **The Marine Patrol knew three o'clock.** "Dane gives his boss the day and keeps back the hour." But the boat arrives at three, so someone told the Marine Patrol the hour. Tess, clearing the landing, would have had to. The boat's crew and its dispatcher had it. So "My boss had the day. Only your three had three o'clock" (Ch 09 s2) is false on the page, and the midpoint's "his office is cleared" rests on it. Dane's original suspicion was of law enforcement ("the state police and his own office"), and the Marine Patrol is a state agency.
2. **They go back to the lighthouse.** Beth picks "the north beach below the lighthouse." Ned walks there. After the ambush, Ch 10 s1 is "the keeper's house, night." Both leads have just concluded that one of the three is the leak, and all three know they were waiting on the beach under the only empty building above it. The safer option, sleeping anywhere else, isn't closed. Ned, the traitor, under blackmail, could infer the lighthouse at once. The outline doesn't say why he doesn't give it up.

**What the architect must answer:** Who outside the three knew the hour, and why is the lighthouse still safe on Day 5 night?
**Fix:** (a) Stop asking Ch 09 to clear the office. Dane says the Marine Patrol had the hour too. Beth argues it, and it stays open until the trap. In Ch 10 s3 Dane gives his boss a fourth place, in front of Beth ("If I'm testing yours, I test mine"). The trap then clears the office on the page, and the reversal at Ch 09 is the rescue failing and the island closing, which is a real reversal. (b) After the ambush they don't go back to the lighthouse. They go somewhere nobody knows (a boathouse, the empty Dyer house, which also pays the Ch 03 plant), and Ch 10 is set there.

### F8 (the trap): CLOSED
Ch 10 s3: "built to learn who, and not to catch him. He won't leave her alone on a hill." Each person gets "don't tell anyone, not even…" Tess's breach is the reader-understandable one. Two lines are still needed (non-blocking): (a) say Dane's boss is told none of the three places, or give her a fourth (see F7); (b) Ned's reason for giving the church. A man who knows it's a test and knows Tess told him the church has picked the one leak that leads back to him the moment Tess admits it. The safer option for him is to give nothing. One line in Ch 12 fixes it: he bet Tess would never admit breaking Beth's confidence, and she did because she nearly burned.

### F9 (the Day 8 crossing): CLOSED
"In this storm the boat can't get out of [the harbor mouth], and the forecast's window opens Sunday night. The crossing will take six hours" (Ch 13 s2). The calendar works: cast off at ten, land at 4 a.m., grand jury at ten, two hours on the road. Closing it opened N4.

### F10 (the boat search): STILL OPEN, new form
The search is on the page (Ch 15 s2: "every space and both stranded trucks"). The man's route aboard contradicts itself in three places:
- Ch 14 s1: after he goes into the water, "the empty skiff swinging under the pier." They have found his skiff.
- Ch 15 s2: "a shape come up out of the black water at the stern." He swims.
- Ch 16 s1: "came over the stern from his skiff and let it go." He has a skiff again.

A competent marshal secures the skiff he found under the pier. And a man shot on Day 5 and again on Day 9, who spent Saturday night in November harbor water, then swims to a moving ferry on Sunday night and climbs its stern. The reader will refuse that, and the architect's own flag 6 fears it.
**What the architect must answer:** How does he get aboard, and where was he for the 24 hours after Ch 14?
**Fix:** In Ch 14 he doesn't go into the water. He gets back into his skiff wounded and away into the dark, and they hear the oars go. Nobody says "presumed drowned." In Ch 15 he comes alongside in the skiff in the harbor's lee as they run slow, and lets it go. Ch 15's last line is the skiff bumping the hull, then a hand on the rail.

### F11 (reader knowledge, Ned): STILL OPEN, new form
Round 1's fixes (a), (b), (c), (d), (e) and (f) were taken, and four of them work: the pier save, the stand on the beach, the hidden Russ–Tess spark, and the retitles. Fix (b) arrived with a new move that undoes them: **Ch 02 s1, on Day 1, Ned confesses that "for two years he's taken Cole's cash to run his trucks over at night… He guessed what was in them."** The outline counts this as clearing him ("A reader thinks his secret is out"). Mystery readers know that a smaller confession hiding the larger crime is a stock move, and readers who don't know it still have the plainest fact in the book: from Day 1, one of the four people who know every hiding place is on Cole's payroll, by his own admission.

What an attentive reader has, in order:
- Ch 02: Ned took Cole's money for two years.
- Ch 03: Russ's house is hit. Ned was told on the phone, in front of Dane.
- Ch 05: "Ned's boy is in rehab again in Portland." A Cole man with an addict son is a blackmail target.
- Ch 09: Ned, out at one in the morning on the night of the fire for no stated reason, points at Russ's truck. A suspect steering suspicion onto another suspect is a classic tell.
- Ch 10: "Whoever it is has another phone, and planned for this." The reader already knows who Cole would have given a phone to: his night runner.

The reader names Ned around Ch 03–05 (17–29%). Beth names him in Ch 10 s1 (~52%), citing as her first reason "Ned took Cole's money for two years," a fact she has had since Ch 02. That's a gap of at least 23 points, and it makes Beth look slow, which brief item 6 forbids.

**It also breaks two other rules:**
- **Adults talk (genre map).** Dane is a marshal with a self-confessed Cole associate on the scene. In Ch 03 he lets Beth phone Ned her location "so the ferry crew knows where she is" (the ferry is crippled, and she's off duty), and he doesn't object. In Ch 05 he says "three too many" and never says the obvious "especially the man Cole paid." A reasonable adult says it on Day 1. The outline gives him no stated, costly reason to keep quiet.
- **The premise.** Ned's written statement makes him a cooperating witness against Cole. Beth stops being "the whole case," Ned becomes a second target who needs protection, and the blackmail lever ("you've admitted the cash") loses force, because he has already admitted it to a federal officer.

**What the architect must answer:** Why does nobody treat a confessed Cole employee as the first suspect, and what keeps the reader from naming him before Ch 10?
**Fix:** Take the cash confession out of Ch 02. Move it to Ch 09 s1, on the north beach before the ambush, where Ned tells Beth and Dane while they wait, as a goodbye. The reader and Beth then learn it at the same moment (~47%), the ambush follows within the chapter, and Beth names him in Ch 10 s1 (~52%). That's a gap of about 5 points. Ch 01's line, softened by edit 2 ("never once seen Ned Haskell afraid"), reads as fear of the men twenty feet under them, and that holds until Ch 09. Ch 02 keeps the pier save. Separately, give the "Russ's truck behind the store" sighting to Dane or to Tess's own gossip, not to Ned.

### F12 (who knew each place): CLOSED
There's no signal at the loft (Ch 04 s1), the lighthouse is reported from the tower (Ch 08 s2), the boss is trusted from Ch 02 s1, and "every night" is gone. One small gap (non-blocking): "Four people knew Russ's house… and Tess" (Ch 03, What changes). Tess radioed the constable, which doesn't show she knew Beth was in his house. On the page, Dane would ask Russ who he told. Have him ask, and have Russ say "nobody." That lie is caught in Ch 11, and it strengthens the Russ–Tess red herring.

## Part 2: new blocking holes

### N1: the Dyer call is traceable
**Where:** Ch 03 s2 ("somebody phoned the store"); Ch 04 end ("a man on the store phone, a bad line"); Ch 12 s2 (Ned "phoned the store about the Dyer roof himself"); traitor's path row 03 ("from the prepaid phone").
**Question failed:** 3.
**The problem:** By Ch 04's end both leads know the Dyer call was a lie to pull the guard off the porch. The obvious competent move is to get the store's incoming call log. That gives the prepaid phone's number on Day 2. An emergency records request on that number shows a call to Cole at about 10 p.m. on Day 0 and a location at Cutter harbor. The only people at the Pruett ramp at ten that night were the ferry crew. The mystery ends in Ch 05, and the outline never says why Dane doesn't ask.
**Fix:** Ned makes the call from the empty Dyer house's own landline (he looks after the place, or any islander knows where the key is). The store's caller ID shows "Dyer," which is exactly why Tess believed it and radioed Russ. The records show Mrs. Dyer's line, and anyone on the island could have walked in. Ch 04 end: Russ found the Dyer back door open. The prepaid phone stays untouched.

### N2: Ch 05. They give the next bed to the same four, one day after the proof
**Where:** Ch 04 end (the Dyer call proven false); Ch 05 s1 (summer house shared with Russ, Tess, Ned and the boss); Ch 06 s2 note ("nobody knew the loft, and nobody came").
**Questions failed:** 4 and 5.
**The problem:** At dawn on Day 2 they know someone sent the guard away on purpose, and that the people who knew Russ's house were Russ, Ned, Tess and the boss. They also know the loft, which nobody knew, worked. Three hours later they tell the same four the next place. "These three are the island" is not a reason a reader accepts the morning after a man shot through the window. The outline's "both are partly right" has no content for Beth's side. This is round 1's F6 moved earlier and made worse by the F3 fix.
**What the architect must answer:** What does Beth have, at that moment, that makes the Ch 03 hit look like something other than a leak?
**Fix:** Two lines in Ch 05 s1. Beth: "Russ sat on his porch with a shotgun in a storm. The whole harbor road knew where I was, and anyone could pick up the store phone." Dane can't refute it. And: they can't last ten days in an unheated net loft with no food, so they need someone with a key and someone with groceries. With that, Ch 03 doesn't prove a leak, Beth's choice is credible, and Ch 06 (an unguarded house nobody could see) is the first proof, which is where the outline already has both of them say "somebody told."

### N3: Dane holds the kid's phone number from Day 7
**Where:** Ch 12 s3 (the prepaid phone buzzes, *where is she*); Ch 13 s3 ("he's alone and bleeding in a shut summer house"); Ch 14 (the bait).
**Question failed:** 4.
**The problem:** The kid texts Ned's prepaid phone, and Dane has it in hand. An emergency location request on the kid's number is the standard move for a hunted armed man. And the outline states where he is ("a shut summer house"), so the reader asks why Dane and Russ, with the man wounded twice, don't go and get him on Day 7 instead of using the witness as bait on Day 9.
**Fix:** One line in Ch 13 s3. The island has one cell tower, so the ping says "Cutter Island," which they knew. "A shut summer house" is Dane's guess, out of forty on the island, and he won't search them door to door in a storm while leaving her with a wounded man loose. Phrase it on the page as his guess.

### N4: the Sunday window. Why the ferry and not a professional boat?
**Where:** Ch 13 s2 (the window opens Sunday night); Ch 15 s2 ("no Coast Guard boat can land at the broken pier"); Ch 09 (the Marine Patrol landed on the north beach on Day 5).
**Question failed:** 4.
**The problem:** If the sea on Sunday night lets a crippled ferry with jury-rigged steering cross for six hours, with a burned-handed deckhand at the wheel for the first time, it lets a Marine Patrol or Coast Guard boat come. A professional boat doesn't need the pier: the Marine Patrol landed on the beach on Day 5, and a boat can stand off and take her by small boat. The "broken pier" is also new in Ch 15. Ch 02 broke the ferry's steering, not the pier. The climax depends on her taking her own boat, and the reader needs one reason she has to.
**What the architect must answer:** Why is the ferry the only boat that can come for her inside the window?
**Fix:** One line from the boss in Ch 13 s2 and one in Ch 15 s2. Every Coast Guard boat on the coast is on the storm's distress calls. The nearest cutter is in Boston and can't be there before Tuesday. The Marine Patrol boat that came on Day 5 is laid up with a bullet through its hull. The north beach is open to the second storm's sea. Plant the pier damage in Ch 02 s2, when the ferry swings into it.

## Part 3: new non-blocking holes (one line each)

- **N5, the bait's reason (Ch 13 s3).** "He needs off this island as badly as she does" doesn't hold: he has a skiff and crossed the bay in sleet on night 1, and what he needs is her dead or kept off the mainland by Monday. Give the real reason: if he disables the only boat that can make the window, Cole walks Monday, so the ferry has to be guarded every night anyway. Dane won't split from Beth, so she's aboard either way, and the text only picks the night.
- **N6, the office suspicion dies in Ch 02.** Russ's skiff line shows the kid left Pruett before Beth called, so no leak was needed on night 1 (the kid saw her see). Dane's stated reason for suspecting his office is gone in the same scene where he uses it to justify staying. Either have Dane say so and keep his suspicion on Russ's house alone, or accept that the office suspicion is weak and lean on the trap (F7 fix).
- **N7, Beth's reason to stay (Ch 02 s1).** "She's the only person who can put a face to him" is false by Ch 05, because Russ and the whole island know the kid's face from two summers. Change it to "the only one who saw him with the drugs" or "the only one he's afraid of."
- **N8, Ned's night-1 call.** The kid saw Beth see the bricks and walked off beside Cole. Cole knew without Ned. "That's why the truck was clean… his betrayal starts the plot" (traitor's path) isn't true on the page. Give Ned's call the one fact the kid didn't have: "She'll call it in tonight. She told me." Then his call is what made Cole dump the load before Route 1.
- **N9, the skiff at Pruett.** The kid rode over in the back of the truck, but his skiff was already tied at the Pruett float. Add one line saying why it was there: he always runs home in it after the unload, because he won't ride back on the ferry. Also say how Russ knows the Pruett times (the Pruett harbormaster, on the phone).
- **N10, Ned out at 1 a.m.** Ned saw Russ's truck behind the store at one in the morning. Why was he out? If the sighting stays with Ned (see F11), the reader asks.
- **N11, the kid's body.** He's shot in the leg on Day 5 and shot again on Day 9, and he's still fighting on a steel deck in a storm on Day 10. The F10 fix (he stays out of the water) carries most of it. Make the Day 5 wound a graze.
- **N12, Ch 17 s3.** Heat at level 4 on the day of a gunshot wound to the side, after Dane signs himself out of the hospital. Romantic-suspense readers tolerate it. "Careful of his side" has to be specific on the page or it will read as a convention.
- **N13, the danger count.** "Something bad happens on the page in 14 of 18 chapters." On the page it's 12 (01, 02, 03, 04, 06, 07, 08, 09, 11, 12, 14, 16). Ch 15's is its last line and Ch 17's is tension. The every-second-chapter rule still holds. Correct the claim.
- **N14, sameness.** Ch 03 and Ch 06 are the same chapter: a night attack where she sleeps, then out a back way into the storm. Ch 06's 2 a.m. also echoes Ch 01's. Move one of the two (the fire to dusk, or the Ch 03 shots to evening, before Russ is called away).
- **N15, Beth and the boy she taught to row.** If any version keeps Ned admitting he "guessed what was in" the trucks, Beth's reaction has to be on the page, because two island kids died of it. Under the F11 fix it lands in Ch 09, where it can drive her choice to stand beside him in the water or not.
