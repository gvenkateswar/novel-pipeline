# Ten Days Out: what changed in the back half, and why

*Story-architect, Nov 5, 2026. The author approved changing the shape of Ch 10–18 ("update the writing based on the above feedback, and finish the book"). The new outline is `outline.md` (revision 1, after the logic audit `reviews/outline-audit-backhalf.md`). Earlier versions: `outline-v4-pre-backhalf.md` (before the rebuild) and `outline-v4-backhalf-r0.md` (the rebuild before the audit).*

## The problem

The cold reader said the book was going in a loop. Four times so far, the same thing happened: Beth and Dane hide somewhere, the man finds them, there's shooting, they escape, and then they argue over who could have told him. The old Ch 10–12 would have run it a fifth time, with a third fire and a second rescue from the water. The reader had also stopped believing Dane is good at his job.

## What changed

1. **They go on the offense.** They follow the kid's blood trail to his boat (Ch 10). They set a trap for the leak and are in the room when it springs (Ch 11). They go into the kid's last hiding place, Beth's own house, and take him (Ch 13).
2. **Dane is good at his job where the reader can see it.**
   - He wins the argument to go after the kid, reads the trail, and gets to the kid when the kid goes for Beth.
   - He tends Beth's cut and picks and secures a safe house.
   - He works out that his boss is clean and matches the lawyer's number.
   - He gets police to Ned's son inside the hour.
   - He plans a raid that would have worked if Russ hadn't stepped into the doorway.
   - He takes the kid alive, cuts the ferry's last rope at the right moment, and holds off the big boat at sea.
3. **No more fires and no more rescues from the water.**
4. **Ned is caught by a confrontation.** They text the leak as if the kid were bleeding to death. Ned comes in the storm with his good blanket to save the boy, and finds Beth under it. The phone rings in his coat, and he owns the Dyer call. That's at about 63%, and Beth and the reader learn it together.
5. **Every chapter is a different kind of chapter:** a hunt, a trap, a reckoning under fire, a raid, a storm, a supper and a departure, a chase at sea, a courthouse, a holiday table. Danger stays active (Ch 12 now has the kid firing on the walk), and each chapter adds a new fact.
6. **The last act has a new threat.** Once the kid is caught, a big boat showing one light, sent by whoever brings the drugs in, comes for the ferry at sea. Beth beats it at the wheel by taking her father's way through the rocks. Dane holds it off and is grazed. It gets away, and that's Book 2.

## What the audit changed (revision 1)

- Ch 10's night has a reason and a watch. Beth has the house key, she's the one hurt and Dane tends her, and she starts it at the door, so heat #3 no longer repeats Ch 04 and Ch 07.
- Beth says on the page why nobody can take the kid's skiff across.
- Russ is shot because he steps into the doorway to talk the kid out, against Dane's plan.
- They're told why no boat can escort them, the minute they see the big boat.
- Ch 14's snapping rope is gone. Dane cuts the line himself, and the danger is the steering jamming toward the rocks. Beth's palms are torn holding the wheel.
- Dane keeps his view when he gives up the decision ("I still think you should go. It was never mine to say.").
- Two boss speaker calls instead of five. Dane's talking-through-danger is saved for the climax.
- Every boat is "it." Ned's line is now "I've got it, Bethie. Go."

## What stayed the same

Ned is the traitor, and Tess and Russ are cleared and together. The boss is cleared. Heat #3 is in Ch 10 and #4 comes after the declaration. The fight about witness protection still passes the one-talk test, and so does the repair. Beth is at the wheel in the climax. The grand jury on Monday the 23rd with her testimony on the page; the Thanksgiving ending; Dane's leaky boat; the September photo; the captain's job in June. The book still lands near 36,500 words.

## Things the author should know

- The content note loses "a suicide attempt" and one of the "two fires."
- The bible has to change to match. The list is at the end of `outline.md`.
- Two facts need the domain checker: the prosecutor asking Beth about the relationship in the grand jury room, and the lawyer staying free until a second witness.
- The reader will probably be sure of Ned before Ch 11, so that chapter's pull comes from how he's caught, not who.
