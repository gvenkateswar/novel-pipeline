## 8. What I think is going on

Somebody on the island is moving the drugs, and somebody is telling the man with the dragging heel where Beth is. Dane's phone points at him, or at someone in Portland he reports to. The "Q" and "Do not disclose to witness" message makes me think the government is watching her or her father's history, not just guarding her. Tess has hundreds in her vest, a yellow tag on the store bag that rides the ferry, a safe, a log Beth didn't want Dane to read, and a face that lit up for Arsenault. She also saw Beth's back-step light at 22:40 the night the mudroom was entered and "didn't put you on the air." So I think Tess's store is how the money moves, and Arsenault's dragger is the supply boat. It went dark the minute they left for Ashby, which I take to mean somebody's coming there next. But Ned is the one who sent her home the first night like he knew, never asked about the crate, carried Tess's bag up, knew she'd be home ("Nobody but me and God"), and now knows she's at Ashby too. Beth is shielding both Ned and Tess from Dane. My guess is that one of the two people she loves told the man in the mudroom where she was, and the book wants me to blame Tess so that it can turn out to be Ned.

**Suspects for the leak (who told the hooded man where Beth was), from the page only:**
- Ned: 40%. He knew she was home, he's lovable and he's everywhere, and he has the key to where she is now.
- Tess: 30%. The money, the bag, the log, she saw the light, Arsenault.
- Someone on Dane's end in Portland (Pike, or the "Q" side) reading his calls: 20%.
- Dane himself: 5%.
- Someone else (Russ, Arsenault directly): 5%.

**What the page has already given away:** Tess's store bag and money read as dirty even with the seal-tag clue moved out. The tag on the bag, the fold of hundreds, the safe and the log are four flags in three chapters. And Ned's "Nobody but me and God" twice, plus his knowing both hiding places, makes him the obvious twist candidate.
