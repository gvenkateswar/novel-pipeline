## Chapter 01 — The Last Truck

*From `chapters/chapter-01/final.md`, 2,510 words, approved by the author Oct 30, 2026. POV: Beth (s1–s2), Dane (s3).*

**What happened:** Thursday Nov 12, night. On the last crossing to Pruett, Cole Varney's refrigerated lobster truck comes aboard with its cooling unit off. A crate splits; Beth sees fentanyl bricks under the lobsters. The young man in the hoodie (thin face, split lip, drags his left heel) says "You didn't see this." Ned tells her, "Then you didn't see it. Not tonight," and she sees him afraid for the first time in eleven years. She photographs the plate at Pruett, calls the state police at midnight, the case goes federal, and a marshal is sent by police boat. A little before three a man breaks in, says her name; she goes out the kitchen window in socks to her father's fish shack and arms herself with his oar. Dane arrives, the man flees by an unlit boat, Beth swings the oar at Dane; sparks; he wants her off the island for ten days to the grand jury; she refuses.
**What changed:** Beth is a witness and a target. She and Dane have met and want each other, plainly.
**Who knows what:** Beth: drugs, Cole drove, the hoodie man drags his left foot and is the intruder, he's local (knew the cove). Ned: knows she saw. Dane: the attacker reached her within three hours of her call; suspects a leak.
**Objects / details introduced:** Dane's new boots from a Route 1 shop that sells fudge, bought for a Saturday boat viewing; his motel ten minutes from Pruett harbor; the oar; the kitchen window; her father's fish shack on the rocks; Dane's coat around her; Beth's gray eyes, blond braid, almost his height.
**Plants placed:** Ned afraid (→ Ch 09–12); the dragging left heel (→ every attack); the boy she taught to row, dead of fentanyl (→ her reason to stay); "Ask me again in ten days" (→ Ch 18); somebody gave the man her name (→ Ch 02, Dane is wrong).
**Numbers stated:** eleven years on the ferry; Cole six years on the Thursday run, $20 at Christmas; $12,000 of lobster; two island kids dead in three years; the trooper's nine-minute hold; maybe forty witnesses protected; ten days.
**Ends on:** the fish shack door, before dawn, Beth in Dane's coat, the unlit boat gone to someone on her island.
