## Chapter 01 — The Last Truck

*From `chapters/chapter-01/final.md`, 2,510 words, approved by the author Oct 30, 2026. POV: Beth (s1–s2), Dane (s3).*

**What happened:** Thursday Nov 12, night. On the last crossing to Pruett, Cole Varney's refrigerated lobster truck comes aboard with its cooling unit off. A crate splits; Beth sees fentanyl bricks under the lobsters. The young man in the hoodie (thin face, split lip, drags his left heel) says "You didn't see this." Ned tells her, "Then you didn't see it. Not tonight," and she sees him afraid for the first time in eleven years. She photographs the plate at Pruett, calls the state police at midnight, the case goes federal, and a marshal is sent by police boat. A little before three a man breaks in, says her name; she goes out the kitchen window in socks to her father's fish shack and arms herself with his oar. Dane arrives, the man flees by an unlit boat, Beth swings the oar at Dane; sparks; he wants her off the island for ten days to the grand jury; she refuses.
**What changed:** Beth is a witness and a target. She and Dane have met and want each other, plainly.
**Who knows what:** Beth: drugs, Cole drove, the hoodie man drags his left foot and is the intruder, he's local (knew the cove). Ned: knows she saw. Dane: the attacker reached her within three hours of her call; suspects a leak.
**Objects / details introduced:** Dane's new boots from a Route 1 shop that sells fudge, bought for a Saturday boat viewing; his motel ten minutes from Pruett harbor; the oar; the kitchen window; her father's fish shack on the rocks; Dane's coat around her; Beth's gray eyes, blond braid, almost his height.
**Plants placed:** Ned afraid (→ Ch 09–12); the dragging left heel (→ every attack); the boy she taught to row, dead of fentanyl (→ her reason to stay); "Ask me again in ten days" (→ Ch 18); somebody gave the man her name (→ Ch 02, Dane is wrong).
**Numbers stated:** eleven years on the ferry; Cole six years on the Thursday run, $20 at Christmas; $12,000 of lobster; two island kids dead in three years; the trooper's nine-minute hold; maybe forty witnesses protected; ten days.
**Ends on:** the fish shack door, before dawn, Beth in Dane's coat, the unlit boat gone to someone on her island.

## Chapter 02 — First Light

*From `chapters/chapter-02/final.md`, 2,215 words, after one reader-fix round (cold reader pull 3 of 5 with "I'd keep reading, but…"; plain reader PASS). POV: Beth.*

**What happened:** Friday Nov 13 (Day 1). Beth wakes on the couch under Dane's coat, "wanting the marshal"; he has nailed a board across her back door; she touches the oar bruise. At first light the Marine Patrol boat waits at the float with ten minutes; she puts one foot aboard and says no. On speaker, Dane's boss: the truck was clean, Beth is the whole case; grand jury Monday Nov 23, the prosecutor wants her in the room, or "he walks. No charge, no trial." Russ (constable) brings news: a skiff left Pruett at half past ten with a thin man with a bad leg. Dane drops his office theory ("I was wrong about that") but asks who told Cole she'd seen; Beth: "He saw me. He'd have told Cole himself." / "Maybe." He lives on the island, rode over with the truck, came home by skiff. Dane stays ("Forty-one, as of this morning"); he reports only to his boss, his habit on every case. The police boat leaves. A ferry rope parts in the storm; Ned hauls Beth clear and hurts his hip; she thanks him; Dane jumps aboard badly and takes a rope. The float breaks apart; the ferry's steering is gone ("It's not answering, Bethie"); the island's lobster boats drag onto the rocks, Russ's among them. The hold: "You can let go." / "I know." Russ: "Where are you sleeping tonight?… Somebody else will be asking before dark." Dane: "Not there." She lets him decide.
**What changed:** No way off the island: no police boat, no ferry steering, no float, no lobster boats. Dane is staying. Beth wants him and has said so to herself plainly.
**Who knows what:** The boss knows Dane stays and reports only to her. Russ knows about the skiff. Open question on the page: who told Cole she'd seen.
**Objects / details introduced:** the board across the back door; the float (low floating dock below the stone pier, glossed); one broken float section chained to the pier, half awash; ferry tied along the pier, ramp out over water, steering gone; the lumber truck and the bait truck stuck on the car deck (boarded after Thursday's last crossing for Friday's first run); Ned's hip; Dane's knees from the jump.
**Plants placed:** Ned can't swim / "I don't go in" (→ Ch 12); Ned looks back (→ later); who told Cole (→ Ch 09–11); talk against silence in danger (→ Ch 16); "I'll keep talking till you tell me to stop" (→ Ch 16).
**Plants paid:** the dragging leg (← Ch 01) via Russ's description; the leak hook (← Ch 01) reframed.
**Numbers stated:** forty-one witnesses; ten minutes; half past ten; Monday the twenty-third; a hundred feet.
**Ends on:** the pier, Day 1 morning, Dane's "Not there," and Beth not arguing.

## Chapter 03 — The Constable's Kitchen

*From `chapters/chapter-03/final.md`, after one reader-fix round (cold reader 3 of 5, "I'd keep reading, but…"; plain reader PASS). POV: Dane.*

**What happened:** Friday Nov 13, afternoon to about 1:20 a.m. Saturday. Dane loses the argument about who goes into Beth's broken house first and wins the one about where she sleeps: Russ's house, the only house on the harbor road with a generator, lit for the whole road to see, Russ on the porch with a shotgun. Beth wanted her old bunk on the ferry; Dane refused (one way off a boat, the man came by water, first place anyone looks). Power fails at a quarter past four. Six o'clock call to his boss in front of Beth; the boss asks who else knows where she is ("Six tomorrow morning, then. Me and nobody else."). At ten a light moves on the black harbor and goes out. The first kiss, on the page: she counts to three, he gets to two. He stops it for the lawyer and the jury; she wins most of the argument ("A jury doesn't get a vote on who kisses me. I do. I voted." / "He'll still say it." / "Let him. I'll still say yes."). "As of tonight? One. I'm keeping count." "Tonight's a bad night." / "Then tomorrow." / "Probably sooner." About one a.m. Tess relays a radio call: the Dyers' nephew, out in his own boat Thursday, a tree through the roof, leg pinned. Beth: the nephew lives in Augusta and hasn't been here in ten years. Dane: "It could be to get you off this porch." Russ goes anyway. Twenty minutes later the window is shot in; Dane is cut on the left forearm by glass, above the oar bruise; he doesn't fire (can't see the man, a road of sleeping houses behind him). Behind the woodpile: "That call was to get Russ off the porch." / "I know." A dragging-heel print under the kitchen window. "Russ." / "Later." Beth: "I know a place."
**What changed:** They have kissed and both said what they want; the attacker is armed; Russ's house is gone as a refuge; Russ is off the page, fate unknown.
**Who knows what:** The boss knows the house layout (porch, generator, stove, one back door). Tess relayed the call. Beth knows the nephew story is false.
**Objects / details introduced:** Russ's generator (choke), his shotgun, his truck; the woodpile; Dane's cut left forearm; the island emergency radio, Tess runs it and the one fire truck.
**Plants placed:** who sent the decoy call (→ Ch 05 Tess's account; Ch 12 Ned); the boss knew the house (→ Ch 10 trap tests the office); "I know a place" (→ Ch 04).
**Numbers stated:** quarter past four; six o'clock; ten; about one; twenty minutes; one kiss as of tonight.
**Ends on:** behind the woodpile at Russ's, the shooter reloading or moving, "I know a place."
