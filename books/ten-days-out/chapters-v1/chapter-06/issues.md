**Issue 1**
- Problem: Sex scene 2 is over the 1,000-word cap. Manual count (about plus or minus 5 percent): the whole scene, from Dane returning from the call to the sleep paragraph, is about 1,150 words. Counted only from the box coming out of the bag to the sleep paragraph, it is about 970, with no margin. The opening "before" beat (notebook pocket, jacket, "not asking") is about 190 words of the total.
- Location: Scene runs from "At twenty past eleven Dane went out under the sky for his call." to "...slowly gave up the damp of the ledge."
- Suggested fix: Cut the notebook/jacket opening to two or three sentences and trim the final sleep paragraph, so the whole scene is at or under 1,000.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: Day 5 (Scene 1) exceeds the 600-word hard cap in the bible calendar and beat sheet. Manual estimate is about 650 to 700 words.
- Location: Scene 1, from "The green ledger lay open by the register, and Beth read it upside down." to "...finished one song and started the next."
- Suggested fix: Trim Ned's shed speech and the opening description of the store to bring Day 5 to 600 or fewer.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: Beth's counting exceeds the cold-read rule of at most twice per chapter, which the beat sheet limits to scenes 2 and (optionally) 5. There is a third numeric count in Scene 6, plus a timing count in Scene 2.
- Location: "From the back door to where the lane dropped behind the seawall: fourteen steps." / "Fourteen steps to the seawall." / "it was six steps to it without counting them" / "She'd timed it herself at breakfast."
- Suggested fix: Keep "fourteen" in Scene 2 and Scene 5, and change the Scene 6 sentence to "she knew exactly where the door was" with no number. Also cut "She'd timed it herself at breakfast."

**Issue 4**
- Problem: Sex scene 2 reuses images and lines from Ch 05's scene. These are the hot-stove side against the cold window side, wind in the shingles, the callus at the base of the fingers, and the palm flat on his chest. The callus detail is from Ch 05's first scene and the others are from its sex scene. The cold-read rule bars reused images.
- Location: "The drum threw its heat across her right side, hip and shoulder and the side of her face, and off the water-side window the cold came steady on her left. July on one side of her, November on the other, and Dane between." / "the callus at the base of his fingers rough on her skin" / "Wind worked at the shingles, less than at supper." / "She laid her hand flat on his chest."
- Suggested fix: Drop the heat/cold split (or the callus line) and use a different sensory anchor, such as the lantern wick, the canvas smell or the cot frame. Change "worked at the shingles" to another verb or cut it.

**Issue 5**
- Problem: Dane already told Beth about the lost witness in Ch 04 ("She asked for ten minutes. I gave them to her."). Ch 06 presents the Marisol story as a fresh disclosure and Beth shows no recognition. The bible's "what she knows" line also says she learns it on Day 5-6.
- Location: "'Four years ago,' he said. 'Boston. I had a witness.'" / "'She wanted to see her sister. On the way to the site. Ten minutes.' He breathed out. 'I said yes.'"
- Suggested fix: Add one beat of Beth recalling the Ch 04 line ("ten minutes"), so that what is new is the name, the parking lot and the sister.

**Issue 6**
- Problem: The tide is wrong for the day. Per the bible, Day 4 low water is about 7:10 a.m., and low water moves later each day. Day 6 low is therefore about 8:50 a.m., so noon is mid-flood, not "near low water". The Day 7 5:50 a.m. line ("three hours and more off low water") matches the table.
- Location: "Near low water. The float boards were green with weed and gave under her boots."
- Suggested fix: Change the Day 6 noon scene to "Half flood" (or move the scene to about 9 a.m.), and soften the ramp pitch line to suit.

**Issue 7**
- Problem: The ambush shot does not match the required details. The "about sixty yards" distance and the skiff as the source are not on the page. The geometry is also unclear. A round that "banged a foot behind her head" with light coming through the wall suggests fire from the lane side, not from the harbor. Dane's wound is never named as a graze to the arm.
- Location: "The shot came through the wall. Corrugated metal banged a foot behind her head and a coin of gray light opened in it. Dane was already at her back, between her and the water side, and he jerked against her and drove her through the door."
- Suggested fix: State that the shot came from the water (for example, Beth hears the skiff turn and the crack from "sixty yards out"). Have the round come through the front wall and hit Dane's left upper arm before it spends itself, and keep the exit hole on the harbor-side wall.

**Issue 8**
- Problem: The bible's pivotal Ch 09 phrase "water side" (Tess's warning) is reused as casual narration here, which dilutes the payoff.
- Location: "Dane was already at her back, between her and the water side"
- Suggested fix: Change to "between her and the harbor".

**Issue 9**
- Problem: Russ is bailing with "a bleach jug cut down for a scoop", the same distinguishing detail used for Doucette in Ch 04 ("He's bailing", the cut-down jug). It creates a false echo and confuses the identification.
- Location: "Russ was on the float bailing the skiff he rowed out to *Second Wind*, with a bleach jug cut down for a scoop."
- Suggested fix: Give Russ a different bailer, such as a coffee can or a hand pump.

**Issue 10**
- Problem: Beth scouts and paces the bait-shed route at noon on Day 6, before Dane's 3:30 call sets the landing, then proposes the same route at 3:30 as if it were new. Russ also already knows she is being "taken off", with no source on the page.
- Location: "At the top she didn't take the lane straight off the ramp. She ducked under the bait shed's roll-up door" / "Shore lane," she said. "Back of the bait shed."
- Suggested fix: Make the noon scouting explicitly a general habit of hers ("She walked every way off a float before she needed one"), and have Beth tell Russ nothing that implies a pickup. Alternatively move Russ's "When are they taking you off?" to a vaguer "How long have you got them?"

**Issue 11**
- Problem: Emotion is stated rather than shown in two places, and "He didn't ask" appears twice as a Dane tic (echoing the retired "not asking" beat). Beth's one "not asking" is also stated outright ("She found she didn't need either one").
- Location: "she liked him for that more than she'd say" / "She found she didn't need either one." / "Dane stood in the back doorway and watched her pace it. He didn't ask." / "Dane was at her shoulder. He didn't ask."
- Suggested fix: Cut "and she liked him for that more than she'd say". Replace the second "He didn't ask" with an action, such as him checking the Icom's channel.

**Issue 12**
- Problem: Three scenes may end on one-line buttons, against the limit of two (Scenes 3 and 6 only). Scene 2 ends on a short closing action line that reads as a button.
- Location: "she pulled the door to and the latch dropped."
- Suggested fix: Extend the Scene 2 ending into an image, for example the cold of the wall still in her palm and the lane gray beyond the seawall, rather than ending on the latch.
