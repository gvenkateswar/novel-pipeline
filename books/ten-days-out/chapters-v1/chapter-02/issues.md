**Issue 1**
- Problem: POV break and premature hint. In Beth's close POV the narration reports what Dane is thinking and what is in his pocket. Beth has not yet seen the notebook, and the credibility order does not exist until Day 2, so a hesitation over it plants a secret that is not there yet.
- Location: "His hand had gone to the notebook pocket inside his jacket and rested there, not opening it. Then it came away. He let it go."
- Suggested fix: Cut the three sentences, or show only what Beth can see ("He said nothing and his hand went to his inside pocket and stayed there a second"), with no mention of a notebook and no sense that he decided against opening it.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: The ferry slip is given a "float" that Key Locations does not have. The slip is a timber-pile slip with a transfer bridge. The floats are the fuel float at the store and the town landing float. Dane's seven-person count and the road distances are built on this invented float.
- Location: "He counted the float while the boat was still a hundred yards out. Seven." and "From the float, the first two hundred yards of road."
- Suggested fix: Change "float" to "slip" or "the head of the slip" or "the transfer bridge" in both places, and in "Seven people on a float on a November morning".

**Issue 3**
- Problem: Father's chair location contradicts Ch 01. In Ch 01 Beth sits in her father's chair in the kitchen, turned to face the mudroom door. Here the chair stands in the front room beside the chimney, with the couch.
- Location: "laid it over the arm of her father's chair, a step from her, and left it there without holding it out."
- Suggested fix: Put the blanket over the back of a kitchen chair, or over the arm of the couch, so the front-room geography matches Ch 01.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: Dane says he has told no one where they are, but he phoned Pike from Beth's kitchen ("I'm with her") that morning. Pike's nightly 11:20 call also goes to him at this house. Beth's later "You" and the circle of "only Dane" rest on this line.
- Location: "We stay here tonight. I haven't told anyone where. I won't."
- Suggested fix: Make the claim narrower and true: "I'm not telling anyone outside my office where we sleep. I won't." Or "I haven't told anyone on this island where."

**Issue 5**
- Problem: Dane explains to Beth things she has known all her life, which is the craft rule against a character explaining what both already know. This includes the exits of her own house and what LifeFlight and the ballfield mean on Cutter. "Leave that off" is also an instruction about her own mudroom, and the bible says he never orders her about things she knows better.
- Location: "The back door's the only way out of this house except the front, and the front opens on the road." and "LifeFlight's medical, and it lands on the ballfield in front of half the island."
- Suggested fix: Cut the exit sentence and the LifeFlight clause down to what Dane alone knows (his reasons about the latch as an alarm, and the trail of whose boat). Have Beth supply the ballfield point herself in four words, or drop it. Change "Leave that off" to a question ("Can that stay off the latch?").

**Issue 6**
- Problem: The deadline is wrong against the calendar and the bible. Monday, November 23 falls inside Thanksgiving week (Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26), so it cannot be "before Thanksgiving week". Bible Rule 4 and Research note 22 say it is the last sitting before Pelletier's Rule 5.1 deadline.
- Location: "It's the last day they sit before Thanksgiving week."
- Suggested fix: Change to "It's the last day they sit before his hearing deadline" or "the last day they sit that week".

**Issue 7**
- Problem: Day arithmetic. Friday night plus ten days is Monday night. Calling that Sunday contradicts the plain count of days, and "one to spare" depends on it.
- Location: "Ten days is Sunday. That's one to spare."
- Suggested fix: Have Dane count the way the case does, using the day numbers: "Ned's ten days runs to Day 10, that's Sunday. Grand jury's Day 11. That's one to spare." Or "Ned says ten. Call it Sunday morning if the state moves."
