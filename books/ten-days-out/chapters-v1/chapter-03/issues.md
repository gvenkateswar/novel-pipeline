**Issue 1**
- Problem: The boat count contradicts Ch 01. Ch 01 gives nine lobster boats plus the dragger on the moorings. With the dragger gone, Beth should count nine, not eleven. No new boats are explained.
- Location: "She counted the boats she could see, left to right. Eleven. The red hull wasn't one of them."
- Suggested fix: Change "Eleven" to "Nine", or add one clause explaining two extra boats (for example, boats that came in off the outer moorings for the storm).

**Issue 2**
- Problem: Tess knows Dane is federal. Beth never told her, and Dane has not introduced himself to her. Ch 02 gives her only "company off the morning boat." Nobody on the page registers this, and it reads as an unplanned leak tell. It also breaks the bible's rule that Tess has no guilt tell before Ch 08.
- Location: "Coffee's a dollar. For you, a dollar fifty. Federal rate."
- Suggested fix: Change the joke so it needs no knowledge of his job, for example "Off-island rate."

**Issue 3**
- Problem: The generator cost contradicts Ch 02 and the bible. On Friday, before the outage, Tess said the generator "eats forty dollars of gas a day." Here it is forty-two, and the price is said to have risen on Thursday, which is before that Friday call.
- Location: "Generator's at forty-two a day now, gas went up on me Thursday"
- Suggested fix: Change to "Generator's eating forty a day" and cut the Thursday price-rise clause.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: The scene cites an action from Ch 02 that is not on the page. On the porch in Ch 02, Beth sees an antenna and "something small and flat" in his other hand. His hand does not go to an inside pocket.
- Location: "the pocket his hand had gone to on the porch last night, and zipped it"
- Suggested fix: Cut the callback: "He put it in the inside pocket of his jacket and zipped it."

**Issue 5**
- Problem: The claim of an established habit is not supported by Ch 02. In Ch 02 Dane stands ("he didn't sit") and dries his hair standing at the stove. No chair against a wall is shown. The "every time since yesterday morning" pattern is unseen.
- Location: "He took the chair against the wall with the window on his left, the one he'd taken every time since yesterday morning."
- Suggested fix: Drop "every time since yesterday morning" and let Beth notice the choice now, as a new observation.

**Issue 6**
- Problem: A logic gap in the check-in log. The landlines were dead from 6 a.m. Saturday, and Beth has kept off the net on purpose (the handheld is on 16 and 13). Nobody counted her at the 0800 net, so she knows she did not check in. A tick beside her name goes unexplained and Beth does not register it, so she appears to miss something she would catch.
- Location: "Saturday's 0800 column had a tick beside her name."
- Suggested fix: Add one line of Beth's thought: she made no call and no net call, so Tess ticked her on assumption. She files it as Tess keeping her off the air.

**Issue 7**
- Problem: Beth leaves out the shore path when asked how many ways into the Ashby place. The bible says she knows it, and Ch 05 relies on it. This also looks like her forgetting something she would definitely know.
- Location: "Lathams' has two. Hallorans' has the road and the church lot. Ashby's has the drive."
- Suggested fix: "Ashby's has the drive, and the shore path if you can cross the bar."

**Issue 8**
- Problem: A promised beat goes unpaid. Dane's condition is "you tell me who in that room is from away." Beth names the six as "Island, all six" only in narration and never tells him. Dane's own line, "A room full of people I don't know," also overstates a room of six. Arsenault walks in later, and Beth gives Dane no account of him.
- Location: "Six people in the room. She named them to herself. Island, all six."
- Suggested fix: Add a low line from Beth to Dane at the stove ("Island, all of them") and have Dane note Arsenault himself. Or cut the "tell me who is from away" condition from scene 1.

**Issue 9**
- Problem: A world-rule risk. Dane's Iridium works only outdoors with open sky (World Rule 5). Here his voice is "just past the boards" of the mudroom wall at the north gable, under the eave line where scene 1 puts him.
- Location: "His voice, just past the boards. Low."
- Suggested fix: Place him out from the wall, in the open dooryard, with his voice carried along the clapboards by the lull. Or say he has walked out past the eave to get sky.
