**Issue 1**
- Problem: The lit kitchen bulb contradicts earlier chapters. Ch 01 ends with Beth switching the kitchen light off. In Ch 02 Dane has the overhead lights off all evening and a kerosene lamp is the only light when the cable goes at nine. No bulb was "left switched on the night the cable went." Beth also has not been back in the house since Day 2.
- Location: "her own kitchen window was one of them, the bulb she'd left switched on the night the cable went, burning in a house with nobody in it."
- Suggested fix: Make the window lit because the grid is back and the switch was found on, or drop the bulb. For example, "her own kitchen window was one of them, lit by a switch nobody had thrown, in a house with nobody in it."

**Issue 2**
- Problem: Nothing on the page returns the subpoena from the cottage. Ch 02 pins it under the barometer frame by the kitchen door. They left the cottage on Day 2 and never went back, because the back door was tried and the house was watched. Dane's own copy was in his pocket.
- Location: "The subpoena was in her float coat pocket, folded in quarters and gone soft from its days under the barometer."
- Suggested fix: Cut "gone soft from its days under the barometer." Or have Dane or Quist hand her a copy at Pruett.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: In Ch 09 Beth put Dane's hand on the box truck's rear binder in the last daylight, with the floods on. The draft says "in the dark." Also, Dane released a binder in the dark during the fight, so that truck's tie-downs were disturbed. Ch 10 never says they were re-secured.
- Location: "Then the box truck, the binder she'd laid a man's hand on in the dark."
- Suggested fix: Change to "in the last of the daylight," or cut the clause.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: The Book-2 hook is not the short exchange the brief calls for. It is one Quist speech to Dane, and Russ never speaks. Dane does not answer. An interpretive sentence then explains Quist's intent, which is teaser-style narration.
- Location: "She said it to the water, not to him. She had already decided. She was only practicing the ask."
- Suggested fix: Cut those three sentences. Let Russ take the envelope on the float with one line to Quist, such as "What's it cost me?" and "A year." If you keep an exchange, make it between Quist and Russ.

**Issue 5**
- Problem: The coda's "echo of Ch 01" is too exact. It lifts the plumber's van and the "quarter turn" from Ch 01, where Beth takes up a quarter turn on the plumber's van chain. The brief asked for an echo without quoting.
- Location: "At the plumber's van her hand stopped, took up a quarter turn, and she never broke stride."
- Suggested fix: Use a different truck and a different adjustment, for example "At the box truck her hand stopped on a handle, laid it flat, and went on."

**Issue 6**

**Issue 7**
- Problem: Scene endings and a repeated gag. Scene 4 repeats Ned's rag-smear gag inside the chapter ("gray arcs" early, "making it worse" at the close). - Location: "up in the wheelhouse Ned was wiping the glass with his rag and making it worse." / "burning in a house with nobody in it."
- Suggested fix: End scene 4 on "Through the rear window the *North Reach* lay in the slip with her ramp down." End scene 3 on "...her own kitchen window was one of them."

**Issue 8**
- Problem: Emotion or significance is stated outright, not shown, in several places.
- Location: "Her hand had closed on the rail. Not for herself." / "Out loud, on an open deck, with an HSI agent listening. The one rule he meant to break on the record." / "Her move, unprompted, with Quist three feet away." / "stood noticing that nothing down there needed him to."
- Suggested fix: Cut "Not for herself.", both coda sentences ("Out loud...", "Her move...") and the "noticing" clause. The hands and the action already carry them.

**Issue 9**
- Problem: In Dane's POV the metaphor comes from Beth's world (weather, crossings). The bible says metaphor comes from the POV character's world, and Dane thinks in sightlines, distances and hands.
- Location: "She said it flat and sure, a forecast she would stake a crossing on."
- Suggested fix: Use a Dane image, for example "She said it flat and sure, her hand steady on his, the way she'd pinned a binder."

**Issue 10**
- Problem: Dane restates costs Beth already heard. Scene 1 reads them aloud ("removed from the case... a referral"). Ch 08 stated them. Scene 3 repeats them with "You heard," which is a character explaining what both already know.
- Location: "Off the case. You heard. There's the referral, and probably a suspension after it."

**Issue 11**

**Issue 12**
- Problem: Beth's answer to Tess's request does not connect. Tess asks her to make Ned eat. Beth pours Tess's cold coffee over the rail. The gesture reads as a non-sequitur. On Day 10 Ned arrives with coffee and nothing pays off the request.
- Location: "Beth crossed the cabin and picked up the cup. She carried it out past the deputy and tipped it over the rail and watched it spread brown on the green water."
- Suggested fix: Have Beth take the cup out and come back with something Ned can eat, such as a sleeve of saltines from the store bag. Or let Ned's Day 10 coffee be the visible gap and have Beth put one saltine on the console.

**Issue 13**

- Suggested fix: Drop "dated the thirteenth" ("a copy of a credit union deposit slip").
