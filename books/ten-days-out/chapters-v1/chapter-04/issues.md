**Issue 1**
- Problem: Narration calls him "Reyes" in scenes 1-4 and "Dane" afterward. Ch 01-03 narration uses "Dane" throughout (Ch 02 is Dane's own POV; Ch 03 is "Dane"). Beth has not spoken either name to him aloud in Ch 01-03 (she addresses him by no name), so "Dane." at the end can stand as her first spoken use. But because the narration never shifts, the bible's "Reyes until Ch 04, Dane after the kiss" never registers.
- Location: "Reyes lay a body-length above her where the granite humped, so he could see the path mouth over his shoulder." (also "Reyes didn't move. 'Why.'", "Reyes put the glasses up.", "Reyes walked back out onto the bare granite with the Iridium", "Reyes put a hand out.", "Reyes stepped left so his right side came clear of her.", "Reyes had topped the day tank from the jugs that morning")
- Suggested fix: Change every narration "Reyes" to "Dane" (including "Reyes's voice"), and add one spoken "Reyes" from Beth in scenes 1-3 (e.g. "Hold them still, Reyes" or her answer to "Why.") so the switch to "Dane." after the kiss is audible.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: Staging contradiction in the kiss. The shed setup says he faced the open door while working. The stop says he looks past her at the door, then claims his back was to it the whole time. He cannot look over her head at the door if his back was to it.
- Location: "Then he lifted his mouth off hers and looked past her, over her head, at the open door. The driveway. The spruce. He'd had his back to it the whole time."
- Suggested fix: Replace the last sentence with "He'd had his eyes shut, and the door open, for the whole of it." (or turn him so the door is behind him and have him look at her over his shoulder). Keep the earlier "open door was in front of him" line consistent.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: No scene break or time marker between the afternoon shed scene and 11:10 p.m. The beat sheet asked for a quick bridge. The text jumps from walking down the path to the furnace ticking, so the night reads as the same moment.
- Location: "Then he picked up the heavier jug and walked ahead of her down the path to the house, watching the drive both ways. / The furnace ticked on at ten past eleven."
- Suggested fix: Insert a scene break (---) before "The furnace ticked on", or add one bridging clause (supper, dark at four, the long evening) so the jump is marked.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: Spatial impossibility with Ch 03. Beth's couch is in the room off the kitchen at the back of the house (Ch 03: she counts "from it to the back door. Nine steps"; Dane works the front rooms). From there she cannot see the front-window gap or the porch. In Ch 03 she hears the 11:20 call but does not see it.
- Location: "She lay on the couch in the room off the kitchen and counted it through" ... "Through the gap where the sheet on the front window didn't meet the frame she saw him on the porch with the Iridium up"
- Suggested fix: Add one clause that she gets up and moves to the front room (and why), or move her to a front-room position from the start of the paragraph.

**Issue 5**
- Problem: Open thread from Ch 03 dropped. Russ said he would walk the shore path "this afternoon" (Day 2) and that a try on her door is "mine". On Day 3 he says nothing about what he found, and neither does Beth. He also never refers to the prowler.
- Location: "You need anything, Calloway, I'm on 68. Or the town office. Mostly I'm in this truck."
- Suggested fix: Add one line from Russ before he leaves, e.g. "Walked your shore path yesterday. Gulls and one boot print going the wrong way, and the tide ate that."

**Issue 6**
- Problem: "Quist" and "HSI" appear in Dane's dialogue for the first time with no introduction. Ch 01-03 never name Quist or HSI on the page. Beth does not react or ask who Quist is, and this is Beth's POV.
- Location: "Quist ran the boat. Gloucester registry, Arsenault the owner, two crew listed. HSI takes her when the sea goes down."
- Suggested fix: Add a short gloss in Dane's line: "Quist, the agent on the case, ran the boat." Drop or gloss "HSI" ("Homeland Security Investigations").

**Issue 7**
- Problem: Beth forgets or ignores a linkage she would make. In Ch 03 she heard "No. She doesn't know" and wondered "Her, or somebody in Portland. A wife. A judge." Here she overhears Dane say "She picked him out", which is plainly about herself, followed by "I'll ask her". Beth is a woman who counts and checks. She would notice that "she" in his phone talk means her. The draft has her file it as "Procedure."
- Location: "'Sternman. Left leg. She picked him out at six hundred yards.' ... A long gap. 'I'll ask her.' / Procedure. She let it go by."
- Suggested fix: Add one line in Beth's head that she notes Dane says "she" for her on the phone, files it beside Saturday's "She doesn't know", and decides not to ask. Alternatively change Dane's line to "The deckhand picked him out", which avoids the pronoun link.

**Issue 8**
- Problem: Beth explains the print to Dane as if he hadn't seen it. In Ch 03 Dane stood beside her at the print and "looked at the heel longest", and she showed Russ the photos with him present. Her lines "I saw it in the morning" and "the print on my back step yesterday" re-explain something both already know, and muddle what she actually withheld (only the link to Thursday's limp).
- Location: "The walk. Thursday night, off the ramp, he walked like that. And the print on my back step yesterday. Right heel deep, both pairs.' She kept the glasses up. 'I saw it in the morning. I wanted to watch him walk before I said.'"
- Suggested fix: Rewrite to "You saw the heel yesterday. I didn't say it matched Thursday's walk. I wanted to watch him walk before I said." Cut the restatement of the print details.

**Issue 9**
- Problem: Beth gives Pike a pronoun with no source. Ch 02 introduces "Pike. My supervisor in Portland" with no pronoun, and Beth has no other source for Pike's sex. This also undercuts Ch 03's open question "A wife. A judge."
- Location: "'What does she want every night?'"
- Suggested fix: Change to "What does Pike want every night?" or have Dane's earlier line in this chapter supply "she".

**Issue 10**
- Problem: "Haskell" is Ned's surname and has never appeared on the page in Ch 01-03. Tess calls him "Captain" (Ch 03, bible). A reader may take "Haskell" for an unknown person or for a name for Beth.
- Location: "'Cutter net secured,' Tess said in her ear. 'Stay warm, stay off the water. I mean it, Haskell.'"
- Suggested fix: Change to "I mean it, Captain."

**Issue 11**
- Problem: Timeline slip in Beth's memory. Tess came to Cutter at 22 as a summer sternman and has run the store for nineteen years. The day Beth's mother left (Beth aged 9, 24 years ago) matches the summer Tess arrived, five years before she ran the store. "The store truck" does not fit that date.
- Location: "The day Beth's mother left, Tess had driven her down to the boat in the store truck and bought her a hot chocolate in Pruett Harbor"
- Suggested fix: Change "the store truck" to "somebody's truck" or "the store's old truck, the one she was borrowing that summer".

**Issue 12**
- Problem: Soup delivery time differs from Ch 03 ("noon" here, "one" in Ch 03). These are different days (Sunday vs Saturday), so it is not a contradiction. The two chapters read as describing one routine, and a reader may see an inconsistency.
- Location: "Chowder's coming up at noon, Ida, and I'll bring Thursday's paper"
- Suggested fix: Change "noon" to "one" to match Ch 03, or leave it and accept the variance.

**Issue 13**
- Problem: Emotion stated, and the narration tells the reader how to read the beat before the reread can. Stated feeling ("she liked that he'd asked twice") where the body or an action could carry it.
- Location: "She took it for a man who wanted to know her, and she liked that he'd asked twice."
- Suggested fix: Replace with an action, e.g. she hands him the wrench a half-second slower than the job needs, and cut the interpretation.

**Issue 14**
- Problem: Prop continuity. She takes the rag out of his hand and wipes his knuckles. When he steps back, he "still had one end" of it, which implies he never released it.
- Location: "She took the rag out of his hand." ... "He still had one end of the rag, and she had the other, and he didn't let go of it."
- Suggested fix: Change the earlier line to "She took the rag by one end" (so he keeps the other), or change the later line to "He took the rag back by one end".

**Issue 15**
- Problem: Button cap. The beat sheet allows one-line buttons only at the end of Scene 3 and Scene 5. Scene 1 also ends on a short stand-alone line, and Scene 2 and the shed scene end on short closers.
- Location: "Reyes put the glasses up."
- Suggested fix: Merge it into the preceding paragraph or end Scene 1 on the longer action beat so only Scene 3 and Scene 5 land as buttons.

**Issue 16**
- Problem: Boat names too close. The invented boat "Second Try" (Hartley's) sits near Russ's boat "Second Wind" (bible), a name a Book 2 lead will carry.
- Location: "the *Gray Lady* and Hartley's *Second Try*, both hauled the week before the storm"
- Suggested fix: Rename Hartley's boat to something unlike *Second Wind* (e.g. *Hartley's Lisa B.*).
