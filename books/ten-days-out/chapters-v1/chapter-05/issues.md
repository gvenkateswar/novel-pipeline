**Issue 1**
- Problem: "The six from Saturday" cannot be named from Ch 03, because Ch 03 never names them. Beth counts "Six people in the room" and says only "Island. All six." Dane never got names from her, so "the six I gave you" is also wrong. The only named islanders in the store are Ned (on the stool) and Tess (behind the counter). Mrs. Dyer is mentioned but not present. Russ is on the road, not in the store. Arsenault is from away and arrives after the six. Ned may be one of the six, which would put him on the lid twice, since "Then *Ned*" comes at the end. Tess may be one of the six, which would make her absence from the lid false.
- Location: "Then the six I gave you Saturday." / "*Russ.* The six from Saturday, one under the other."
- Suggested fix: In Ch 03 final, name the six and state that they are customers other than Tess and Ned. Use islanders already on the page, for example Hartley (Lisa B.), a Pinkham, a second Pinkham, Kenny Thibodeau, Walt Grindle and one more. Then change the Ch 05 line to "the six I named Saturday" and list those names on the lid.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: The suspect-pool logic is chronologically wrong. The back door was tried Friday at 11:40 pm. The people offered as readers of the log (Arsenault and the six) were all in the store on Saturday, after the attempt. The pool for the Friday leak is whoever stood at the counter between the 5:40 pm entry and 11:40 pm. Both leads also ignore what Beth knew in Ch 03 ("The booster. Of course."): only Tess's phone works, so the log reader would need some other way to pass the news on.
- Location: "Arsenault stood there Saturday," he said. "Bought his cigarettes and took his time over my face, like a man pricing a boat he didn't plan to buy. That's one."
- Suggested fix: Have Beth restate the window ("Friday evening till the power went. Who was in?"), or add a line that Saturday's visitors only show who could read it from then on. Add one line on how a reader would pass it on without a phone.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: Dane's account of Arsenault contradicts Ch 03, where Arsenault "looked at Dane once, about the length of a handshake" and Dane looked back. The boat-pricing simile is also Beth's world, not Dane's (sightlines, hands, distances), which blurs the two voices the cold reads flagged.
- Location: "took his time over my face, like a man pricing a boat he didn't plan to buy"
- Suggested fix: Change to "Arsenault stood there Saturday. Looked at me once, and not at my hands. That's one." Keep the Dane-voice detail on hands or exits.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: The crew count conflicts with Ch 04. Dane reports "two crew listed" on the Margaret Ann. One of them is the heel (Doucette). The lid has "The heel. Crew. Crew.", which makes three. No crew member is shown at the counter in Ch 03, so "His crew. The heel." has no support on the page.
- Location: "His crew. The heel. Then the six I gave you Saturday." / "*Arsenault. The heel. Crew. Crew.*"
- Suggested fix: Change the lid to "Arsenault. The heel. Crew." (one other crew member). Change the spoken line to "His crew, if they came ashore for anything. The heel." Or have Beth say she saw no crew at the counter.

**Issue 5**
- Problem: Beth forgets what she knows. In Ch 03 narration she establishes that Ned "told Tess everything. The whole island knew that." Here she says flatly that Ned doesn't talk.
- Location: "Ned doesn't talk."
- Suggested fix: Keep the required line but narrow it to what Beth believes: "Ned doesn't talk about people. Boats, maybe." That matches her own Ch 03 reasoning ("He didn't lie about people").

**Issue 6**
- Problem: The shore-path reasoning is not reconciled, though Flag 2 of the beat sheet requires it on the page. Ch 03 has Beth say "Nobody comes in that way". Here she answers Dane with "Anybody can," which undercuts that. The page never says why nobody watches the bar at night on a young flood and swell. Russ, now a suspect, walked "ledge to the cove road" (Ch 04), right past the fish house at the head of the cove. Scene 5 puts Russ on the list and never notes that he knows the route and the cove.
- Location: "At low water. In daylight. Anybody can."
- Suggested fix: Replace with "At low water, in daylight, with a constable's reason. Nobody sets a watch there, and nobody tries it on a flood." Add one line in the Scene 5 suspect talk, after "Put him in", that Russ knows the way to the cove but not the fish house.

**Issue 7**
- Problem: Beth explains the fish house to someone who already knows it. In Ch 04 she told Dane about "his fish house on the cove, which nobody's opened in four years", and Dane asked "What's in it?". The scene presents it as new information. Beth's POV narration also calls her father "Hollis" here, though it says "her father" everywhere else.
- Location: "That's my father's fish house. Nobody's been in it in four years." / "in Hollis's square capitals"
- Suggested fix: Make it a callback, for example "That's the fish house. The one I told you about. Nobody's been in it in four years." Change "Hollis's" to "her father's".

**Issue 8**
- Problem: The geometry in Scene 1 contradicts itself. Dane stands "at the top of the ledge", and Beth climbs to "the rock below him". Then "He went up first", although he is already above her.
- Location: "She climbed to the rock below him and looked up at the house." / "He went up first, turned and put his hand down."
- Suggested fix: Have Dane come down to the steep pitch and then go up first, or change "He went up first" to "He was already at the top. He turned and put his hand down."

**Issue 9**
- Problem: The opening of Scene 4 reproduces Style Reference Sample 4 almost line for line. The beat sheet said to follow its beat and register and not copy it word for word. Matches include the sentence about the shaking hands and three matches, the heat on her shins through wet canvas, "hadn't said a word since the bar", "soaked black", the T-shirt, deciding to leave it on, "the way she'd read a ..." with palms flat, the heart going hard under her right hand, the breath stopping, and "Her name, like a warning he was giving himself."
- Location: "Her name, like a warning he was giving himself." / "Water ran off his jacket onto the planks." / "She laid both palms flat on his chest, the way she'd read a reefer box, and his heart was going hard under her right hand, and his breath stopped."
- Suggested fix: Rewrite these beats in new words and imagery. Keep only the required lines ("When this is over, I report this", "I know what you are. I'm choosing anyway."). Cut the borrowed warning simile.

**Issue 10**
- Problem: The sex scene is probably over length. I counted by hand, and "The stove caught on the fourth match" to "Then I wasn't the only one who knew." comes to about 950–970 words. The brief calls for 850–900. The bible allows 800–1,000, so the bible limit is met but the brief is not.
- Location: from "The stove caught on the fourth match." to "Then I wasn't the only one who knew."
- Suggested fix: Run a word count. If it is over 900, trim about 60 words, for example the lantern and fish-house inventory paragraph and "She looked at it once and then at him."

**Issue 11**
- Problem: Heat Rule 5 limits touching description to two sentences in a row. The cot sequence and the climax run longer.
- Location: "She took his cock in her hand. Her palm was rough as his, the hawser scar across it, and he said her name once into her hair as though it cost him something. She put her mouth on his throat, on the pulse there. He didn't last long. He came against her hip with his forehead on her shoulder, and then he was heavy and quiet and warm."
- Suggested fix: Break it with a non-touch sentence, for example the wind in the shingles or a thought about the burning shed. Do the same in the cot run ("He put his hands on her ribs … He put his mouth on her breast … She pulled his head up … His hand went down her belly").

**Issue 12**
- Problem: A phrase from the Ch 04 kiss is reused word for word.
- Location: "His mouth was cold and then it wasn't. She tasted rain on him."
- Suggested fix: Describe the mouth differently, for example "His mouth tasted of rain and chowder, and shook once before it steadied."

**Issue 13**
- Problem: The ferry-issued Icom handheld, which Beth carried on Day 3 and which the bible has her taking in Ch 07, is missing from the escape pack. It is not packed, not left behind, and not mentioned. The fish house has no radio. Beth's pack list is otherwise careful.
- Location: "Dry socks in a bread bag, twisted shut. Strike-anywhere matches in an amber pill bottle with the label scraped off. A headlamp with the red lens clipped on. Her wool watch cap. The granola bar she'd carried since Friday and not eaten."
- Suggested fix: Add "The Icom, switched off, in a freezer bag" to the pack list. Or say it stays at the Ashby place, but then Ch 07 must account for it.

**Issue 14**
- Problem: "Walk me through it" is used three times by Dane in one chapter, including both lines that open and close the plan. Beth also gives an unprompted speech of about 100 words, against her profile of conclusion first and reasons only if asked. Both weaken the distinct-voice requirement.
- Location: "Walk me through it," he said when she reached the ledge foot. / "Walk me through the counter." / "Walk me through it."
- Suggested fix: Keep the first and the last. Change the middle one to "Who was at the counter?" Cut Beth's turn speech to the first conclusion and two reasons, and let Dane ask for the rest.
