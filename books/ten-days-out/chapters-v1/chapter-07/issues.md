**Issue 1**
- Problem: The draft says Beth sees her own name on the notebook page, but the entry as quoted (the bible/outline text) contains no name for her, only "subject's acct" and "Quist". The chapter's last paragraph repeats the claim.
- Location: "At the top of the left-hand page was her name, in a hand that wasn't hers or her father's. Under her name, a dollar sign." Also: "held the page to the light on purpose, because it had her name on it." Also: "open at the page with her name and the money on it."
- Suggested fix: Replace "her name" in all three places with what is on the page (the date line, "Quist", the dollar figure). Beth then works out that "subject's acct" is hers. This keeps the exact notebook text.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: The narration dwells on the bank-bag line after Beth reads it. The beat sheet says the narration must not touch "store bank bag" again. A second pass on "Bank bag, D1, paycheck" spotlights the clue and makes Beth's miss look staged.
- Location: "Her eye went over the line again. Bank bag, D1, paycheck. It came back to Saturday, and to him."
- Suggested fix: Cut the three sentences. Let "Saturday. He had known since Saturday." carry straight into the generator-shed paragraph.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: Beth recalls Tess's ledger-smoothing, the Ch 06 tell, as a flagged image and "lets it go". The beats call for one fond, un-underlined thought about Tess. - Suggested fix: Replace it with a fond, non-clue image of Tess (the Hershey bar, the cold-store joke) and no ledger.

**Issue 7**

- Suggested fix: "She had pressed it twice that morning, and again at noon, until it quit about two, because strips won't stick to a cut that's still weeping."

**Issue 9**

- Location: "The tide was on the ebb, running out toward low water at ten to four."
- Suggested fix: Orchestrator decision, outside this chapter. Either fix Ch 06's Day 7 "three hours past low" line to match the bible and rewrite this passage, or update the bible's tide note to Ch 06 and amend Ch 05 to match.
