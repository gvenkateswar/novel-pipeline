# Chapter 08 issues (draft.md, 1,978 words)

**Issue 1**
- Problem: The chapter takes place on Monday, November 16, but the boss speaks of "Monday" for the hearing and the grand jury, which a listener will hear as today. The clock the book depends on is blurred in the one scene that restates it. Beth's interior "Portland was seven days off" comes earlier and does not fix the boss's lines.
- Location: "The judge set Cole's hearing for Monday at two, to decide whether there's enough to keep him locked up. The grand jury has to hear her by Monday morning, or the prosecutor says Cole walks."
- Suggested fix: Change both to "next Monday" ("The judge set Cole's hearing for next Monday at two" / "The grand jury has to hear her by next Monday morning, or the prosecutor says Cole walks"), or add "a week from today" to the first.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: A listener hears "she" as Beth when Ned says it about the wind (the author's rule: no "she" for weather or boats). The line is also odd for Ned. It is a flat, personal promise about the weather, and it is more certain and earlier ("by noon") than the boss's "any time from noon to four" and the bible's "uncertain" lull. The bible has Ned answer a hard question with the weather first; here the weather is also a confident prediction that the boss and the bible do not support.
- Location: "Wind's going round to the north, Bethie. She'll lie down for you by noon."
- Suggested fix: Replace with "Wind's going round to the north, Bethie. It ought to lie down by tomorrow noon, or near it." This removes "she", and "or near it" keeps it from contradicting the noon-to-four lull.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: The PLAIN LANGUAGE budget is broken in scene 3. The scene runs about 550 words (from "He answered it crouched against the foot of the lamp" to "Then the click."). It contains seven different budget terms: LifeFlight, Coast Guard, Marine Patrol, emergency director, hearing, grand jury, float. That is about 9 per 1,000 even if LifeFlight and float are excused (five terms), and about 13 per 1,000 if all seven count. The limit is two. Most are bible-mandated lines, so the density comes from stacking them in one call.
- Location: "LifeFlight won't fly in icing, and the Coast Guard won't send its helicopter up from Cape Cod through icing for somebody who isn't dying." / "Marine Patrol wants somebody on the island radio who can see the beach and tell them when to come in. That's your emergency director." / "The judge set Cole's hearing for Monday at two, to decide whether there's enough to keep him locked up. The grand jury has to hear her by Monday morning" / "with the float gone nothing that size can lie there in a sea."
- Suggested fix: Move the hearing and grand jury lines out of scene 3 into the boss's second call in scene 4, where a clock reminder fits. In Beth's line, drop "float" ("The pier's a mile of open road through the village, and no boat that size can lie there in a sea"). If the orchestrator wants the mandated lines kept verbatim in scene 3, log a waiver for this scene.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: Dane misreports what Russ said in Ch 05. In Ch 05 Dane asked "Who'd you tell she was sleeping at your place?" (Russ's house, the Ch 03 night) and Russ answered "Nobody. Who'd I tell?" The north-side house was not yet chosen. Here Dane makes Russ's "nobody" a statement about the north-side house, which Dane never asked about in the truck. A reader who remembers Ch 05 will catch it. Dane also drops Tess from his list, though she knew the north-side house.
- Location: "Ned knew the north-side house. So did Russ, and Russ sat in his own truck and told me he'd told nobody."
- Suggested fix: "Ned knew the north-side house. So did Russ, and Russ sat in his own truck and told me he'd told nobody where you slept the night before." (Or drop the clause and keep only "So did Russ.")

**Issue 5**
- Problem: Beth asks about Dane's Saturday boat viewing, but nothing on the page or in the continuity log says she was ever told. The viewing and the boots appear only in Dane's own thoughts in Ch 01 ("for a boat he was going to look at on Saturday"). In Beth's POV she has no way to know it. Dane's answer also does not confirm the viewing.
- Location: "Why was a marshal looking at boats on a Saturday?"
- Suggested fix: Give Dane the fact first. For example: "I had a boat to look at in Pruett Saturday, before your phone call." Then Beth asks: "Why a boat?" Dane then gives the Marisol line.

**Issue 6**
- Problem: Dane says he "won't be around to defend" himself when Beth asks Ned. In Ch 09 Dane is on the beach when she asks Ned, and this chapter never says Dane leaves on the boat, so the line contradicts what follows or invents a plan. It also runs against the bible's "I'm going to get you off this island alive" arc.
- Location: "if he says no you can blame me, since I won't be around to defend myself."
- Suggested fix: Replace the tail with a dry turn that does not say he leaves: "and if he says no, you can blame me, I'm used to it."

**Issue 7**
- Problem: Dane says his phone needs power, but Ch 07 shows it lighting up on the floor after the sea ("His phone lit up on the floor with no signal"). Power was not the problem; the tower was. The line contradicts Ch 07 and makes the generator look like a phone-charging task, which the page then reinforces with "His phone went on the charger."
- Location: "I can't tell her different until that phone has power and a tower."
- Suggested fix: "until that phone finds a tower." Drop "has power", and keep the charger line only as a passing detail, or cut it.

**Issue 8**
- Problem: Restatement of known facts. Dane tells Beth that the man thinks they drowned. She said it herself in Ch 07 ("He thinks we drowned."), and he said it back to her then ("Since dusk"). It is said again in the cost speech in scene 4. The beat sheet asks for one inside the action, not two between the same two people.
- Location: "The man thinks we drowned, and my boss may think so too, and I can't tell her different until that phone has power and a tower. So I'm rooting for you." / "Right now the man with the gun thinks you're at the bottom of the bay," he said. "That makes this the safest you've been since Thursday."
- Suggested fix: In scene 1 cut to "My boss may think we're both dead, and I can't tell her different until the phone finds a tower. So I'm rooting for you." Keep the scene-4 line, which carries the cost and is new in what it spends.

**Issue 9**
- Problem: "That house" has no antecedent in this scene. A listener who has just heard Dane say "the old lighthouse" will take the boss to mean the lighthouse, so she seems to say Russ saw two people in the lighthouse. The leak channel needs it clear: Russ knows the burned north-side house only. The log also has no earlier record that the boss and Russ spoke, so this is the first contact.
- Location: "The constable told me there were two people in that house."
- Suggested fix: "The constable told me there were two people in the house that burned."

**Issue 10**
- Problem: "bar" is on the never-list at count zero ("gut, bar (not in this book)"). Here it means a phone signal bar. The bible uses the same word ("One bar"), so the audit tool may flag it even though the sense differs.
- Location: "It found the bar and started going off, buzz after buzz, two days of missed calls stacking up."
- Suggested fix: "It found a signal and started going off." Add "one bar" nowhere on the page.
