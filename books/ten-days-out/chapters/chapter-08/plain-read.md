# Plain read: Chapter 8, "The Tower"

Draft read: `chapters/chapter-08/final.md` (with chapters 01 to 07 `final.md` read first for context). No other file opened.

## 1. Retelling

The morning after the wave, Beth is fixing the dead generator at the lighthouse keeper's house and counting her pulls on the cord. Dane stands in the doorway with his gun and tells her, out of nowhere, that he's in love with her. She doesn't say it back; she says "ask me in five days," which is before they have to go to Portland. They climb the lighthouse tower to get his phone a signal. At the top, on the ice, he slips and nearly goes over the low railing, and she grabs the back of his belt, sits down on the ice and pulls him back. His boss calls: the wind may drop tomorrow afternoon, the state is sending a police boat, and someone on the island radio has to bring it in, which means Tess. Beth picks the north beach, and Dane tells her to stand by the rocks at the far end, out of sight of the trees. Tess sets it for three o'clock. Dane wants nobody else told, but Beth calls Ned and Russ anyway because they spent the night looking for her body. At the end Dane reads the three names to his boss, and the boss says "And me," so now four people know where Beth will be standing.

## 2. What's at stake

Beth has to get off the island alive and talk to the grand jury on the morning of Monday the twenty-third, or Cole walks free. The man with the dragging foot is still out there and will probably wait in the trees above the beach, and one of the four people who now know the time and place (Tess, Ned, Russ or Dane's boss) may be the one telling him.

## 3. The romance

Yes, I felt it, clearly. He says it straight out: "I'm in love with you." Her side came through too: "She wanted to drag him down onto the cold step in the sleet." And when she holds onto his belt while they argue: "she had held him over that drop, she wanted him, and she was going to make these calls anyway." The fall from the railing, with her fist in his belt, felt like the two of them, and it pays back him rescuing her earlier and her pulling him out of the sea.

One soft spot: "Ask me in five days... Five was less than seven, and she meant him to notice." I had to stop and do the sum while listening. I took it to mean "I'll answer you before we split up," but that's a guess. One plain sentence saying what she means would land it.
