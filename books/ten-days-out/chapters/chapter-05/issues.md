# Chapter 05 Issues (continuity check on draft.md)

**Issue 1**
- Problem: The seating geometry contradicts itself. Dane sits at the passenger door with Beth in the middle (beats), so the hand beside Beth is his left, and his gun is on his right hip (bible). A hand on the door handle would be his gun hand, not the hand beside her. As written, the reason given for keeping the hand off her thigh does not work.
- Location: "He kept it on the door handle, because his gun hand was the one beside her."
- Suggested fix: Rewrite the sentence so the hand he wants to put on her thigh is his left, and the reason he keeps it on his knee is that the right (gun) hand stays free by the door, or move Dane to the middle so his right hand is the one beside her and say so.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: The house is described as fully shuttered, but the kid watched the road through a front window from a chair, and Russ's opening line says the house has "its back window out." The page never shows the broken back window. A shuttered house cannot supply the sightline the chair implies, and Russ's stated reason for coming here is never confirmed on the page.
- Location: "Every window was shuttered, the snow lay smooth on the front steps, and smoke was coming off the chimney of a house that ought to be dead cold." and "A kitchen chair stood at the front window, facing down the road they had come up."
- Suggested fix: Change to "Every window was shuttered but one" (the front window the chair faces), and add one clause when Dane walks in or out showing the back window broken, with the drift under it.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: Dane asks for "all three of their phones" before the reader or Dane has been given a third islander. Only Russ and Tess have been named as knowing the north-side house at this point, and Beth has not yet told Ned (Scene 4). Also, "their phones" can be heard as taking the phones themselves, not the records, and "the carriers" in Scene 4 is never glossed; the chapter's own plain-language job is to say what records are and when they come.
- Location: "Dane kept his voice polite and slow. "Then my boss hears it too. And I'm asking for all three of their phones.""
- Suggested fix: Change Scene 3's line to "And I'm asking my boss for Russ's and Tess's phone records" (two people, and "records"), and in Scene 4 add the third name (Ned) when Dane gives the list. Gloss "carriers" in the boss's line ("The phone companies take three days").

**Issue 4**
- Problem: Dane tells Beth something the page did not show. The narration shows Russ only putting his phone away after Tess hung up. It never shows Russ with the phone at his ear while Tess's phone rang, which is the observation Dane reports.
- Location: "Through the window Dane saw Russ on the steps, putting his own phone away." and "Her phone rang while Russ had his at his ear."
- Suggested fix: Add one clause before Tess's phone buzzes ("Through the window Russ stood on the steps with his phone at his ear") so Dane's line to Beth rests on something the reader saw.

**Issue 5**
- Problem: POV slips in a Dane-POV chapter. Dane cannot know that Beth heard no shot or why she came, and "owed nobody anything, on purpose" is Beth's interior wound (bible), stated as narration in Dane's head.
- Location: "She had heard no shot, and he'd been in too long, so she had come to look." and "Beth owed nobody anything, on purpose."
- Suggested fix: Make the first Dane's inference ("There had been no shot, and he'd been in too long, so she had come to look") and make the second observed behavior ("Beth never left a debt unpaid, and her mouth went flat").

**Issue 6**
- Problem: World rule 12 and the beat sheet say Ned gets two short lines and the coffee with no look the narration lingers on. The draft gives Ned a narrated inspection of his hands and a verdict on them ("steady"), which is the only place in the chapter Dane clears someone by their hands. Ned also gets a third utterance (the line to Dane).
- Location: "Dane watched the hands. They were steady, a ferry captain's hands."
- Suggested fix: Cut the two sentences, keep the physical description in the opening clause ("big and stooped, with both hands around a mug"), and fold Ned's line to Dane into one of his two lines or accept it as the second.

**Issue 7**
- Problem: Restatement of facts the reader already has. Ch 03 gave Russ's lit porch and shotgun and the whole road seeing it; Ch 04 gave the Dyer house as empty with the back door open. The beat sheet allows Beth one clause of this, not a stand-alone sentence. Narration in Scene 1 also repeats that someone used Mrs. Dyer's phone (Ch 04).
- Location: "Russ sat on his porch with a shotgun in a storm. The whole road knew where I was, and anybody could walk into the Dyer house." and "Somebody had sat in Mrs. Dyer's dark house, watched Russ on that porch, and picked up her phone."
- Suggested fix: Compress Beth's to one clause inside the argument ("Russ held his porch with a shotgun and the loft got found anyway, and I can't do nine more nights without a key and groceries"). End the Scene 1 narration sentence at "watched Russ on that porch" so only the new fact lands.

**Issue 8**
- Problem: Two sentences explain the beat the dialogue has just shown, which the beat sheet's tic list forbids. "All day" is also wrong: Tess has said "Calloway" only in the morning visit, and this is early afternoon. The beat sheet said the "rude and right" longing was to be one clause with no verdict.
- Location: "All day it had been Calloway. Dane heard the difference." and "She was rude to him and right, which was the thing about her he liked best."
- Suggested fix: Cut "Dane heard the difference" and let "Beth." land on its own; change the second to "She was rude to him and right." and stop.

**Issue 9**
- Problem: Two different "she" referents in adjacent Ned lines (the storm, then Beth) will confuse a listener. "Till Monday" will also be heard as the grand-jury Monday (the twenty-third) in a chapter that deliberately avoids the clock; the storm's Monday is the sixteenth.
- Location: ""She'll blow till Monday, Bethie," Ned said." and "To Dane he said, "She'll feed you. She won't be nice about it.""
- Suggested fix: Change the first to "It'll blow till Monday week, Bethie" or "It'll blow two more days, Bethie," and keep "She'll feed you" for Beth; or have Ned say "Beth will feed you. She won't be nice about it."

**Issue 10**
- Problem: Continuity with Ch 04. Russ says he saw the open window at five while looking for them, but in the loft at dawn (Ch 04 final) he reported the Dyer house and nothing about a broken summer-house window, though he had been out all night and found them soon after. His only explanation there is "Took me all night to think of it."
- Location: ""Summer house up the east road has its back window out," Russ said. "I saw it at five, looking for you.""
- Suggested fix: Add a clause explaining the delay ("I didn't think anything of it till the light came up, and then I had to find you first") or move his sighting to after he left the loft.

**Issue 11**
- Problem: Russ identifies the owner of the camp as "the kid off Cole's boat" with no stated basis. The page never connects the pinned picture, the dragged foot or the thin man in the skiff (Ch 02) to a person Russ knows, and he knows the trailer has been "empty since Thursday" without saying how he knows.
- Location: ""That's the kid off Cole's boat," Russ said, his eyes on the picture. "Worked the back of it two summers for cash, and lived in that trailer of Cole's out the east road. It's been empty since Thursday.""
- Suggested fix: Add one clause that gives the basis, such as Russ looking at the prints by the back door ("That foot. I've watched that kid walk down the co-op wharf for two summers") and "I drove past the trailer at dawn, nobody's been in it since Thursday."

**Issue 12**
- Problem: The box is already filled before Beth says where it is going. The north-side house was decided in the kitchen of the east-road house, so nothing on the page explains when Beth gave Tess a list, or how Tess knew to put in candles and "food for two" before being told. The list also sits oddly with Scene 1, where no list is handed over.
- Location: "Tess was in the back room filling a box from Beth's list." and ""North-side house," Beth said. "Russ's. Food for two.""
- Suggested fix: Open Scene 4 with Beth handing Tess the list ("Beth put a list on the counter and Tess took it to the back"), then have Beth give the destination as Tess is already filling the box.

**Issue 13**
- Problem: Beth's penny debt is set up and never settled, and she also takes two coffees and a box of groceries without paying. The bible says she pays back every favor at once and owes nobody a dollar on purpose; the page leaves the penny open as a thread.
- Location: "Beth counted out four dollars and change and came up a penny short. "You owe me," Tess said."
- Suggested fix: Have Beth find a penny in her coat pocket and put it on the counter before the scene moves on, or pay it with the first thing she does at the urn (a dollar on the counter for the two coffees).

**Issue 14**
- Problem: The bible's binding Dyer geography still places the house "above the harbor road on the hill behind the village." The draft follows Ch 04 (on the point across the harbor, with a sightline to Russ's porch), which is correct for the book, but the bible is not updated and now contradicts the draft in two places. The bible and outline also give the call as "all static"; the draft replaces that with the caller covering the phone and never mentions static or the "nephew."
- Location: "From the Dyer house on the point you would look straight across the black water at the one lit porch on the harbor road."
- Suggested fix: Orchestrator or author approves a bible change: the Dyer house sits on the point across the harbor (an hour round by road), and the call was low, muffled and full of static. Fix the Key locations entry and the Island geography line, and the CASE FACTS "all static" line.
