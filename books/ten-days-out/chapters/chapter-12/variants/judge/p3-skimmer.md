# Judge: Skimmer, pair p3 (A vs B)

1. **Winner: B**

2. **Why:** B opens with the gunshot and me already in the ditch, so I'm listening before Ned starts confessing, and when three people talk at once on the step it tells me who's who. A makes me walk a mile on a rope through the confession first, and then hands me lines I can't put a name to.

3. **Where A lost me:** About 29%, at the Russ and Tess bit on the step: "Late," Beth said. / "Later than that." / "Not one word, Calloway." By ear I lost track of who was talking, then Russ's day-by-day confession started and my attention went elsewhere. It went again around 60% at Dane's tally paragraph: "On the tower she had made the calls. On the cove rocks she had jumped with a pistol on her. Last night she had lain down under a quilt..." That's a list read in his head, so I drifted.

4. **Where B lost me:** About 33%, at Russ's confession: "Friday night, from my porch, before the shooting. Saturday at the store, when her phone buzzed and she hung up on it... Tuesday I kept to the harbor road because her store's on it." It's a ledger of days, and I let it go by. I came back for the chair ("like it owes you money") and the text from the lawyer. I glazed again briefly at about 82%: "I wanted to tell your people five. My boss wanted her test..." I couldn't follow that by ear in either version.

5. **Confidence: medium.** Most of the lines are the same in both. B wins on the order (the shot first) and on tagging who speaks, and that matters a lot when you're listening. The ferry-hatch ending is about equal in the two.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. It's the same lines in a different order, almost sentence for sentence.
