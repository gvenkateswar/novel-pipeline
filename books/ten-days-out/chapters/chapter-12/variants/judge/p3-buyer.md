# Judge: Buyer, pair p3 (A vs B), Chapter 12

1. **Winner: B**

2. **Why:** B starts with the bullet already in the air and the two of them in a ditch, so I got the danger first and Ned's confession second, when I was finally ready to listen. A made me walk a mile of snow hearing Ned's whole two years of truck runs before anything happened.

3. **Where A lost me:** "Russ turned to Dane, his ears red. 'You asked me who I told you were sleeping at my place. I told her. Friday night, from my porch, before the shooting. Saturday at the store...'" (~30%). That's a list of days and alibis, and my thumb sped up there. I came back for the chair and the "Once is how everything starts with you" bit, then lost it for good at Dane's head-count in his own head: "On the tower she had made the calls. On the cove rocks she had jumped with a pistol on her. Last night she had lain down under a quilt..." (~60%). That's a recap of things I already read.

4. **Where B lost me:** "The rod from the wheel back to the rudder hung loose at its far end. The first storm bent it and broke it off the rudder. The pin at the top was frozen in." (~75%). A woman alone with a machine part. B pulled me back fast, though: "He didn't make a joke after it" and her holding his hand at the top of the ladder. Russ's day-by-day confession (~32%) dragged in B too, but B gives Tess standing in a man's coat where the whole road can see, and Beth leaving her sleeve against Dane's, so the couple is in that scene.

5. **Confidence: medium.** Most of the lines are the same. B wins on the opening, which comes in order and puts danger first, and on a few small touches that put the couple in the same space. If both had opened the same way, it would be close to a coin flip.

6. **Same-author check: Yes.** The dialogue is nearly word for word the same, and so is the cuff chain ticking at the end.
