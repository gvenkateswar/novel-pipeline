# Judge: Buyer persona (opus), p3 A vs B

1. **Winner: B**

2. **Why:** B gets me off the chains and onto the drugs, and then onto him, a little quicker. When he grabs her wrist I can feel his pulse, and I want to see what that pulse does in chapter two.

3. **Where A lost me:** "Handle down. Chain bar-tight. Hook seated all the way in the D-ring, where it couldn't walk out on the first sea." (~2%). That's a woman checking a checklist in bed with me, and my thumb moved. The herring joke pulled me back, but then on the ramp, just when he finally shows up, I'm handed a paragraph about her doing voices at the sink: "Other people's voices were a thing she did, alone, at the sink, on the dark end of the night run." (~37%). Nice, but he's standing right there and I'd rather watch him.

4. **Where B lost me:** "Russ Whitcomb was the *Second Wind*, four hundred traps and a radio he talked to like a dog." (~72%). It's a list of who owns which boat. It's short and it lands on her trying to fit him on one, so I only skimmed for a breath. Otherwise it didn't.

5. **Confidence: medium.** These are near twins. B wins on the small banter ("You could say I jumped well." / "She could have."; "He's freight. Sign here.") and on more heat in the grab. A has the better jokes in its first page.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. The same scenes and the same lines, sometimes word for word ("Those boots are for a different ocean," "It was never your boat").
