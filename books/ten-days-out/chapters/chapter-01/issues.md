**Issue 1**
- Problem: Bare hands / gloves-off is noticed three times in one chapter. The bible's cap is once per chapter, and the beat sheet says "noticed once in this chapter". The opening beat is the planned one. The s2 bow line and the wrist add two more. The Ch 10 s5 payoff weakens if the detail is already spent.
- Location: "the ice cracked off it in plates in her bare hands as she paid it out." and "his hand closed over her bare wrist and held."
- Suggested fix: Keep "with her gloves off" in the s1 opening. In s2 change to "in her hands" and "closed over her wrist". The grip is already the one sensory detail without the adjective.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: The boots run as a repeating bit well beyond the beat sheet's allowance. The plan was one occurrence in s2 ("1 of 3" for the book), and s3 and s4 were not given any boots beats. The chapter has about ten boot mentions. Several are in s3 and s4, which dilutes the Ch 4 and Ch 10 payoffs.
- Location: "and that I'd have done the same for you, in better boots." / "He stood there in the wrong boots and waited for her to be sensible." / "She looked at the marshal's boots before she looked at his face." / "hands, jacket, boots again" / "so the boots could keep up"
- Suggested fix: Keep the s2 meet and the "wrong boots" waiting line. Cut "in better boots" from s3 ("which was decent of you, and that I'd have done the same for you"). Cut "boots again" from Tess's pricing and change "so the boots could keep up" to "so he could keep up". Tess can look at his hands first.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: The call summary contradicts the scene on the key reefer clue. The scene establishes the box as "the temperature of the night and no colder", so not cold and not warm. The call summary calls it "warm". That overstates the clue and misreports to the trooper.
- Location: "the dark unit, the warm box, the crate, the tape."
- Suggested fix: Replace "the warm box" with "the box that wasn't cold".

**Issue 5**
- Problem: The bait truck and plumber's van conflict with the bible's deck facts and with each other. The bible says the co-op's bait truck is one of the two trucks chained on deck from Day 1 to Day 9. Here the co-op boys meet it on the slip on Friday morning, as if it were offloaded. The plumber's van went over at night on the Cutter-to-Pruett run, yet he is on the Cutter slip next morning "to come for his van".
- Location: "the plumber come for his van, the co-op boys at the bait truck, an old man down for nothing but the news."
- Suggested fix: Drop the bait truck from the slip crowd (use "the co-op boys at the fish pier"). Make the plumber walk down for freight rather than "come for his van", or have him come in as a foot passenger on this boat.

**Issue 6**
- Problem: The texture budget for s1 is exceeded, and the stern group is written as a list sentence, which the beat sheet forbids. The budget is five items, cap six. The herring joke and the plumber's billing joke are two extra pieces of trade texture. The stern-chain / ring-buoy / quarter-ladder / outside-ladder / breaker sentence strings the plants in a row.
- Location: "The co-op's bait truck smelled like a decision somebody had made about herring in August. The plumber had chained his own van, the way he billed, generously and with gaps," and "went aft, a hand along the stern chain, a kick at the ice on the ring buoy's bracket, past the quarter ladder, and up the outside ladder with her shoulder brushing the breaker box on the aft bulkhead."
- Suggested fix: Keep one of the two jokes (the plumber's) and cut the herring line. Split the stern sentence in two, with the stern chain and buoy in one action and the ladder and breakers in a second action carrying a sensory beat (the rungs iced, the breaker box humming).

**Issue 7**
