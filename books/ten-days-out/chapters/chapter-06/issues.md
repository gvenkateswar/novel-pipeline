**Issue 1**
- Problem: "The box" has two referents in four paragraphs. The grocery box is "the grocery box" in the second paragraph, then "The box from Tess's counter" is zipped in her coat. Heard once by a listener, it sounds like the groceries are in her coat. The word "condoms" never appears, so the plant for Ch 07 s3 does not land in text-to-speech (Ch 05 final: "zipped the box into the pocket inside her coat"). World rule 6 requires plot facts in plain words.
- Location: "The box from Tess's counter was still zipped in her coat on the hook."
- Suggested fix: Replace with "The condoms she had bought at Tess's counter were still zipped in the inside pocket of her coat, on the hook."

**Issue 2**
- Problem: The three broken-off verdicts will read badly aloud. A text-to-speech voice drops the dash after "was" and runs straight on, so a listener hears "Tess was Russ Whitcomb had taught her..." and "Russ was Ned Haskell had hauled her...". The failure to finish, which is the point of the beat, is lost.
- Location: "Tess Marr had walked her home from every dance and every wake on this island, and Tess was— Russ Whitcomb had taught her to bait a trap when she was six, and Russ was— Ned Haskell had hauled her off the float by her collar two mornings ago, and Ned was— For the first time in her life she couldn't finish one."
- Suggested fix: Give each attempt its own sentence that ends with a stated stop in plain words, for example "Tess Marr had walked her home from every dance and every wake on this island, and Tess was the one who... Beth stopped there. Russ Whitcomb had taught her to bait a trap when she was six, and she stopped there too. Ned Haskell had hauled her off the float by her collar two mornings ago, and she stopped there as well. For the first time in her life she couldn't finish one." Do not use trailing dashes or ellipses.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: The narration says Dane stopped "in the middle of the word", but his line breaks off after "he", which is a whole word. The interruption is mid-sentence. The same dash cut-off will also read as a trailing pause in text-to-speech, so the hand-press cause and effect is blurred.
- Location: "He stopped in the middle of the word. Nobody in her whole life had ever stopped for her that fast."
- Suggested fix: Change to "He stopped in the middle of the sentence." In the line above, end Dane's speech on a complete clause ("He's listening, so he'll hear us.") and let her hand-press cut off the next thing he starts to say.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: The first storm is absent from the page. Ch 05 final has Ned say "It'll blow two more days, Bethie", and the bible puts snow, sleet and high northeast wind through Day 4. Sunday dusk in this draft is windless. She "could hear the whole hillside", Dane whispers at her ear and is heard, and snow falls straight. This also leaves her missing the fire unexplained, since the draft says only that fire "made almost no sound".
- Location: "Twenty minutes ago she'd heard him go under, and she'd let him sleep. From this stove she could hear the whole hillside."
- Suggested fix: Put the wind on the page. Make it the reason she hears late, for example "From this stove she could hear the hillside under the wind, and the wind had been covering everything since Friday." Add one line of sleet or wind in the trees in s2 or s3, so the whispering and "Quieter" read as risk.

**Issue 5**
- Problem: Contradiction between the fire "making almost no sound" and the roar one paragraph later. A listener hears the sentence about silence and then hears something "roaring". The explanation for why she missed it does not work.
- Location: "Fire made almost no sound until it was too late. She had listened all day and heard it late."
- Suggested fix: Change the earlier line "something was roaring, low and soft" to "something was working, low and soft, like a kettle just before it boils". Then the sentence about silence and the sound agree.

**Issue 6**
- Problem: Where the kid is, is inconsistent. He starts at the foot of the front steps. His first shot comes "from the corner of the house below them", so he is already round the side. Dane then says the light is "behind the house" and that he is coming "the long way, round the woodpile", which is the burning back wall, as if he had not yet come round. "He's found the window" does not say which window. A listener cannot tell where the kid is relative to them or to the fire.
- Location: "'Light's left,' Dane said, low, behind her. 'Behind the house. He's found the window.' Then: 'He's coming the long way, round the woodpile, because of the foot. He's slower than us.'"
- Suggested fix: Keep the kid at the front corner for the first shot, then have Dane say, "Light's coming up the side. He's found the pantry window. He's following our prints, and the foot makes him slow." Drop the woodpile, which is the part that is burning.

**Issue 7**
- Problem: The dash-ended verdict of Dane's silence leaves a plot fact to inference. The new fact the chapter exists to deliver is that Dane fell asleep on watch and the kid used it. Dane's stillness is read as "what he was thinking about himself" with no content. World rule 6 and the beat sheet's Say-it-twice require it in plain words from Beth. The beats hold back only Dane's own spoken line, which belongs to Ch 07.
- Location: "In the fire's light his face was gray and still, and she knew exactly what he was thinking about himself."
- Suggested fix: Replace with "In the fire's light his face was gray and still. He had slept, and the man had been waiting for it, and she knew he was telling himself so."

**Issue 8**
- Problem: Beth's own Saturday night is not accounted for. The bed upstairs is "nobody had used", but she must have slept somewhere after the loft night and after arriving at the house Saturday afternoon. If she slept in it, the line is false. If she did not sleep, her three nights without sleep are unmentioned, while only Dane's cost is stated.
- Location: "Tonight she was going to wake him up on purpose, in the big bed upstairs that nobody had used, and he wasn't getting the chair."
- Suggested fix: Replace with "Tonight she was going to wake him up on purpose and take him up to the big bed she'd slept in alone last night with the door open, so she could hear the chair, and he wasn't getting the chair."

**Issue 9**
- Problem: Dane's laces are called "frozen stiff" after sitting all day on a mat inside a house with a lit stove, and Beth's boots, which sat on the same mat, went on in two seconds. Hers are dry, his are wet, which is why only his freeze, but nothing on the page says the mat is by an outside door in a cold hall. The reader gets freezing indoors with no cause.
- Location: "The laces had sat wet on the cold mat all day, and they were frozen stiff."
- Suggested fix: Say the boots had been left on the back-hall mat, away from the stove: "His boots had sat wet on the mat in the cold back hall all day, and the laces had gone stiff as wire." Delete "frozen".

**Issue 10**
- Problem: The paragraph restates the known four-people-and-three-are-hers fact. The facts are said, then repeated in Dane's line and Beth's italic thought, all inside about ten lines. The beat sheet asked for the deduction once with a payoff. Dane's "Nobody could see this house" is also immediately restated in the narration as "which nobody could see from any road or any window on the island".
- Location: "This house, which nobody could see from any road or any window on the island, four people had known about by Saturday afternoon, and now it was burning behind her, and three of the four were hers."
- Suggested fix: Cut the restatement of "nobody could see" and keep "three of the four were hers" in the narration. Then drop the "She thought it again" line or keep it as the only echo. For example, delete "*Three of them would die for me.* She thought it again, and this time it sounds like..." and keep only its second half, "This time it sounded like something a woman says right before she finds out she's wrong."

**Issue 11**
- Problem: "Russ had knocked" does not match the Ch 04 final. Beth said his name "before he knocked", and Dane's line ("the next man to think of it might not knock") sets up knocking as a courtesy Russ did not actually perform on the page. Beth's deduction now rests on a thing that did not happen.
- Location: "The loft, which nobody had known about, nobody had found but Russ, and Russ had knocked."
- Suggested fix: Replace "and Russ had knocked" with "and Russ had come up the outside stair in the open, with her bag".

**Issue 12**
- Problem: "ploughed" is British spelling in an American Maine setting. The rest of the book uses American spelling.
- Location: "one print, then a long line ploughed by the bad foot."
- Suggested fix: Change to "plowed".

**Issue 13**
- Problem: "damper" is stove jargon a listener may not know. Plain-language budget and rule 5. The line also has no payoff, because shutting a damper on a burning stove would fill the kitchen with smoke.
- Location: "On the way past she shut the damper on the stove. It was habit, and it was crazy."
- Suggested fix: "On the way past she shut the little air vent on the stove. It was habit, and it was crazy."

**Issue 14**
- Problem: "the names of her three people" is unclear in text-to-speech. A listener hears that she has lost their names, and has no meaning to attach. What she means is her certainty about them.
- Location: "She had lost a house tonight, and her bag, and the names of her three people, all in one hour."
- Suggested fix: "She had lost a house tonight, and her bag, and her trust in her three people, all in one hour."

**Issue 15**
- Problem: Scene 4 opens by restating the last image of scene 3 (the fire at their backs, "standing in its light") before moving on. Beat sheet rule: no scene opens by summarizing the last one.
- Location: "The fire lit their backs. Their shadows ran long down the granite toward the water."
- Suggested fix: Open scene 4 on the new action ("The rocks below were glazed with ice where the spray had frozen.") and cut the two sentences about the fire at their backs. The flashlight stopping at the top of the bank is the new event.
