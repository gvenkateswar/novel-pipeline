# Plain read: Chapter 9, "The North Beach"

Draft read: `chapters/chapter-09/final.md` (with chapters 01 to 08 `final.md` read first for context). No other file opened.

## 1. Retelling

Dane has hidden Beth behind the rocks at one end of the north beach to wait for the police boat. Ned turns up early with a lantern and a pole, and Dane holds him at gunpoint until he shows his hands are empty. Beth finally asks Ned for his ferry captain job in June, and he says yes. He says he wrote the letter years ago and was waiting for her to ask. Then Ned confesses: for two years he has carried Cole's trucks across alone at night for cash and never opened them. He guessed it was drugs, and his own son in Portland uses them. Beth hits him in the chest. Dane says that if Ned sold the meeting time, the gunman is in the trees right now. The boat comes in with Tess talking it in over the radio. As Beth wades out, someone moving in the trees shoots one of the officers in the shoulder and puts a hole in the boat. Dane fires once and thinks he hit the man. Ned, who can't swim, walks into chest-deep water to stand between Beth and the trees. Dane waves the boat away and drags them both back behind the rocks. They find blood and the dragging-foot track going into the woods. Ned goes home alone. Dane points out that the shooter knew the exact hour, so the leak is one of Beth's three people (Tess, Russ, Ned) or one of his (his boss, the Marine Patrol). No boat can come until the storm passes, and the grand jury is six days off.

## 2. What's at stake

Beth has to stay alive and get to the grand jury in Portland on Monday. If she doesn't testify, Cole goes free. The kid with the dragging foot is trying to kill her, and someone she trusts, or someone on Dane's side, keeps telling him where she'll be. Now she is stuck on the island with no boat.

## 3. The romance

Yes, from Dane's side. I felt it here: "He had been afraid for her that whole minute. Now she was shaking under him, alive, and he shut his eyes against her hair and was so glad it hurt." Also here, which is plain enough to catch while half-listening: "whether a man could ask to stay on in Maine... A man had no business working that out about a witness." Then "Does the ferry run all winter?" / "No reason." I got that he's thinking about staying for her.

From Beth's side I didn't feel much in this chapter. She's taken up with Ned and with the shooting. That fits the chapter, but nothing on her side moved the romance forward.
