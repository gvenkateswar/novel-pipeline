## 1. Retelling

Dane and Beth go back into her house so she can pack. It's empty. She wants to sleep on the ferry, but he says she'll be safer at Russ the constable's house, because Russ has a shotgun and a generator and the whole road can see his porch. The island's power goes out, Russ gets the generator going, and his is the only house with lights on. Russ sits on the porch with the shotgun, and Dane's boss reminds them that Beth has to talk to the grand jury by Monday or Cole goes free. Late that night, in the dark kitchen, Beth tells Dane she's going to kiss him, and she does. It gets heated fast. He stops because Cole's lawyer could tell the jury the marshal was sleeping with the only witness, and then nobody would believe her. She says she'd just tell the truth and it's her choice, not the jury's. Around one in the morning a radio call says the Dyers' nephew is trapped under a fallen tree, so Russ leaves. A few minutes later someone shoots through the window. Dane gets Beth on the floor, his arm is cut by flying glass, and they run out the back and hide behind the woodpile. There's a dragging-heel footprint under the window, so it's the same man from the truck. Beth says, "I know a place."

## 2. What's at stake

Beth is the only person who saw the drugs, and the man with the bad leg (working for Cole) is trying to kill her. If she dies, or doesn't get to the grand jury by Monday, Cole walks free.

## 3. The romance

Yes. I felt it clearly, and it wasn't a small gesture. It happened out loud:

> "I'm going to kiss you," she said. "If you've got a reason why not, you've got about three seconds, and it had better be better than a jury."
> He counted. He counted everything. He got to two.
> She took a fistful of his shirt and kissed him.

And the reason they stop is said plainly, and it's a real reason (the lawyer could use it to wreck her testimony). I followed that. "Then tomorrow." / "Probably sooner." told me it's not over. During the shooting, "He would do anything to keep this woman breathing where he could hear it" kept it going.

One small thing: Dane says "he wanted her" several times before the kiss (in her house, at the coffee, at the window). By the third time I'd stopped hearing it.

Or just that yes, they have a nephew? Nobody says "that call is fake" or "it's a trick to get Russ out of the house." I only worked it out after the shooting started, from "The call had come from the Dyer house, and Russ had gone." I got there in the end, but half-listening I almost didn't. One plain line from Beth or Dane (for example, "He's never set foot on this island" or "That's a trick to get Russ off the porch") would fix it.
- **The generator scene, Russ answering his own questions** (lines 57 and 177). "Is the choke off?" Russ said. "It is." ... "Who taught you that? Your father. Who taught your father? Mine..." and later "That's Tess. Who's Tess? The store." On text-to-speech this sounds like two or three people talking, and I couldn't tell who was saying what. - **"He had been wrong at the shack about his own office."** (line 27). I don't remember him saying anything about his office at the shack. And this whole paragraph (left Pruett at half past ten, who told Cole) is the same reasoning as Chapter 2, so I lost interest in it.
