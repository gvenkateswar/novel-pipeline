# Judge: Buyer persona (opus), p2-A vs p2-B, Chapter 03

1. **Winner: B**

2. **Why:** When the Gloucester boat's lights all go out at once and the harbor goes black, I sat up in bed. A gives me a skiff idling off the point, and I've read that one before. B's kiss is also hotter (the lip in her teeth, his thumbs waiting on her), and B lets Dane be funny ("like a man looking for the restroom"), so I like him more going into the shed.

3. **Where A lost me:** around 57%, at "The Ashby place was a big summer cottage on the north point that belonged to people from Connecticut, shut up for the winter, with water on three sides and one road in. Ned started its generator every storm..." It's a whole paragraph of real-estate listing right when I wanted them out of that store and alone, and I skimmed it. A almost won me back at the end: "stayed there in the snow while he told Portland where she was" is a lovely line, and she's standing beside him.

4. **Where B lost me:** around 45%, at "Net had you aboard. Your back steps had you home, twenty-two forty." Tess's ballpoint hand, the storm log and the deposit-bag colors pile up into a ledger. I lost track of who saw what and just skipped ahead to the milk. Once they were on the north road it didn't lose me again.

5. **Confidence: medium.** The two are word for word for the whole first scene. B wins on the blackout and the kiss. A's ending puts the couple side by side, and that's the thing I buy these books for, so it was close.

6. **Same-author check: Yes.** The razor scene is identical, and you can hear the same hand in every rhythm.
