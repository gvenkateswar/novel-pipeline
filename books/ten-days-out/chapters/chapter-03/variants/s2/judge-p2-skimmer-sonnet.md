1. Why: B puts the chase first and lets me see it, so by the time the store scene starts I know what happened and why everyone is wound up. In A the stranger shows up inside a scene whose geography I never got.

3. Where A lost me: "Beth went round behind the counter. The storm log lay open there in Tess's hand. She picked up a carton of milk left by the till." (~33%). I didn't know who was holding what or where anyone stood. "Inside, the generator quit, and the lights with it" and the hard cut to "Ned was by the store's side wall with an urn cup" had already started my thumb moving. It sped up again at "he went through Tess, coming in from the panel, and she went down on the ice", which I had to reread. The creepy close-up at the curtain ("bait and diesel and wet cotton") was good, and I slowed down for it.

4. Where B lost me: "the two keys for Tess's Ford lay on the charging shelf on their MARR'S tag; she hung them back with her knuckles in the cords, among the island's phones, Ned's in its rubber band" (~42%). That is an inventory of hooks, tags and cords, and I skimmed it. Tess's yellow tag and red tag speech right after it is a mild ledger too, but her voice carries it. Nothing else slowed me down.

5. Confidence: medium. The two scenes share most of their best lines and differ mainly in order and clarity. A's stranger in the curtain gap is the single most alive moment in either version, and a different tired reader could pick A for it.

6. Same-author check: Yes. They share the same sentence habits and the same Tess, Russ and Ned lines. The versions differ only in how the scene is ordered.
