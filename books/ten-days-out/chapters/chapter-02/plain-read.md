## 1. Retelling

Beth wakes up on her couch under the marshal's coat and admits to herself that she wants him. He's been up all night with his gun and is now nailing a board over the back door the man broke, and she touches the bruise her oar left on his arm. A police boat comes to take her off the island before a storm shuts the bay. She almost gets on, but she sees old Ned struggling with the ferry's ropes alone and says no. Dane's boss explains on the phone that the truck was found clean, so the only evidence is what Beth saw, and she has to tell it to a grand jury on Monday the 23rd or Cole Varney goes free. The island's part-time cop, Russ, reports that a small boat with no lights left the mainland side at 10:30 last night carrying a skinny young man with a bad leg. Dane says that means nobody had to tip the man off, since he set out before Beth even called. Dane stays with her, and the police boat leaves. Then the storm hits. While Beth and Ned are adding extra ropes to hold the ferry, one rope snaps and the ferry swings at her. Ned yanks her up out of the way, Dane jumps onto the ferry to tie a new rope, and they pull it back in. But the ferry's steering is broken, the floating dock is wrecked, and the island's boats, Russ's among them, get blown onto the rocks. Nobody can get off the island now, and Dane says she can't sleep at her father's house tonight.

## 2. What's at stake

Beth is the only person who saw the drugs. Cole Varney's people, starting with the young man with the dragging leg who knows her hill in the dark, want to stop her. If she doesn't make it to the grand jury on Monday the 23rd, Cole goes free. Now she's trapped on the island with them until the storm ends.

## 5. Where I lost the thread

- **"she" meaning a boat.** "She'll hold till it's done" (line 119), "She's not answering, Bethie" (line 161) and Russ's "Saw her go" (line 199) all use "she/her" for a rope, the ferry or a boat. Beth is also "she" in every paragraph. Half-listening, I kept hearing Beth. With "She'll hold" I truly didn't know whether Ned meant the rope, the ferry or Beth.
- **"Dane! Get on the boat!"** (line 131). The police boat had already left, so for a second I thought she meant that one. I didn't understand why she wanted him on the ferry until he was already on it and asking for a rope. It was also odd that she yells the order while he's "already going over the edge."
- **Was there a leak or not?** Dane says, "Nobody had to tell anybody anything. I was wrong last night" (line 77), and Beth is relieved. A few lines later he tells his boss he'll report "to you, and nobody else in that office" (line 89), which sounds like he still thinks someone is leaking. In Chapter 1 Beth also said the man must be from the island because only a local could find the cove, and the end of this chapter says he "knew every step of that hill in the dark." So is the man local or from the mainland? I came out unsure who is safe to trust.
- **Russ's line** "Where are you sleeping tonight? Who's asking? Me, for now. Somebody else will be, before dark." (line 199). In one breath it sounds like he's asking and answering himself. I got that it's a warning, but it was hard to follow by ear.
- **"Russ Whitcomb was a good man who was bad with money."** (line 73). Not confusing. But it came out of nowhere, flat, and stuck out like a hint. I noticed it.
- "the scar in her palm" (line 71). A new detail with no explanation. It didn't stop me.
- Timing: "Six hours, and I'm already 'the witness'" and "where the police boat had been tied forty minutes ago". Fine, I didn't stop.
