# Judge: Skimmer (opus), p3-A vs p3-B

1. **Winner: B**

2. **Why:** In B, Tess is actually on the pier, writing my name on the back of her hand, saying "Like the dog," and flashing a wad of hundreds. In A, Tess is a voice on a handheld reading off a roll call, and I'm tired and I skim radios.

3. **Where A lost me:** At about 23%: *"Cutter storm net, this is Marr's, net control. Roll call by house. Calloway."* It's a man next to a radio while a net runs its procedure: call signs, "Copy, Calloway, aboard," candle prices on the air, then "Margaret Ann, Margaret Ann" and two clicks. I could tell the clicks were supposed to matter, but by then I was skimming and only caught them on the way past. My thumb didn't slow down until the tower screamed.

4. **Where B lost me:** Only for a moment, at about 49%: *"She's not carrying anybody till somebody from the Coast Guard has his hand on that crack. I'll call it in tonight, by the book."* Ned doing paperwork right after the bridge comes down. It's two lines, and Dane's "This boat's got a hole in the front and no back door" pulled me straight back in. Before that, the tackle under the cable, with "You're very good at lying down. Best thing you've done all day," had me reading every word.

5. **Confidence: medium.** Everything from the walk home along the shore to the end is almost word for word the same in both, and that's the best part of either one. So the whole choice comes down to the pier scene, and in that scene B has people in it and A has a radio.

6. **Same-author check: Yes.** The back half is the same text, line for line.
