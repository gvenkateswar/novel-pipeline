# Judge: Skimmer (opus), p2 A vs B

1. **Winner: B.**

2. **Why:** B brings Tess into the kitchen in person, writing on her hand, with a wad of hundreds in her vest, so I'm watching a person who might be a suspect. A gives me the same beat as a radio roll call, and my thumb went right through it.

3. **Where A lost me:** At about 23%, `*"Cutter storm net, this is Marr's, net control. Roll call by house. Calloway."*`, and then "Beth keyed it." Call signs, keying, "Copy, Calloway, aboard." It's a woman working a machine. I skimmed down to "Does she do that for everybody?" and only half caught the Margaret Ann clicks, which I think were meant to matter. I slowed down again at the cable.

4. **Where B lost me:** At about 31%, "Over the bow, the steel ramp the cars drove aboard on ran down from the shore onto the *North Reach*, hung at its seaward end from two towers. The island called it the bridge." That's a diagram, and it comes right after a list of trucks ("the fire company's propane truck and the co-op's bait truck"). I skimmed the two paragraphs until "something above them screamed." It was short, and the scream pulled me back.

5. **Confidence: medium.** The second half of the two is nearly word for word the same, so the whole choice rests on the first 30%. There, B gives me a person and a want ("she wanted to put her fingers there"), and A gives me a radio net.

6. **Same-author check: Yes.** Whole passages are identical (the knocking door, the brick, "Who knew you were here?"), so it's one draft revised two ways.
