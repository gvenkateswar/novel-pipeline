# Judge: Skimmer, pair 2 (p2-A vs p2-B), Chapter 14

1. **Winner: B**

2. **Why:** Listening by ear, B tells me who's talking when it matters and breaks the boss's phone speech into pieces I can take in, so I stayed on the boat. In A I kept losing track of the speaker and lost the whole call in one block.

3. **Where A lost me:** About 8%, right at the pier: "By light it's holed, and a holed boat takes nobody to Portland. I'll hold it off inside the harbor till the wind goes round." No tag. The last person named was Tess, so on audio I didn't know it was Beth until two lines later. I came back for the rope cutting and the wheel, which is the best stretch in either version. At about 44% my thumb went for good on the boss's speech: "Your Hal Mercer comes back to nobody... There's no license out of Gloucester in that name... Nothing flies before Tuesday... And a text from a phone in nobody's name won't arrest that lawyer." Six facts in one breath. I heard none of them. It happened again near the end, about 94%: "At her word every light aboard went off. 'It's against every rule.'" Who said that? Ned? Beth? I couldn't tell.

4. **Where B lost me:** About 82%, under the bait truck: "Friday night the car deck had taken spray till morning, and every chain and truck on it had worn ice. This bag was dry, with no ice on it, and the battery was near full." That's Dane doing detective sums, and it runs straight into the ledger of who was where on Saturday. I drifted until "Then we go dark. Let them find us by ear," which pulled me back. The boss call at about 45% nearly lost me too, but B cuts it with Dane gripping the shelf, so I got through it.

5. **Confidence: medium.** The two are about 90% the same text, and the wheel scene and the kiss read almost identically. B wins on things you can hear: tagged lines, a broken-up phone call, and Tess "would save your life and charge you for the gas," the one line in either version that made me smile.

6. **Same-author check: Yes.** It's the same chapter almost word for word, with a different editor's hand on the tags and the order of the lines.
