# Issues, merged Ch 14 and revised Ch 15–17 (manuscript revision, Nov 6)

*Labeled by new chapter number. Source folders: new Ch 14 = chapter-14/draft-ms.md; new Ch 15 = chapter-16/final.md; new Ch 16 = chapter-17/final.md; new Ch 17 = chapter-18/final.md.*

**Issue 1 (Ch 14 and Ch 15)**
- Problem: High water is set at about 11 p.m., but the manuscript domain check puts high water on Sunday Nov 22 at 8:58 p.m. At 10:30–11:00 the tide is one to two hours past the top. Ch 15 repeats the error as fact.
- Location: Ch 14: "High water's an hour after. I want every foot of it under us at the harbor mouth." Ch 15: "The tide was at the top."
- Suggested fix: Ch 14: "High water's just gone by. I want every foot of it still under us at the harbor mouth." Ch 15: "The tide had only just started down."

**Issue 2 (Ch 14)**
- Problem: The switch-off logic does not work. Dane finds the phone at eight and switches it off then. A phone going dead at eight cannot tell the boat they are "moving", because the ferry leaves at ten. What the dead phone tells them is that it was found. The phone's mechanism (how a cheap phone "tells" anything) is never stated either, so a listener cannot follow the cost.
- Location: "It tells the boat off the point when we leave the pier. Can I switch it off?" / "When it goes dead, they'll know we're moving."
- Suggested fix: "It tells the boat off the point where we are. Can I switch it off?" / "When it goes dead, they'll know we found it." Beth: "Then they'll watch the pier. We go dark and let them find us by ear."

**Issue 3 (Ch 14, carried into Ch 17)**
- Problem: The dating of the phone rests only on "dry, no ice", which proves it went on after Friday night. It does not prove Saturday. Saturday night and all Sunday morning are open, and the big boat's skiff (Mercer used one on Thursday) is never excluded. Ch 17's "Somebody on Cutter" and the Book 2 hook therefore have no support on the page. The beat sheet's elimination clauses (nothing put off from the big boat; Ned and the kid tied) were cut.
- Location: Ch 14: "Somebody had come aboard Saturday, while the ferry sat empty and he held the light for her at the co-op vise." Ch 17: "Somebody on Cutter walked down and taped it there."
- Suggested fix: Add one clause in Ch 14: "Nothing had put off from the big boat all day. Tess had watched it through her store glasses." Date it "since Friday night" and let Dane own the whole stretch.

**Issue 4 (Ch 14)**
- Problem: A phone cannot watch. The metaphor misleads a listener about what the phone does.
- Location: "All weekend he had watched that boat, and it had been watching him from under a bait truck."
- Suggested fix: "All weekend he had watched that boat, and it had been listening to him from under a bait truck."

**Issue 5 (Ch 14)**
- Problem: The rod's history is unclear. Scene 1 says the rod is already back in Friday night. Scene 5 says Dane held the light at "the co-op vise" on Saturday, and Beth finds a "bright new mark". A listener never hears that the rod came out again Saturday after it jammed.
- Location: "The rod from the wheel down to the steering blade under the boat was back in, straight as the co-op vise could get it." / "Beth ran her thumb along the rod to its bright new mark."
- Suggested fix: Add to the Scene 3 end or Scene 5: "The rod had come out again Saturday, and gone back in with a new bright mark where the vise held it."

**Issue 6 (Ch 14)**
- Problem: Three set-up facts a listener needs are missing from the page. (a) Why Ned and the kid cross at all. The reason was "Tess can't nurse Russ and guard two men", and it is cut. (b) How Beth has the ferry. The "ferry board gave her the boat" clause is cut, and Ch 17 only echoes it. (c) Who casts off the lines at ten, since Dane is at her shoulder, Russ is shot, and Tess is on the steps.
- Location: "Ned looked down at his life jacket as if it were somebody else's." / "At ten she took it off the pier and across the harbor by feel."
- Suggested fix: One clause each: "Tess couldn't nurse Russ and guard two men, so both crossed"; "the ferry board had given Beth the boat by Dane's phone that morning"; "Tess threw off the last line from the pier and went back to the steps."

**Issue 7 (Ch 14)**
- Problem: "Down there" cannot be followed by a listener. The alternative to Ned at the wheel is never stated, and "down there" has no referent.
- Location: "Down there he's an arm's length from the kid."
- Suggested fix: "If he stays below, he's an arm's length from the kid. Up here he's an arm's length from me."

**Issue 8 (Ch 14 and Ch 16)**
- Problem: Dane's removal does not match the boss's promise. On Saturday the boss says "Then she's on her own after Monday", and Dane stays her guard through Monday. In Ch 16 he is "taken off Ms. Calloway's protection at six this morning", which is Monday before the testimony. Nothing explains why the boss pulled him early, when he had disclosed the relationship two days earlier. A listener hears a contradiction.
- Location: Ch 14: "Then she's on her own after Monday," his boss said. Ch 16: "Deputy Reyes was taken off Ms. Calloway's protection at six this morning."
- Suggested fix: In Ch 16, add the boss's text or the prosecutor's line: "The marshals kept him on until the ferry landed. At six this morning he was taken off."

**Issue 9 (Ch 14)**
- Problem: Plain wanting four times in one chapter, against a cap of once. Three are the same "thought about … there" construction.
- Location: "thought about it on the inside of her wrist"; "thought about her mouth there, and she was angry enough to keep both hands on the wheel"; "wanted to be asked"; "thought about his mouth there."
- Suggested fix: Keep the hollow of the throat (Scene 2). Turn the other three into actions. For example, Scene 5: he steps closer to her right shoulder and takes his hand off the rifle strap.

**Issue 10 (Ch 14)**
- Problem: Dane's last longing line copies the Ch 08 construction almost exactly ("the back of his neck … thought about her mouth on it"). It is the same image in the mirrored direction, in the same book.
- Location: "He stood at her right shoulder with the rifle and looked at the back of her neck under the braid, and thought about his mouth there."
- Suggested fix: Cut the line. The engine is the next beat.

**Issue 11 (Ch 14)**
- Problem: Where are Ned and the kid during the Sunday kiss? Ch 12–13 put both prisoners in the town-office back room. Scene 4 puts the oven, the counter, the taping and the kiss in "the back room", and neither prisoner is mentioned. The beat sheet wanted no Ned in a tender scene, but the page leaves them in the room.
- Location: "The back room smelled of cloves." / "Beth sat on the counter by the sink and held out her hands."
- Suggested fix: One clause moving them: "Russ had dragged Ned and the kid into the front room by the stove, and the back room was theirs for ten minutes." Or name the oven room as the old clerk's kitchen, apart from the prisoners.

**Issue 12 (Ch 14)**
- Problem: The boss's reply to the withdrawal is split into two paragraphs of the same speaker, with "The line went quiet." between them. The cold reader has already tripped on this pattern. The second paragraph also reads as if it follows "Written." with no new reason.
- Location: "Then his boss said, 'Written.'" / "'Then she's on her own after Monday,' his boss said."
- Suggested fix: Merge into one beat: "'Written,' his boss said. 'Then she's on her own after Monday.'"

**Issue 13 (Ch 14)**
- Problem: Three lines a listener cannot parse. "Not all of her" has no antecedent. "By light it's holed" can be heard as "by the light". "Then we split Wednesday" does not say what is being split.
- Location: "Not all of her," Dane said. / "By light it's holed," Beth said. / "Then we split Wednesday," he said. "I'll take the bigger half."
- Suggested fix: "Not all of her. I've put in for the Maine office, so I'm still in the state." / "By daylight it's holed." / "Then we split the blame for Wednesday. I'll take the bigger half."

**Issue 14 (Ch 14)**
- Problem: The stakes line is gone. The boss's block omits "If she isn't in the room Monday, Cole walks at two", and the "no one comes" reason is thin. The beat sheet and logic gate keep the Coast Guard "on the storm's distress calls". The page says only "in this sea nobody can get aboard". A listener who joins at this chapter does not know why Monday matters or why no one stands by.
- Location: "Nothing flies before Tuesday." / "'Sunday night or not at all.'"
- Suggested fix: Add one clause to the boss: "The Coast Guard's on the storm's distress calls, and if she's not in that room Monday, Cole walks at two."

**Issue 15 (Ch 12 vs Ch 14, term)**
- Problem: The steering piece is "the rudder" in Ch 12 and "the steering blade under the boat" in Ch 14. A listener will not link them.
- Location: Ch 12: "The rod from the wheel to the rudder hung loose". Ch 14: "the steering blade under the boat".
- Suggested fix: Use one word in both, with a one-clause gloss in Ch 12: "the rudder, the blade under the boat that steers it".

**Issue 16 (Ch 15)**
- Problem: Direct contradiction with Ch 14. Beth lands the ferry at Cutter on Saturday ("She brought the boat's nose toward the pier"), so "She had never landed it before" is false.
- Location: "She brought the ferry in against the pier slowly, on the first try. She had never landed it before."
- Suggested fix: "She had never landed it at Pruett, or in the dark." Or cut the sentence.

**Issue 17 (Ch 15 vs Ch 14 and Ch 17)**
- Problem: Tess's silence rule is broken without notice. Ch 14 says she "still wouldn't say one word to her on the radio." Ch 15 has her speak to Beth by name on the radio. Ch 16 and Ch 17 then say Tess won't speak to Beth at all. A listener cannot tell whether the radio line counts.
- Location: Ch 15: "The radio clicked, and Tess's voice came out of the speaker. 'Truck's on the point, Calloway. Light's on you.'"
- Suggested fix: Either have Tess say it to the air ("Truck's on the point. Light's on the ferry"), or add one Beth beat: "It was the first word Tess had said to her since Wednesday, and the last she'd say for days."

**Issue 18 (Ch 15)**
- Problem: Ned's cuffs cannot be reconstructed. In Ch 12 Russ's cuffs close on both of Ned's wrists. In Ch 14 Dane "cuffed Ned to the rail". In Ch 15 one cuff "hangs open from his wrist" and Ned works the wheel with it. At Pruett Ned lifts "his cuffed hands" (plural).
- Location: "She unlocked the cuff from the rail, left it hanging open from his wrist" / "At the top of the ramp he looked back once and lifted his cuffed hands."
- Suggested fix: Say once in Ch 14 that Ned's wrists are in one cuff each with a short chain between them and the rail cuff on the chain. At Pruett add "a deputy closed the open cuff on his free wrist".

**Issue 19 (Ch 15)**
- Problem: The kid's pistol is unaccounted for. Dane pocketed it in Ch 13. The Pruett inventory lists "the rifle, the pistol", which a listener reads as Dane's own gun, already taken one sentence earlier.
- Location: "The rifle, the pistol, Ned's knife and the planted phone went into one bag."
- Suggested fix: "The kid's rifle, the kid's pistol, Dane's pistol, Ned's knife and the planted phone went into one bag."

**Issue 20 (Ch 15)**
- Problem: Emotion stated rather than shown, and flat plain wanting. It is also tonally off, since she presses a hat into a bullet wound.
- Location: "She wanted him, here, bleeding on the upper deck of her boat in a borrowed shirt, and she had never wanted anything so much in her life."
- Suggested fix: Cut. If a beat is needed, her hands: "Her hands would not come off him when the hat was tied."

**Issue 21 (Ch 15)**
- Problem: The rod plant is never paid and an earlier plant is quoted back. Ch 14 sets "If it sticks, I'll feel it first." Ch 15 has no moment of fear, and line 95 says only "The steering was fine." The narration then restates Friday: "as it had swung off the pier on Friday night with the boat off the point watching." The predictability note says not to quote earlier plants back.
- Location: "The steering was fine. She could feel every spoke of it turning true under her skinned palms." / "as it had swung off the pier on Friday night with the boat off the point watching."
- Suggested fix: Add one fear clause: "She waited for the wheel to go heavy the way it had on Friday. It didn't." Cut the Friday-night comparison.

**Issue 22 (Ch 15)**
- Problem: The ferry's draft is wrong against the domain check. A hundred-foot steel car ferry cannot float "where Alton's lobster boat had". The listener hears that the ferry draws no more than a lobster boat.
- Location: "The ferry floated where Alton's lobster boat had, and a dragger drew twice what the ferry did."
- Suggested fix: "At this tide the ferry would just clear the bottom in there, and a dragger would not."

**Issue 23 (Ch 15)**
- Problem: How Mercer knows "the boy" is aboard is not answered. The beat-sheet clause (the kid's phone went silent; Tess's radio named a boy in ties) was cut.
- Location: "Stop your engine and we'll take you and the boy. The rest of you go home."
- Suggested fix: One line after it: "Tess's radio had told every boat in the bay there was a boy in ties at the town office."

**Issue 24 (Ch 15)**
- Problem: Contrast construction ("X and not for Y").
- Location: "on water deep enough for the ferry and not for it."
- Suggested fix: "on water deep enough for the ferry. The big boat drew twice as much."

**Issue 25 (Ch 15)**
- Problem: Dane says "everybody in one room", but the kid ends in the wheelhouse doorway and Dane stays alone on the upper deck, bleeding, for about four hours, with no further care from Beth. This makes his survival unconvincing and the stated plan not delivered.
- Location: "I want everybody aboard in one room where I can see them." / "She sat him in the wheelhouse doorway under the ladder, where Dane could see him."
- Suggested fix: Change to "I want them where I can see them." After the statement, add one beat in which Beth checks and re-presses the hat before the Pruett lights.

- Location: "The hotel room was on the third floor, over the corner of Middle Street, and the afternoon came straight in through both windows."

**Issue 27 (Ch 16)**

- Location: "Mercer had watched Dane step in front of Beth on the town office step."

**Issue 28 (Ch 16)**

- Location: "He did what she said, in order, and wanted every word of it." / "Her taped hands wanted to close." / "her taped hands up on his chest."

**Issue 29 (Ch 16 and Ch 17)**
- Problem: The same fact is told twice across the last two chapters. Ch 16: "The big boat's still out there… The Coast Guard looked all night and found nothing." Ch 17: "The Coast Guard never found the big boat. It's still out there." A listener hears a repeat, and Beth already knows.
- Location: Ch 16: "The Coast Guard looked all night and found nothing." Ch 17: "My boss called Tuesday. The Coast Guard never found the big boat. It's still out there."
- Suggested fix: Cut the Ch 17 pair of sentences. Keep only Beth's thought about the gold tooth, now reading as a memory. Dane moves straight to "the kid's name is Kyle Pelletier."

**Issue 30 (Ch 17 vs Ch 13)**
- Problem: The bullet hole in Beth's front door is at "chest height" in Ch 13 and "hip height" in Ch 17.
- Location: Ch 13: "Her front door had a splintered hole at chest height." Ch 17: "the pale new plug of wood in her front door, at hip height, where the bullet had come through."

**Issue 31 (Ch 17)**
- Problem: The "marshal price" joke muddles. Dane says Tess charged him full price and then asks why she called it the marshal price. Tess then says she "should have charged him the marshal price", which implies she did not. A listener cannot tell which way the price went.
- Location: "Tess charged me full price. She watched me count it out." / "Thirty-eight dollars at my register," Tess told the table. "I should have charged him the marshal price."
- Suggested fix: Dane: "Tess charged me the marshal price. She watched me count it out." Beth: "She charges everybody full price." Dane: "Then why did she call it the marshal price?" Tess's line stays.

**Issue 32 (Ch 17)**
- Problem: The phone line is garbled and repeats Ch 14. "dry under Friday's ice" says the opposite of the Ch 14 fact (the bag had no ice on it). "By Saturday he was tied" misdates the kid, who has been tied since Thursday. Beth heard all of this in Ch 14.
- Location: "The bag was dry under Friday's ice, and the battery was near full. By Saturday he was tied on the town office floor and Ned was cuffed to the pipe."
- Suggested fix: "The bag was dry, with no ice on it, though everything else on that deck was iced Friday night. The kid and Ned were tied up at the town office all Saturday." Cut the part Beth already knows if she is the only listener.

**Issue 33 (Ch 17)**
- Problem: Russ's self-answered question tic appears five times in one chapter.
- Location: "Did you fix that? You did." / "Is there more? There's more." / "Can he stay? He can stay." / "Didn't they? They did." / "couldn't they? They could."
- Suggested fix: Keep two (the carving and the "Can he stay?"). Rewrite the others as plain statements.

**Issue 34 (Ch 17 vs Ch 01)**
- Problem: Ch 01 has Beth estimate the hoodie man at "maybe twenty-five". Ch 17 gives "He's nineteen" with no word on the gap. For a man who has worked "two summers" on Cole's boat and lived alone in a trailer, the six-year difference is noticeable.
- Location: "And the kid's name is Kyle Pelletier, by the way. He's nineteen."
- Suggested fix: Make him "twenty-two", or add Beth's thought: "He had looked twenty-five on the ferry. He looked nineteen in a statement."

**Issue 35 (Ch 17)**
- Problem: Russ cites New Bedford as something Dane told him, but no scene shows Dane telling Russ. Russ was shot in Ch 13 and flown Tuesday. The New Bedford answer is also already given in Ch 16 (Beth and Dane on the bench), so this repeat of "they burned her house" is the second telling in two chapters.
- Location: "That woman in New Bedford you told me about," Russ said to Dane. "They burned her house after she testified."
- Suggested fix: "That woman in New Bedford," Russ said. "Beth told me on the ferry." Shorten to Russ's question only: "Does a cell stop people like that?"
