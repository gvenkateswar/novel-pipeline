Her father's boat had just got to me, and then it turned into a marshal taking her statement. "Counter to teller" sounds like a form. I skimmed until she tied the tag on his wrist. I also slowed down at the Monday geography, "At dead low the bar lay dry from the Ashby shore toward Calloway Cove, the cove on the east shore that carried her family's name…" (~85%), where I had to draw myself a map before I could get to the good part.

4. **Where B lost me:** "Envelopes from the store. Slip inside. My name on the front in Tess's hand. Flap tucked. Never sealed." (~41%). It's the same questioning, and it's still the two of them doing business. It hurt less here because the line right before it, the bank man saying "thank you, dear" and taking the box "as if she'd brought him a pie," was still stinging, so I kept going. I never stopped.
