# Issues: Chapter 04 draft.md (rebuild)

**Issue 1**
- Problem: The Ashby generator ran all of Saturday night with the house lit behind the sheets (Ch 03 ends on it hammering and the windows yellow). Beth's reason for not starting it now, that a running diesel "would tell a man which house it was," ignores that it has already done that for about sixteen hours. The threat beat rests on a rule the page has already broken.
- Location: "A diesel hammering at a sheeted summer house would tell a man which house it was."
- Suggested fix: Add one clause that answers the overnight run (for example, the windows face the point and the set was only heard from the north end), or have Beth's worry be the daylight and the man's line of sight instead of the noise.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: Beth reasons that Ned's every-storm generator is no cover against this man ("That man wasn't an islander"). Forty lines later the same cover is the reason to run it ("Ned ran it every storm"). She reverses with no stated reason. The man has also just glassed the point, and Dane said "We move tonight," so staying and starting a diesel at the house he glassed is unexplained.
- Location: "That man wasn't an islander." / "Ned ran it every storm. Her hand was already on the cord. She put the handle in his fist."
- Suggested fix: Give the reversal one reason on the page. For example, the man is gone and Ned's own diesel arriving at dusk is the ordinary thing the island expects. Or have Dane's "tonight" give way to "Then Monday" in a line that says they stay.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: "That man wasn't an islander" is stated flatly. Ch 02 and Ch 03 plant the hooded man as someone who rows like an islander and knows the shore and the surge. The rowing detail that carried that plant in the earlier Ch 04 S1 is gone from this draft, so the plant is unpaid and Beth's certainty from a long-range glimpse has no basis. Bible: Doucette is from down east, not from Cutter.
- Location: "That man wasn't an islander."
- Suggested fix: Make it "wasn't one of ours," or restore one clause in S1 that has him rowing like an islander. Either way, tie it to what Beth can see.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: Dane narrates the search of the cottage ("opening drawers") but has just passed the binoculars to Beth. He cannot see into the house from the hill. The line is written as if he had the glasses.
- Location: "He's opening drawers," Dane said, very low. "Small house. Won't take him long."
- Suggested fix: Have him say it as inference ("He'll be opening drawers"), or have him take the binoculars back for the line.

**Issue 5**
- Problem: Dane knows about the man on the spine but has no stated way of seeing him. He was on his back with his head and shoulders under the set. He drew breath to speak, and Beth said "Don't." Beth never tells him what she saw. When he sits up the man is already over the rise, and Dane speaks of him as known.
- Location: "Dane sat up. "He's checking houses. We move tonight.""
- Suggested fix: Add a beat where Dane sees the man (his eyes go to the door as she says "Stop"), or one line from Beth ("Spine. Hood.").

**Issue 6**
- Problem: Staging gap. Dane sits up and plans, then the "not yet" exchange, which depends on the hip-to-hip posture, happens with fuel "coming nearly clean," meaning he is pumping again. The page never puts him back under the set or at the lever.
- Location: "The fuel was coming nearly clean now, a bead and a bead."
- Suggested fix: One clause putting him back on the pad, hip against hers, before the fuel line.

**Issue 7**
- Problem: Dropped plant: Tess's emergency-management title. The old Ch 04 carried it in Russ's mouth, and the continuity log's Carried-forward table still marks it "Paid, Ch 04 S2." The new draft has no mention of it. Beats-v2 ruling 9 moves it to Tess's own mouth in Ch 05 5.1, so this is not a beat violation. But the log row, the plant ledger row ("Tess, emergency management since town meeting | 04 S2") and the Ch 06 "You told the county?" dependency are now wrong. The same applies to "Tess's phone is the only one that works," also dropped from Ch 04.
- Location: (absent; the Russ exchange runs from "Nothing's flying," Russ said. to "That tower's been on the district's list since Labor Day.")
- Suggested fix: Reset both ledger rows to "Ch 05 s1" and make Ch 05 5.1 carry the title and the phone rule. If Ch 05 fails to, restore the title as one Russ clause here.

**Issue 8**
- Problem: LifeFlight is not named, so the continuity plant ("LifeFlight | 04 S2 | 07, 08") and the motif row have no page anchor. "Nothing's flying" is also a bare statement of a fact Beth and Dane already have: the Day 2 refusal for Mrs. Dyer happened in Ch 03's off-page time, and Beth is an islander. Russ explains it to both of them.
- Location: ""Nothing's flying," Russ said. "Pilot wouldn't lift an old woman with a bad heart yesterday, so he won't lift you two. That leaves boats. Minute it lays down, I'll run you over to Pruett myself.""
- Suggested fix: Name it ("LifeFlight") in Russ's line so Ch 07 and Ch 08 can call back to it, and cut "so he won't lift you two" or keep it as Russ's guess rather than news.

**Issue 9**
- Problem: Plants the old S3 carried that the rebuild drops without a ruling covering them:
  - "I watched that pouch go by on the ramp" (ledger: the pouch and Dane watching it → Ch 07).
  - "Tess bagged them Thursday night."
  - The pouch rides over "store bag and fare bag both," handed to the courier at Pruett.
  - "Who sees the slip? Tess. Me. The teller." The teller is the person whose "Seal noted: red" drives the Ch 07 and Ch 09 seal clue.
  - "Until yesterday he had angled every screen away from her." This is the payoff of the bible's lit-screen lapse; without it, turning the screen to her reads as nothing.
  - "Webbers Cove across the top" of the listing sheet (logged object).
  - Numbers-ledger rows "twice (the district's lot)" and "a year" (carrying the words).
- Location: ""Envelopes from the store. Slip inside. My name on the front in Tess's hand. Flap tucked. Never sealed.""
- Suggested fix: Restore the teller and the two-bags-in-one-pouch fact in one line ("Tess. Me. The teller." and "Pouch takes both bags") because the seal clue cannot be fair without them. Update the ledger and numbers rows for the rest.

**Issue 10**
- Problem: "Thursday's spent seal" is ambiguous and conflicts with the log. Thursday night's fare bag was sealed on Day 0 and left on the Friday boat with the courier (Ch 01 s2; CASE FACTS), and Ned resealed the store bag red. The old text said the credit union cut a seal off and sent it back in the empty pouch. That explanation is gone, so a reader takes this week's seal and cannot see how it is in Beth's coat. The log's Master flag already warns this must stay consistent with the courier.
- Location: "She took Thursday's spent seal out of her coat pocket, a red plastic tail with the lock cut through."
- Suggested fix: "Last Thursday's spent seal," or restore the clause that the credit union sent it back in the empty pouch.

**Issue 11**
- Problem: The Ruthie C's sale is dropped entirely. The father's story stops at the tailgate. Ch 01's plant "the picnic boat" (the summer man) was paid in the old chapter ("Paid, Ch 04 S3"), and the bible's "she places people by their boats" depends on the buyer. The beats cut the phrase "picnic boat," not the sale. "Two soft winters" also no longer says what was soft (the log: "the price soft two winters running"). "Soft winters" can read as mild weather.
- Location: "There were two soft winters, and then the bank called the note, which had been signed in a good year by a man who believed in good years."
- Suggested fix: Restore "the price went soft two winters running," and add one clause for the boat's sale to a summer man without repeating "picnic boat," or reopen the ledger row.

**Issue 12**
- Problem: Ruling 7 says the license paragraph is cut and at most one sentence of the wheel survives, with the license object moved to Ch 06 s4. The draft keeps four sentences: the wheel, "Put my name in," "Four words," and the license in her wallet pressing her ribs. This is also the passage the cold reader called the sag. It leaves the Ch 06 s4 license beat with nothing to introduce.
- Location: "Put my name in. Four words. Her hundred-ton license was in her wallet, and the corner of it pressed her ribs when she breathed. She held the funnel and didn't say them."
- Suggested fix: Cut the license sentence and keep "Put my name in. Four words. She held the funnel and didn't say them."

**Issue 13**
- Problem: Ned's look-back plant 3 of 3 has no look-back. Ned does not turn. The flashlight swings up, and the page never returns the light to him after Dane held it ("Dane held the light for the funnel"). The Ch 08 payoff ("he doesn't") needs a turn on the page. The old text had Ned take the flashlight back.
- Location: "At the foot of it the flashlight swung up once toward them, a single arc, and went on toward the harbor."
- Suggested fix: Have Ned take the light back from Dane before he goes, and have him turn at the foot of the drive before the arc.

**Issue 14**
- Problem: Ned gives a run of five or six unprompted facts, including his own whereabouts through the barge, which is the detail-heavy shape the bible reserves for his lying tell. The ruling is that Ned gets no new tell. Only the diesel, the impellers and "back aboard till the barge" are allowed.
- Location: ""Glass is coming up. Be down to a breeze by morning, squalls in it. Pump on the *North Reach* is eating impellers. I'm going back aboard and staying aboard till the barge. She's taking water slow up forward. She'll keep.""
- Suggested fix: Cut "She's taking water slow up forward" or the squalls clause, leaving weather, then the impellers and aboard-till-the-barge.

**Issue 15**
- Problem: Beth's voice drifts. She asks "To where?", although the profile says she almost never asks a question except in tenderness or desire. She then gives four sentences of unprompted reasons with her subject in every one ("The one place I've got..."), where the profile says conclusion first, subject dropped, reasons only if asked.
- Location: ""To where? He's been through mine." She looked at the dead engine. "The one place I've got that nobody looks at is the fish house at the head of Calloway Cove. The cove's been over the sill on every tide since Friday's surge. Floor's under water at the top of the tide, and there's one door. The surge drops on Monday's tides.""
- Suggested fix: Make it a statement ("Nowhere. Mine's been through."), then let Dane ask about the fish house so the reasons come only when asked.

**Issue 16**
- Problem: The fish-house tide facts contradict each other. S2 says the cove is over the sill "on every tide" but the floor is under water "at the top of the tide," so at low water the sill is clear. S3 says that at dead low Monday "the sill was out of the water for the first time since Friday," which a clear low on Saturday or Sunday would already have done. The map clause the beats required, "the cove on the east shore that her family was named for," is also missing, so the reader has no idea where the fish house is relative to Ashby or her house.
- Location: "The cove's been over the sill on every tide since Friday's surge." / "The tide line was dark on the new shingles, and the sill was out of the water for the first time since Friday."
- Suggested fix: State that the surge has held the water over the sill even at low ("even at the low"), keep "first time since Friday" in S3, and add the east-shore clause to S3.

**Issue 17**
- Problem: Binoculars appear with no source. Ch 03 says Dane "had come over with nothing but what was on his back," and the bible's kit list has no binoculars.
- Location: "Dane lay flat beside her, and after a while he passed her the binoculars without a word."
- Suggested fix: One clause putting them in the Ashby house ("the Ashbys' binoculars off the hook by the door").

**Issue 18**
- Problem: Russ's timing is muddled. He says the man rowed out "at sunup" and then says he would ask "before daylight." Beth watched the skiff go minutes ago at first light, yet Russ speaks of it as earlier news and plans to go "after breakfast." Nothing explains why he is climbing the hill.
- Location: ""Somebody rowed out to that dragger at sunup," Russ said, unscrewing the cup. "Saw it from my kitchen. I thought I'd take the *Second Wind* out after breakfast and ask the *Margaret Ann* what she wants with a skiff before daylight.""
- Suggested fix: Cut "before daylight," and have Russ say he saw it a few minutes ago from his kitchen and came up for a look.

**Issue 19**

- Location: "he passed her the binoculars without a word." / "Russ took it without a word, and drank."
