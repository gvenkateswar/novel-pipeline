# Plain read: Chapter 7 ("Cold Water")

Draft read: `chapters/chapter-07/final.md` (with chapters 01 to 06 `final.md` for context). No other files opened.

## 1. Retelling

Beth and Dane are lying flat on the shore rocks, holding hands, when a huge wave comes over them. The water pulls Dane off the rock as it goes back out, and he lets go of her hand so the sea won't drag her in too. Beth goes into the water up to her thighs, grabs him by the collar and hauls him out, then hides him behind a big rock and holds his mouth shut so his chattering teeth won't give them away. The gunman shines his flashlight on the water where Dane went in for a long time, then leaves, and Beth whispers, "He thinks we drowned." She half-carries Dane a mile to an empty lighthouse keeper's house, climbs in a window, strips off his wet clothes and her own, piles blankets on the bed and warms him with her body. Once he can talk, they argue gently about him falling asleep on watch and letting go of her hand, and she laughs at his joke. Later she tells him straight out that she wants him, and they sleep together in the dark. Afterward he says what's been under all of it: he'll get her off the island alive and then lose her, because she'll never leave and he'll go back to Boston. She doesn't answer. She just holds his hand hard.

## 2. What's at stake

Beth is the only witness against Cole Varney, the man whose lobster truck carried fentanyl. The limping kid who works for Cole is trying to kill her before the grand jury meets on Monday. If she dies, Cole goes free. Someone she trusts (Tess, Russ or Ned), or Dane's boss, told the killer where she was sleeping. For now the killer thinks she's dead, so if anyone comes looking, that proves somebody told them she's alive.

## 3. The romance

Yes, I felt it clearly. This is the chapter where they come together. The moments that landed:

- "I want you. I'm saying it out loud so you can't pretend I didn't." And then the line right after, telling me this is the first time she's asked anyone for something for herself.
- "Come here. You're shaking. Let me be the warm one for once."
- "You don't get to decide what I can hold."
- "I'm going to get you off this island alive, and then I'm going to lose you, aren't I?" She doesn't answer, she just holds his hand. That hurt, in a good way. It's the line I'd remember.

The love scene works by ear because she's telling him what to do in the dark. I always knew who was doing what.

## 4. Words I didn't know

- "He shoved the gun into its holster." I know roughly what this means from TV (a pouch for the gun). It didn't stop me.
- "The next swell lifted him." I guessed it meant a wave. It didn't stop me.
- "I tried one of those apps once... It found me nine men inside fifty miles." I guessed dating apps, but the page never says so. It didn't stop me, and the joke still worked.
- "Foil." / "The box is soup," she said. "These are fine." **This stopped me for a second.** I had to work out that "foil" meant a condom wrapper and that "the box is soup" meant the condom box she bought in Chapter 5 got soaked to mush in the sea. I did get it from "the box", but only because I remembered the store scene. If you hadn't been paying attention back then, it would go right past you.
- "The tide's making" (end of Chapter 6). I didn't know the phrase, but she explains it right away (the sea will cover the low rocks in ten minutes).

None of these carried a plot fact I'd have missed.

## 5. Where I lost the thread

- "Every kid on this island has been in that window." This comes before the house is described, so for a moment I didn't know which window she meant. The next line explains it.
- "Four people had known about that house, and it had burned. Only Russ had found the loft, and nobody had come to the loft. He couldn't stop hearing that." I couldn't tell what Dane was concluding. Does it mean Russ is probably innocent, since the loft was safe even though Russ knew about it? Or does it point at Russ? I'm guessing it clears Russ, but the page doesn't say that, and I wasn't sure.
- "Her knuckles stopped on two sore places below his collarbone. 'That's me,' she said." It took me a second to understand these were bruises from her grabbing his collar to drag him out. I got there, but slowly.
- "I tried one of those apps once..." This comes out of nowhere in the middle of the walk. I understood why she says it (to get him to laugh and keep moving), but it was a jump.
- "Somebody had taken her face off Tess's board in September... and he still couldn't make it add up." That's fine. I couldn't make it add up either. It's a question the book is clearly holding open, not a confusion.

## 6. Verdict: PASS

I could retell the main event: the wave takes Dane, Beth saves him, the killer thinks they drowned, and they warm up and sleep together in the lighthouse house. I could say what's at stake. No plot fact depended on a word I didn't know, and the romance moved, ending on him knowing he'll lose her. Small fixes worth thinking about: say what the loft-and-Russ thought means, and give "foil / the box is soup" one more plain word so a half-listener knows it's the condom box.
