3. **Where A lost me:** "'—while Ms. Marr was on her rounds, he came up the back way for an impeller off that shelf, the box with her price sticker on it; that he took the spare key to Ms. Marr's truck off her hook…'" (~35%). It's a statement read out from a legal pad in one block of semicolons, and my thumb went fast through it.
4. **Where B lost me:** "'The captain's float-light key was handed over Wednesday and kept. The town's key, which is understood to hang by your safe—'" (~45%). Too many keys. Tess's "Never left the nail" cut it short, so I kept reading.
5. **Confidence:** medium. Both are a woman with a pad and a list of facts, and Dane isn't in the store in either one. B just gives Tess more to say back.
