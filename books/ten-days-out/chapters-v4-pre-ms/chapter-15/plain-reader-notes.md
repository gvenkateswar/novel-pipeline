# Plain Reader Notes: Chapter 15, "Eleven Pounds"

Draft read: `chapters/chapter-15/final.md` (Nov 6, 2026). Read as context: `final.md` for chapters 01 through 14, in order. Nothing else.

## 1. Retelling

It's Sunday, the last night they can get Beth to Portland in time. In the town office kitchen Dane is trying to fit a huge ham into a small oven when Beth gives him her terms: he asks her every time, even when he's sure, and he never tells anybody she said yes when she didn't. That's about Wednesday, when he told his boss "She'll go" for her. He says yes to all of it. She also admits she did the same thing to him when she said "And so do we," and takes that back. She bets him a dollar the ham will fit, and with Tess's roasting pan and Beth calling out each move, it goes in with a quarter inch to spare. Then she tells Russ and Tess he's going to ask her something and she'll say yes. He asks if he can kiss her, and they kiss in front of Russ while Tess walks out without a word. That night Dane searches the ferry, puts life jackets on the two prisoners, the kid and Ned, and brings them along, because nobody ashore can guard them. Beth wants Ned up at the wheel because he can hear the rocks in the dark. Dane cuffs him to a rail there and puts the key in Beth's pocket. At ten Beth takes the ferry out with no escort. At the harbor mouth they hear a bigger engine coming out of the dark from the point where the strange boat has been waiting, and Dane takes the rifle to the back window.

## 2. What's at stake

Beth has to get across to the mainland tonight and testify Monday morning, or Cole goes free at two. The men on the strange boat off the point want to stop her, and now they're coming at the ferry in the dark with the Coast Guard an hour away.

## 3. The romance

Yes, I felt it. The two of them are making up after the Wednesday fight, out loud and in plain words. The moment I felt it most:

> "Can I kiss you? I'm asking."
> "Yes. Come here."

followed by her hooking her taped wrists over his shoulders and kissing him slowly "in front of a man she had known since she was six, and she didn't hurry any of it." Her telling Russ and Tess first ("I'm telling you myself, so nobody ever says it for me") also landed. It ties straight back to what he did wrong.

One thing I noticed: when she says "He's going to ask me something, and I'm going to say yes," for a second I thought he was going to propose, or answer "What do you want?" from last chapter. Then it was a kiss. It works because it matches her terms, but it felt a little smaller than the setup. Also, he told her he loved her back in Chapter 8 and she said "Ask me in five days." I'm still waiting on her answer to that. It didn't come here.

## 5. Where I lost the thread

- **"So that part's off too."** Half-listening, I didn't know what "that part" was, or what else was "off." I worked out that she meant her "And so do we" (the breakup), and that "off too" meant his "She'll go" was cancelled as well. It took me a replay. Saying it plainly, something like "So we're not done," would land on one listen.
- **The search of the car deck.** "Dane lay on his back on the car deck with his flashlight on the underside of the bait truck... Nothing anywhere." I didn't know what he was looking for. A bomb? Somebody hiding? More drugs? The page never says. I'd only be guessing from TV. Nothing was found, so it didn't break the plot, but I couldn't feel any danger in the scene because I didn't know what the danger was.
- **Why Tess walked out.** "Tess stood, put the shotgun under her arm, and walked out the front door onto the steps without a word." I couldn't tell if she was angry about the kiss, still angry at Beth, or just giving them privacy. Later she's on the radio, so she's still helping, but I didn't know where she stood.
- **Beth's taped hands.** Last chapter Dane wrapped her hands in his torn shirt, and the first-aid box was empty. Now they're "taped." That's a small thing, but I noticed it.
- **The paragraph about why the prisoners come along** ("Nothing would fly in this icing before Tuesday... would bring the big boat's men to Russ's door.") had a lot of reasons in one breath. I got the gist (nobody ashore can guard them, and the kid can name the lawyer, so leaving him puts Russ in danger), but I had to listen hard.
- **"Under their own engine Dane heard a bigger one come up."** "A bigger one" means a bigger engine. I nearly missed it on one listen. "That boat's still out there" just before, and Beth's "Tell me where it is," saved it.

## 6. Verdict: PASS

I could retell the main events: they make up and kiss, then the ferry leaves Sunday night and the strange boat comes for them. I could say what's at stake. No plot fact reached me only through a word I didn't know. I felt the romance. The weak spots are the car-deck search with no stated purpose, "that part's off too," and her still unspoken answer to "I'm in love with you."
