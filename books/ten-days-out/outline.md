### The leak: Ned Haskell

**Motive.** For a year he has signed for the dealer's Thursday reefer knowing something was wrong. The unit always ran, but the weights didn't match the manifest and the sternman was from away. He thought it was shorts, the dealer cheating the state, and the contract is the winter boat. On Day 0 he sees the unit dark as the truck drives on, which is new to him too, and he tries to send Beth home before she can put a hand on it. At 11:15 p.m. Arsenault comes aboard and tells him what it was. If she testifies, Ned goes down as a co-conspirator whatever he believed. Arsenault gives him $9,000 in hundreds for her envelope, to make her a bought witness nobody believes. Ned cuts Tess's yellow tag, adds the cash to Beth's envelope, writes nothing, and reseals the bag with a red tag from his own drawer.

**His reasons, as he gives them in the confession (never as narration).**
- Day 1: "He said they wanted to talk to you about the money."
- Day 2–4: he holds Ashby two days, which is why Doucette searches the empty cottage. At noon on Day 4, while Tess is out on rounds, he lets himself into the store with his own key for an impeller and his phone, and from the back step he gives Ashby up, because Arsenault has started talking about the store with Tess in it.
- Day 5: he guesses the fish house and never tells.
- Day 6: "They said they only wanted to watch you go. With the Coast Guard right there." He works out the hour from her tide question, and gives them his key to the float-light timer box.
- Day 8: the whole boat, and Dane and Russ. Then he hears Arsenault send Doucette with gasoline.

**Means.** His cell, which works only inside the store's booster; he leaves it charging behind Tess's counter. The *Margaret Ann*'s Iridium on the other end. A word at the propane cage when Arsenault is in the store. Tess is never in earshot.

**His one act for her.** Day 8, 11:02 p.m., channel 9: "Bethie. Water side."
