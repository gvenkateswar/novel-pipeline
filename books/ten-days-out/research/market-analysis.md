## 3. Where the open-door line sits on KDP

- ALLi (first published March 2025, updated July 2026): the "Adult content" flag ("the dungeon") hides a book from default search; it is nearly undiscoverable even to the author's existing readers. Amazon publishes no trigger list. Reported causes: suggestive cover art, certain keywords, the phrase "content warning" (some authors say "content notes"). Categorising as erotica does not itself trigger the flag but removes access to Amazon ads. Since June 2023 authors can self-flag explicit content at setup. The article gives no dated individual reports. https://selfpublishingadvice.org/erotica-authors/
- Anecdote from one author blog: after February 2024 policy changes he could no longer upload erotica and moved a series to Lulu. Details unclear, and the post's date is inconsistent (likely edited). https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/24598066-as-of-feb-2024-amazon-kdp-has-changed-their-content-pol
- Not verified: the KDP Community thread and a Medium guide on blocked erotica both failed to load. I have no 2025-2026 first-hand report of an open-door (non-erotica) romance being flagged or not flagged. Open-door interior with a PG-13 cover is consistent with ALLi's list of triggers (cover, keywords), but that is inference, not a tested finding. Recommend the author review KBoards / Facebook groups manually or ask KDP support.
