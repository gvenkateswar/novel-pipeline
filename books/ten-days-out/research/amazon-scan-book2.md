*Collected October 27, 2026, 7:45–8:15 AM ET, through the author's own Chrome (personal profile), signed in to Amazon.com. Top 50 Kindle bestsellers in 12 romance categories the October 26 scan did not cover: 600 books, each checked on its product page. Same definitions as `research/neutral/amazon-scan.md` so the two tables can be read together.*

## How to read this

- **Indie** = no publisher listed on the product page.
- **Recent indie** = indie and released April 27 – October 27, 2026.
- **Indie <300** = indie books with fewer than 300 ratings (lesser-known authors reaching the list).
- **Short indie** = indie and 200 pages or fewer. A 30,000-word novella is roughly 110–130 Kindle pages.
- **Median pp** = median print length of all 50.
- **KSR at #25** = Kindle Store rank of the #25 book. Lower is more sales. For comparison with the October table, Kindlepreneur's calculator puts rank ~300 at roughly 300+ sales/day, ~500 at ~200/day, ~1,000 at ~100/day, ~3,000 at ~40/day.
- **In a series** = product page shows "Book N of M". **Book 1s** = how many of those are first books.

**Limits:** one snapshot on one morning; late October inflates Halloween titles. Kindle+ membership couldn't be read reliably and is omitted. The three Amazon "Historical Romance" books with no publisher line may be small presses rather than self-published.

## What the numbers say

**1. The spicy money categories are open to indies but not to short or unknown books.** Mafia (31 indie, 28 recent) and Billionaires (35 indie, 23 recent) have the strongest sales of anything scanned in either round: the #25 book sits around Kindle rank 300. But only 3 and 4 of those indie books have under 300 ratings, only 1 and 0 are 200 pages or fewer, and the median length is nearly 390 pages. The top of Mafia on October 27 was Nicole Fox, Jagger Cole, K.C. Crowne, Luna Mason, L.J. Shen: established indie names with 300–680-page books released in the last six weeks. April's claim that this is where sales concentrate holds. The brief's 30,000-word, first-book constraint is what doesn't fit here.

**2. Short indie books in open-door categories are almost all one kind of book.** Across 600 slots there are 17 indie books of 200 pages or fewer. Leaving out one cozy mystery and one Christian military series entry, they are: Imani Jay (three titles, 27–156 pages, omegaverse and curvy-girl Bratva), "Knotted by" omegaverse shorts, a "Shared by the Kingpins" why-choose, a 36-page stepsister Halloween short, Theodora Taylor's orc romance, and five "neglected wife" or "cheating husband" betrayal books of 137–159 pages. Short spicy books that chart are either high-heat kink or trope-stamped betrayal dramas, released fast under pen names, and most are at heat 5 rather than the brief's 3–4.

**3. Second Chances is the most open spicy category by the counts, and the counts are misleading.** 34 of 50 indie, all recent, 18 under 300 ratings, 5 short. But the list is dominated by a single flood: "My Cowboy Husband Neglected Me, Left Our Baby for...", "My Doctor Husband Neglected Me...", "Neglected and Humiliated by My Doctor Husband", from three or four pen names (Lira Rain, CM Maya, Nora Halston), several of them 900–1,500 pages long, published weekly. These are the same "neglected wife" titles the October scan noticed under Women's Fiction. The category is open because it has been taken over by a rapid-release content operation, not because readers are waiting for a new voice.

**4. Multicultural & Interracial has the most short indie books (6) and the most low-review indie books (11), and the weakest sales.** The #25 book is at Kindle rank 2,922 (roughly 40 sales a day), far below every other spicy category. The top is Ana Huang's backlist (Twisted and Kings of Sin, 60,000–150,000 ratings each), then omegaverse and BWWM shorts. This is the category where a South Asian romance would list. One South Asian-authored book appears in all 600 slots: Swati MH's *In Another Lifetime* (#17, traditionally published, 170 ratings, released October 13, 2026). No self-published South Asian romance reached any top 50 scanned in either round.

**5. Historical Romance is closed to indies.** 3 of 50 with no publisher listed. The one short book is Cara Devlin's *House of Peril*, the historical cozy mystery the October scan already flagged, which cross-lists here. April's "South Asian historical romance vacuum" is real on the supply side, but the category it would list in has no indie door at any length.

**6. Sports, Workplace, Small Town, New Adult, Alpha Male, Western, Military** sit in between: moderate indie share, strong series behaviour (38–43 of 50 in series for Sports, Small Town, New Adult, Western), almost no short books, and only 1–5 low-review indie authors each. A new name with one novella would be competing on backlist it doesn't have.
