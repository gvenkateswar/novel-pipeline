# Judge: Buyer persona, opus, p2 A vs B

1. **Winner: A**

2. **Why:** In A she's noticing him, and letting him notice her, from the ramp all the way to her kitchen ("I'll try to look at you more," the wrist she keeps on the line, the sweater folded twice), so it reads like a romance. B has the funnier lines, but by the kitchen he's talking to the window about forecasts and I'd stopped feeling the two of them in the room.

3. **Where A lost me:** About 11%, the walk aft: "a hand down the stern chain across the transom to feel it bar-tight, a kick at the ice on the ring buoy's bracket, past the quarter ladder with its rungs gone white, and up the outside ladder to the wheelhouse..." That's a list of chores right after the bricks, and my thumb sped up. It picked up again with the man dragging his heel, and I didn't slow down after he showed up on the ramp.

4. **Where B lost me:** About 93%, in the kitchen: "He told the window the forecast she'd heard before light and the marine forecast she'd heard after it, and asked the window whether forecasts out here were considered advisory." Then "Twice he took the phone out and put it back without reading it. She watched his hands do that, and then she watched the harbor, because the harbor was her business." He's in her house and she's looking at the harbor. A put a small spark in that same moment and B let it go flat. I also slowed at about 35%, where she lists the voices she imitates ("Tess on the storm net. Russ saying 'somebody.'"). That's the narration describing her, not her doing anything with him.

5. **Confidence: medium.** The two chapters share the same plot almost beat for beat, and B's jokes (the plumber who "chained the way he billed," the "bucket of steam," "Don't ma'am me. I charge extra for it") are better than anything A has. But I read this for the couple, and A gives me more of them.

6. **Same-author check: Yes.** Same scenes in the same order, many of the same lines word for word ("Those boots are for a different ocean," "You're welcome." / "I didn't say anything." / "I noticed.").
