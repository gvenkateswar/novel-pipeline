# Judge: Buyer persona (sonnet), Ch 01 p1-A vs p1-B

1. Winner: A

2. Why: I wanted to be on the boat with these two, and A gives me more of him looking at her hands and her pretending not to look back (the wrist, the counter joke in the kitchen, "I'll try to look at you more") while B keeps the same scenes but cuts most of the heat.

3. Where A lost me: "Russ was the *Second Wind* and four hundred traps. Ned was the *North Reach*. The summer man was a varnished picnic boat with her father's lines under the paint. She had a hull for everyone she knew..." (~68%). It's a pause to explain her head when the couple is standing right there. I didn't quit, but my thumb sped up.
