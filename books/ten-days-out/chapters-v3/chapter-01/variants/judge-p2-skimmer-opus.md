# Judge: Skimmer (opus), p2 A vs B

1. **Winner: A**

2. **Why:** I was tired, and A got the two of them grabbing each other on the ramp faster, with fewer stops along the way, and it kept the heat going into the kitchen ("she let him catch her watching him back once... She folded the sweater twice"), where B just turns into him reading the weather to a window.

3. **Where A lost me:** about 11%. "Then she went aft the way her feet always went: a hand down the stern chain across the transom to feel it bar-tight, a kick at the ice on the ring buoy's bracket, past the quarter ladder with its rungs gone white, and up the outside ladder to the wheelhouse, where she stopped on the top rung with her shoulder against the breaker box on the aft bulkhead and listened." It's a walking checklist, and my thumb sped up. It slowed down again at the limping man.

4. **Where B lost me:** about 36%. "Other people's voices were a thing she did, alone, at the sink, on the dark end of the night run. Tess on the storm net. Russ saying 'somebody.' The summer woman who called the ferry 'the little boat'..." The marshal has just walked up to her and the book stops to tell me her hobby. I skimmed down to "Wade Pelletier." I skimmed again at about 15% ("Up the outside ladder. Steel, iced. The gray box of the floods' breakers...") and at about 93% ("He told the window the forecast she'd heard before light and the marine forecast she'd heard after it..."). That last one is the couple alone in her kitchen, and it reads like a summary.

5. **Confidence: low.** B has the better jokes ("chained the way he billed, generously and with gaps," "Don't ma'am me. I charge extra for it"), and on a less tired night they might have won it. The scenes are the same, beat for beat. A just stops less often.

6. **Same-author check: yes.** Same scenes, same lines of dialogue, same images. One looks like a revision of the other.
