# Judge report: p3, Skimmer, Sonnet

1. Winner: B

2. Why: B gets to the dark reefer box and the open crate faster and doesn't make me wade through a row of chained trucks first, so my thumb stayed slow longer.

3. Where A lost me: the opening chain-check run, at about 3%: "Handle down. Chain bar-tight. Hook seated all the way in the D-ring, where it couldn't walk out on the first sea. The propane truck was right. The co-op's bait truck was right..." It reads like a checklist of trucks, and the thumb sped up. It picked up again at "It came aboard quiet." A also lost me a second time, briefly, at about 65%, in the paragraph "Russ was the *Second Wind* and four hundred traps and a radio voice like a man reading a will..." That one is a list of who sits in which boat.

4. Where B lost me: at about 65%, in "Russ Whitcomb was the *Second Wind*, four hundred traps and a radio he talked to like a dog. Ned was the *North Reach*..." It is the same boat-roster paragraph. I also slowed at about 92%, at "Twice he started to say something and stopped. The wind leaned on the house. She knew how it would go, Ned up there with his coffee going cold..." That is waiting-in-the-kitchen filler.

5. Confidence: low. The two are nearly the same chapter. A has the funnier lines (the herring truck, the plumber who chains the way he bills, Beth doing voices at the sink), and a better reader than me tonight might prefer it. I picked B because it is leaner at the start.

6. Same-author check: Yes. Same scenes, same beats, same lines of dialogue, with A more ornamented and B more trimmed.

Calibration note: neither one reads like A1. Both have a hook in the first page and a couple in a room together by the end. In both, the procedure is short and sits inside a scene with someone watching.
