# Judge: p3, Buyer persona, opus

1. **Winner: A**

2. **Why:** In A she keeps the limping man to herself, steps right over his track with the marshal beside her, and only gives him up at the window when the skiff turns up and she has no choice. I wanted to see how he'd take that. In B she tells him on the dock straight away, and after that the two of them are just working the case together.

3. **Where A lost me:** "Hood up. Oars shipped. Not fueling. Not fishing. Facing the hill." (~93%). It's a bit of a list and I skimmed it, but "Sitting down, he had no limp to show her" pulled me right back. It didn't really lose me.

4. **Where B lost me:** "Okay. One man, down the dock and not back up, which means a boat. Which boat?" (~92%). That's him doing his job out loud, and her secret is gone the moment she says it. "They watched a long while." (~96%) is where my thumb moved. B's last line, "his right hand gone to his hip," is a better hook than A's, but by then the scene had already gone slack for me.

5. **Confidence: medium.** B has the stronger closing image and the nice line about her words going into a stranger's inbox "in his spelling". A has her hiding something from him, which is the thing that makes me want chapter 2.

6. **Same-author check: yes.** Same voice and same details (the barometer, the dollar bet, the arm across her). They're plainly two cuts of one draft.
