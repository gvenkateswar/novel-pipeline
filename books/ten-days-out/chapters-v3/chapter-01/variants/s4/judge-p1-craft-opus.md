# Judge: p1, Craft persona (opus)

Read: blind/p1-A.md, blind/p1-B.md, docs/calibration.md. Nothing else.

1. **Winner: B.**

2. **Why:** In B, Beth lies to him first ("Nobody.") and the man in the skiff gets the line that turned my stomach ("which is what I'd do if I wanted to know which house was yours"), so I go into chapter 2 scared for her and annoyed with her. A hands over the heel straight away and Reyes answers like a man filling in a form.

3. **Where A lost me:** "Okay. One man, down the dock and not back up, which means a boat. Which boat?" (~89%). That's a marshal running a checklist out loud, the couple doing business, and it's the closest either version gets to A1. A gets me back with "touched her nowhere else" and the hand going to his hip, but "Beth said nothing. They watched a long while." (~95%) just sits there.

4. **Where B lost me:** "She stayed. He went on talking, low, and she answered none of it." (~96%). His talk gets summed up instead of shown, right where I wanted to hear what he sounds like when he's frightened for her. The ending is also softer than A's: "He was already going, past her and through the house toward the back" has no hand on the hip, so the closing image carries less threat. And the sudden switch from "Reyes" to "Dane" in the kitchen made me blink.

5. **Confidence: low-medium.** A has the better callback ("in his spelling, before she had her coat off," which ties back to her father in blue pen) and the sharper cliffhanger. B has better sentences ("not fueling, not fishing. He sat facing the hill. Sitting, he had no walk to show her."), the withholding beat that gives the two of them friction, and stakes that point at her house. I'd take B's middle with A's last line.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. The rhythm, the habit of watching hands, and Reyes's dry hedging are the same in both, and only the order of the beats changes.
