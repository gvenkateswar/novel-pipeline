# Chapter 05 issues (read: chapters/chapter-05/draft.md)

**Issue 1**
- Problem: The red tag is missing from S1 and its mechanics are inconsistent with Ch 04. Ch 04 S3 knots it "round his wrist over the cuff of his fleece." In S1 Beth is wearing that fleece and Dane's sleeves are pushed up, yet the tag is never mentioned, so the Ch 06 and Ch 07 plant has no S1 anchor. In S4 the fleece and shirt come off and the tag turns up on a bare wrist "where the sleeve had dragged out from under it." That contradicts the tag sitting over the cuff, and nothing says how it left the fleece cuff when Dane gave her the fleece.
- Location: S1: "Beth sat in Dane's fleece with the sleeves past her hands and her bare feet on the oven door." / S4: "The red tag rode loose on his bare wrist where the sleeve had dragged out from under it."
- Suggested fix: Add one clause in S1 showing the red tag on Dane's bare wrist as he feeds the stove (slid down over his hand when the fleece came off), and change the S4 clause to "The red tag rode on his bare wrist" so it matches.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: Beth tells Dane something he already knows. Dane rode up in Tess's truck at two, in the back with the cylinders, and he stood at the Ashby window beside her ("I've got the shed and the truck"). The "third thing" reads as a delivery, not a cost, and Dane's "Three things you didn't count" then rests on information he held already.
- Location: "The truck in the drive tonight. That was Tess's truck. Rode up the north road in it at two."
- Suggested fix: Make the cost Beth saying it as an admission and pointing it at Tess, not giving the truck's owner. For example: "That truck in the drive. I'm putting Tess's name next to it, out loud, to you." Cut "Rode up the north road in it at two," which he knows because he rode in it.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: Two consecutive quoted paragraphs from Beth, the second with no tag and no action. It reads as a new speaker (Dane), and the second line can't be placed until the following paragraph. The chapter-wide rule is that every line is attributed.
- Location: "\"I didn't give you either one of them Friday night. I knew it when I said *you*.\""
- Suggested fix: Merge it into the previous Beth paragraph, or tag it ("she said, and then, "I didn't give you...").

**Issue 4**
- Problem: Beth's reason for walking into town in daylight is never given, and neither is the exposure. The beats say S2 "frames it as hiding" and that she goes "to ask to stay hidden," which is what lets the S5 reversal ("tomorrow I go to the store... and I watch who watches me buy it") turn. The draft jumps from her dressing at Ashby to the bell with no reason. Dane then stands on an open dock in sight of the harbor and the *Margaret Ann*, and the page does not register it.
- Location: "The bell went over the door at half past one, and Tess didn't look up from the ladle." / "Out front, past the steam, Dane waited on the store dock."
- Suggested fix: Add one clause of Beth's interior in S2 giving her reason (to ask Tess to keep Ashby off the net) and that she knows the dock can be seen from the dragger's mooring.

**Issue 5**
- Problem: Tess's logistics for Ned's pot don't work. Ned is aboard the *North Reach* at the slip, in town. Tess says she carries his pot down, then gathers both pots and drives them up the north road to Ashby. The alibi line ("I carry it down") reads as a habit that today's blocking contradicts.
- Location: "He hasn't come off that boat since he brought you the diesel. I carry it down." / "She lidded both pots, gathered them into her arms and came round the counter, and her hip was on the door and the bell already going."
- Suggested fix: Have Tess say Haskell's goes down after Ashby ("Haskell's on the way back") in her order at the door, or have her carry only one pot out to the truck.

**Issue 6**
- Problem: Timeline inside S1. The scene opens in the morning after the 7:10 gut and the 7:45 stove ("You were blue an hour ago"), then jumps to noon with no marker. The jeans hung on the stove rail since the morning are still "damp at the seams" four hours later, next to a lit stove.
- Location: "\"You were blue an hour ago.\"" / "Beth took her jeans off the rail, damp at the seams, and began to put them on."
- Suggested fix: Mark the passage of time before the noon truck ("By noon the squalls had thinned") and change "damp at the seams" to "stiff and dry."

**Issue 7**
- Problem: The gun disappears. In S3 Dane draws it ("Dane had the gun out") and the Glock rides on his belt. In S4 she undoes his belt and strips him, and the gun is never holstered, set down or mentioned. In S5 only the jacket and notebook are placed.
- Location: "She got her hands on his belt. Her fingers were stiff from the bar and the buckle beat her twice, and he laughed against her ribs"
- Suggested fix: Place the gun with one clause when he hangs the jacket (on the bench, within reach), in S4 before the kit check.

**Issue 8**
- Problem: Emotion stated in narration instead of shown. The hand that waits already carries it, and the clause explains it.
- Location: "and she let it wait, because nobody had ever waited for her like that, and then she took his wrist and moved his hand under." / "She was tired of smaller."
- Suggested fix: Cut "because nobody had ever waited for her like that" and end the S5 paragraph on "Here." or on a physical action (her feet on the planks), not "She was tired of smaller."

**Issue 9**
- Problem: A forbidden contrast construction in narration ("He didn't X. He Y."), used as the S5 opening beat.
- Location: "He didn't ask her anything. He let it be hers to go on with."
- Suggested fix: Replace with a single physical sentence ("He waited, looking at the roof boards.") and cut the contrast.

**Issue 10**
- Problem: Repeated phrasing that has become a tic inside one chapter: "let it be hers" appears in S4 and S5, "let it sit" and "let it lie" appear within a few paragraphs of each other, and "Nobody ..." opens six sentences. The bar paragraph also states the same image twice in a row: "fist in his belt," then "his belt in her fist."
- Location: "and she shut her eyes and breathed it in and let it be hers." / "He was quiet a long time, and she let it sit." / "She let it lie." / "She reached back and got her fist in his belt."
- Suggested fix: Keep "let it be hers" once; rewrite "let it sit" and "let it lie" as actions; drop the first "fist in his belt" sentence and keep the bible's line 4 as the only statement.

**Issue 11**
- Problem: The answer to the "not yet" callback is ambiguous when heard. "No" after "Is it still a not yet?" reads as a refusal for a beat before the report sentence clarifies. This is the payoff for "One I can't afford" and "Then save up," and the sample reader and listener will hear a refusal.
- Location: "\"No,\" he said. \"When this is over, I report this.\""
- Suggested fix: Replace "No" with "Not any more" so the first word answers the question as she asked it.

**Issue 12**
- Problem: Clock logic after midnight. S5 is set past midnight (the tide near the top, about 1:45), so "tomorrow" is a calendar day away, but the store trip it promises is the morning low the same day (Ch 06 s2). "That morning" also gives the grand jury a morning sitting that the bible does not set.
- Location: "\"Tomorrow I go to the store and buy what I need, and I watch who watches me buy it.\"" / "You're in Portland that morning in front of the grand jury"
- Suggested fix: Cut "that morning" from Dane's line. For the final line, an orchestrator ruling is needed (the beats bind this wording): change "Tomorrow" to "At the morning low" or leave it and log it as spoken usage.

**Issue 13**
- Problem: The narration explains the "I'll do what I can" beat, which the beats say the page doesn't explain, and which World rule 9 says must stay readable two ways. A reader is told it is a non-yes.
- Location: "She had heard men say yes. She knew how long it took them, and this came up short by exactly that much. She let it lie."
- Suggested fix: Cut the middle sentence and leave "She let it lie."

**Issue 14**
- Problem: Tess's cause is stated three times in about ten lines ("I didn't put you on the air" / "I kept you off the air" / "I kept you off it anyway"). The optional thumb clause is a full sentence here and adds a second grievance (Beth with the marshal) beside the single stated cause the beats bind.
- Location: "\"I kept you off the air, Calloway,\" Tess said. \"I saw your hand on that man's neck Saturday, and I kept you off it anyway. And you let me find you by your smoke.\""
- Suggested fix: Cut the thumb sentence and "I kept you off the air, Calloway," and keep "You let me find you by your smoke" as the cause.

**Issue 15**
- Problem: Doubled tags in S2. An action beat and "Tess said" both attribute the same line, repeatedly, in a two-person exchange. This makes the fight stilted, and the beats allowed either one.
- Location: "Tess set the ladle across the pot. \"I put you to bed the winter your mother got on the Bangor bus,\" Tess said."
- Suggested fix: Keep the action beat as the tag and cut "Tess said" on every line that already follows a Tess action in the same paragraph.

**Issue 16**
- Problem: Unclear antecedent. Socks are never named in S4 (the S1 "socks" is the only use), so "his second one" cannot be placed on the first read, and TTS gives the listener no help.
- Location: "Dane wrung his second one out over the coal hod and kept on wringing it"
- Suggested fix: "Dane wrung his second sock out over the coal hod" (the motif cap for socks leaves room).

**Issue 17**
- Problem: Unlogged new facts and a recycled detail in the S2 and S5 interiors. The S2 paragraph adds a backstory beat (a boy from Pruett at fifteen, the Friday boat through high school) where the beats call for one interior line about the counter looking up at her steps. S5 reuses Ch 01's "ice in his beard" and Ch 02's "couldn't swim a stroke" in one sentence.
- Location: "From here Tess had seen her come home off the *North Reach* every night for eleven years, and off the Friday boat all through high school, and once at fifteen with a boy from Pruett Beth had sworn nobody knew about." / "Ned on the stern of the *North Reach* in the sleet, with ice in his beard. Ned who couldn't swim a stroke and had run that boat since she was in diapers."
- Suggested fix: Cut S2's paragraph to "The counter looked straight up the slope at Beth's back steps. It always had." In S5, drop "with ice in his beard."
