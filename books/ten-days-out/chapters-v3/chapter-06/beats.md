## Scene 6.1 — The fish house, the top of the tide

**Want:** Beth wants not to be found, and then she wants to know where he went. Dane wants back in before she knows he was gone. The reader learns Beth's want at the damper.

**Subtext:** He went up for sky to report, saw the skiff from the ledge, and came down fast through the wet weed to get back to her. That is why he's wet, and she can't know it. She knows the lie for a lie and keeps it, because tonight she chose him and he kept Tess's name out.
