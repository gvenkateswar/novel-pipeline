**Issue 2**
- Problem: "We go down to look" reads as an instruction for now. It comes straight after Beth stares at the dragger. The next paragraphs take them along the riprap to the ferry, not to the ramp head, so the plan appears to be dropped. The plan is for tomorrow's first light.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: The heat rules allow no more than two sentences in a row about who touches what. Two runs break the limit. The first is four sentences of hands and ribs. The second is five sentences of undressing, from "He undid" through "did those too".
- Location: "He kissed her. His hands were cold from the boot. She took him by the wrists and brought his hands up under her shirt to her ribs to warm them. He drew a breath through his teeth at the warmth of her." and "He undid her shirt from the top, one button at a time, and his hard fingertips caught on her skin at every one. At the fourth she lost patience and did the rest herself. Her shirt went on the planks. Her bra went after it; he got lost at the hooks. She reached back and did those too."
- Suggested fix: Cut the run to two sentences each and put a sound, a line or a thought between them. For example, drop "Her shirt went on the planks" and "He drew a breath through his teeth at the warmth of her", and put a spoken line after the buttons.

**Issue 4**

- Location: "He gave them to her going down off the rock, one at a time, the way he'd read her a route." and "Then he was inside her, slow, the way she'd asked for."

## Rulings on the writer's seven flags

1. **"He'd had Pike's answers from before light."** Accept as a text, not a call. The beat sheet (6.4, movement 1, item 1) asks for exactly this clause, so the one-call count (s3) stands. Caution: it implies Dane left her side unobserved before light and quotes Pike verbatim with no screen. Replace "from before light" with something that puts the arrival on the ledge, for example "Pike's answers had come in on the ledge before light." Location: "He'd had Pike's answers from before light."
2. **Marisol buried in Lawrence.** Accept. The bible's Lowell record (Lowell childhood, Lowell PD) does not conflict, and Ch 01's "The last witness who knew her own town better than I did is buried in it" reads consistently with a Lawrence woman. It is a new fact, so log it. Also see Issue 20.
3. **Dane answering his knock with his own name.** Accept. The beat sheet specifies it ("his name and nothing else"). It also parallels Ch 02's "Evening" through the boards.
4. **Tess moving Ned's phone aside with two fingers.** Accept the action, which leans the phone toward Tess's hands as intended. Change "two fingers" (Issue 15).
5. **Lamp oil at nine forty a gallon.** Reject the number as written. Change to nine fifty and log it (Issue 5).
6. **The blanket nailed over the window.** Accept. It answers the cold reader's stove-and-lantern disbelief. Give it a reason on the page (Issue 19).
7. **"Boxes rode heavy too."** Accept the fact, which the bible requires, but not the wording (Issue 8).
