# Chapter 02 issues (draft.md, tournament rewrite)

**Issue 1**
- Problem: The bridge exchange contradicts the beat sheet's staging and the plot logic. Dane has just stressed that Monday is the last sitting, yet when Ned says "Ten days" he has no reaction. His only line is "At least she's steel," which answers nothing. The collision between Monday (three days) and ten days is the chapter's turn and goes unmarked.
- Location: "'Ten days, if the state gets a barge out.'" / "'At least she's steel.'"
- Suggested fix: Give Dane one short, specific line after "Ten days" that registers Monday (a number or a date, in his long-sentence shape with a dry turn) before "At least she's steel."

**Issue 2**
- Problem: Speaker attribution is ambiguous. Ned speaks in the previous paragraph, so the new paragraph reads as Dane's. "I'll call it in tonight, by the book" also sounds like Dane's procedural voice. The paragraph then puts an action beat for Ned between two quotes, so the reader can't tell who says the first one.
- Location: "'She's not carrying anybody till somebody from the Coast Guard has his hand on that crack. I'll call it in tonight, by the book.' Ned watched the snow come through his light. 'Ten days, if the state gets a barge out.'"
- Suggested fix: Open the paragraph with Ned's action beat ("Ned watched the snow come through his light."), then give the quote, and break "Ten days" into its own tagged line.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: "that crack" has no antecedent. No crack is shown or named on the page. Ned only says water is coming in "up there."
- Location: "'Water's coming in up there.'" and "'...has his hand on that crack.'"
- Suggested fix: Have Ned say "Water's coming in through the plating up there" (or give the crack one clause in the same line) so "that crack" lands.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: The boat-handling sequence contradicts itself. Beth has already "doubled the bow lines" to the pier posts and taken turns round an iron post. When the bridge comes down, she then "threw the bow line across the gap" as if no line were ashore. Ned then backs the boat off while the boat is held by those doubled lines.
- Location: "Beth doubled the bow lines at dusk and again in full dark" / "She threw the bow line across the gap, and the eye dropped over the iron post on the pier the first time."
- Suggested fix: Make the thrown line a new, named line (a long spring or the heavy line) and say the doubled bow lines are slipped or cast off first, so the sequence reads in order.

**Issue 5**
- Problem: Geometry contradiction in the kitchen. Dane stands on the front step "two strides" from Beth, who is on the kitchen floor against the central chimney. A few paragraphs later she has to go "down the hall" to reach him through the open front door. The two cannot both be true. Opening the front door onto a house meant to read empty is also unexplained beyond "so the phone could see the sky."
- Location: "stood on the step, two strides from her, with his back half turned to the house so the phone could see the sky." / "She went down the hall without a light, along the wall where the boards didn't talk, and reached through the open front door and got him by the sleeve."
- Suggested fix: Replace "two strides from her" with a distance that fits the hall ("the length of the hall from her") and have Dane say it as the exact position he'll hold.

**Issue 6**
- Problem: World rule 6 and the beat sheet's numbers table allow only 8:40, 7:40, 1:15 and 11:40 as clock times in the sample chapters ("no other clock time"). "Net's at six" adds another.
- Location: "'Calloway. Net's at six. Where you sleeping?'"
- Suggested fix: Replace with a light or event cue ("Calloway. Net's when the light goes. Where you sleeping?").

**Issue 7**
- Problem: "Where you sleeping?" is Russ's signature line in the bible. Plant ledger: "Russ: 'Where you sleeping?' | 03 | 09". The bible says it means he is watching out for her. Giving the identical line to Tess in Ch 2 spends the plant before Russ says it.
- Location: "'Calloway. Net's at six. Where you sleeping?'"
- Suggested fix: Give Tess a different net question in her headline voice ("Calloway. Aboard or ashore for the roll?") and keep "Where you sleeping" for Russ in Ch 3.

**Issue 8**
- Problem: The danger patter in the cable moment is mostly past tense and summarizing ("It went over us... I heard it go in"). The bible's Dane under fear is present-tense transport patter, continuous, so he knows where she is. "Best thing you've done all day" is a quip in the middle of the danger.
- Location: "'Cable. It went over us and over the bait truck and into the water, I heard it go in. That's my knee by your hip, sorry about the knee... You're very good at lying down. Best thing you've done all day.'"
- Suggested fix: Rewrite the first two sentences in present tense about what is happening now ("It's over us, it's going over the bait truck, it's gone in the water, I can hear it") and keep the "lying down" line.

**Issue 9**
- Problem: Narration states the meaning of Beth's relief, which the bible says must stay unexplained and never explained in narration. The sentence also sums up the danger (flagged in the continuity log) and shades into a maxim. It sets the relief up as "the dread has arrived," not the bible's secret ("glad she didn't have to leave"). The first reading leaves the Ch 6 payoff unsupported.
- Location: "Whatever had been coming since the night freight had come, and she could see it. Her shoulders came down."
- Suggested fix: Cut the first sentence and keep only the physical beat ("Her shoulders came down."), or make the beat tied to leaving (her eyes going to the road end and back).

**Issue 10**
- Problem: Emotion stated rather than shown in the S1 "thought about him as a man." "somebody would get to see it" and "she let the thought stand" name the longing and her handling of it.
- Location: "She thought he would look like that at sixty, and that somebody would get to see it, and she let the thought stand."
- Suggested fix: Replace with one physical, specific observation and a small act (she looks at the snow in his hair and puts a stick in the stove) with no commentary.

**Issue 11**
- Problem: Two ledgered background plants are placed in neither Ch 1 nor this chapter. The tournament rewrite cut them. Ned can't swim: bible plant ledger "01 or 02 (Beth knows)", needed for Ch 9 s5. Ned's gloves: continuity log "Ned's gloves | 02 s4 | 10 s5". With them gone, both payoffs have nothing behind them.
- Location: (absence) Ned's whole exchange: "Where'll you be?" / "Home. Don't tell anybody." / "Nobody but me and God. You need me, nine. Like your dad."
- Suggested fix: Record both as unplaced in the continuity log and place them in Ch 3 or Ch 4 s3, since a Ch 2 placement is now ruled out by the reader notes.

**Issue 12**
- Problem: Numbers ledger drift. The continuity log's Ch 02 ledger (and the "Easter" line) lists Tess's price line as "milk up a quarter." The draft now says D batteries. The ledger entry will be wrong unless updated.
- Location: "'D batteries are up a quarter till the boat runs,' Tess said, loud and quick, 'and the boat's not running. Lamp oil I've got. Sense I'm out of.'"
- Suggested fix: Update the Ch 02 numbers entry to "D batteries up a quarter," and drop "Easter," which no longer appears.

**Issue 13**
- Problem: Unattributed reply. "In for the weather" has no tag, so the speaker could be Tess or Beth. Beth places boats by name and would know the dragger, so it matters. The beat gave the reply to a defined speaker.
- Location: "'That one?'" / "'In for the weather.'"
- Suggested fix: Tag the reply ("Tess said") or have Beth answer, in her dropped-subject voice ("Gloucester boat. In for the weather.").

**Issue 14**
- Problem: Trade-term budget exceeded in the bridge scene. Beat Flag 14 allows at most one unexplained term per scene. This scene carries "bow lines," "dragger," "visitors' mooring," "pilings," "cleat," "bulwark/rail" and "gangway wheels" with no action defining them.
- Location: "Beth doubled the bow lines at dusk and again in full dark" / "Dane had the tail round the cleat and was taking his turns the wrong way."
- Suggested fix: Trim to the terms that carry the action (cleat, gangway) and describe the others in plain English ("the lines holding the boat to the pier," "the Gloucester boat").
