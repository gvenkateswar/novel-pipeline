# Judge: Buyer persona (opus), p2-A vs p2-B

1. **Winner: B**

2. **Why:** In B she actually wants to touch him ("she wanted to put her fingers there and find out whether it was as soft as it looked"), and Tess comes into the kitchen in person with a wad of hundreds and ink on her hand. A gives me the same things through a radio call and keeps the wanting at arm's length.

3. **Where A lost me:** ~23%, "He was still watching when the handheld on Beth's coat opened up, and she thumbed it off nine to the net." Then *"Cutter storm net, this is Marr's, net control. Roll call by house."* I'm lying in bed listening to a radio roll call. The "Margaret Ann" coded bit pulled me back a little. But for half a page it was channels and call signs, and I started skimming. The cable and the bridge coming down woke me up again.

4. **Where B lost me:** ~31%, "Over the bow, the steel ramp the cars drove aboard on ran down from the shore onto the *North Reach*, hung at its seaward end from two towers. The island called it the bridge." I don't need the ferry explained to me while he's a stride behind her in the snow. It's a short stretch and the cable shows up right after, so I didn't really drift.

5. **Confidence: medium.** The last third is nearly word for word the same in both, the brick wall, the door going quiet, "Who knew you were here?", and it's good in both. So it comes down to the first half: B has Tess in the room and a heroine who wants something, and A has a radio.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. The house scene is close to identical, line for line.
