# Judge: Buyer (sonnet), Ch 02 p2-A vs p2-B

1. Winner: A

2. Why: I'd keep reading A because Tess's voice on the radio, the dragger's two silent clicks and the "Allegedly" joke in the dark all fit together, and they make me like Beth and want to be in the room with these two.

3. Where A lost me: "Cable. ... Line. Your line's under my hand, and it's staying there." at about 33%. He's calming her down, and the rope line stops making sense. It reads like a man reciting. It didn't lose me for long. I never skimmed A.

4. Where B lost me: "Batteries went up a dollar at noon. Storm price. They come back down when the boat runs or when I die, whichever's first." at about 15%. This is store business, and it only exists to plant the fold of hundreds. I felt the plot nudging me there. My thumb sped up through Tess's visit. B also sets up "Allegedly" worse, because the net happens off the page ("Tess had done the net by now"), so the joke lands as a line I was told about rather than one I heard.

5. Confidence: medium. The two are about 85% the same text, and the bridge collapse and the doorknock ending are nearly identical. B has the better hair-touching line ("she wanted to put her fingers there") and a smoother cable speech. A has the better middle.

6. Same-author check: Yes. They share the same skeleton, the same lines, and the same habit of ending on "You."
