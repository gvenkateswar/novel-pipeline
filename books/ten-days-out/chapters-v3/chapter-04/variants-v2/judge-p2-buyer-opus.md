1. **Winner: A**

2. **Why:** In A the two of them push against each other the whole time. She keeps the binoculars ("He'll see them move." She kept them.), he keeps his rule ("It's the one rule I've got." / "Keep it from there."), Russ gets the "That's three" laugh, and when Dane starts quizzing her about the money she notices it: "So that was what this was. She gave it to him anyway." That one line made me feel the question was a small betrayal. In B the same questions are just questions.

3. **Where A lost me:** At about 84%, the Monday opening: "At dead low the bar lay dry from the Ashby shore toward Calloway Cove, the cove on the east shore that carried her family's name, a long way round from her house on the harbor. Gravel and cobble, hers end to end. Ashby had one road out." That's map talk while I wait for her to get in the water. A smaller snag at about 18%: two "Russ said" lines in a row ("I'm not a complicated man." / "Nothing's flying, either," Russ said), which made me think a line had been dropped.

4. **Where B lost me:** At about 45%: "'Walk me through it,' he said. He was looking past her, out the door at the drive. 'Your pay. Counter to teller.'" Right after her father's boat, he's taking a statement, and nothing on the page shows that it hurts her. Then I skimmed the envelopes, slips and red and yellow seals until she ties the tag on his wrist.

5. **Confidence: low.** The two are nearly line for line the same. B reads smoother and has the better flashlight joke ("from people who were annoyed with me"). A has more of the couple getting under each other's skin, and that's what I keep reading for.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. Same scenes, same lines, mostly the same sentences, and they differ only in a few cuts and tags.
