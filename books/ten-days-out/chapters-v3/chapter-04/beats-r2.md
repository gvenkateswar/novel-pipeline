## Scene 4.2 — Ashby, the generator shed: the near-miss (Sunday afternoon into dusk)

**Threat:** the generator is dead and they're on the pad bleeding it. A man comes down the one drive, tries the house, walks into the shed and lays his bare hand on the housing to feel for warm. Beth is a foot below his hand in the dark, and Dane has a gun on him he's been ordered not to use.

**Setting:** The Ashby generator shed on its concrete pad below the house, Sunday afternoon (Day 3), the light going an hour before sunset.
- The set ran dry at noon and sucked air. The last of the fuel can is in it.
- The door is propped on its frozen track. It can't be shut quietly, and a door shut that was open is a sign.
- The shed is narrow. The set fills the middle of it, and behind the housing is the dark. Through the door the long drive runs straight up to the spine of the north road.
- **One clause, for the plot auditor:** the gale off three sides of water has scoured the point to frozen gravel and ledge; no snow holds on it, and there are no tracks to read.
- Sensory anchor: diesel stinging in the cracks of her knuckles.

**Characters present:** Beth, Dane. Then Ned at dusk. The hooded man is never named.

**Want:** Beth wants the set running before dark, and later she wants him. Dane wants her alive, and then, inside the aftermath, he wants to know about the money. The reader learns Dane's second want at "Who's alone with it?"
