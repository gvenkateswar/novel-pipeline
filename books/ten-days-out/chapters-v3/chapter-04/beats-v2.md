## Scene 4.1 — Lookout Hill, then the islanders' path

**Threat:** a man is searching her house in daylight, looks straight up at the hill they're lying on, and then goes aboard the boat that's hunting her.

**Setting:** The bare granite top of Lookout Hill, Sunday (Day 3), first light. They are flat in blueberry stems. Then the islanders' path down through close spruce. Sensory anchor: the cold coming up out of the wet granite into her breastbone by inches.

**Characters present:** Beth, Dane. Then Russ. The hooded man is seen, never named.

**Want:** Beth wants to see him and not be seen. Dane wants her off the skyline the moment the man's aboard. Russ wants to be the first to know something for once. The reader learns Beth's want at "She moved nothing but her eyes."

**Surface action:**
1. **Open:** flat on wet granite, Sunday, first light, with his binoculars. The hood comes up the path from the store side: a right boot set down clean, the left heel dragging. Beth doesn't say "Him." She puts a finger on the rock between them, and Dane goes still.
2. **The latch at the first try.** He circles the house once, front and back, goes into the mudroom, and the kitchen door gives at the first try: one lift, then his shoulder. Her interior offers two readings and doesn't settle: she showed one person how that door goes, on her own back step on Friday; or he taught himself on her door that same night. Keep the current text's paragraph.
3. **Dane, two lines at most, no commentary:** what the man's hands are doing, and that a small house won't take him long. Then quiet.
4. **His face.** On her back step he pushes the hood back and turns his face up to the hill. Young, wide, pale, a face she doesn't know. She places men by their boats, and she has no slot for this one. He keeps looking at the top of the hill, as if somebody had told him where.
   - **New, one interior sentence (the reader flagged the brazenness):** he doesn't care who on the island sees his face, and that's the part she likes least.
5. **The dragger.** He runs the skiff out from under the store dock. A man on **the dragger's afterdeck** takes his painter, and the skiff goes up the *Margaret Ann*'s side on a line.
6. **The one screen (Ch 04's only call).** Dane types once, flat on his stomach. The reply lands, and **he turns the screen to her himself**:

   - **The cable.** Dane asks, walking: did that cable on the slip tower have help? Russ: somebody'd have to beat the rust to it; the tower's been on the district's list since Labor Day.

## Scene 4.3 — Monday, dead low: the gut

**Setting:** The gravel bar from the Ashby shore toward Calloway Cove, Monday (Day 4), dead low, early light between squalls. Clarify the map in one clause: **the cove on the east shore that her family was named for**, with the fish house at its head, a long way from her house on the harbor. Sensory anchor: cobbles knocking under the wash, and the long suck as it draws back.

**Characters present:** Beth, Dane.

**Want:** Beth wants a back door, which means finding where the gut drops while she can still see the bottom. Dane wants to walk it first. The reader learns Beth's want in the first paragraph.

**Surface action:**
1. **Open:** Monday, said once; "a week before the Monday that mattered in Portland" (keep). Ashby has one road out; she wants a back door.
