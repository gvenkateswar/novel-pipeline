# Chapter 04 issues (draft.md)

**Issue 1**
- Problem: Length. A hand count (no shell was available to run `wc -w`) puts the draft near 3,000 words against a 2,650 target and a 2,438–2,862 band. By my count S1 is about 700 (target 550), S3 about 910 (target 800), S5 about 590 (target 470); S2 and S4 are near target. The beat sheet's trim order (S4 first, then S2) would cut the two scenes that are already on target. Run `wc -w` to confirm.
- Location: whole chapter, from "# Chapter Four: Glassing the Harbor"
- Suggested fix: Run the orchestrator word check. If over 2,862, trim S1 (the latch paragraph and the girl-picture simile), S3 (the *Ruthie C* paragraph) and S5 (the cold paragraph) rather than S4 and S2.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: The weather contradicts the bible and the chapter. Day 3 is "storm easing to a gale", with spray hissing even on the hill. Russ says "Minute it lays down", and wind is "up in the tops" in S2. Yet S1 opens on a flat harbor, a skiff is rowed through the moorings with no sea, and no wind or spray is on the page.
- Location: "Below them the harbor lay flat and gray and exact as a chart."
- Suggested fix: Replace "flat" with a gale detail (white tops, spray hissing even up here) and give the skiff a visible sea.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: More than three named people in a scene. S1 has Beth, Dane, Pike and Q, if screen text counts. S2 has Beth, Dane, Russ and Tess, because Tess is named four times in Russ's speech. The beat sheet exempted both, but the standing rule is three named people per scene.
- Location: S1: "*Pike: Q says hands off the vessel. Observe only. Keep witness in place.*" S2: "\"Who's emergency management on this island?\" Russ said. \"Tess is. Since town meeting."
- Suggested fix: S1: drop "Pike:" from the screen line, or write "Q says" as "the office says". S2: have Russ say "she" or "the store" after naming Tess once, and keep the "Tess" count in the scene to a single use.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: Tess's tenure contradicts the bible. Tess came at 22 and is 46, so she arrived 24 years ago, when Beth was nine. She has run the store nineteen years, since Beth was fourteen. She cannot have written on her hand "since before Beth could read it upside down across the counter."
- Location: "and she always had, since before Beth could read it upside down across the counter."
- Suggested fix: Cut the clause, or change it to something true, such as "as long as Beth had been buying milk from her".

**Issue 5**
- Problem: A new Ned tell. The flat order is "nothing new against him" and "no beat of anyone noticing" Ned. Dane keeps the light on Ned and looks at him over it as Ned announces he is going back aboard. This is a suspicion beat aimed at the real leak, and it comes in the scene that plants the alibi and the look-back.
- Location: "Dane kept the light where it was and looked at Ned over it."
- Suggested fix: Cut the sentence and let Dane keep holding the light.

**Issue 6**
- Problem: Ned's speech drops subjects, which is Beth's tag, in his weather and news lines. The bible says Ned keeps his subjects.
- Location: "\"Bethie. Glass is coming up. Be down to a breeze by morning, and squalls in it.\"" and "\"The pump's eating impellers. I'm going back aboard. Staying aboard till the barge.\""
- Suggested fix: Restore subjects: "Glass is coming up. It'll be down to a breeze by morning, with squalls in it." and "I'm going back aboard, and I'll be staying aboard till the barge."

**Issue 7**
- Problem: Beth sealing the fare bag conflicts with the record. CASE FACTS has Ned resealing the store bag with a red tag from his own wheelhouse drawer. Ch 01 has Ned carrying both bags up the ladder after Beth was sent home. Beth carrying a spare strip of the same red tags "since Thursday" and sealing the fare bag after the last run gives her the same means as Ned. It also muddies the Ch 09 "red from Ned's drawer" reveal and makes the fare-bag seal Beth's act. Ned's resealing red still works, but "that's the boat's" now reads partly as Beth's.
- Location: "She had the strip in her coat pocket, where it had been since Thursday: spare tags for the *North Reach*'s fare bag, which she sealed after the last run."
- Suggested fix: Make the strip a district stock item Beth happens to have pocketed, or have Ned seal the fare bag. Keep Beth's knowledge to "Fare bag's red." Do not make her the sealer.

**Issue 8**
- Problem: The pouch sequence is stated differently from the bible. CASE FACTS and the bible's walk-ons have a zipped pouch holding both bags riding the first boat, which Beth hands down at Pruett. Beth's own bible line is "Pouch is zipped, tagged and going to the bank." Here the courier zips the bags into the pouch at Pruett.
- Location: "The boat carried the store bag over with the fare bag, and at Pruett the courier zipped them both into the pouch."
- Suggested fix: "...and the pouch rode over zipped, and at Pruett I handed it down to the courier."

**Issue 9**
- Problem: Overclaim that contradicts Ch 03. Dane left the phone face up and Beth read it (Ch 03 S1). Dane also stood with the phone to his ear and his face turned up while she stood beside him on the porch (Ch 03 S3). "Every screen" since the ramp is untrue.
- Location: "Since the ramp he had angled every screen away from her."
- Suggested fix: "Until yesterday he had angled every screen away from her."

**Issue 10**
- Problem: Staging in S5 is contradictory. Dane has gone past her toward the gut and offered to walk it first. In the next beat she is already in to the shins. She then tells him to "Stand there", and he stops "at the edge of the dry bar", which is behind them both.
- Location: "Dane went past her toward it. He walked every place before she went there, and he had since the ramp. \"Let me. These are waterproof.\" / She was already in to the shins."
- Suggested fix: Put Dane at her shoulder, not past her, when he says "Let me", then have her step into the wash ahead of him.

**Issue 11**
- Problem: The narration explains the joke after Beth lands it, to readers who just read Ch 03.
- Location: "His boots had kept Saturday's sea in till supper."
- Suggested fix: Cut the sentence. Beth's line already carries it.

**Issue 12**
- Problem: Emotion stated rather than shown. This sits right after the envelope questions, where the beat sheet wants the reader to hear an interview and Beth to hear care.
- Location: "He was very exact, and she liked him for it."
- Suggested fix: Replace with a physical beat, such as her answering a shade faster each time, and let the reader hear the interview.

**Issue 13**
- Problem: Same-speaker paragraphs run back to back in S2. Russ's lines are split with no action or reaction, which breaks his ask-and-answer rhythm and makes his offer read as an afterthought.
- Location: "\"Feds told me stay off that boat. So I'm staying off that boat. I'm not a complicated man.\"" followed by "\"Minute it lays down,\" Russ said, \"I'll run you over to Pruett myself. *Second Wind*'s got the legs.\"". Also "\"Suit yourself. Somebody cut that cable? ... since Labor Day.\"" followed by "\"Who's emergency management on this island?\" Russ said."
- Suggested fix: Put one beat between each pair (the cap, the thermos, the lean on the spruce), or merge each pair into one paragraph.

**Issue 14**
- Problem: A weather simile, a variant of the retired "the way he looked at weather".
- Location: "asking her about money as if it were the weather."
- Suggested fix: "asking her about money as if he were asking for the wrench."

**Issue 15**
- Problem: Negated-contrast phrasing ("X, and none of this was Y") in a chapter that bans contrast constructions. The desire is also stated, not shown.
- Location: "and she was thinking about last night's engine housing at her back and none of this was the bleed."
- Suggested fix: Drop the clause after "diesel on both their hands" and give her one physical detail (his pulse in the arm against hers).

**Issue 16**
- Problem: The latch clue's two readings exclude Ned, Tess and Russ, who know the house and its latch from years of visits. The bible says only that nobody who has been in the house makes that sound. "She had shown one person" and "Or the man had taught himself" cover only Dane and the intruder. This skews the fair-suspect balance the chapter is meant to spread, and it reads as a clue that points at Dane alone.
- Location: "She had shown one person how that door went: a marshal, Friday, on her own back step, up first and then the shoulder, three tries to do it quiet."
- Suggested fix: Add a half-clause such as "Half the island had stood in that kitchen and knew the latch." Then "she couldn't make it come out one way" is true across four suspects.

**Issue 17**
- Problem: The chapter ends warm and closed on a togetherness image. Rule 7 says no scene ends soft and only Ch 10 s5 may. The beat sheet prescribed "motion", but the last sentence is a soft image after the consequence line.
- Location: "Then they went up the shore toward the house together, his jacket round both of them."
- Suggested fix: End on Beth's line "...And you stay inside where I put you." and cut the final sentence, or end on a concrete detail (the red tag at her back).
