8. **Doucette is unnamed throughout.** He has his one line, "Where's your cop?", and right after it Beth gets one interior line: "Her cop was halfway up the pier road with Tess's name in his ears." That line shows the cost of "Get Tess." Cut it if it reads as a narrator nudge.
