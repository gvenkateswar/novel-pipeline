Winner: A

**Why:** A has a turn I can feel: Ned's long, comfortable lie about the phone and the unlit skiff, cut off by "Where's your cop," and then a Tess who sounds like nobody else in the book ("Save it for Whitcomb, Haskell. Whitcomb'll want it in order"; "I lit it, Beth"). B is quieter and in places finer, but nobody in it surprises me.

**Where A lost me:** "So the teller had written down the store's bag, and the red on it was Ned's." (~40%). For one line the narrator is solving the puzzle on my behalf.

**Where B lost me:** "The red the teller wrote down had ridden on the store's bag, then, wrapped round Beth's own envelope the whole way to Pruett." (~45%). The same puzzle-solving, at greater length. Ned turning the hearing aid off and on is the best silent beat in either version, and the dome light going dark is close behind. I nearly picked B for those two alone.

**Confidence:** medium-low. B's gestures against A's voices, and I went with voices.

**Same-author check:** Yes. Shared ending and shared dialogue spine. They differ in which instrument does the work, voice in A and gesture in B.
