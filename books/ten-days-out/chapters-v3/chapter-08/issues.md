# Chapter 08 — Issues (continuity checker, first draft)

Read: `chapters/chapter-08/draft.md` (3,085 words by wc, band 2,920–3,150), against `story-bible.md`, `continuity-log.md` (Ch 06 and Ch 07 entries, Spent words, warnings, plant and numbers ledgers, Master flags, Carried forward), `beats.md` with the orchestrator rulings, `chapters/chapter-07/final.md`, `chapters/chapter-06/final.md` (for the shore light, the jeans and Tess laughing at Arsenault) and `writer-notes.md`.

**Issue 1**
- Problem: The scene never says it is last light Thursday. The chapter opens with no time or day word, then the night passes, so "Toward the top of the tide" has nothing to measure from. The Ch 07 warning says the hour is last light Thursday.
- Location: "She took the second mug from Ned and wrapped both hands round it, and her eyes went down to the deck plates."
- Suggested fix: Open "At last light she took the second mug from Ned..." so the day and hour are on the page without a recap.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: Ned's hip is a tic. "Slow on the hip" opens three separate Ned beats, "hip first" and "the bad hip leading" add two more, and the phrase is already in Ch 06 and Ch 07. Five uses in one chapter.
- Location: "Once she heard Ned's step on the deck overhead, slow on the hip, going forward to the lines and coming back." Also: "Then he backed up the brow ahead of him, slow on the hip"; "let himself down onto the bench hip first"; "Ned came along the deck in the green jacket, slow on the hip, going ashore"; "the bad hip leading".
- Suggested fix: Keep only s6's "the bad hip leading" (the exit). Rewrite the other four with a different physical detail, or none.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: Ned volunteers three extra facts about the clothes right after "Good. Don't tell me." That is the over-detail lying tell, which the beats' own "No new Ned tell" cap says this chapter must not use. (The beats quote the line, so the beats conflict with their own cap.)
- Location: "Kenny's. He won't miss them. He's in Pruett, sick as a dog."
- Suggested fix: Cut to "Kenny's. He's in Pruett." and let the weather carry the rest of the speech.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: Wrong day word. Day 5's report was at ten to two on Tuesday morning (Ch 06: the top of the tide, "Tuesday, the morning low" is the next scene). He left her Monday night.
- Location: "Tuesday night I left you asleep behind a door nobody could bar from outside."
- Suggested fix: "Before two on Tuesday I left you asleep..." ("Monday" is capped at one in this chapter, and Pike has it.)

**Issue 5**
- Problem: Page order contradicts Ch 07. In Ch 07 "One page back was her plan from Wednesday night," and she "turned back further" to reach *every place we've slept*. The line is before the plan, not after it.
- Location: "The page after your plan. 'Every place we've slept, and B's family property.'"
- Suggested fix: "The page before your plan."

**Issue 6**
- Problem: Dane states more than he saw. Ch 06 shows one flashlight at the Ashby end of the bar and an answering skiff; nothing shows two men, or whose crew, from a ledge in the dark at the top of the tide.
- Location: "The light at the Ashby end, short, then long, was two men off the *Margaret Ann* calling their skiff in off the burned shore."
- Suggested fix: "was a man off the *Margaret Ann* calling his skiff in off the burned shore," and keep Dane's certainty to the skiff, which he heard.

**Issue 7**
- Problem: Nothing on the page accounts for the twelve hours between Beth leaving the fish house (morning low Thursday) and Dane arriving at the top of the tide Friday. Beats Flag 1 says s2 explains it ("he waited the whole night low for her"), but the beats' s2 subtext line says to keep it unsaid, and the draft says neither. The cost the beats assign to s5 is also nowhere on the page.
- Location: "There's one boat on this island you'd sleep on."
- Suggested fix: Add one clause to Dane's last speech: "I sat the night low at the fish house waiting for you to come back by the shore, and at the top of the tide I came by the road."

**Issue 8**
- Problem: The s2-to-s3 time gap is made worse, not closed. s2 ends with her out the door at about a quarter past four with Dane following. s3 opens "full light" (sunrise is 6:36) and says she "still hadn't dialed", which means she stood in sleet for two hours; Dane's "Before you do" then reads as immediate. Dark is not an option for s3 either: she reads pencil pages in the lee, which she can't at four. Also the call connects with no dialing.
- Location: "It was full light, and she still hadn't dialed." Also: "between them the open water ran gray to the south with nothing on it" and "She put it on speaker and lifted it to the sky between them, and they bent their heads to it."
- Suggested fix: Keep s3 in daylight and say what bridged the gap: "The gray came up behind the sleet while she waited in the lee of the deckhouse for light enough to read by, his phone in her fist, and Dane waited with her." Then one line of her dialing before "Pike."

**Issue 9**
- Problem: The reason Dane can't hold the phone is false. His left arm won't lift above the shoulder; his right is good (he reaches the door with it, spoons glaze with it). Also his wet coat is left steaming at the heater, and he then stands in a gale in a cut shirt with no mention of it.
- Location: "He couldn't get the phone up past his shoulder, so she held it." Also: "In the crew mess his wet coat steamed at the heater."
- Suggested fix: Drop the arm excuse and make it her choice: "She held it herself; she wasn't handing it back." Have Dane take the coat off the heater on the way out in s2's last sentence.

**Issue 10**
- Problem: s3 and s5 both close on Dane not reaching for the notebook, in nearly the same words. The repeat dulls the s5 ending, which is the chapter's turn.
- Location: s3: "his eyes on the book all the way in and on the shut coat after, and he let it go there without asking for it." s5: "and his good hand rested on the table beside it and didn't move to take it."
- Suggested fix: End s3 on the phone going away instead (Dane thumbing it dark and putting it inside his shirt, the sleet running off its case); keep the unaccepted offer for s5 only.

**Issue 11**
- Problem: The ham can't be scored, glazed, baked and sliced in one unbroken scene. The scene opens with the ham already in the open oven, then she scores the fat (a pre-bake step), then Dane cuts the first slice (a done ham) minutes after the first glaze, with "ten minutes" to the second coat; the fat has "crisped" along lines cut a moment ago.
- Location: "The mess oven stood open on the ham, and the heat off its door lay along her shins." Also: "Diamonds. Across one way, across the other, so the glaze has somewhere to sit." and "The fat had crisped along her lines exactly where she had cut them."
- Suggested fix: Make it a fully cooked ham heating through: it sits on the board beside the open oven, they score and glaze it, it goes back in, and the slice comes off the end of a ham that was already sliceable; or drop "crisped along her lines" and the first-coat timing.

**Issue 14**
