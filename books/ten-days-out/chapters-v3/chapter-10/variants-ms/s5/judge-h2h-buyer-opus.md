1. **Winner: B**
2. **Why:** B ends with her running down the stairs to a boat she's told him she'll be sailing at three. A ends with her forehead against a window, alone. This late in a romance I want her going toward him, not missing him.
3. **Where A lost me:** About 45%. "Inside, the prosecutor had asked her about the money once. Nobody had asked twice, because a deputy's pencil had answered it at the foot of every night." I had to work out what it meant, and the scene is so short there's nothing after it to carry me. The last sentence about his pencil and her bed in a file is lovely, but it closes a door I wanted left open.
4. **Where B lost me:** About 30%. "With an indictment there's no preliminary hearing, so Pelletier stays held." Then the brick on Route One. It's the legal wrap-up. "She buttoned it wrong and did it again" pulled me straight back.
5. **Confidence:** high. B has a want, a message to him and a clock. A has a mood.
6. **Same-author check:** Yes. The same corridor sentences, nearly word for word (good shoes, hot dry radiators, knowing Pike by the voice).
