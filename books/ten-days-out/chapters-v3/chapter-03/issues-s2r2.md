**Issue 1**
- Problem: Ambiguous "She" in the loyalty sentence. It follows a sentence whose subject is Tess, so "She hadn't given him Ned last night" reads as Tess. "giving him Tess" then becomes confusing. The bible and the log both flag this "she" problem. The sentence also says "last night", but the S1 addition places the unanswered question "toward morning".
- Location: "Tess looked from one hand to the other. She hadn't given him Ned last night, and she wasn't giving him Tess now."
- Suggested fix: Open the sentence with "Beth" as the subject ("Beth hadn't given him Ned...") and change "last night" to "in the dark".

**Issue 2**
- Problem: The round 2 ruling (3d) says Russ is posted where he "hears nothing said at the counter", and the who-knows table says Russ knows "nothing said at the counter while he was posted". The draft puts him in the office doorway, a few feet from the counter, and only the generator's roar separates him. Tess then says 22:40 and the "I can see your steps" lines aloud, and later the tag line. Tess also walks past the doorway to the safe. Nothing on the page shows that Russ cannot hear. If he does, he knows Beth was home that night, which contradicts the table.
- Location: "Russ leaned in the office doorway, in the generator's roar, where he could see the back window and the counter both." / "Twenty-two forty. I can see your steps from right here, Beth. I didn't put you on the air."
- Suggested fix: Add one clause that puts Russ out of earshot: he has his back to the counter, facing the back window, and the roar swallows the counter talk. Or move him to the back step.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: Ashby is introduced with no identifying clause. The round 1 ruling ("Ashby before it's known") requires one at the first mention, and round 2 does not override it. Ned's line gives only the generator. The page never says it is a shut-up summer house, and S3 never says so either. S3 only says "a big shingled shape with water on three sides", and the sheeted furniture comes much later. The plant "the cove over the fish house sill till Monday" (bible plant ledger, 03 to 05) and the reason Beth wants Ashby are also gone. Without them Beth's request has no motive on the page.
- Location: "\"I need the Ashby key, Ned,\" Beth said."
- Suggested fix: Add one sentence of Beth's thought before or after the key: Ashby is the Connecticut family's summer cottage on the north point, shut for the winter, and the cove is over her father's fish house sill till Monday.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: "The call" has no referent. A listener cannot tell it means a call to the broker. The ruling (3e.4) also asks for Dane to stay "with the coffee Arsenault bought him", and the coffee is absent at noon, so the Arsenault link is weaker.
- Location: "*Saturday noon*, the screen had said: the deadline on the Webbers Cove. The call meant going outside and leaving her at the counter beside Arsenault. Dane stayed where he was."
- Suggested fix: Replace "The call" with "A call to the broker" and add "with Arsenault's coffee in his hand" to the last sentence.

**Issue 5**
- Problem: Nothing shows where Arsenault goes after he leaves. He exits by the front door, and Beth and Dane use the same door and stand outside while Beth says "Ashby". The who-knows table says Arsenault must not learn Ashby.
- Location: "Arsenault nodded to Beth and went." / "Beth and Dane went out the front, the bell behind them, round to the side wall."
- Suggested fix: Add one clause showing Arsenault walking down the dock toward the float, away from the side wall and out of hearing, before Beth and Dane go round.

**Issue 6**
- Problem: A zero-tolerance contrast construction ("Not X. Y.") in dialogue, which the bible's forbidden list bans in narration and dialogue. The line is bible-sanctioned (Russ's profile) and was kept by the beats, so the tool will flag it against a rule the bible contradicts.
- Location: "Where you sleeping tonight?\" Russ said. \"Not asking for me. Asking because somebody will."
- Suggested fix: Orchestrator ruling needed. Either reword to a single statement ("Somebody will ask where you're sleeping. Might as well be me.") or record an explicit exception in the bible.

**Issue 7**
- Problem: The S1 addition does not sound like Dane. His profile gives him whole sentences with "because" and "which", and his questions are made easy to answer. Three bare words are Beth's register, so the line fails the strip-the-names test. Separately, the unanswered question is followed at once by Beth stepping out alone onto the back step at first light. Nothing shows Dane watching or following, which weakens "Dane never leaves Beth alone". Ch 02 closed with the back door tried.
- Location: "Toward morning Dane said, \"Who else knew?\"" / "At first light she took the kettle out onto the back step for snow"
- Suggested fix: Lengthen his line into his syntax ("Who else knew, Beth? Anyone you told, or anyone who'd have heard it from somebody who did?") and add a clause that he stands at the kitchen window while she is on the step.

**Issue 8**
- Problem: Timeline strain in the tournament-kept line. Tess came to Cutter at 22 and is 46, so she arrived 24 years ago. Beth was nine when her mother left, which is also 24 years ago. Tess arrived as a summer sternman, and the line has her putting a nine-year-old to bed that winter.
- Location: "I put you to bed the winter your mother got on the Bangor bus."
- Suggested fix: Change "the winter your mother got on the Bangor bus" to "the winter after your mother got on the Bangor bus", or add a clause that Tess was boarding with the Calloways then.

**Issue 9**
- Problem: The hand-off from S1 to S2 is unclear. S1 ends in the kitchen at first light. S2 opens outdoors with no mention that Dane is with Beth, and he first appears in "Dane was already past her." The time jump from first light to late morning (high tide) is unmarked, and nothing says why they are going to the store. The reader cannot place the scene at the open.
- Location: "The planks down from her back steps were iced, and Beth heard it before she saw anything" / "Dane was already past her."
- Suggested fix: Add one clause naming Dane beside her on the planks and the errand ("Dane a step behind her, on the way to the store for milk"), which also seeds "Hand me the milk".

**Issue 10**
- Problem: Dane says "I'm not leaving you on that step", then goes down the lane and leaves Beth alone at its head. The hooded man's crew is still on the harbor. Russ is only brought in afterward at the back door, which is not what 3d promised (Dane puts Russ on her by name before he goes). Dane never leaves Beth within a clause of her being guarded.
- Location: "Dane went in after him. Beth stopped at the head of the lane."
- Suggested fix: Add one clause that Dane keeps her in sight from the lane ("with one eye on her at the lane's head"), or have him call to Russ at the back door before he goes in.

**Issue 11**
- Problem: S3 claims more than S2 now provides. Dane has only a few short exchanges with Russ and two lines with Tess (the floor, the restroom). The round 2 thinning removed his longer talk with Russ, so "talked all day to Russ and Tess" overstates what is on the page.
- Location: "He looked at her, a man who had talked all day to Russ and Tess and the snow, and now he didn't say a word."
- Suggested fix: Change "all day" to "all morning" ("a man who had talked all morning to Russ and Tess and the snow"), or give Dane one more line with Russ in S2 (a reply to "Get a look at him? Didn't, did you.").
