**Issue 1**
- Problem: POV break. The chapter is Beth-only (World rule 9, "Dane's head stays shut until Ch 9 s3"). The closing sentences of S2 state what Dane saw and chose not to do. Beth can only see him watch, not know what he asked or didn't ask.
- Location: "Dane had seen the key go from Ned's ring into her glove. He didn't ask whose it was."
- Suggested fix: Put it in what Beth can see ("Dane, at the corner, watched the key go from Ned's ring into her glove and said nothing") and cut "He didn't ask whose it was," which reports his inner choice.

**Issue 2**
- Problem: The new closing beat contradicts the staging. Dane is at the corner within sight and hearing. Ned takes the key off his ring in front of him and says he runs Ashby's generator and "Nobody'll look twice at a light." Dane therefore already has Ned as the person who knows where they are going. Nothing is left for Beth to "not give" him, so the loyalty sentence has no referent on the page. "In the dark" also has an unclear anchor: S1's silence ("She didn't answer him.") happens toward morning, and Ch 02's "You." happens in the dark kitchen.
- Location: "Beth hadn't given him Ned in the dark, and she didn't give him Ned now."
- Suggested fix: Have the thing she withholds be specific and not visible to Dane. For example, move Dane to the front corner, watching the moorings with his back to them, out of earshot. Then Beth pockets the key unseen, and the sentence can say she told Ned "Home" last night and has not told Dane. Otherwise cut the sentence.

**Issue 3**
- Problem: "Nobody but—" / "Me and God. I know." is now said in Dane's hearing for the first time. Ch 02 had it out of his earshot (Ned and Beth at the stern, after Dane went up the gangway). Beth finishing Ned's sentence with "I know" shows Dane that she and Ned already share a private oath about her whereabouts. That undercuts the closing beat (Issue 2) and hands Dane the thing she is supposed to be withholding. The who-hears table in the earlier beats had Dane told about Ashby "off the page."
- Location: "Nobody but—" / "Me and God. I know," Beth said.
- Suggested fix: Keep Dane out of earshot for the key exchange (see Issue 2). The line then stays between Beth and Ned, as it did in Ch 02.

**Issue 4**
- Problem: Suspect balance and the continuity-log flag. The log's Master flag says the "You." / Ned reasoning in Ch 03 is to be "paired with her hiding Tess's log, so that it reads as character and not as a clue," and that nothing new should go against Ned. In the bridge the loyalty beat names Ned alone. The only Tess content left is her silence and one line, "Restroom's for customers." The result is a chapter whose only new Ned content is a protected-secret beat, with no Tess clue to balance it. The same flag required Ch 03 to "swing hard to Tess," and bible rule 10 says Ned is never narrated as guilty.
- Location: "Beth hadn't given him Ned in the dark, and she didn't give him Ned now."
- Suggested fix: Drop the Ned sentence from S2 and carry it into the Ch 05 Tess scene, where Tess's log gives it its pair. End S2 on the key and "Me and God. I know." Or, if the author wants it here, name no person: "She'd told him one name last night, and it wasn't the one she'd have to give him now."

**Issue 5**
- Problem: A prop pointing at a cut scene. The storm log is introduced with no payoff in Ch 03. The pre-bridge scene used it for 22:40, the palm and "Don't you look at my book like the bank does." Now Beth never looks at it and Tess never touches it, so the reader is invited to expect something and gets nothing. It is the only Tess detail in the bridge that points to the cut fight.
- Location: "Tess was at the counter with the storm log open, and she looked up and didn't say a word."
- Suggested fix: Replace the open log with a plain task for Tess's hands (for example "Tess was at the till counting the morning's bag"), and let Ch 05 introduce the log fresh.

**Issue 6**
- Problem: S3's description of Dane is unsupported by the chapter as it now stands. Nothing in S1 or the bridge shows him talking "all day" to Russ, "the snow," or "anybody else." In S1 and S2 he speaks perhaps a dozen short lines and exchanges two with Russ. The Russ talk, which was the only support, is cut. The line also attaches to a morning-to-night span that the page doesn't show (see Issue 9).
- Location: "a man who had talked all day to Russ and the snow and anybody else who'd stand still, and now he didn't say a word."
- Suggested fix: Rework to what is on the page, e.g. "a man who had talked her up and down the north road and now didn't say a word." That is only valid if the road talk is added; otherwise "a man who never stopped talking on a danger" should be left to Ch 02's whisper. Simplest: "He looked at her, and for once didn't say a word."

**Issue 7**
- Problem: "Dane never leaves Beth alone" is stated and then broken in the same two paragraphs. He says "I'm not leaving you on that step," then gives the opposite instruction ("Stay up top where I can see you") and goes down the lane, leaving her at its head, ahead of an unknown second man (the dragger is lit all night and has a crew). A few lines later he hands her to Russ and stays outside, out of her sight, until he returns. The noon logic ("leaving her at the counter beside Arsenault") is then at odds with his having left her at the head of a lane and in the store with Tess and Russ. The sequence is defensible (Russ is posted), but the first line promises something the action doesn't deliver.
- Location: "On me," Dane said, going. "I'm not leaving you on that step. Talk to me."
- Suggested fix: Cut "I'm not leaving you on that step." Make his instruction to her at the lane "Stay up top where I can see you," and leave "Russ, take her in and keep her where you can see her" as the handover.

**Issue 8**
- Problem: Two back-to-back lines by Dane, each opened with "Dane said," with a "He" in the second that follows directly after "Ms. Marr." The reader (and the listener pass) reads "He went in between them like he'd built them" as being about Russ or Tess's store. "Them" has no antecedent for the listener (the fish houses were mentioned 18 lines earlier). It also reads as one speech chopped into two beats with no action between.
- Location: "I owe you a floor, Ms. Marr," Dane said. "Put it on the government's account." / "He went in between them like he'd built them," Dane said to Russ.
- Suggested fix: Put an action between them (Dane turns to Russ) and make the antecedents plain: "The man went in between those fish houses like he'd built them," Dane said to Russ.

**Issue 9**
- Problem: Unbridged time jump at the S2/S3 join. S2 ends at noon at the side wall with the key in her glove. S3 opens "in the dark" on the north road. The page doesn't say where they spent the afternoon or why they waited, and Russ's question ("Where you sleeping tonight?") is never answered or acknowledged again. The beat sheet's S3 had "full dark," but the removal of S2's ending (the cot blankets, Russ's closing question) took away the only transition.
- Location: "The north road went up over the spine of the island in the dark, and the snow squeaked under their boots."
- Suggested fix: Add one clause at the top of S3 that covers the afternoon and answers Russ by action (for example "They waited out the afternoon in the cottage with the stove banked and left by the back when the light went, and nobody asked where they were going").

**Issue 11**
- Problem: Ned's location is ambiguous, which is also a who-hears problem. "Russ stayed at the counter. Ned was there with an urn cup" reads as Ned standing inside at the counter, next to Russ. The earlier rulings have Ned outside only and Russ never hearing "Ashby." As written, either Russ hears the Ashby request or the reader can't tell where Ned is.
- Location: "Russ stayed at the counter. Ned was there with an urn cup, and Dane kept the corner, where he could see her and the moorings."
- Suggested fix: Name the place: "Russ stayed at the counter. Ned was waiting at the side wall with an urn cup, out of the wind."
