Winner: B

**Persona:** Skimmer. **Model:** opus. **Read:** `chapters/chapter-03/variants/s2/blind/p3-A.md` then `p3-B.md`, plus `docs/judging.md` and `docs/calibration.md`.

1. **Winner: B.**

2. **Why:** In B's store scene I always knew who was in the room and what Tess wanted, and the two folded cot blankets going back under the counter hit me. In A, I spent the middle working out where everybody was standing, and I'm too tired tonight to do that.

3. **Where A lost me:** "She got Tess in, hanging the keys straight on the hook, her knuckles in the charging cords among the phones, Ned's in its rubber band." (~48%). Before that I was already squinting. At "Nobody answered him. Tess wasn't there to." (~33%) I had to go back and check who was where. Then the hood at arm's length woke me up, the scariest moment in either version. But the chase was a blur ("he went through Tess, coming in from the panel"), and after it came keys, cords, phones, rubber band, and then Tess's speech starting with "Twenty-two forty." My thumb went fast through "Your pay rides to Pruett in my bag. Yellow's mine. Red's the boat's." and didn't slow down until the shed.

4. **Where B lost me:** "Going back in, Beth picked the keys for Tess's Ford off the charging shelf where the hook had thrown them and hung them up again, her knuckles in among the cords and phones, Ned's in its rubber band." (~45%). It's the same inventory as A and I skimmed it the same way, and the bag colors right after ("Yellow's mine. Red's the boat's.") read like a ledger. B got me back two lines later with "I put you to bed the winter your mother got on the Bangor bus." / "Then you know I don't go where I'm put." I stayed until the end.

5. **Confidence: medium.** The two stores are close in how much ledger they carry, and A has the better menace beat. B wins because I could follow it on a tired read and it has a real fight between the two women. The shed and the porch are word for word the same in both, and those are what I'd keep reading for.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. The opening and closing scenes are identical, and the middle scenes share lines ("Sea coming. Stand still.", "like a man looking for the restroom").
