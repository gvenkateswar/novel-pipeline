Winner: B

**Why:** In B I always knew where everyone was standing: the hood runs, the sea comes up the lane, then we're in the store. In A the store scene turned into lights going off, Tess going out and somehow still talking from behind the curtain, and I'd lost the thread before the scary part even started.

**Where A lost me:** "Inside, the generator quit, and the lights with it. / 'Mind the counter, Calloway.' Tess was through the curtain, and the back door banged open on the wind behind her." (~28%). Two lines later "The storm log lay open there in Tess's hand," and then "From behind the curtain: 'Put that away, Lyle.'" I couldn't tell where Tess was, so I started skimming. The hooded man at arm's length ("bait and diesel and wet cotton... his breath moving the dark there") pulled me back for a paragraph. Then "She got Tess in, hanging the keys straight on the hook, her knuckles in the charging cords among the phones, Ned's in its rubber band" (~50%) is a stockroom list, and my thumb moved fast through that and the yellow-tag/red-tag money speech until the generator shed.

**Where B lost me:** "In Tess's office the hook by the back door hung crooked from Dane's shoulder, and the two keys for Tess's Ford lay on the charging shelf on their MARR'S tag; she hung them back with her knuckles in the cords, among the island's phones, Ned's in its rubber band" (~38%). It's the same inventory, and I skimmed it. I skimmed again at "Your pay rides to Pruett in my bag every other week. Yellow's mine. Red's the boat's." The scene runs out of steam at "The minute hand went past twelve. Dane stood at the counter, wet to the knee, and lifted the coffee to his mouth, and didn't go out" (~58%). Nothing happens there, and I was waiting for the shed.

**Confidence:** medium. A has the single best moment in either version (the man breathing in the curtain gap). If its staging were clear I might flip, but tired me wouldn't have made it there paying attention.

**Same-author check:** Yes. Same rhythm, same props (keys, phones in rubber bands, the yellow tag), and everything outside the middle scene is word for word the same.
