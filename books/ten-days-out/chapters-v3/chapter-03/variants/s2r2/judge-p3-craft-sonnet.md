Winner: B

1. Why: B's middle scene lets Tess's anger, the Arsenault interruption and the chase braid together through the curtain gap, so the people sound different and the geography holds. A makes me stop and work out who is talking to whom.

2. (see 1) Persona: Craft. Chapter opening and the generator shed ending are identical in both. Only the middle scene differs.

3. Where A lost me: "You went down my back slope after him on top of the tide," Tess said. She held up her left hand... "I can see your steps from right here, Beth." (about 40%). The line is addressed to Dane, then she turns to Beth mid-speech with no signal. A has more of this. Russ says "Take her in" and then "Where you sleeping tonight?", which is asked with no one clearly spoken to. The scene opens on "The planks down from her back steps" while Beth is at the store, so I lost track of where she was. The dialogue is also heavily tagged ("Beth said," "Dane said," "Tess said" every line), which flattens the voices.

4. Where B lost me: "Nobody but—" Ned said. (about 52%). Ned's own line is already running, so this reads as a speaker-tag slip. Tess's single long block ("Net had you aboard. Your back steps had you home...") is dense and a little expository, but it sounds like her. Otherwise I stayed.

5. Confidence: medium. The scenes are close in content. A's chase opening is more kinetic, but its muddled address and location cost it with me.

6. Same-author check: Yes. Both share the same shaving and phone opening and the same generator-shed ending, with the same clipped declaratives and the "Me and God. I know" rhythm.
