That's the deposit-bag bookkeeping, and I skimmed it the same way I did in A. I came back at Beth's thumb on his neck with Tess watching. I drifted a little again at "The clock over the door came to noon. *Saturday noon*, the screen had said: the deadline on the Webbers Cove." That's logistics, and I didn't care.
