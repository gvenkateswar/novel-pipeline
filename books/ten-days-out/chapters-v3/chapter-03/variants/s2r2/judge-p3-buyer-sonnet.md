3. Where A lost me: "You went down my back slope after him on top of the tide," Tess said. (about 38%). For a second I thought Beth was speaking. Tess is also suddenly lecturing about the net and the time on her knuckles, and the scene stops for a speech. I didn't quit there. Just before that, the chase opens with "The planks down from her back steps were iced," and I had to work out where Beth was standing.

4. Where B lost me: "Dane stopped at the door. 'I'll be on the dock. I can see this counter from there.'" (about 28%). That puts the couple in separate rooms for most of the scene, and I started to drift through Tess's long run of "I can see your steps from right here." There is also a slip at about 60%: "Nobody but—" Ned said. The line was already Ned's, so it reads as a typo. The chase itself is told from a distance, through a gap in a curtain, and has less life than A's.

5. Confidence: medium. The shed scene and the opening are identical in both. The difference is the middle, and A's middle keeps the couple together, which is what I'd pay for. A's dialogue tags are messier, though, and that keeps this from being high.

6. Same-author check: Yes. Everything outside the middle scene is word for word the same, and the middle scenes share Tess's voice and the "restroom" joke.
