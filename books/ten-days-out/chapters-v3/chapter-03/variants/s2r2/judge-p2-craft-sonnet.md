Winner: A

1. Why: A cuts the store scene tighter and keeps Dane at the window as a refrain while Tess and Beth fight, so the talk has a threat under it. B says the same things in cleaner, flatter staging.

2. Where A lost me: "Arsenault. The *Margaret Ann*, the dragger lit all through the blackout." (~33%). The fragment tags the character like a label on a map, and I felt the author pointing. Both versions have it, but it sits more heavily in A. Smaller drag: "Dane went after him to see which way he walked." is a stage direction.

3. Where B lost me: "Russ took the office doorway as Beth went back in, where he could see the lane and the till, inside the roar." (~38%). That is a guard-positioning sentence. B also lets Tess say "That's what's out back of my store" and then runs straight into the yellow-tag speech. The chase has just happened, and the pivot back to her grievance is too neat.

4. Confidence: medium-low. The scenes share most of their lines, and the difference is order and clipping. I'd pick A again but wouldn't bet on it.

5. Same-author check: Yes. Same chapter, same voice, same lines in the other scenes. Only the middle scene is rearranged.
