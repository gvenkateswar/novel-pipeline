Winner: B

**Why:** In B, Dane is mostly inside the store with me and Beth, Tess gets a line that made me smile ("Marshal can eat on the porch"), and the chase comes sooner. In A, he keeps getting parked "through the front window" at the dock rail while women talk about money, and I kept losing track of which side of the glass he was on.

**Where A lost me:** "Arsenault took his cigarettes, nodded to her and went out, and Dane went after him to see which way he walked." (~38%). Two lines before that, Dane had been out at the rail. Then he came in behind Arsenault. Now he's out again, and soon he's "back at the rail" a third time. My thumb sped up through the yellow tag and the bag, and it didn't slow down again until the man in the hood showed up. That only happens because "Tess... left the curtain hanging open," and the curtain is there so the plot can see out the back window.

**Where B lost me:** "Russ took the office doorway as Beth went back in, where he could see the lane and the till, inside the roar." (~45%). Right after the sea comes up the lane, I get a sentence about where the bodyguard is standing, and then we go back to Tess's money bag. The air goes out of the chase. It's a smaller dip than A's, and "his face turned up the slope to her" just before it carried me past.

**Confidence:** medium. The rest of the chapter is the same text, and the best of both scenes is in both (the restroom joke, the collar, "Me and God. I know"). B moves people around less, and Dane is in the room with her more, and that decided it for me.
