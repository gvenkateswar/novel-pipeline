# Judge: Craft persona (opus), p3-A vs p3-B

1. **Winner: A**

2. **Why:** The two are nearly the same chapter, but A ends with her walking out to stand against his arm "while he told Portland where she was," and that line made the whole chapter matter to me, while B ends on a dropped sheet and a phrase I had to read twice.

3. **Where A lost me:** ~40%, "Beth got as far as the office doorway: the hook with the two keys for Tess's Ford on their MARR'S tag, the phones charging on the shelf under it, the back door banging open on the wind, and the back step empty." The chase turns into an inventory of props, and I could feel things being set up for later. Then the Tess money-bag patter right after it ("Yellow's mine. Red's the boat's.") kept my thumb moving until she put her hand on the counter. Dane's "Thank you. I can't, because any boat on this island could be theirs, and I can't read this sea well enough..." is also one clause too long for him.

4. **Where B lost me:** ~45%, "Behind her Tess was snapping a yellow tag through the zipper of the morning's deposit bag and talking the whole time." It's the same ledger business as A, but in B it comes after a chase where nobody runs (Dane "didn't go down onto it"), so nothing was carrying me past it. B came back strong with the dragger going dark at the top of the spine, which is a better image than A's skiff. Then it lost me again on the last line: "the sheet dropped back over the glass between them, out of her opening hand." That reads as written for effect, not felt.

5. **Confidence: low.** B has the better lines in places ("a man who had talked all day to Russ and Tess and the urn and the snow", Russ's "Somebody ought to offer, so I'm offering", the dragger blacking out). A has the better ending, and A's Russ ("Beth watched him not look it") and Dane ("Everybody on this island walks that shore like that except me") sound more like people. Close to a coin flip, and I went with the ending.

6. **Same-author check: Yes.** Most of it is the same sentences word for word, so it's one draft revised two ways.
