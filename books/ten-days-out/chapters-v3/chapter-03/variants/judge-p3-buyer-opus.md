# Judge: Buyer persona (Opus), Ch 03, p3-A vs p3-B

1. **Winner: A**

2. **Why:** At the end of A she walks out onto that porch and puts her arm against his while he tells Portland where she is, and I put the phone down feeling something. B ends with her letting a sheet drop over the window, which is pretty but leaves me outside the glass.

3. **Where A lost me:** around 45%, in the store. "Yellow's mine. Red's the boat's. I won't have my money in Ned's color. Calloway, you look like a wet cat." After that comes the 22:40 on the back of Tess's hand and in the storm log. I read it twice and still couldn't say what the bags were or why I should care. My thumb sped up from there to "Hand me the milk." It slowed again on the north road when the dark skiff showed up, and I was all the way back in the shed.

4. **Where B lost me:** around 43%. "Going past, she saw the hook with the two keys for Tess's Ford, and the island's phones charging on the shelf under it." It's a list of things on a shelf, dropped right after the hooded man walked out. Then the deposit-bag colors come, and B has the same dead patch as A. B did one thing better: putting the hooded man in the store, dripping, staring at her, was creepier than A's man seen through a back window. And the dragger going dark was a good chill. But the ending sent me off cooler.

5. **Confidence: medium.** The razor scene and the shed are nearly the same in both. What decides it is the last page, plus the fact that B has the scarier store scene.

6. **Same-author check:** Yes. Most of the sentences are the same, word for word.
