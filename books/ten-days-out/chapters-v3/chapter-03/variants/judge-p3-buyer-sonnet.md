# Judge: Buyer (sonnet), Ch 3 variants p3-A vs p3-B

1. Winner: A

2. Why: The ending of A has her walk out onto the step and press her arm against his while he tells Portland where she is, and that is the moment I'd keep reading for; B ends with the sheet dropping between them, which is neater but colder.

3. Where A lost me: Dane's refusal of Russ's boat, about 35%: "Thank you. I can't, because any boat on this island could be theirs, and I can't read this sea well enough to know a bad one when I'm standing on it, and whoever takes her across leaves a wake the whole bay can follow." Nobody talks like that. It is the one place my thumb sped up. The chase through the curtain and "Lost him in the fish houses" is also a bit of a hurry-up-and-nothing, but I didn't skim it.

4. Where B lost me: Dane explaining himself after the row, about 48%: "One of me against a dragger crew on the water is a body count, and I don't get to pick whose." That is the author explaining the plot to me. The line "The store had always looked up at the back of her house." is also too thin to carry the feeling it is reaching for, about 45%.

5. Confidence: medium. The two are about 90% the same text. The kitchen/razor scene and the shed kiss are identical to my eye, and both are good.

6. Same-author check: Yes. They are two passes on the same draft, with the same razor, shed and Tess beats and the same sentences.
