Persona: Craft. Judge: sonnet.

1. Winner: A

2. Why: A keeps the people in the room doing things to each other (the hand over 22:40, "Hand me the milk," Dane breathing hard at her elbow, the kiss with a bite in it), where B, the cleaner version, stops to explain itself.

3. Where A lost me: "Then every deck light on her went out at once, and the harbor was black where Beth had been." (~58%). Where Beth had been? It reads like a slip for "where the boat had been," and it knocked me out of the best image in the chapter. A smaller one is the speech after the stall, "What you write every day. Am I in it? Where I sleep. Who gave me the key." (~85%). It tidies her anger into a list.

4. Where B lost me: "One of me against a dragger crew on the water is a body count, and I don't get to pick whose." (~45%). Dane explains the stakes in a speech, and it's the same kind of competence-on-display that flattened the anchor A1. A runner-up: "and maybe he could" after the hand on his chest (~62%). That's the narrator winking at me.

5. Confidence: medium. They are about 90% the same text, so the call rests on a handful of choices. A's are a bit better: the chase lost in the fish houses, Russ "told the price of bait," the thumbs that wait on her. B's choices are better in two places: the silent hooded man standing in the store, and the closing "she had nothing to check it against but his mouth." Neither reads like A1, because the couple is in a room together in both.

6. Same-author check: Yes. They share the same spine, the same razor, the same tapped basin and the same shed. A is the other version of the same draft.
