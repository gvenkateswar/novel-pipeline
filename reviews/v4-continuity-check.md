# v4 continuity check (C6): Ten Days Out, Ch 05–10

Read: revision-brief-v3.md, story-bible.md, continuity-log.md, Ch 05 final, Ch 06 v4, Ch 07 v4, Ch 08 v4, Ch 09 final, Ch 10 v4; Ch 03–04 final checked for the facts the new lines depend on.

Tide clock used, from bible note 36 (Day 4 lows 7:10 a.m. and 7:35 p.m., about 50 minutes later each day): Day 6 low about 8:50 a.m.; Day 7 high about 3:27 a.m., low 10:05 p.m.; Day 8 high about 4:17 a.m., low about 10:30 a.m., high about 4:40 p.m. Ch 05, Ch 06 (noon Day 6 at half flood; 5:50 a.m. Day 7 two hours past high and ebbing) and Ch 07 (church about 1 a.m., three hours into the flood; ferry at 2) all agree with it.

## Issues

**1. Ch 08 / Ch 03 / Ch 04: the three o'clock "skipped name" clue is contradicted earlier in the book. MUST FIX**
- Ch 08: "Eleven years of storm nets, and Tess had never once gone from Burgess to Coombs." / "She skipped me. Allen, Burgess, Coombs."
- Conflicts with: Ch 03: "Off the air, as Beth had asked." Ch 04 (Day 3 net): "She didn't say Calloway. She didn't need to." Ch 09: "*Keep it off the air.* The Day 1 words. Tess had carried them once already." Since Day 1, leaving Beth's name off the air is exactly what Beth asked Tess to do. So the skip is not a sign of anything, and the evidence behind "Waiting kills her" fails for a careful reader. Ch 04's net also opens "Dyer, go ahead," not in alphabetical order.
- Fix: base the clue on something else, for example Tess reads "Calloway" aloud on the air for the first time, or she skips Mrs. Dyer, whom she never skips.

**2. Ch 08 / Ch 09: Russ comes along the ledges under the pier about an hour before high water. MUST FIX**
- Ch 08: "tell Russ to come down by the ledges under the pier at dusk"; "Russ would come the other way, along the ledges under the pier". Ch 09 (quarter to four): "Russ came up the seaward ladder from the ledges under the pier".
- Conflicts with: the tide clock (Day 8 high about 4:40 p.m.) and Ch 07. At about 2 a.m., more than two hours before high, Beth found "Under the pier the ledge was black and streaming, the flood already at its foot", and "the ledges under the Harbor Road were going under one by one." At 3:45 p.m. those ledges are under water. The bible's revision note ("from the ledges under the pier") has the same problem.
- Fix: bring Russ in by skiff to the quarter ladder, or along the top of the riprap (the route Ch 07 says stays dry). Or move his arrival to a time the tide allows.

**3. Ch 09: "The sea already allowed it" undoes Pike's first-light limit. MUST FIX**
- Ch 09, 3 a.m.: "Relief would come the first hour the sea allowed. The sea already allowed it."
- Conflicts with: Ch 08, Pike at 3:30: "nobody from the mainland sets foot on Cutter before first light," and with relief arriving at noon in Ch 10. Ch 09 also says "The harbor lay flat" at 6 p.m. and "Over water this still". That weakens the swell part of Pike's reason; only "not in the dark" still holds it up.
- Fix: replace the line with the real timing (boats leave at first light; Portland deputies have to drive to Pruett first), and keep the darkness and the harbor-mouth crossing as the stated limit.

**4. Ch 08 / Ch 09: the floods go dark before the man is aboard, not after. SHOULD FIX**
- Ch 08 (Dane's addition): "A man coming over the stern under those lights is a man I can see." / Beth: "And when he's aboard, they go dark."
- Conflicts with: Ch 09, where Beth throws the breakers right after the brow comes in, and "The floods went out" while Doucette is still paddling, before "Aluminum touched steel at the port quarter." Ch 09 also restates the signal a different way: "If the floods go dark, it's the water. The stern." That is a second version of a fact Ch 08 already settled.
- Fix: in Ch 09, have her kill the floods at the knock or the first boot on the rung, and cut the "And the signal" exchange down to what Ch 08 didn't cover.

**5. Ch 08: the reason given for suspecting Russ doesn't match Ch 04. SHOULD FIX**
- Ch 08: "He checks the summer houses for frozen pipes. He'd have seen smoke at the Ashby place by Monday."
- Conflicts with: Ch 04 (Sunday), where Russ says "Heard a Generac running out on the point last night." He knew about Ashby on Saturday night, from the generator, before the Day 4 leak. "Smoke ... by Monday" points at the fire, not at knowing beforehand.
- Fix: "He heard the Ashby generator Saturday night and told us so on Sunday."

**6. Ch 08: "Nobody hands a landing time to a constable" goes against the book's own logic. SHOULD FIX**
- Ch 08: "Russ didn't have the landing. Nobody hands a landing time to a constable."
- Conflicts with: World Rule 9 (the coordinator was asked to keep the landing clear, which on an island is a constable's job) and Ch 06 (Russ watches Beth pace the bait-shed exit at the landing on Day 6, asks "How long you got him for?" and times the sets). The fire company did get the time: Walt is at the firehouse at 6 a.m. offering the EMT.
- Fix: give Dane a concrete basis, for example that Pike's 7 a.m. answer named only the coordinator and the fire company standby. Then "at one table" points at Tess and Ned's supper without the generalization. Nothing in Ch 09–10 contradicts clearing Russ.

**7. Ch 10: "about where he'd told her they would" has no setup now. SHOULD FIX**
- Ch 10 coda: "The costs had landed about where he'd told her they would: a suspension served without pay, a letter in his file ... and a transfer to the District of Maine".
- Conflicts with: Ch 08, where the only costs Dane names to Beth are being off the detail and the case, Moreau telling the defense, and an internal affairs referral. Ch 10's Night 9 no longer mentions a suspension or a transfer. Ch 03 ("on loan to Portland because they were short this month") sets up Maine being short-staffed, but nothing on the page says he would be suspended or transferred, or that he asked to be.
- Fix: add a few words to his Ch 08 list ("a suspension, likely, and wherever they send me after"), or change the coda to "worse than he'd told her, and better".

**8. Ch 10: retired father still driving a shift. SHOULD FIX**
- Ch 10: "My father drove the Bridge Street route for thirty years, and he's retired, and every Christmas he still takes the holiday shift".
- Conflicts with: itself, since a retired driver doesn't take shifts. Bible and Ch 04 ("drove a city bus there for thirty years") are fine. Bridge Street matches Ch 08; "three of us" fits (the bible gives no siblings); Kenny covering fits the bible.
- Fix: "he still rides the holiday route in the front seat" or "he worked every Christmas shift for thirty years".

**9. Ch 08 / Ch 09: Beth's condition is broken at the 3 a.m. call. SHOULD FIX**
- Ch 08 Beth: "Nothing about me goes in that book or up that phone that I haven't heard first." Dane: "From here you hear everything I hear, both sides of every call".
- Conflicts with: Ch 09: "Dane stood on the open deck with the Iridium and gave Pike the night in order." Beth is not shown there. This weakens completion question 2.
- Fix: put her beside him, or add a line saying he gave it to her first.

**10. Ch 08: "had run a roll at three as well" implies something that happened more than once. SHOULD FIX**
- Ch 08: "Since the second system came in Tess had run a roll at three as well, for the back-road people who went to bed at dark."
- Conflicts with: World Rule 5 (net on 68 at 0800 and 1800 only) and the timing of the system (the bible says "arriving Day 7 evening"; Ch 06 says "in by tomorrow evening"). At most one earlier three o'clock roll is possible. Channel 68 is right and doesn't conflict with Ch 09's six o'clock net or the channel 9 call.
- Fix: "Since the second system came in Tess had added a roll at three". The orchestrator should add the 1500 roll to World Rule 5.

**11. Bible: the emergency-management route still says Portland called the EMD directly. SHOULD FIX (bible)**
- Research note 28: "Pike's office calls the EMD on Day 6". World Rule 9: "Pike's office called 'local emergency management'". Tess entry 4: "As emergency management director she was asked that afternoon".
- Conflicts with: v4 Ch 08 (Ops → county → island coordinator). No chapter line conflicts. Ch 06's "Your office coordinated with local emergency management" works with either route.
- Fix: update the bible's three lines to the county route.

**12. Ch 09: Ned "heard sixteen at midnight". NOTE**
- Ch 09: "He had sat the night in Russ's kitchen by the scanner and heard sixteen at midnight."
- Russ's call on 16 comes a few minutes after an 11:10 fight, so not midnight. The scanner on 16 matches Ch 08's spare handheld and Ned's daylight walk. Ned's second walk, up the Harbor Road at dusk with his duffel, is consistent but isn't covered by Dane's daylight condition.
- Fix: "heard sixteen before midnight".

**13. Ch 08: harbor "bar" vs the Ashby bar. NOTE**
- Ch 08 (Pike): "it can't get over the bar or lie alongside this pier".
- Until now "the bar" has meant the Ashby gravel bar, and the bible gives the harbor mouth no bar. The Day 7 lifeboat came to the town landing float, and Pike's reason doesn't address that option. Also, a 47-foot motor lifeboat is the heavy-weather boat, yet the dragger is expected to sail that same evening.
- Fix: "can't cross the harbor-mouth ledges in the dark".

**14. Ch 07 / Ch 08 / Ch 09: Icom location drifts. NOTE**
- Ch 07 ends with it "on the console beside the fixed set". Ch 08 first light: "zipped in its bag inside her float coat". 3 p.m.: "On the console the Icom". 3:30: "took the Icom out of its freezer bag and set it on the chart table". Ch 09: in her float coat pocket.
- Fix: one consistent placement per scene.

**15. Headings. NOTE**
- Ch 06: "**Days 5–7 — Tuesday to Thursday**" doesn't use World Rule 3's form ("Day 5 to Day 7"). Ch 07 and Ch 08 now have identical headings, "Day 7 to Day 8 — Thursday night to Friday". That is correct (Day 8 is Friday, November 20), but Rule 3's examples don't include "night", and the bible calendar table still lists Ch 08 as Day 8 only. The scene headers in Ch 07–08 agree with the tide clock and the times in the text.
- Fix: make Ch 06 "Day 5 to Day 7 — Tuesday to Thursday". Consider "Day 8 — Friday" for Ch 08 or "Day 7 to Day 8 — Thursday to Friday", and update the calendar table.

**16. Ch 07: Beth's plan to call Pike after concluding that Portland leaks. NOTE**
- Ch 07: "every one had gone up a phone to Portland and come back down with a man at the door" then "ask for the marshals in Portland. For Pike, by name."
- "Half the bay listening" partly covers it. One clause on why a public daylight call is safe even if Portland leaks would close it.

**17. Ch 10: light at Pruett. NOTE**
- Ch 10: they leave Cutter as the "sky [is] coming pink"; after a 45-minute crossing, "At Pruett the sodiums still burned over the ramp in the half-dark". Sunrise is 6:30, so it should be full daylight by then.

## Completion questions (page only)
1. **Why does Beth make her Ch 07 choice? Yes.** The notebook, an armed man she can't check, the 17 minutes, the shore route, and a locked wheelhouse with a fixed set to wait for daylight. The Ned risk is covered by the lock. Her denial about Tess is visible. Small wrinkle: issue 16.
2. **Have both lovers named what they hid, and do they behave differently afterward? Mostly.** Both admissions are on the page, and the 3 p.m. and 3:30 calls are shared word for word. The 3 a.m. call (issue 9) is a lapse, and Night 9 has Dane on the porch alone after telling her first.
3. **Why must the Ch 09 confrontation happen, given the safer options in Ch 08? Partly.** The structure is there: the dragger stowing to sail at dark, Pike closing the landing, the state police and blue-light options, and Pike authorizing the hold. But the roll-call clue is contradicted by Ch 03, 04 and 09 (issue 1), and "The sea already allowed it" (issue 3) reopens the safer option.
